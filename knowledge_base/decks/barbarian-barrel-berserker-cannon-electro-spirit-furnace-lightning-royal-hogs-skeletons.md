## Classification

Primary archetype: [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md). 3.0 average elixir with 1-elixir Skeletons and Electro Spirit and a 3-elixir Cannon cycling back to a single 5-elixir win condition, with Lightning used as the opening tool rather than as a beatdown finisher.

## Why this deck works

The deck's whole plan is to defend for negative-to-neutral elixir with a five-card cheap core (Cannon, Skeletons, Electro Spirit, Berserker, Barbarian Barrel) and then convert the cycle advantage into repeated Royal Hogs sets backed by Lightning. In this match nothing decisive happened in regular time — both towers only took chip — and the game was won in overtime by sending hogs at the left bridge on every window where the opponent could not afford both the Bomb Tower and the Poison. Furnace fills the gaps: played whenever the opponent's Poison is out of cycle, it stacks passive chip and covers swarms so the Cannon does not have to. Lightning is treated as an opening tool, not a snipe — Ryley twice refuses to spend it on a single building and instead cycles an evolved Cannon. Ryley pitches it as an anti-meta answer deck: "you just have a lot of answers to everything with this one" (t=59-61s), with Graveyard decks the harder half of the mirror-ish Royal Hogs matchup.
