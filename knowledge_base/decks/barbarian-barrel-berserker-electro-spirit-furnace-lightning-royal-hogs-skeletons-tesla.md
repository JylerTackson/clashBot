## Classification

Primary archetype: [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md). 2.9 average elixir with 1-elixir Skeletons and Electro Spirit cycling back to a single 5-elixir win condition, defending with an Evo Tesla and Furnace and using Lightning as a defensive clear rather than a beatdown finisher.

## Why this deck works

The deck defends for neutral-to-positive elixir with a five-card cheap core (Evo Tesla, Skeletons, Electro Spirit, Berserker, Barbarian Barrel) and converts the cycle advantage into repeated Evo Royal Hogs sets at the bridge, always sent while a defence is already running so the opponent cannot spend a big spell offensively. Furnace is played on the back line as a defensive spawner whose Fire Spirits chip the tower for free - "you just kind of use it as a broken defensive unit now and don't really worry about getting any offensive pressure with it" (t=173-183) - which is where most of this match's damage came from. The Heroic Berserker's Savage Survival gives the cheap defence a way to survive a Giant Skeleton or stay alive on the tower, and Lightning is held for pushes with two or more targets rather than sniped at a building. It is the Tesla variant of Piggies Lightning: the Evo Tesla trades the Cannon's cheaper cycle for air coverage, which is what answered the opponent's Balloon in overtime.
