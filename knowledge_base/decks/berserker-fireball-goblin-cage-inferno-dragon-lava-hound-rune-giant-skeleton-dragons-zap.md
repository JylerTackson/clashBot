## Classification

Primary archetype: [beatdown](../archetypes/beatdown.md). A 7-elixir Lava Hound win condition supported by Rune Giant, Inferno Dragon and Skeleton Dragons, played as slow full-lane air pushes with Goblin Cage and Berserker holding the ground behind it.

## Why this deck works

The deck stacks a Lava Hound with three air support cards, so any opponent short on anti-air simply cannot interact with the push — which is exactly the matchup Ryley describes here ("I knew that Ian's deck had no air defense", t=920). Rune Giant is the glue: she is the cheapest Giant at 4 elixir, tanks for the Lava Pups after the Hound pops, and enchants the fast-hitting Berserker, Skeleton Dragons and Inferno Dragon for bonus damage on every third attack. Goblin Cage and Berserker cover the ground lane for 6 elixir combined so the expensive air package can keep cycling, while Fireball and Zap are held back as a finishing pair rather than spent early. The known hole is dedicated ground control: Ryley says on camera that the list has no Goblin Drill answer and no Valkyrie-type card, and the opponent's drills chip one of his crown towers down to 152 HP over the game.
