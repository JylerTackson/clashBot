- [Royal Delivery](../cards/royal-delivery.md) — 3 elixir, Common Spell, targets ground_and_air
- [Wall Breakers](../cards/wall-breakers.md) — 2 elixir, Epic Troop, targets buildings

## Classification

Primary archetype: [bait](../archetypes/bait.md). Goblin Barrel and Wall Breakers are the win conditions and the spirits, barrel and Wall Breakers all bait the small spells that answer each other, while the 2.9 average cost keeps the barrel coming back around.

## Why this deck works

The bait core is unchanged from any Log Bait list - Goblin Barrel plus Wall Breakers, thrown as a pair so one spell cannot answer both, backed by 1-elixir spirits for cycle. What Ronin adds is the one thing bait decks normally lack: a 5-elixir mini-tank that beats the heavy melee cards those decks fold to, which is how Ryley frames the matchup here ("obviously Ronin does really, really well into mega knight", t=29-31). Magic Archer and Bomb Tower cover the ranged and swarm defence the Ronin cannot, and Royal Delivery is the catch-all for anything that gets past them. The deck's damage comes from repetition rather than one big push: in this game the enemy left tower fell from 4858 to 277 across a single 25-second barrel-and-Wall-Breakers sequence played while the opponent committed a Mega Knight in the other lane. The stated weakness is cycle - "it does kind of mess up your card cycle a lot" (t=33-37) - and Ryley's own left tower was ground to 2091 before the swing, so the list can be out-chipped while the expensive cards are stuck in hand.
