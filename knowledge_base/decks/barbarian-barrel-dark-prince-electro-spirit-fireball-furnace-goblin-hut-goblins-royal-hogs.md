## Classification

Primary archetype: [bridge-spam](../archetypes/bridge-spam.md). No tank and no big spell win condition: every card is a cheap threat dropped at the bridge (Royal Hogs, Dark Prince, Barbarian Barrel, Goblins) to force answers, with the two huts holding the ground behind it.

## Why this deck works

Two spawners (Furnace and Goblin Hut) generate free chip and free defenders, so the deck can defend a Lava Hound push with elixir left over and immediately spend it at the bridge. Royal Hogs are the pressure valve rather than the whole plan — the threat of the Evolution in cycle stops the opponent from spending a spell on the huts, and stops them committing to a Hound at all. Dark Prince covers the ground defence that the huts cannot (Valkyrie, Barbarians) and doubles as a bridge threat, with the Heroic Destructive Dismount available when the opponent is already stretched. Fireball is both the defensive answer to Skeleton Dragons and the finisher, since two Fireballs is a real win condition once the hut chip has worked. The weakness on show is air defence: the one Hound push that did get through took a tower from full to ~1720.
