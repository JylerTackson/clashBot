- [Mighty Miner](../cards/mighty-miner.md) — 4 elixir, Champion Troop, targets ground
- [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md) — 1 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground
- [The Log](../cards/the-log.md) — 2 elixir, Legendary Spell, targets ground

## Classification

Primary archetype: [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md). Seven of the eight cards cost 1-3 elixir and the deck wins by replaying a 4-elixir Hog Rider plus a 3-elixir Earthquake every rotation rather than by building one push.

## Why this deck works

The cheap four-card cycle of Skeletons, Electro Spirit, The Log and Earthquake means the Hog Rider is back in hand before the opponent's building or swarm is, which is exactly how this game is won: eight Evo Cannon placements and eleven Skeletons deploys absorb an Elixir Golem beatdown deck while the Hog collects a couple of hits every rotation. Evo Cannon in the middle columns covers both lanes for 3 elixir, so a beatdown opponent cannot force a lane commitment, and Mighty Miner's Explosive Escape gives the deck a tank-killer and a swarm answer in one card. Earthquake does the deck's real thinking: behind a bridge Hog it is both chip and a pre-emptive Skeleton Army answer, which is what closes this match (t=237 and t=264). The known hole is a big supported tank plus Freeze — one such push here cost 2300 tower HP and forced overtime.
