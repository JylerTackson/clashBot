## Classification

Primary archetype: [bridge-spam](../archetypes/bridge-spam.md). Ryley names it himself at 972s — "this bridge spam deck" — and pilots it that way: no tank and no build-up, with Battle Ram, Elite Barbarians and Cannon Cart placed on bridge or pocket tiles the moment a defence survives, and Heal Spirit layered on top.

## Why this deck works

Every offensive card is a lane-specific rush that can be sent the instant defence survives, so the deck never has to build a push: at clock 2:07-2:03 Elite Barbarians on the right bridge plus a Battle Ram in the opposite lane stripped a crown tower from 3052 to 806 HP in twenty seconds. Against expensive decks that is close to free — Ryley's own reason for the pick was "everything in his deck is really, really expensive." Cannon Cart is the flexible slot: a defensive body behind the towers, a bridge threat that forces out the opponent's hardest defensive card (here the Evolution Executioner), and a pocket play once a tower is low. The Hero Valkyrie provides the splash the bridge units need plus a 3-elixir Wild Whirlwind dash he uses to reach towers and back-line support directly, and Mother Witch turns the opponent's own defenders into Cursed Hogs that keep an Executioner/Bomb Tower core distracted. With Arrows as the only spell and no defensive building, the deck has to win the damage race: in this match neither crown tower ever fell and it was decided on the overtime tiebreaker.

Result: a tiebreaker win — no crown fell, enemy towers finished on 455 and 135 HP against his 2764 / 3052.
- The core loop is a two-lane commit the moment defence survives: Elite Barbarians at (14, 14) plus Heal Spirit, Battle Ram at (2, 15) in the opposite lane, all inside five seconds at clock 2:07-2:03, behind "the surviving cannon cart". The enemy left tower went 3052 -> 806 in the next twenty seconds. His justification is the elixir asymmetry: "everything in his deck is really, really expensive" (1019-1022s).
- Cannon Cart is used as a deliberate Evolution-bait at the bridge, not just a defensive body: at clock 1:49 he plays it at (3, 15) "knowing he had no zappies, no miner, no elixir for giant skelly. So he had to evo executioner on my Cannon Cart", and rates pulling the Evo out of rotation as the key gain — "he'd have to play two more just to get to the Evo" (1027-1052s).
- The Hero Valkyrie is the deck's swing card: he spends the 3-elixir Wild Whirlwind five times (t=1068.3, 1090.5, 1122.9, 1199.5, 1205.8) and always as a dash into the tower or onto the Executioner — "I popped the Valkyrie ability just to go all the way up to the tower" (1068s), "I wanted to go up to the executioner before he had a chance to take out my mother witch" (1179s). Its failure mode is timing: "my Valkyrie just waited a second too long and I wasn't able to get the value that I wanted" (1100s).
- Mother Witch is played as cycle and as a defence-disruptor, never as a push leader — on the Giant Skeleton "just to cycle" (1083s), behind his own tower with Arrows against the Executioner push (1119s), and in the pocket for Cursed Hogs: "the piggies really disrupt the defense cuz he has an executioner and a bomb tower" (1171-1177s).
- Overtime is played as continuous pressure rather than defence: Battle Ram at the bridge, then Cannon Cart, Elite Barbarians and Heal Spirit dropped in the opponent's pocket. "I had to keep up the pressure because if I just let go then he would just build up a massive push [and] win the game" (1136s) and "it was at the point where like he couldn't ignore any damage. He had to respond to everything" (1157-1162s).
