## Classification

Primary archetype: [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md). A 2.6-elixir eight-card list with no troop win condition: Rocket and a bridge Evo Princess are the only damage, and every other slot (Evo Cannon, Berserker Hero, Ice Spirit, Skeletons, The Log, Royal Delivery) exists to defend cheaply and cycle back to them.

## Why this deck works

Everything in the list is 1-3 elixir except the Rocket, so the deck can answer a bridge-spam opponent's threats one at a time and still be back at the Cannon before the next one arrives. The Evo Cannon is placed reactively and centrally to pull Battle Rams and line up on Mother Witches, with Ice Spirit, Skeletons and Berserker layered on top of it; the Berserker Hero's Savage Survival turns a 2-elixir troop into a Giant Skeleton block for a few seconds. Damage comes from an Evo Princess dropped at the bridge only when a Cannon is already banked, plus a Rocket that is as often spent on defence as on a tower. The trade-off is that the deck has exactly one heavy answer: hold the Rocket a beat too long, or misplace the Cannon, and a bridge-spam push with a Mother Witch behind it becomes unanswerable.
