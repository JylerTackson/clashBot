## Classification

Primary archetype: [beatdown](../archetypes/beatdown.md). A 7-elixir Electro Giant is the only win condition and every other slot is defensive support or a spell, so the deck wins by surviving to a heavy push rather than by cycling to chip damage.

## Why this deck works

The Electro Giant's Zap Pack punishes exactly the fast, cheap attackers a cycle opponent leads with, so the same card that defends the Mighty Miner and Hog Rider becomes the push once it survives. Bowler and Tornado are the engine behind it: Tornado at the tile in front of the King tower bunches a ground push into Bowler's rolling boulder, and whatever is left walks back over the bridge with the tank. Baby Dragon covers air and the Goblin Hut buys permanent distraction value against building-targeting win conditions while Golden Knight runs the flank for damage the tank cannot reach. Its weakness showed up clearly in this match: against a defensive building (Tesla) plus a chip engine the Electro Giant cannot be made to connect, and the deck has no second win condition to fall back on.
