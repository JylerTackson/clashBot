## Classification

Primary archetype: [bait](../archetypes/bait.md). Goblin Barrel and Wall Breakers plus a swarm of 1-elixir cards bait the opponent's small spells, and the 2.0 average cost lets the barrel come back faster than any answer.

## Why this deck works

The list runs no spell at all: every card is either a bait threat (Evo Goblin Barrel, Wall Breakers) or a cheap defensive body (Cannon, Evo Dart Goblin, Hero Berserker, Ice Spirit, Electro Spirit, Skeletons), so at a 2.0 average it re-presents the same left-lane barrel-plus-Wall-Breakers threat faster than the opponent can rotate a counter. The defensive core is the Hero Berserker with the Evolved Dart Goblin behind her - Ryley's stated claim is "You're just able to defend pretty much anything with hero berserker plus evo dark goblin cycle" (t=38-40) - with the Cannon reserved for building-targeting pushes and the two spirits as resets. Because it cannot spell anything away, it wins by picking one lane and taxing elixir, then holding the other side with three or four cheap cards per push. Its weakness shows in the same game: a Three Musketeers deck with double Elixir Collector and two evolutions eventually forces him to eat 2140 HP on the right tower, and the win comes down to tower health rather than a crown.
