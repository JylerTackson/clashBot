- [Battle Ram](../cards/battle-ram.md) — 4 elixir, Rare Troop, targets buildings

- [Zappies](../cards/zappies.md) — 4 elixir, Rare Troop, targets ground_and_air

## Classification

Primary archetype: [bridge-spam](../archetypes/bridge-spam.md). Bandit, Battle Ram and the Heroic Valkyrie are dropped at the bridge off counter-pushes with no tank and no slow build-up, and Ryley states the plan outright at t=98: "we are playing bridge spam so we can always counter push off of that".

## Why this deck works

Every threat in the list is fast and lane-specific, so a counter-push starts the moment a defence survives: Bandit dashes past the first defender, Battle Ram forces a building or a swarm, and the Heroic Valkyrie plus her 3-elixir Wild Whirlwind ability covers the last tiles to the tower — the play Ryley repeats all game and calls "just guaranteed to go up to the tower". Mother Witch is the value engine behind all of it: her curse turns whatever the opponent defends with into building-targeting Hogs, which in this match ate an Evo Mortar outright, so a failed defence still costs the opponent a second wave. Heal Spirit and Zappies keep the cycle cheap (3.75 average elixir) and let him re-open a lane every few seconds, with the split Zappies doubling as standing pressure. Freeze is the one purely reactive slot, held specifically for an opposing ability-boosted Heroic Valkyrie, which he says is the only way to stop it.
