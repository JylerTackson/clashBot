## Classification

Primary archetype: [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md). Eight cards at 2.75 average elixir with no tank and a single 4-elixir chip win condition, won by out-cycling the opponent and answering every push on his own half.

## Why this deck works

The only win condition is Goblin Drill in the enemy corner, and the other seven cards exist to make the cycle back to it survivable. Cannon, Bomber, Fire Spirit and Goblins cover almost every ground push for 1-3 elixir, and Hero Valkyrie is the one 4-elixir body held for Cannon Cart and Elite Barbarians — with the 3-elixir Wild Whirlwind available to finish a threat rather than trade her for it. The two spells split the work: Evo Giant Snowball is a prediction tool against Skeletons and Minions that also adds real tower damage on top of a drill, and Poison removes the recurring ranged problems (Goblinstein, Evo Mortar) that no cheap troop answers. Against a slower siege deck the plan is not to build a push at all — drill on cycle, defend on your own half, and let chip accumulate faster than theirs, which is exactly how this match was won.
