## Classification

Primary archetype: [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md). A 3.1-average list whose 1/1/2/2 defensive core lets Royal Hogs come back before any counter does, which is exactly how Ryley pilots it ("a permanent state of just going Royal Hogs over and over again", t=162-164); the two spawners give it a defensive, trade-first control layer underneath the cycle.

## Why this deck works

The two spawners — [Furnace](../cards/furnace.md) and [Goblin Hut](../cards/goblin-hut.md), both placed on the centre of Ryley's own half rather than in a back corner — mean there are always Fire Spirits and Spear Goblins on the board, so an opponent building a Royal Giant or beatdown push has to fight through chip before it ever reaches a tower. That buys the time for a 3.1-cost cycle to bring [Royal Hogs](../cards/royal-hogs.md) back at the river every rotation; against a deck without a reliable splash answer the Hogs connect over and over instead of once. Defence is entirely cheap and entirely on his own half — [Berserker](../cards/berserker.md) in her Hero form, [Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md), [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md) and [The Log](../cards/the-log.md) — so no defensive exchange ever costs him the tempo he needs for the next Hogs. [Lightning](../cards/lightning.md) is the one expensive card and it is deliberately hoarded: Ryley refuses it twice on camera and cashes it only as the closing spell, which keeps the deck's effective cycle far shorter than its 3.1 average suggests.
