## Classification

Primary archetype: [bait](../archetypes/bait.md). Three cheap cards that all pull the same small spell (Goblin Gang, Dart Goblin, Suspicious Bush) around a ~2.9-elixir cycle, with Elite Barbarians and a bridge-placed Berserker as the punish once the spell is gone.

## Why this deck works

The deck baits with volume rather than with a single barrel: Goblin Gang, Dart Goblin and Suspicious Bush all want the opponent's Log or small spell, and at 2-3 elixir each they cycle back before it does. Once the spell is committed, the two real threats land — Evolved Elite Barbarians placed centre-back so the pair splits and forces answers in both lanes (t=33-35s, t=113.1s), and the 2-elixir Hero Berserker dropped straight at the bridge as a standalone threat that is too cheap to trade against (t=60.1s, 114.1s, 128.1s). Defence stays cheap and layered — Goblin Gang or Dart Goblin first, The Log on the swarm, an Electro Spirit on the leftovers — which is what lets him keep spending on the bridge instead of on his own side. In this match that pattern ground the opponent's left tower from full to 176 HP while conceding 538, but the deck's own commentary caveat applies: both carries are on the emergency nerf list, the Berserker's Savage Survival going 4s to 3.5s and the Evo spears 284 to 220 damage with the rage trail cut from 3.5s to 2.5s.
