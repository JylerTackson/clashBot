## Classification

Primary archetype: [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md). At 2.6 average elixir with two cheap win conditions (Wall Breakers and Miner) replayed every rotation for chip rather than assembled into one push, it is a cycle deck; the Bomb Tower plus Poison defensive core gives it a control second gear.

## Why this deck works

Two 2-3 elixir win conditions mean the opponent has to answer something almost every cycle, and neither Wall Breakers nor Miner cares which lane the defence is set up in. The Hero Berserker is the piece that makes the chip stick: because the opponent cannot safely ignore a Berserker that might turn invincible, cheap distraction plays force real defensive commitments, and the Wall Breakers land behind them — Ryley says outright that "the threat of me going for the ability is actually stronger than me literally clicking the button." Defensively the deck is complete for its cost: The Log and Evo Bats handle swarms, Bomb Tower holds ground pushes and siege buildings, Spear Goblins cover air, and Poison is the one value spell held for a clumped defence or a committed Mortar. The trade-off is that it has no answer to a big tank beyond the Bomb Tower and no way to close a game quickly, so it needs the full three minutes of chip — which is exactly how this match played out.
