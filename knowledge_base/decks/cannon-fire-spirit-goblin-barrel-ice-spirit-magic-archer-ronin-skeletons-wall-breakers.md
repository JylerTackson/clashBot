# Ronin Log Bait Ryley

- [Cannon](../cards/cannon.md) — 3 elixir, Common Building, targets ground
- [Fire Spirit](../cards/fire-spirit.md) — 1 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground_and_air

- [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md) — 1 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground
- [Wall Breakers](../cards/wall-breakers.md) — 2 elixir, Epic Troop, targets buildings

## Classification

Primary archetype: [bait](../archetypes/bait.md). Ryley calls it a "Log Bait deck... with these quick cycle Log Bait decks" (21-27s): Goblin Barrel, Wall Breakers, Skeletons and the spirits all bait the same small spells, and the deck cycles back to them constantly.

## Why this deck works

The bait half is standard — Goblin Barrel, Wall Breakers, Skeletons, Ice Spirit and Fire Spirit all die to The Log or Arrows, so the opponent's one small spell can never cover everything, and at roughly 2.6 average elixir the barrel comes back before their answer does. What Ronin adds is a defensive anchor the archetype normally lacks: for 5 elixir a fast mini-tank whose parry deflects 200% of a melee attacker's own damage, which turns the mid-ladder heavies these decks fear (P.E.K.K.A., Mega Knight, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Golden Knight, Bandit, Royal Ghost) into the deck's best matchups — in this match the P.E.K.K.A. "almost one shot itself" (138-140s) into a Cannon-plus-Ronin defence. The Cannon covers the ranged and building-targeting threats the parry does nothing about, and the Hero Magic Archer supplies the air coverage and long-range chip that Ronin cannot, with Triple Threat held back to dodge spells and dashes. The recurring cost is that resets and stuns cancel the parry — Electro Dragon is called out by name — and that a swarm or anything ranged simply walks around the mechanic.
