## Classification

Primary archetype: [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md). A 3.13-elixir shell whose plan is to cycle Skeletons, Electro Spirit and Barbarian Barrel back to the Evolved Royal Hogs faster than the opponent can re-answer them keeps Cycle primary, while the Goblin Hut value engine and a defence built almost entirely around the Hero Ice Wizard's freeze make Control a real secondary.

## Why this deck works

The Hero Ice Wizard restores a 3-elixir splash slot to the old Royal Hogs / Goblin Hut cycle deck, which is exactly what Ryley says killed it: "you had to replace the ice wizard with the wizard, but then you would just lose most of the cycle versus cycle matchups." At 3.13 average elixir the deck can throw a bridge Royal Hogs away purely to reach the Evolution, because the defence behind it is cheap and repeatable. Goblin Hut placed in the middle of his own half every ~30 seconds pulls building-targeters off the tower and stacks Spear Goblins for free, so Ryley can hold Lightning for the moment a Bomb Tower and a second target line up behind the hogs. The hero's ability is the safety valve that makes the greedy spell affordable — "I'm even comfortable going for the lightning here because I just know that we have the hero ice wizard defensively" — and against a Balloon it is close to a hard counter on its own: "what is he even supposed to do about that? That just frozen in place." The weakness he names himself is swarm air pressure: with only Lightning and Barbarian Barrel as spells, "I don't really have the greatest answers for bats."
