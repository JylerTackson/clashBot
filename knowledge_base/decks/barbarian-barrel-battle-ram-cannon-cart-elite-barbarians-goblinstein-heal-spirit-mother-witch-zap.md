# Best 8 Cards Bridge Spam (Ryley)

## Cards

- [Barbarian Barrel](../cards/barbarian-barrel.md) — 2 elixir, Epic Spell, targets ground
- [Battle Ram](../cards/battle-ram.md) — 4 elixir, Rare Troop, targets buildings
- [Cannon Cart](../cards/cannon-cart.md) — 5 elixir, Epic Troop, targets ground
- [Elite Barbarians](../cards/elite-barbarians.md) — 6 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground
- [Goblinstein](../cards/goblinstein.md) — 5 elixir, Champion Troop, targets ground_and_air
- [Heal Spirit](../cards/heal-spirit.md) — 1 elixir, Rare Troop, targets ground_and_air
- [Mother Witch](../cards/mother-witch.md) — 4 elixir, Legendary Troop, targets ground_and_air
- [Zap](../cards/zap.md) — 2 elixir, Common Spell, targets ground_and_air

## Classification

Primary archetype: [bridge-spam](../archetypes/bridge-spam.md). Every win-condition card (Battle Ram, Evo Elite Barbarians, Goblinstein, Cannon Cart) is dropped at or past the bridge to force an immediate answer, with no tank-behind-the-tower push at any point; Ryley calls it 'a very real bridge spam deck' himself (t=88).

## Why this deck works

The eight cards were chosen by Royal API usage/win-rate ranking rather than by synergy, but they land on a coherent bridge-spam shell: three threats that demand an answer the moment they touch the bridge (Battle Ram, Evo Elite Barbarians, Goblinstein), a Cannon Cart that doubles as the deck's only real defensive anchor, and four cheap cards (Barbarian Barrel, Zap, Heal Spirit, Mother Witch) that keep a committed push alive instead of building a new one. Mother Witch is the piece that makes the shell more than a pile of good cards: her curse turns the cheap swarm defenders that bridge spam normally gets stonewalled by into Cursed Hogs, which is exactly how this game is won. The weakness is equally clear — with no building and no big spell, a Hog Rider connects freely and a Bomb Tower plus Evo Knight plus Evo Firecracker defence blanks every ground push, which is why Ryley spends most of regulation behind on damage.
