## Classification

Primary archetype: [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md). A 6-elixir Royal Giant win condition supported by a ~3.1-elixir list whose bottom half (Skeletons, Electro Spirit, Barbarian Barrel) exists to bring him and the Fireball back to hand faster than the opponent's answer.

## Why this deck works

This is the popular EvoRG FishBoy Hunter cycle list with Royal Ghost cut for the Heroic Valkyrie, and the swap changes what the deck does with a completed defence. Royal Giant still does the chip work from outside tower range while Fisherman hooks the high-DPS defender off him and Evolution Hunter melts anything that walks into point-blank range, and the 1-elixir Skeletons and Electro Spirit keep the cycle short enough that Ryley can spend the spirit on predictions ("E-Spirit here to try to predict the Skeletons", 353s) rather than saving it. The Valkyrie adds a second, independent threat: at 4 elixir she can be dropped at the bridge in the opposite lane to the Royal Giant, and her 3-elixir Wild Whirlwind converts that presence into a tower kill on its own — at clock 0:15 it took the tower from 1517 to 222 HP with the Fireball still in hand. Against a control deck full of mid-court support the ability is also the deck's best value spell, which is how Ryley frames it: "he's going to need to go for some Magic Archers and a lot of cards in the middle, which will give us some value kind of like the Golden Knight." The cost is the same one the base list has — the anti-air is only Hunter, Electro Spirit and Fireball, and the Valkyrie cannot touch air at all.
