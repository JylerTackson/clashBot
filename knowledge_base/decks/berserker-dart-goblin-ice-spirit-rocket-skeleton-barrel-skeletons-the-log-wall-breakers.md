## Classification

Primary archetype: [bait](../archetypes/bait.md). Wall Breakers, Skeleton Barrel, Skeletons and Dart Goblin all demand a different small-spell or swarm answer and are thrown in overlapping pairs at 2.6 average elixir, with The Log and Rocket as the spell win conditions behind them.

## Why this deck works

Ryley's post-nerf take on hyper bait swaps the old stack of spirits for a stack of win conditions: "instead of having a lot of spirits in hyper bait, now you just have a bunch of win conditions with a rocket and a log" (42-46s). Wall Breakers, Skeleton Barrel and Skeletons overlap so heavily that one small spell can never cover a wave, and at 2.6 average elixir the deck comes back around before the opponent's answer does. The Hero Berserker is what makes the constant aggression affordable — Savage Survival means she cannot be removed from the board, so a 2-elixir card holds a defence on its own and frees the rest of the hand for the bridge. The Log and Rocket are treated as damage, not insurance: the Rocket is spent on an Elite Barbarians pair mid-game and again on the tower to close, which is the intended sequence when the chip damage has already stacked up.

Match [TOP 5 Decks in Clash Royale for the New Meta](../matches/5bU83eIW8Yg-m0.0.md):
- Ryley names the deck himself at 36s — "we are going to be starting off here with this hyper bait deck" — and frames it as the post-balance-change rebuild of the archetype: "instead of having a lot of spirits in hyper bait, now you just have a bunch of win conditions with a rocket and a log" (42-46s).
- The Berserker slot is the Hero variant ("The Hero Berserker", 56s) and is the reason the list can defend so cheaply: "you're able to just abuse the fact that it cannot be taken off of the board to just get some super nice defenses and kind of get away with just spamming your opponent like crazy" (65-72s). The Skeleton Barrel and Wall Breakers slots are evolutions by his own description (87s, 150s).
- Piloting rule stated at 93-103s: "you just never want to let go. You just always want to keep on going aggressive. Never really give them a chance to breathe and chill out." In practice every defensive rotation is immediately followed by a bridge threat — Ice Spirit + Skeletons defence at clock 1:31 into a bridge Berserker at 1:25; Skeletons + Dart Goblin defence at 0:42 into Wall Breakers, Skeleton Barrel and Skeletons at 0:38.
- The threats are always stacked in pairs so one spell cannot cover them: Wall Breakers + Skeleton Barrel at clock 0:56-0:55, and Berserker + Wall Breakers at 0:07. The enemy right tower fell from 4858 to 460 HP across those waves.
- Rocket does double duty — defensively on top of Elite Barbarians at clock 0:50 ("they're way too scary to actually think about dealing with in any other way") and as the finisher on the opponent's half at 0:03 ("just rocket cuz that princess locked in").
- Result: a win, called at 176s — "Very nice win to start off the video."
