## Classification

Primary archetype: [beatdown](../archetypes/beatdown.md). Giant plus Sparky with Rage support is a heavy, elixir-hungry push deck that wins in double and triple elixir, with Evolved Elite Barbarians providing the bridge pressure that fills the gap while elixir is banked.

## Why this deck works

The deck banks elixir behind Wizard and Valkyrie defences and then commits everything to one lane: Giant in front, Evolved Elite Barbarians beside the tower, Rage on top. Rage is the glue — it is never used to open a push, only dropped on one that has already connected, which is what turns a Skeleton Army block into tower damage. Arrows is the deck's only other spell and does two jobs Ryley names explicitly: killing the Princess he cannot otherwise reach, and clearing the Skarmy on the closing push. The weakness he states himself is that the list carries no stun, so a defensive Inferno Tower cannot be reset and the answer is simply to out-elixir it. Sparky is played almost entirely as a defensive anchor in the back rather than as a win condition.
