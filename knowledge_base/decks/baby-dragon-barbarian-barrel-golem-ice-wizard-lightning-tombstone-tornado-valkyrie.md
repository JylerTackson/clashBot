## Classification

Primary archetype: [beatdown](../archetypes/beatdown.md). An 8-elixir Golem is the win condition and everything else (Tombstone, Ice Wizard, Baby Dragon, Valkyrie, Barbarian Barrel, Tornado, Lightning) exists to survive to double elixir and then support one big central tank push.

## Why this deck works

The deck plays as a slow, central beatdown: Golem goes down in the middle of the court so a siege opponent can never open the opposite lane, and Tombstone is pre-placed centrally both to tank an X-Bow and to bait spells. Tombstone, Ice Wizard, Baby Dragon and Barbarian Barrel cover the ground and air chip that beatdown decks normally leak damage to, while the Heroic Valkyrie doubles as the bridge answer that deletes a siege building outright with her ability instead of defending it. On offence the payoff is Valkyrie ability plus Tornado to drag defenders off the tower, with Lightning reserved for whatever building or support survives. Ryley's own framing of the matchup is that the spell cycle, not the tank, is what has to be protected: "I wasn't worried about him actually stopping the golem. I was worried about just like him stopping my spell cycle" (1644-1650s).
