## Classification

Primary archetype: [beatdown](../archetypes/beatdown.md). The Rune Giant is played from behind the king tower at clock 1:27 and everything else is stacked on top of her for one escalating push, which is beatdown shape — but the support cards (Berserker, Elite Barbarians, Mother Witch) are also dropped straight at the bridge for tempo, which is why bridge-spam is the secondary.

## Why this deck works

A 3.25-elixir list with no conventional win condition — Ryley says so himself: "you don't really have what seems to be a win condition, but you just have so many strong cards in here that it is actually really hard to defend." The Rune Giant is a 4-elixir tank whose enchant adds bonus damage on every third attack, so the deck is built out of fast attackers that convert that buff: the Hero Berserker (0.6 s hit speed) and Elite Barbarians, with Mother Witch turning the cheap swarm the opponent throws at the push into Cursed Hogs. Because the tank is only 4 elixir, the deck can defend cheaply for a minute and a half and still afford a full push in double or triple elixir; Heal Spirit keeps the Mother Witch and Berserker alive through the spell chip, and Fireball plus Evolution Zap stacked on one tile clears the ranged answer. Its weak point, which Ryley names in this game, is the Berserker's 3-elixir Savage Survival: spend it early and a follow-up Vines or a swarm spam gets through with nothing left to hold.
