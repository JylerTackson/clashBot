- [The Log](../cards/the-log.md) — 2 elixir, Legendary Spell, targets ground

## Classification

Primary archetype: [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md). A 3.25-elixir list whose Skeletons / Electro Spirit / The Log / Cannon shell cycles Royal Hogs back faster than the opponent can re-answer them, while Mighty Miner, Baby Dragon and Lightning make it defence-first enough for Control as a genuine secondary.

## Why this deck works

Every card except Lightning is a defensive answer first: Cannon pulls the tank, Skeletons and Electro Spirit body-block and reset, Baby Dragon covers air and swarms, and Mighty Miner melts whatever the Cannon has pulled — so the Royal Hogs go in only when the opponent has just spent, and never as a committed push. The Log is held almost exclusively for Skeleton Army, the standard hogs counter, which is why Ryley counts elixir out loud before throwing it (t=122-129). Lightning is not a defensive spell here but the deck's finisher: it removes the one card the deck genuinely fears (an evolved Witch) and is then cycled twice into a lane the hogs have already chipped. Ryley's own framing is that the shell beats ground beatdown outright — "it absolutely destroys like Giant, Golem, you know, Giant Skeleton" — and struggles only against Lava Hound (t=77-90).
