## Classification

Primary archetype: [bridge-spam](../archetypes/bridge-spam.md). Ryley names it himself at 186s — "this battle ram bridge spam deck with the hero Valkyrie and the evo elite barbarians" — and it is played that way: no tank, no build-up, every Battle Ram, Bandit and Elite Barbarians on a bridge tile the moment elixir allows.

## Why this deck works

Every threat is fast and lane-specific, so the deck can open a lane on the first 10-elixir tick and never has to build a push: the Evolution Battle Ram at the bridge took a crown tower from 4858 to 1048 HP by itself in this match. The second gear is the double-lane commit — Battle Ram in one lane and Evolution Elite Barbarians in the other, topped with Heal Spirits — which finished both towers inside twelve seconds at clock 1:47. Ryley's stated reason for the Heroic Valkyrie slot is that bridge spam forces the defender to line their answers up, and a splash unit with a dash-style ability punishes exactly that: "hero Valkyrie ... it just makes your offensive life so much easier." Mother Witch and Zappies are the cheap value/defence half, with Mother Witch played high as a tanking bait body and Zappies kept on his own half as kiting and anti-air, while Arrows is the small spell for swarms (used here on a cloned Minion pack). The deck carries no building, so a committed air push chips a tower for the rest of the game — his left tower ended this match at 150 HP.
