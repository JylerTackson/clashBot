# Classic Log Bait (Ryley)

## Cards

- [Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md) — 3 elixir, Epic Spell, targets ground
- [Goblin Gang](../cards/goblin-gang.md) — 3 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground
- [Ice Spirit](../cards/ice-spirit.md) — 1 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground_and_air
- [Inferno Tower](../cards/inferno-tower.md) — 5 elixir, Rare Building, targets ground_and_air
- [Knight](../cards/knight.md) — 3 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground
- [Princess](../cards/princess.md) — 3 elixir, Legendary Troop, targets ground_and_air
- [Rocket](../cards/rocket.md) — 6 elixir, Rare Spell, targets ground_and_air
- [The Log](../cards/the-log.md) — 2 elixir, Legendary Spell, targets ground

## Classification

Primary archetype: [bait](../archetypes/bait.md). Goblin Barrel, Princess and Goblin Gang are all small-spell bait thrown on cycle, with Inferno Tower and Rocket as the only heavy answers.

## Why this deck works

Three cards — Goblin Barrel, Princess and Goblin Gang — all die to the same small spells, so the opponent's Log or Zap can only ever answer one of them at a time; Ryley barrels on cycle and simply switches the lane once he has counted their Log ('we out cycled his log so I could barrel on the left as well', 132s). Defence is cheap and pre-placed: Goblin Gang predicts the Miner, Hero Knight cycles into the back corner as a standing body, and Ice Spirit tops up whatever is left, which keeps enough elixir free to keep the barrel cycle running. Inferno Tower is the only expensive card and is held for tanks or for late-game anchoring — in this match it did not appear until 0:14 of regular time. Rocket converts the chip into a finish when troop pressure stops working, cycled onto the tower opposite the barrels ('I would rather rocket cycle the opposite lane anyways', 251s). The Hero Knight's Triumphant Taunt adds a 2-elixir defensive reset that the classic list never had, pulling enemy Heroes and support off their target.
