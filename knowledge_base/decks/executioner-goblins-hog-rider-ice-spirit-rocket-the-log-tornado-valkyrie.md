## Classification

Primary archetype: [control](../archetypes/control.md). Every card except the Hog Rider is a defensive answer - Executioner and Tornado for swarms, Valkyrie for ground pushes, Goblins and Ice Spirit as cheap resets - and the win condition is repeated bridge Hog chip plus Rocket cycling rather than a build-up push.

## Why this deck works

The Executioner + Tornado core answers everything the deck is otherwise weak to: the Tornado groups swarms into a single axe pass and doubles as a King Tower activator against Wall Breakers and other cheap ground threats. The Hero Valkyrie replaces the usual mini-tank with a card that both survives on defence and threatens the tower on the counterattack, and her Wild Whirlwind is an optional 3 elixir you spend only to win a specific fight - Ryley declines it as often as he uses it. Ice Spirit, Goblins and The Log give the deck a cheap enough cycle to keep the Executioner and Tornado available every push, and Goblins pre-placed behind the tower is a standing answer to Miner-style chip. Offence is deliberately thin: bare Hog Riders at the bridge to force out a building, with Rocket as the overtime finisher when the ground stalls.
