## Classification

Primary archetype: [control](../archetypes/control.md). Two spawners plus Knight/Barbarian Barrel/Ice Spirit defend and chip while a single repeatable Graveyard + Poison package is the only offence, which is the control pattern rather than cycle or bait.

## Why this deck works

The deck plays one lane and one push: Graveyard on the enemy tower with the Hero Berserker dropped at the river to tank it, and Poison ahead of the Skeletons to clear the cheap answers. The Hero's "Savage Survival" (3 elixir, 4 seconds, cannot drop below 1 HP) turns a 2-elixir troop into a tank that survives the Crown Tower long enough for the Skeletons to accumulate, which is why Ryley casts Graveyards he says he otherwise would not. Behind it, Furnace and Goblin Hut sit in the centre of his own half, so the same buildings feed defensive bodies against Goblin Drill and Bomber pressure and passive chip into the tower he is attacking. Knight, Barbarian Barrel and Ice Spirit cover the ground defence cheaply enough that the 9-elixir Graveyard + Poison + ability package can be repeated every rotation. The cost is that the ability's -75% Crown Tower damage means the Berserker chips very little herself, and the deck has no air answer beyond the Furnace spirits.
