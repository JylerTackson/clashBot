## Classification

Primary archetype: [siege](../archetypes/siege.md). X-Bow is the win condition and every other card is a cheap answer that protects it, so it sieges on a 2.9-elixir cycle.

## Why this deck works

The list is the familiar X-Bow 2.9 cycle — Evo Tesla, Evo Archers, Electro Spirit, Skeletons, The Log and Fireball — with the **Hero Berserker** in the mini-tank slot. Her Savage Survival ability makes her briefly undestroyable, so a 2-elixir card placed next to a defended X-Bow clears whatever the opponent drops on it: "it's like a 5-second invincibility wall right next to your X-Bow… anything that your opponents try to do to actually take out the X-Bow will just get cleared up by the Berserker." In this match one X-Bow at (13, 12) plus Electro Spirit, Evo Archers and a bridge Berserker took a crown tower from 4858 to 1201 in a single lifetime, and a second X-Bow with the ability popped finished it to 261. The defensive half is all 1-4 elixir, so the deck can answer an Inferno Dragon three separate times and still be first to the next X-Bow — Ryley's own summary is "our cycle is way too quick for him to really do anything about us." He positions it as an anti-meta pick against Lava Hound / Rune Giant beatdown and other cycle decks that "have a really, really difficult time dealing with the X-Bow."
