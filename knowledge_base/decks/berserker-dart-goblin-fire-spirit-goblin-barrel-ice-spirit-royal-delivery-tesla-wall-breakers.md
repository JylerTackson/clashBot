## Classification

Primary archetype: [bait](../archetypes/bait.md). Goblin Barrel, Wall Breakers, Dart Goblin and two spirits all bait the same small spells and cheap answers, and Ryley calls it "this bait deck" and "log bait" himself, but at 2.4 average elixir every deployment is bridge pressure in alternating lanes.

## Why this deck works

The list is the standard Goblin Barrel / Wall Breakers bait shell with the Hero Berserker replacing the usual mini-tank: a 2 Elixir body that, with Savage Survival for 3 more, cannot be killed for four seconds and shreds the swarm answering a push. That gives a 2.4 Elixir bait deck a defensive trump it normally does not have, which matters because the deck's only other defensive anchors are one Tesla and one Royal Delivery. Offence is pure two-lane pressure - barrel one tower, Wall Breakers the other, a spirit tanking for the barrel and the Dart Goblin left deep to defend rather than support. The weak point in this match was cycle: Ryley says repeatedly he is being out-cycled and cannot afford to defend Wall Breakers cleanly, and the post-nerf spirits no longer walk up fast enough to tank for the barrel, so the deck has to win on volume of chip rather than on clean value trades.
