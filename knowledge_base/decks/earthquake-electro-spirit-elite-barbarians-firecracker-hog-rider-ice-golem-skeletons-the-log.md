# Hog EvoEBarbs EQ HeroIceGolem Cycle

## Cards

- [Hog Rider](../cards/hog-rider.md) — 4 elixir, Rare Troop, targets buildings
- [Elite Barbarians](../cards/elite-barbarians.md) — 6 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground
- [Firecracker](../cards/firecracker.md) — 3 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground_and_air
- [Ice Golem](../cards/ice-golem.md) — 2 elixir, Rare Troop, targets buildings
- [Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md) — 1 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground_and_air
- [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md) — 1 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground
- [The Log](../cards/the-log.md) — 2 elixir, Legendary Spell, targets ground
- [Earthquake](../cards/earthquake.md) — 3 elixir, Rare Spell, targets ground

## Classification

Primary archetype: [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md). A 2.75-elixir Hog Rider + Earthquake cycle shell (Skeletons, Electro Spirit, Hero Ice Golem, The Log) that returns to its win condition faster than the opponent can answer, with a 6-elixir Evolved Elite Barbarians as a second, bridge-spam win condition.

## Why this deck works

The cheap half of the list — Skeletons, Electro Spirit, the 2-elixir Hero Ice Golem and The Log — exists to get back to the Hog Rider and to keep both evolutions cycling, while Earthquake is the building answer that makes the Hog cheap to send (it goes down on the enemy left tower behind the opening Hog at clock 2:31); Ryley states the cycling rule directly at clock 0:37: "I do want to start cycling my Firecrackers really, really early on." The Evolved Elite Barbarians are the second win condition and are played strictly as offence at or past the bridge, because the Evo adds no defensive value: "This card is really aggressive-based... the Evo not being too great defensively" (clock 1:37-1:23). The Evo spear's rage trail is what makes the 6-elixir commitment safe into other ground pushes — it wins the plain Elite Barbarians mirror outright at clock 1:21 — while the Evolved Firecracker sits behind the towers as the deck's whole answer to air and to beatdown support, which is why the E-Barbs are sometimes spent just to escort her ("let's go for a set of E-Barbs here just to keep the Evo Firecracker alive", clock 0:33). The Hero Ice Golem does triple duty as opener, kite and pre-placed speed bump against a Golem the opponent has not played yet.
