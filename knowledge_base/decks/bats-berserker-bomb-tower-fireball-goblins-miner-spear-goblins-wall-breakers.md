## Classification

Primary archetype: [bait](../archetypes/bait.md). Bats, Goblins, Spear Goblins and Wall Breakers all demand the same small spell, and Ryley pilots the match by tracking the opponent's Zap and attacking the moment it is out of cycle; the 2.6 average elixir gives it a cycle second gear.

## Why this deck works

Four of the eight cards — Bats, Goblins, Spear Goblins and Wall Breakers — die to the same 2-elixir spell, so one Zap can never cover the rotation, and at 2.6 average elixir the rotation comes around faster than the opponent's answers do. The Hero Berserker is what turns that bait into damage: at 2 elixir she is cheap enough to lead every push, and Savage Survival means a defender who commits to killing her simply fails for four seconds, so the Spear Goblins and Wall Breakers behind her connect. Ryley uses the ability strictly as a shield rather than a finisher — it deals -75% to Crown Towers — and says the card 'seems super strong when your opponent is either really low on elixir or if you're like on defense.' Defensively the list is complete for its cost: Bomb Tower holds ground pushes and its death bomb clears Lava Pups, Bats and Spear Goblins cover air, and Fireball stays home on support troops. The weakness is that it has no way to close quickly against a tank — the whole win here is chip in one lane while the other lane bleeds.
