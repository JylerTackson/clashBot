## Why this deck works

The deck runs two win conditions that punish different mistakes: the Miner, a 3 Elixir mini-tank deployable anywhere in the arena for reliable chip, and the Heroic Balloon, whose very high damage per strike destroys a Crown Tower outright if the opponent has no air answer in hand. At 2.88 average Elixir with a seven-Elixir four-card cycle of Skeletons, Barbarian Barrel, Giant Snowball and Ice Golem, the Balloon usually arrives on the back of a defence already won, with the Ice Golem placed in front to soak Tower shots and clear Bats with his death nova. Bomb Tower is the defensive anchor — 1.5 tile splash that shuts down every ground swarm and stalls Hog Rider or Giant pushes — while Evolved Musketeer covers the air it cannot touch and her three Sniper Shots kill ranged defenders and outrange buildings from anywhere in front of her. Evolved Giant Snowball supplies the disruption: it slows for four seconds and carries the troops it hits away untargetable, dragging anti-air defenders off the Balloon's path, and the Heroic Balloon's Coffin Cadet sends a Skeletrooper at the nearest ground unit specifically to remove the Musketeer or Wizard that would shoot the Balloon down. The weakness is anti-air and cost: only Evolved Musketeer and Giant Snowball can hit air, so Minions, Bats and Minion Horde both stop the Balloon and are awkward to answer, and the Balloon plus its ability totals 7 Elixir, so a cheap counter to either half wins the trade.
