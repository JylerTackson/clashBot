## Classification

Primary archetype: [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md). Eight cards averaging ~2.6 elixir with no tank and a single cheap chip win condition, played by out-cycling the opponent and answering every push with 1-3 elixir defenders.

## Why this deck works

The deck has one win condition — Goblin Drill in the enemy corner — and seven cards whose job is to survive the cycle. Cannon, Fire Spirit, Bomber, Berserker and Knight cover almost every ground push for two or three elixir, so the drill chip accumulates faster than the opponent's does. Giant Snowball and Poison are chosen specifically for the bait matchup: Snowball handles Goblin Barrel and Wall Breakers on his own half, Poison answers a deep Magic Archer and adds to the drill's damage on the far tower. Ryley's own framing is that this pair flips a matchup that normally goes the other way — "normally log bait does lose to Goblin Drill Snowball or Goblin Drill normally loses to Log Bait" — but only if the pilot keeps cycling fast enough not to be out-cycled, and accepts negative spell trades to keep the Magic Archer off the bridge.
