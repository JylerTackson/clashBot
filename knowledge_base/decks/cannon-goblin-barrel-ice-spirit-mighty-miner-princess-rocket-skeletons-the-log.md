- [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md) — 1 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground

## Classification

Primary archetype: [bait](../archetypes/bait.md). Goblin Barrel and Princess are both Log/Arrows bait played behind cheap cycle cards, and the game plan Ryley states is to outcycle the opponent's single Goblin Barrel answer rather than to build a push.

## Why this deck works

Classic Log Bait: Goblin Barrel and Princess force the opponent to spend their small spell, and once it is spent the other threat gets through for free. Ryley's version swaps the usual mini-tank for a Mighty Miner, which gives the deck both a bridge play that forces out enemy support ("I mighty mined the bridge knowing he had to go for the magic cartridge", 1307-1309s) and a reliable answer to Miner and tanky ground units on defence. Cannon, Skeletons, Ice Spirit and The Log are the whole defence, so the deck is cycle-dependent by design - Ryley refuses to lay the Cannon when Ice Spirit is out of cycle (1159-1172s), and gates every Goblin Barrel on having counted the opponent's answer out of hand (1322-1350s). Rocket is the closer, used both as real damage and as a hovered bluff (1310-1320s), and the evolved Princess is what he says brought the deck back into competitive play (1145-1150s).
