## Classification

Primary archetype: [control](../archetypes/control.md). Tesla, Zappies, Electro Spirit and Zap answer pushes cheaply while Fireball and Poison do the actual tower damage, with Ronin and Berserker converting surviving defence into bridge pressure.

## Why this deck works

The deck defends the centre with a Tesla and Zappies pair and cleans up swarms with 1-2 elixir stun cards, which is cheap enough that the Fireball comes back around every few plays. Its damage is spell-led: Fireball as a predicted cast and Poison dropped on the opponent's half to remove the defenders of an already damaged tower. The Zappies are the swing card, defending in the centre and then being replayed at the bridge, where their stun means a defender that answers them still takes tower damage. Ronin and Berserker are the bodies that turn a held defence into a left-lane push, and with two damage spells plus Zap the deck rarely needs a big commitment to finish a tower off.
