## Classification

Primary archetype: [control](../archetypes/control.md). Every card except Miner and Wall Breakers exists to defend for less elixir than the push cost — Goblin Hut, Mighty Miner, Skeletons, Barbarian Barrel, Poison and the Hero Ice Wizard reset — and the two cheap win conditions chip the tower while the opponent is spending on defence, which is the control gameplan; the low average cost and 1-elixir Skeletons give it a cycle sub-plan.

## Why this deck works

The deck answers pushes for less than they cost and converts the saved elixir into Miner + Evo Wall Breakers chip on the same beat, so defending and attacking are the same action. The Goblin Hut is the anchor: its Spear Goblins grind out Elite Barbarians and Battle Rams for free, which is why Ryley plays it centrally at (9, 9) and treats protecting it as the priority over protecting the tower. The Hero Ice Wizard supplies the one thing a spawner-control shell normally lacks against bridge spam — a cheap reset. At 2 elixir the snowman freezes a whole lane until it is destroyed, and only The Log and Arrows remove it, so an opponent without those spells cannot cheaply undo it. Poison covers the swarms and support the freeze does not kill. The result in this match was a game where the opponent never connected a Battle Ram and both of Ryley's crown towers finished at over 96% health.
