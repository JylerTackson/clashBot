## Classification

Primary archetype: [bait](../archetypes/bait.md). Goblin Gang, Dart Goblin, Suspicious Bush and Skeleton Barrel all pull the same small spell at 2-3 elixir each, and the payoff is a Berserker and Evo Elite Barbarians dropped straight at the bridge once that spell is committed.

## Why this deck works

Four cards in the deck — Goblin Gang, Dart Goblin, Suspicious Bush and the Evolved Skeleton Barrel — all want the opponent's Log or small spell, and at 2-3 elixir each they cycle back faster than the answer does. Because the same cheap cards also do all the defending (gang first, Dart Goblin behind it, The Log only as cleanup), Ryley never has to spend his threats on his own side of the river. The payoff is deliberately mispriced against a spent rotation: a 2-elixir Berserker at the bridge is too cheap to trade against, and the Evolved Elite Barbarians either split from a centre-back placement to force answers in both lanes or land straight at the bridge for a tower. He calls the plan explicitly — 'I'm going to berserker and gang here because he doesn't have the fire spirit, he doesn't have the log, and he didn't have the demolisher' (106-111s) — and describes the deck as 'the definition of pressure... your opponent can be playing a perfect game. They make the slightest mistake... and ebarb just take their entire tower' (66-83s), which is exactly how this game ends in overtime.
