# Ryley Log Bait Bridge Spam (Goblin Barrel / Wall Breakers)

## Cards

- [Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md) — 3 elixir, Epic Spell, targets ground
- [Wall Breakers](../cards/wall-breakers.md) — 2 elixir, Epic Troop, targets buildings
- [Magic Archer](../cards/magic-archer.md) — 4 elixir, Legendary Troop, targets ground_and_air
- [Berserker](../cards/berserker.md) — 2 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground
- [Bomb Tower](../cards/bomb-tower.md) — 4 elixir, Rare Building, targets ground
- [Royal Delivery](../cards/royal-delivery.md) — 3 elixir, Common Spell, targets ground_and_air
- [Ice Spirit](../cards/ice-spirit.md) — 1 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground_and_air
- [Fire Spirit](../cards/fire-spirit.md) — 1 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground_and_air

## Classification

Primary archetype: [bait](../archetypes/bait.md). Goblin Barrel plus Wall Breakers plus two spirits bait the opponent's small spell and cheap answers, but every deployment is a bridge-lane pressure play, which is how Ryley describes it himself: "people like to call these types of decks log bait, but in reality, they're more like bridge spam" (t=75-80).

## Why this deck works

At 2.4 average elixir the deck always has four playable cards, so pressure never pauses: in this game Ryley threw 12 Goblin Barrels, 11 Wall Breakers and 11 spirits in a little over four minutes, forcing the opponent to answer with Cannon, Firecracker or Mighty Miner instead of playing his own Hog Rider. The two win conditions bait different answers and are placed apart on purpose, so one Barbarian Barrel or Log cannot cover both, and the lane is switched as soon as the relevant spell leaves the opponent's cycle. Defence is deliberately cheap and repeatable — Bomb Tower on the centre tile, a spirit in front of it, Berserker as the plug and Royal Delivery for a committed ground push — so the elixir saved goes straight back into bridge plays. Magic Archer is the swing card: at the bridge it chips a spent hand, and from the back line it stacks up behind the barrels as permanent support ("the barrels tank for by the magic archer").
