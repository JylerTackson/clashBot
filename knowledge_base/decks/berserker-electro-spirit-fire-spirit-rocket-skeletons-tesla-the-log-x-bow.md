## Classification

Primary archetype: [siege](../archetypes/siege.md). X-Bow is the win condition and every other slot is a 1-4 elixir answer that protects it or cycles back to it, with Rocket as the closer.

## Why this deck works

The standard X-Bow 2.9 cycle with **Rocket** instead of Fireball and a **Fire Spirit** in the Archers slot, built around the **Hero Berserker** as the bridge tank. Because Savage Survival makes her undestroyable for four seconds, a 2-elixir card next to a fresh X-Bow clears whatever the opponent drops on the building — Ryley's pitch is that defending a siege building normally means putting cards at the bridge, and this deck punishes exactly that: "you can just instantly wipe out their defenses and they just don't have enough elixir to kind of recoup". Tesla doubles as a defensive pull and as pre-emptive X-Bow armour at the bridge, which leaves an opponent with only a heavy spell as a real answer. Rocket then converts that into a win condition of its own: once the enemy has to spend Rocket on every X-Bow, Ryley rockets the tower back for free damage and wins the cycle race in overtime.
