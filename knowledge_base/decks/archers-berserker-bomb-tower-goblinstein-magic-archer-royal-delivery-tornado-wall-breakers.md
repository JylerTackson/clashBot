## Classification

Primary archetype: [control](../archetypes/control.md). Four defensive answers (Bomb Tower, Tornado, Royal Delivery, Magic Archer) built to trade up against every push, with only cheap Wall Breakers chip as a win condition — defence-first, with a 3.25 average cost that is too high for a true cycle deck.

## Why this deck works

Bomb Tower plus Tornado is the engine: the building holds the centre of the half inside both crown towers' cover, and the Tornado drags a push into its splash, with Magic Archer piercing the clump from behind and Royal Delivery covering the single bridge-crosser the building cannot reach. Berserker is the 2-elixir plug that buys the building a second against anything that gets past it, and Goblinstein placed centrally splits the Doctor and Monster into both lanes for one 5-elixir card. Damage comes almost entirely from Wall Breakers sent unsupported whenever the opponent commits elixir in the other lane, which makes the deck a slow grinder rather than a race — in this match it never lost a tower and never took one either, going the full distance into triple-elixir overtime. The obvious hole is heavy single-target beef: with no tank killer, a P.E.K.K.A. has to be ground down by the building and splash instead of answered outright.
