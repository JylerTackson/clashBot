## Classification

Primary archetype: [bridge-spam](../archetypes/bridge-spam.md). Battle Ram, Royal Ghost and Elite Barbarians are dropped at the bridge for tempo and card trades, with Mother Witch, Valkyrie and Zappies as the defensive core — the standard bridge-spam shape.

## Why this deck works

A bait-counter build of Ryley's bridge-spam list: Royal Ghost and Arrows take the Bandit and Freeze slots so that every one of Skeleton Army, Princess, Dart Goblin and Goblin Barrel has a cheap answer, while Mother Witch turns an enemy swarm into Cursed Hogs instead of just clearing it. The bridge cards are used to force specific cards out of hand — Royal Ghost to make the opponent spend a Dark Prince, Elite Barbarians to break their cycle — and the damage arrives afterwards as an Evolution Battle Ram plus Heroic Valkyrie (Wild Whirlwind) push supported by Zappies, Heal Spirit and Arrows. The weakness Ryley shows in the same game is placement risk: the deck defends with kites, and one mis-clicked Battle Ram kite cost him half a tower.
