## Classification

Primary archetype: [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md). A ~2.75-elixir shell that spends Skeletons, Electro Spirit and Barbarian Barrel to cycle back to Royal Hogs is Cycle first, but the two permanent spawners (Furnace and Goblin Hut) and a game plan of outlasting a Golem rather than out-tempoing it make Control a real secondary.

## Why this deck works

The deck answers a heavy, aggressive meta by never contesting the opponent's lane. Two spawners — Furnace deep on his own half and Goblin Hut in the centre — generate free defensive bodies every cycle, so the cheap cards (Skeletons, Electro Spirit, Barbarian Barrel) only have to fill gaps rather than solve pushes outright, and the Hero Berserker's Savage Survival gives a 2-elixir troop the survivability of a much larger one at the exact moment a beatdown deck commits. Ryley's case for the Furnace after its nerf is the deck's thesis: "for decks that were using it only for its defensive potential, I actually was so wrong because it's so much better defensively now" (2:23). Royal Hogs then only needs to connect once, in the lane the opponent's tank has abandoned — "let's just Royal Hogs opposite lane cuz, obviously, he can't go for the Golem on the left" (1:16). The weakness the game exposes is that the spawners cannot hold a fully committed Golem push on their own: he loses a crown tower to it and has to win on the crown he took in the other lane.
