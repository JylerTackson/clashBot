# Ronin Deck (Ryley)

## Cards

- [Ronin](../cards/ronin.md) — 5 elixir, Legendary Troop, targets ground
- [Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md) — 3 elixir, Epic Spell, targets ground
- [Wall Breakers](../cards/wall-breakers.md) — 2 elixir, Epic Troop, targets buildings
- [Magic Archer](../cards/magic-archer.md) — 4 elixir, Legendary Troop, targets ground_and_air
- [Tesla](../cards/tesla.md) — 4 elixir, Common Building, targets ground_and_air
- [Royal Delivery](../cards/royal-delivery.md) — 3 elixir, Common Spell, targets ground_and_air
- [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md) — 1 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground
- [Fire Spirit](../cards/fire-spirit.md) — 1 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground_and_air

## Classification

Primary archetype: [bait](../archetypes/bait.md). Goblin Barrel, Wall Breakers, Skeletons and Fire Spirit all die to the same small spells, so whichever one the opponent cannot answer connects, and a 2.9-elixir average lets him replay it before their answer cycles back.

## Why this deck works

The deck pairs four small-spell-bait threats (Goblin Barrel, Evo Wall Breakers, Skeletons, Fire Spirit) with a defence that costs almost nothing to hold: Tesla for air and building-targeters, Royal Delivery for clumped air support, and the Ronin, whose parry deflects 200% of a melee attacker's own hit. Against any deck whose best defensive DPS is a melee troop the Ronin effectively removes the answer — "his best DPS unit is the Mini Pekka, but of course, the Mini Pekka just one shots itself" — so the barrels and Wall Breakers keep connecting for free. Ryley plays the Ronin on his own half as the anchor and only pushes it near the bridge to deny a heavy opponent the free back-tank placement, then closes with a Hero Magic Archer at the bridge once a tower is low. Ryley describes it as a revision of an earlier Cannon-based list, with Tesla and Royal Delivery added specifically because the older version "was quite a bit weak to air units".
