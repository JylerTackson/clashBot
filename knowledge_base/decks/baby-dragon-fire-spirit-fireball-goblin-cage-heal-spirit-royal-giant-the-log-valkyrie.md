## Classification

Primary archetype: [control](../archetypes/control.md). A 6-elixir Royal Giant played at the bridge for chip while Goblin Cage, Valkyrie and Baby Dragon hold the ground and the two spirits plus Log and Fireball cycle back to it - he wins by defending value trades and repeating the bridge Royal Giant, not by building a push.

## Why this deck works

The Royal Giant never goes down behind the tower: Ryley plays it at the bridge as the answer to the opponent's own back placement, on the logic that "if I just set up a defense in the back, he's able to do the exact same thing, except he just has more elixir to push with" (341-348s). Goblin Cage is what makes that safe - placed dead centre it pulls anything ground and its Goblin Brawler doubles as a bridge-push body, and against a deck without a clean answer "it does get really, really awkward for him whenever I go for the Goblin Cage in the middle" (372-379s). Valkyrie plus the Brawler plus Heal Spirit is the pre-planned Elite Barbarians answer, Baby Dragon covers air and corner value, and Fireball, The Log and Fire Spirit clean up support while cycling back to the Evolved Royal Giant, whose knockback is the point against Elite Barbarians (359-366s). The list's one real hole is an air-mobile splash unit - a Heroic Wizard using Fiery Flight cannot be answered by Log or anything else in the eight (418-428s).
