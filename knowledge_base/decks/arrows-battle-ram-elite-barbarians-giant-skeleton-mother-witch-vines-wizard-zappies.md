## Classification

Primary archetype: [bridge-spam](../archetypes/bridge-spam.md). Evolution Battle Ram and Evolution Elite Barbarians are placed at the bridge on cycle as the whole win condition, with Mother Witch and Giant Skeleton as the heavier follow-ups; Ryley calls it "this bridge spam deck" himself.

## Why this deck works

The two evolutions give the deck two independent bridge threats that both punish a slow answer, and the Elite Barbarians ability lets Ryley pre-dodge the exact cards a bait opponent must hold ("I went for the ability predicting him going Valkyrie... I dodged the log and I dodged the Valkyrie"). Behind them, Hero Wizard plus Zappies covers the swarm and Magic Archer that bait decks counterattack with, which is what lets him keep committing elixir at the bridge instead of holding. Giant Skeleton is the escalation once the opponent has spent down, and Vines converts a stalled push by deleting the defending building — the game-winning play here. It is a variant of the popular list `barbarian-barrel-battle-ram-elite-barbarians-giant-skeleton-mother-witch-vines-wizard-zappies`, trading Barbarian Barrel for Arrows, which pairs with Vines as a finisher and answers Goblin Barrel and Wall Breakers at range.
