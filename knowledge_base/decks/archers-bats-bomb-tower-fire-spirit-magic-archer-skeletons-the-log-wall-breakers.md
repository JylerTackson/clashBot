## Classification

Primary archetype: [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md). An eight-card list averaging 2.4 elixir whose win condition, Wall Breakers, is also its cycle card, with Bomb Tower plus 1-2 elixir troops doing all the defending.

## Why this deck works

Every defensive answer is cheap enough that the deck out-cycles what it is defending: Fire Spirit, Skeletons and The Log clear swarms and Graveyard skeletons for 1-2 elixir while Bomb Tower, placed centre-left, absorbs the Miner and covers the Graveyard radius. Because nothing on defence costs more than four, Wall Breakers come back around faster than the opponent's building, and Ryley alternates a genuine bridge pair with a cycle pair behind the king tower to control which one the opponent has to answer. Archers and Magic Archer supply the only ranged damage, so the deck has no real counter-push - the plan is chip damage from Wall Breakers plus defensive value, which in this match traded evenly with a Miner/Graveyard control deck into triple-elixir overtime.
