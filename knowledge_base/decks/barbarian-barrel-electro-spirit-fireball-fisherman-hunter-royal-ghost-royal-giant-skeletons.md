## Why this deck works

The win condition is the Evolved Royal Giant, a 6 Elixir building-targeter who hits a Crown Tower from outside its reach, and the seven-Elixir four-card cycle of Electro Spirit, Skeletons, Barbarian Barrel and Royal Ghost brings him back to hand faster than the opponent can redraw their answer. His evolution's recoil shockwave wipes Skeleton Army and Goblin Gang and shoves bulkier ground swarms back a tile per shot, so the support cards only have to cover what he cannot touch: Fireball and Barbarian Barrel clear the ranged defenders hiding behind the push, Electro Spirit's 0.5 second stun resets an Inferno Tower or a Mighty Miner, and Fisherman hooks high-DPS defenders such as a Musketeer or a weakened Inferno Tower away from him. On defence the deck is built around cheap stalls feeding a heavy hitter — Skeletons and the Fisherman's hook hold a tank in place while Hunter, whose ten pellets do their most damage point-blank, melts it. Evolved Royal Ghost turns a completed defence into offence: he spawns invisible, his attacks summon two Souldiers that do not despawn, and the Royal Giant is an especially good partner for him because a ranged tank gives the Ghost time to re-cloak and renew the ability, while the Heroic Barbarian Barrel's Rowdy Reroll adds a second roll that heals its Barbarian for one more cheap body in front of the tank. The weakness follows straight from the recoil: air units are immune to the knockback, so Minion Horde, Bats or an Inferno Dragon still counter the Royal Giant cleanly, and Hunter, Electro Spirit and Fireball are all the anti-air the deck has.
