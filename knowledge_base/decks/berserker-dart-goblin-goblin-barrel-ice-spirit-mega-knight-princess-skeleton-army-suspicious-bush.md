# Mega Knight Log Bait

- [Mega Knight](../cards/mega-knight.md) — 7 elixir, Legendary Troop, targets ground
- [Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md) — 3 elixir, Epic Spell, targets ground

- [Suspicious Bush](../cards/suspicious-bush.md) — 2 elixir, Rare Troop, targets buildings
- [Skeleton Army](../cards/skeleton-army.md) — 3 elixir, Epic Troop, targets ground
- [Berserker](../cards/berserker.md) — 2 elixir, Common Troop, targets ground

## Classification

Primary archetype: [bait](../archetypes/bait.md). Five of the standard log-bait threats (Goblin Barrel, Princess, Dart Goblin, Skeleton Army, Ice Spirit) each demand a different small spell and are thrown whenever the opponent's answer is out of cycle, with Mega Knight added as a defensive bruiser that converts a held defence into a bridge counter-push.

## Why this deck works

The bait half does the damage: Goblin Barrel, Princess and Suspicious Bush are all cheap enough to throw on information about the opponent's spell cycle, so at least one connects every rotation, and Ryley chips the enemy left tower from 4858 to 2586 HP in this game without ever committing a real push. Because there is no small spell and no building, the deck's ground defence has to be swarm-based — Skeleton Army plus Berserker plus Ice Spirit answered evolved Royal Hogs for five elixir here — and Ryley names that missing Zap/Snowball/building slot as the list's real weakness. The Mega Knight is what makes it more than a chip deck: he uses it in the back as a hard answer to a Champion, and at the bridge as a tank that either forces a King activation or walks in behind a Princess and an Evo Goblin Barrel. At 3.0 average elixir it still cycles fast enough to re-throw the Evo Goblin Barrel on schedule while holding seven elixir for the bruiser.
