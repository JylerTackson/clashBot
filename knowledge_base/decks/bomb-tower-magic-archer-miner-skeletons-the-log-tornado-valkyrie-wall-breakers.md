## Classification

Primary archetype: [control](../archetypes/control.md). Two cheap chip win conditions (Miner, Evo Wall Breakers) behind a fixed Bomb Tower / Tornado / Log / Evo Skeletons defensive core that answers pushes elixir-positively and wins on accumulated tower damage rather than one big hit.

## Why this deck works

At 2.875 average elixir the deck defends with a repeatable four-card stack — Bomb Tower on the central tile, Evo Skeletons, The Log and Tornado — and converts every saved elixir into Miner plus Evolved Wall Breakers at the bridge. The Hero Valkyrie is what turns that chip plan into a real threat: at 4 + 3 elixir she splashes the ground swarms the Bomb Tower cannot reach, and Wild Whirlwind lets her walk through a defence and spin on the tower, which Ryley calls "extremely broken because your opponent has such a hard time reacting to it cuz of the moving speed" (t=85-93). The pairing that matters is Valkyrie plus Miner: the Crown Tower targets the Miner, the Valkyrie survives to connect, and the Wall Breakers follow behind — "Miner Golden Knight vibes where like they just can't defend properly" (t=195). The cost is that the ability is 3 elixir on top of the card and needs a tank in front, so it can only be spent when the defence is already stable, and nothing in the deck kills a tower quickly: this game went to overtime with the enemy tower on 116 HP.
