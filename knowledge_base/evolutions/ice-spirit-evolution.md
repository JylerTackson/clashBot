# Evolved Ice Spirit

> *In-game description:* "Stay frosty when the Ice Spirit is about! Literally. It now infuses a target with an Ice Blast spell, which deals freezing Area Damage over time."

**Base card:** [ice-spirit.md](../cards/ice-spirit.md)

## Overview

The Ice Spirit's Evolution is a Common card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns an Ice Spirit with a splash radius 0.5 tiles larger than the original. When 3 seconds have passed after it hits an enemy, it will deal the same stun and damage to the enemy for 1.1 seconds. It resembles a small snowball with indigo eyes and a mouth, along with its hands, legs, teeth and crystals on its head being in the form of ice. The Ice Spirit's Evolution costs 1 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
