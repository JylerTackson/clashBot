# Evolved Goblin Drill

> *In-game description:* "Let’s play Whack-A-Drill!! When deployed near enemy Crown Towers, Goblin Drill submerges and reappears around the tower as it takes damage, spawning Goblins each time."

**Base card:** [goblin-drill.md](../cards/goblin-drill.md)

## Overview

The Goblin Drill Evolution is an Epic card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Goblin Drill with identical stats to the original. Once its hitpoints reach 66% and 33% respectively, the Goblin Drill will resurface nearby, leaving a Goblin behind each time it's destroyed. If the Goblin Drill is not deployed next to the Crown Towers, it will resurface in the same spot. If the Goblin Drill is deployed next to the Crown Towers, it will resurface 90 degrees around a Crown Tower. It appears as a building with purple spikes that have golden frames as their base, a wooden hull, a Card Evolution-shaped golden hatch, a purple Goblin emblem on the side, a team-colored stripe in the center, and a purple metal drill on its top. The Goblin Drill Evolution costs 4 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
