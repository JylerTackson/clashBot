# Evolved Zap

> *In-game description:* "This Evolution comes gently zapping, zapping at your Arena floor... Get two Zaps for the cost of one, with the second Zap hitting a larger area!"

**Base card:** [zap.md](../cards/zap.md)

## Overview

The Zap's Evolution is a Common card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It is a Zap with identical stats to the original. Once the initial hit is unleashed, the radius stays in the Arena, growing 0.5 tiles while doing the same damage and stun as the initial hit. It is a deep blue liquid filled inside a thin oblong vial. The vial appears with a purple lid, a golden neck, and a Card Evolution symbol in the middle, which is secured by a golden rope. The Zap's Evolution costs 2 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.

## What changes mechanically

- Identical base stats to the regular Zap: 2 Elixir, 2.5 tile radius, 0.5 second stun, hits air and ground.
- After the initial hit, the radius stays in the Arena and pulses a second time, growing 0.5 tiles (2.5 to 3 tiles) and dealing the same damage and the same 0.5 second stun again - effectively x2 Zaps for the cost of one.
- Total output per cast is doubled: area damage and Crown Tower damage are both x2 (e.g. 309 x2 = 618 area damage and 93 x2 = 186 Crown Tower damage at Level 16).
- Because there are two stuns, charge-up attacks are reset twice instead of once.
- Unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards; requires 2 cycles to deploy the evolved version.
- The larger second pulse is a liability as well: it can reach further than the base Zap, so a sloppy placement can clip the enemy King Tower.

## Notes

- Strong on both offense and defense for 2 Elixir: it wipes low-health troops and repeatedly stuns tougher ones, crippling pushes and defenses so other cards can do the real damage.
- Double reset on charge attacks - Mighty Miner, Ram Rider, Battle Ram, Sparky, Evolved Royal Recruits, Dark Prince, Prince, Inferno Dragon and Inferno Tower all get their charge cancelled twice.
- Its doubled damage fully counters low-health troops for an even or positive Elixir trade: Minion Horde, Wall Breakers, Archers, Bomber, Evolved Bats, Goblin Gang, Dart Goblin, Princess and Firecracker.
- Deals slightly more Crown Tower damage than Arrows for one less Elixir, making it a good last-resort cycle card to finish a tower.
- Kills Lava Pups from a Lava Hound - the second zap finishes them and the grown radius catches most of them; the same works on Elixir Golem's Elixir Blobs and the Phoenix's Egg.
- Excellent with the Cannoneer: the double stun and damage stops Goblin Gang, Evolved Skeletons, Minion Horde, Evolved Bats and Skeleton Army from connecting, and may also clip nearby tanks (Lava Hound, Goblin Giant, Electro Giant, P.E.K.K.A, Golem, Giant, Giant Skeleton) so the Cannoneer gets extra damage in.
- With Cannoneer support it counters a Balloon for roughly a +3 Elixir trade (before death damage) - place it 2 tiles in front of the Balloon once the Balloon enters the Cannoneer's range.
- Strong offensive synergy with Hog Rider: the repeated stun and damage on defenders nearly guarantees chip damage each cycle. Weaker when the opponent has a building, since a well placed building plus both Crown Towers can still kill the Hog Rider. A similar plan works with Ram Rider.
- Pairs well with Evolved Royal Giant (and less effectively the regular Royal Giant): the Royal Giant's knockback blunts melee DPS like Monk, Mini P.E.K.K.A, Lumberjack, Evolved Barbarians and Elite Barbarians while the Evolved Zap stuns them and neutralizes Evolved Bats, Minion Horde, Inferno Dragon, Mighty Miner and Inferno Tower. Placed in the pocket of the opponent's side, this nearly guarantees tower damage.
- Decent answer to a center-placed Goblin Barrel - only two Goblin stabs connect. If you also run The Log or another small spell, use that on the Barrel and save the Evolved Zap for your own pushes.
- The second pulse raises total damage enough that Evolved Zap + Fireball also kills an Archer Queen or Phoenix (a slight negative Elixir trade), on top of everything the regular Zap + Fireball combo beats; it is an alternative to Lightning for the same total cost.
- Never cast it near the King Tower or behind the enemy Crown Tower - the growing radius can activate the opponent's King Tower and ruin your later offense.
- Giant Snowball, Arrows and Rage can substitute for it depending on which Evolution and small spell you prefer.
