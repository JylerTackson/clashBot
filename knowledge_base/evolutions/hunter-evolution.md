# Evolved Hunter

> *In-game description:* "Be very, very quiet. Evolved Hunter throws a net that stuns the closest troop. If that troop is flying, it'll ground them."

**Base card:** [hunter.md](../cards/hunter.md)

## Overview

The Hunter Evolution is an Epic card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Hunter with identical stats to the original. The Evolved Hunter will throw a net at the closest unit every 5 seconds. The net will freeze the troop in place and render it unable to move or attack, and allow ground units to hit the trapped unit for 3 seconds. He appears as a man who wears a team-colored coonskin cap with purple hair, a golden stripe and a Card Evolution symbol in the middle, a brown cloth, gloves, belt, pants and boots, an indigo shirt, and team-colored wristlets, is armed with a double-barreled shotgun, wield nets with purple points, and has fuzzy black eyebrows and mustache covering his eyes and mouth respectively. The Hunter's Evolution costs 4 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
