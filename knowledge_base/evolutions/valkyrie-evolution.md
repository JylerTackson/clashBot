# Evolved Valkyrie

> *In-game description:* "If you thought her battle cry was scary...wait until you see her new axe! Evolved Valkyrie draws all enemies towards her with each swing."

**Base card:** [valkyrie.md](../cards/valkyrie.md)

## Overview

The Valkyrie's Evolution is a Rare card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Valkyrie, with identical stats to the original. Every time she attacks, a Tornado will appear for half a second, pulling all units towards her, and dealing damage within a 5.5 tile radius around her. She takes an appearance as a woman who bears orange hair and eyebrows, have purple eyes and two piercings in her right ear, wears a purple suit, loincloth, wristcuffs and bands on her legs, brown straps with spikes on them and a Card Evolution symbol in the middle, and a team-colored waistcloth, wields a purple-tipped double-sided axe with a large, silver-plated golden knob on the bottom, and attacks with a powerful whirl of her axe along with a tornado effect. The Valkyrie's Evolution costs 4 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
