# Evolved Executioner

> *In-game description:* "That's not an axe… This is an axe! Evolved Executioner's axe deals more damage to enemies in close quarters and pushes them back."

**Base card:** [executioner.md](../cards/executioner.md)

## Overview

The Executioner's Evolution is an Epic card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns an Executioner with identical stats to the original. The Executioner launches an axe that travels up to its maximum range, and returns back to him after some time, while piercing through enemies in both ways. If an enemy troop is within 3.5 tiles from the start of the attack, it will be knocked back, while also receiving extra damage. They will also receive the extra damage on the returning phase on the attack. He resembles a shirtless man with glowing pink eyes, wears a purple mask with gold stitches, glove with a golden stripe and boots, a team-colored kilt and pants, a belt, and a pair of straps, carries a double-sided axe with a Card Evolution symbol and a large golden knob on the bottom, and has an axe-shaped tattoo on his arm, which glows alongside his axe whenever the axe is within 3.5 tiles of range. The Executioner's Evolution costs 5 Elixir and requires 1 cycle to deploy.
