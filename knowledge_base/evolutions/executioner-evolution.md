# Evolved Executioner

> *In-game description:* "That's not an axe… This is an axe! Evolved Executioner's axe deals more damage to enemies in close quarters and pushes them back."

**Base card:** [executioner.md](../cards/executioner.md)

## Overview

The Executioner's Evolution is an Epic card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns an Executioner with identical stats to the original. The Executioner launches an axe that travels up to its maximum range, and returns back to him after some time, while piercing through enemies in both ways. If an enemy troop is within 3.5 tiles from the start of the attack, it will be knocked back, while also receiving extra damage. They will also receive the extra damage on the returning phase on the attack. He resembles a shirtless man with glowing pink eyes, wears a purple mask with gold stitches, glove with a golden stripe and boots, a team-colored kilt and pants, a belt, and a pair of straps, carries a double-sided axe with a Card Evolution symbol and a large golden knob on the bottom, and has an axe-shaped tattoo on his arm, which glows alongside his axe whenever the axe is within 3.5 tiles of range. The Executioner's Evolution costs 5 Elixir and requires 1 cycle to deploy.

## What changes mechanically

- Costs 5 Elixir and requires only 1 cycle to evolve (6 Evolution Shards to unlock), so the evolved version can be used several times in one match.
- Base behavior is unchanged: the axe travels to maximum range and returns, piercing enemies on both passes.
- New ability, Axe Smash: enemy troops close to him when the attack starts are knocked back and take extra damage, and they take the extra damage again on the axe's return trip.
- Axe Smash damage roughly doubles his output at close range — 473 x2 (946) against the normal 271 x2 (542) at Level 16, for 394 damage per second instead of 224.
- Axe Smash proximity range: the Evolution Attributes table lists 2.5 tiles, while the source lead describes the effect triggering within 3.5 tiles from the start of the attack.
- The knockback applies to anything not immune to it, effectively giving him a Bowler-style pushback that also works on air troops.
- All other attributes (hit speed 0.9 sec, axe time 1.5 sec, range 4.5, projectile range 7.5, hitpoints) are identical to the base card.

## Notes

- He fills the Bowler's role while also hitting air, letting him defend against things the base card could not — Elite Barbarians are countered for a +1 Elixir trade with minimal damage taken.
- Troops stalled efficiently by the pushback: Knight, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Valkyrie, Hog Rider, Mega Minion, Skeleton Dragons, Baby Dragon, Royal Hogs, Battle Healer, Ram Rider, Phoenix, Electro Dragon, Rascals, Golden Knight, Monk, Balloon and Lumberjack.
- The extra damage lets him kill win conditions like Hog Rider, Royal Hogs, Ram Rider and Balloon before they do much Tower damage, and beat tanky troops such as Giant, Royal Giant, Elixir Golem and Goblin Giant.
- He still needs support against Golem and Lava Hound, and loses quickly to P.E.K.K.A., Giant Skeleton, Sparky, Mega Knight, Goblin Machine, Mighty Miner and Boss Bandit (though Boss Bandit can at least be pushed back).
- Like the base card he nullifies Barbarians, Royal Recruits, Goblin Gang, Minion Horde, Wall Breakers, Lava Pups and Elixir Golemites and Blobs, and he handles both forms of the Evolved Wall Breakers unsupported.
- The pushback shoves Balloon and Goblin Demolisher away from a Crown Tower so their death damage misses it; this does not work on Giant Skeleton, who cannot be knocked back.
- His knockback constantly resets the charges of Battle Ram, Evolved Battle Ram, Evolved Royal Recruits and Ram Rider, plus the Barbarians they spawn.
- He resets an Inferno Dragon's ramp-up damage when directly targeting it, so Inferno Tower (a building) or Mighty Miner (a heavy unit) must be used against him instead.
- Tornado synergizes even harder now, pulling scattered troops into Axe Smash range; conversely, an enemy Tornado is a decent counter that drags him out of his own pushback and bonus damage range.
- His axe outranges him, hitting support troops behind tanks or Crown Towers on offense — but a Fisherman pull, or an Ice Spirit freeze followed by a Tornado, can make that axe activate the King's Tower.
- Rage is a waste of 2 Elixir on him: he throws slower to compensate for the shorter airtime, so his damage per second is unchanged.
- Ranged troops with moderate hitpoints — Musketeer, Wizard, Electro Wizard, Cannon Cart and Archer Queen — beat him easily since they stay outside the extra damage and pushback range. Witch works too via her Skeletons distracting him, but Night Witch does not, being melee.
- Fireball, Rocket, Lightning or Poison bring him to very low health or kill him outright and can hit other targets at the same time, though he can be used to bait them for other spell-weak cards.
- Tanky melee units (P.E.K.K.A., Giant Skeleton, Mega Knight) absorb a good number of his boosted hits; do not pair them with fragile support unless it is placed away from him.
- An Ice Golem can tank and distract him long enough for a melee or frail air unit to kill him without eating the pushback and extra damage.
- Well positioned Bats stall and damage him enough for them plus a Crown Tower to defeat him for a +3 Elixir trade.
- Monk's Pensive Protection stalls him — a deflected axe deals the extra damage and pushes him back — but it is a negative Elixir trade and his slow speed still lets the Executioner win afterwards.
