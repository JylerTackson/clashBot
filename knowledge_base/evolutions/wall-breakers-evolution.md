# Evolved Wall Breakers

> *In-game description:* "Who let Wall Breakers into the gunpowder stores? They’ve run off with more explosives! It’s double trouble with this Evolution as they continue their attack on foot if their barrel is destroyed!"

**Base card:** [wall-breakers.md](../cards/wall-breakers.md)

## Overview

The Wall Breakers' Evolution is an Epic card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns 2 Wall Breakers with identical stats to the originals. When defeated, they will explode, dealing high damage (but less against Crown Towers) and will spawn a Runner each, which is an area-damage, building-targeting, melee, ground troop with low hitpoints and moderate damage. They drop their dynamite at their target when attacking, destroying themselves on impact. They resemble Skeletons donning purple bandanas and gloves with golden cuffs, along with a team-colored belt with the Card Evolution symbol in the center, have purple eyes and a nose, ride larger, purple-spiked explosive barrels, and carry sticks of dynamite in their hands. The Wall Breakers' Evolution costs 2 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.

## What changes mechanically

- Costs 2 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to evolve (6 Evolution Shards to unlock); it still spawns 2 Wall Breakers with stats identical to the originals.
- New ability: when a Wall Breaker is defeated its barrel explodes for high death damage and spawns a Runner in its place, so the card no longer dies to a single small answer.
- The death damage is reduced against Crown Towers — the stats table lists a separate Crown Tower Death Damage column (e.g. 311 versus 469 normal death damage at Level 16).
- The Runner is an area-damage, building-targeting, melee ground troop with low hitpoints and moderate damage that drops its dynamite on its target, destroying itself on impact.
- Runner damage is roughly half the barrel's: the table lists Runner Area Damage against the Wall Breaker's own Area Damage (e.g. 316 versus 630 at Level 16), with Runner Hitpoints of 264 at Level 16.
- If the Wall Breakers are killed too early no explosion happens, and if a barrel connects to a building the Runner deals no additional damage.

## Notes

- For 2 Elixir they deal very high Crown Tower damage and apply immense pressure, useful for distracting an opponent from building up or defending a push.
- Deployed as a pair, so splitting them down the middle of the Arena divides the attack — fully countering that with only 2 Elixir is nearly impossible.
- Single-damage spells (Fireball, Rocket, Zap, The Log, Giant Snowball) are mostly ineffective: the barrel acts as a shield and simply shifts them into Runner form.
- Evolved Zap or Arrows can fully counter them for a neutral Elixir trade; the Zap must be played 2 tiles in front of them so all three zaps land.
- Arrows placed quickly, with Tower Princess help, fully counter them, but Arrows used too late let a Runner connect.
- Buildings such as Cannon or Goblin Cage placed directly on top are the best counters, albeit for a negative Elixir trade.
- A properly timed and placed Valkyrie fully counters them for a negative trade, but she counterpushes afterwards and can force a response.
- Piercing splash cards (The Log, Executioner, Barbarian Barrel, Evolved Firecracker, Evolved Bomber, Bowler) carry their fatal damage through to the Runners; place the troops at a distance so the death damage does not hit them.
- Giant Snowball plus Goblins fully counters them since the death damage is not quite enough to kill Goblins, but only as a last resort — Goblins placed before a barrel explodes die because they cannot take the death damage twice.
- Do not deploy units next to a building the Evolved Wall Breakers are heading for: Minions, Mother Witch, Dart Goblin, Bomber or Magic Archer are instantly destroyed or take heavy unnecessary damage.
- On defense they are a positive Elixir trade against medium-hitpoint units and swarms such as Minion Horde and Barbarians when dropped directly on top, thanks to the barrel's death damage.
- With Cannoneer help they fully counter Rascals — they clear the Rascal Girls and damage the Rascal Boy for the Cannoneer to finish.
- Cannoneer is a much worse answer than against the regular Wall Breakers: left alone, both Runners and one barrel's death damage connect to it.
- Dagger Duchess can mostly handle them alone at the cost of most of her daggers, letting only 1 Runner through.
- They can be situationally ignored when attacking a Crown Tower with significantly more health than the other.
- Miner synergy is excellent — he takes the Crown Tower's attacks so the Evolved Wall Breakers connect easily.
- Ice Golem synergy: he cheaply tanks the Crown Tower and depletes the Dagger Duchess' daggers, and the Wall Breakers push him toward the Tower for minor extra chip.
- Skeleton Barrel works as both spell bait and a tank, making full Crown Tower damage more likely.
- Evolved Royal Recruits pair well with split Evolved Wall Breakers, taking hits on both sides while applying equal pressure in both lanes.
- Arrows can also be used offensively to clear the Skeleton Army or Goblins sent to defend, letting the Wall Breakers themselves connect instead of only the Runners.
- Rage makes them hard to react to at the bridge and can get both to connect against decks lacking area damage or buildings, but it does not raise their damage since the hit is instant.
- When cloning, clone the Evolved Wall Breakers rather than the Runners — even regular cloned Wall Breakers deal more damage than Runners at the same 1 hitpoint.
