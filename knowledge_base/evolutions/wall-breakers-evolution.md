# Evolved Wall Breakers

> *In-game description:* "Who let Wall Breakers into the gunpowder stores? They’ve run off with more explosives! It’s double trouble with this Evolution as they continue their attack on foot if their barrel is destroyed!"

**Base card:** [wall-breakers.md](../cards/wall-breakers.md)

## Overview

The Wall Breakers' Evolution is an Epic card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns 2 Wall Breakers with identical stats to the originals. When defeated, they will explode, dealing high damage (but less against Crown Towers) and will spawn a Runner each, which is an area-damage, building-targeting, melee, ground troop with low hitpoints and moderate damage. They drop their dynamite at their target when attacking, destroying themselves on impact. They resemble Skeletons donning purple bandanas and gloves with golden cuffs, along with a team-colored belt with the Card Evolution symbol in the center, have purple eyes and a nose, ride larger, purple-spiked explosive barrels, and carry sticks of dynamite in their hands. The Wall Breakers' Evolution costs 2 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
