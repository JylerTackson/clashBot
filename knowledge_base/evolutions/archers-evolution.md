# Evolved Archers

> *In-game description:* "These Archers have been specifically trained by Archer Queen herself as her personal sharpshooters. They deal more Damage to far away enemies with Power Shot!"

**Base card:** [archers.md](../cards/archers.md)

## Overview

The Archers' Evolution is a Common card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns 2 Archers with a range 1 tile further than the originals. If there are units between 4 and 6 tiles of the Evolved Archers, they will shoot their Power Shot, which deals 25% bonus damage. The Archers take the appearance of women who have purple eyes, deep pink eyebrows and short hair with a ponytail, and wear golden bands on their arms, team-colored capes with a golden lining in the bottommost part of them, purple gloves, tunics and leggings, and a dark brown belt with a buckle, along with a bag and a quiver around their waists and on their backs respectively, while wielding a recurve bow with purple stabilizers and a golden handle, having arrows with purple fletchings and a dark purple point as their ammunition. The Archers' Evolution costs 3 Elixir and requires 2 Cycles to deploy.

## What changes mechanically

- Unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards; costs the same 3 Elixir and requires 2 Cycles to deploy.
- Range increases from 5 to 6 tiles (+1 tile), matching the Musketeer's range.
- New ability **Power Shot**: any unit between 4 and 6 tiles away is hit for 25% bonus damage (e.g. at level 16, 225 damage / 250 DPS instead of 180 damage / 200 DPS).
- Hitpoints, hit speed (0.9 sec), movement speed, deploy time, projectile speed and troop count are unchanged from the base Archers — only range and the conditional damage bonus differ.
- The bonus is positional, not permanent: enemies closer than 4 tiles are hit with normal damage, so keeping distance is what makes the evolution work.

## Notes

- Place them roughly 5-6 tiles from enemy troops to keep targets inside Power Shot range for as long as possible, yielding positive Elixir trades and two surviving Archers for a counterpush.
- Pair with a mini-tank (Knight, Golden Knight, Prince, Mighty Miner, Monk, Skeleton King, Goblin Machine, Dark Prince, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Lumberjack, Valkyrie) to hold enemies inside the Power Shot band; the combo also becomes a cheap threatening push.
- Evolved Knight is the standout partner — also 3 Elixir and 2 Cycles, and his damage reduction lets him tank ranged/air troops (Musketeer, Wizard, Electro Wizard, Three Musketeers, Electro Dragon, Mega Minion, Executioner, Baby Dragon, Inferno Dragon, Witch, Hunter, Minion Horde, Archer Queen) while the Archers finish them.
- Miner synergy: he tanks Crown Tower shots so the Evolved Archers hit the tower from the bridge untouched, and they keep chipping even after he dies.
- Excellent in siege decks with X-Bow and Mortar — played far from the building, they kill the tanks and hard counters (Royal Giant, Cannon Cart, Bowler, Goblin Giant, Giant Skeleton, Golem) that the siege building cannot; the Mortar version costs 2 Elixir less and gives less spell value. Do not place them next to the siege building or the Power Shot and safety are lost, and a Fireball or Rocket gets value.
- Conversely they counter Mortar and X-Bow themselves by sniping from outside the support troops' reach; add a Knight, Valkyrie, or Ice Golem to soak the building's damage and keep them healthy.
- The extra range outranges Cannon and a distracted Tesla and reaches buildings placed farther from the river, making them strong support for Battle Ram, Hog Rider, Royal Hogs, Ram Rider, Wall Breakers, and Balloon.
- Against an unsupported Inferno Tower they counter it for a +2 Elixir trade at the cost of one Archer, no distraction needed — clearing the way for Giant, Mortar, Royal Giant, Balloon, Golem, or X-Bow.
- Royal Giant pairing: he tanks and hits the tower while they delete his defenders; especially good with the Evolved Royal Giant, whose recoil clears the light swarms so the Archers focus on real threats.
- Placed in the center they beat a Hog Rider for a +1 Elixir trade and severely cripple a Ram Rider — though a preemptive center Fireball invalidates the Hog Rider version.
- Placed at the back and split slightly, they heavily damage or kill slow tanks: P.E.K.K.A., Giant Skeleton, Goblin Giant, Golem, Bowler, Electro Giant, Rune Giant, Lava Hound, Elixir Golem; with support they also handle Mega Knight (potentially triggering a King's Tower activation), Cannon Cart, and Sparky.
- Great with split-lane pressure cards (Royal Recruits, Royal Hogs, Elite Barbarians) and, with a mini-tank (or a building vs Royal Hogs), also a neutral-to-positive answer to those same cards.
- Tornado synergy: pull troops that got too close back out to 4-6 tiles to re-enable Power Shot, ideally alongside a distraction like Skeletons, Goblins, or Guards. The opponent can use Tornado in reverse to cancel the bonus, but it is a large investment.
- Behind the Crown Tower as a last-resort defense they still get Power Shot range; combined with Arrows, Giant Snowball, and/or Rage they can gut a Lava Hound + Balloon push for a +5 or +6 Elixir trade.
- Their range keeps them outside Electro Dragon's chain (very valuable versus the infinite chains of the Evolved Electro Dragon), outside Electro Giant's Zap Pack reflection, and outside Goblin Machine's Rocket range at maximum distance.
- Synergize with Evolved Battle Ram and Evolved Wall Breakers by sniping the distracting buildings and punishing defenders already crippled by pushback or death damage.
- Still countered by spells: Arrows and Royal Delivery for an even trade (hitpoints are unchanged), Barbarian Barrel and The Log for +1 Elixir when they are unsupported and correctly placed, and Void kills both within 2 hits if they are the only troops inside.
- Cannoneer OHKOs them and Dagger Duchess kills them fast, letting heavy decks ignore them; Tower Princess and Royal Chef instead take heavy chip damage.
- Viable Musketeer substitute — one Power Shot deals nearly a Musketeer's projectile damage, and both together roughly double it.
- Alternative evolutions: Evolved Knight, Evolved Musketeer, Evolved Mortar, Evolved Dart Goblin, Evolved Tesla, Evolved Firecracker. Evolved Tesla + Evolved Archers suit X-Bow decks, Evolved Knight and Evolved Mortar suit Mortar decks; Evolved Musketeer trades damage for range/sniping and bulk; Evolved Dart Goblin baits Arrows/The Log but is frailer; Evolved Firecracker handles pushes and swarms better, while Evolved Archers are preferred for single-target damage, bulky air (Balloon, Lava Hound), and decks weak to King Tower activation such as Graveyard.
