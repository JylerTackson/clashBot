# Evolved Skeleton Army

> *In-game description:* "Look alive! General Gerry's Skeletons are devoted to him, becoming invisible and indestructible in his presence. But alas, an army cannot survive without its General and will perish in his absence."

**Base card:** [skeleton-army.md](../cards/skeleton-army.md)

## Overview

The Skeleton Army's Evolution is an Epic Card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns 15 Skeletons in a scatter formation, with General Gerry at the back. When the Skeletons are defeated, their shadows will spawn. The shadows have unlimited hitpoints, and can't be targeted by troops, buildings and tower troops, but can be affected by spells. When General Gerry is defeated, all the shadows are defeated as well. Gerry appears as a Guard with purple eyes, wears a helmet with a golden handle and a team-colored crest on top and a purple cape with gold linings, and carries a spear with an attached purple banner that has a skull printed on it and a wooden shield with the Card Evolution symbol on it. The Skeleton Army's Evolution costs 3 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
