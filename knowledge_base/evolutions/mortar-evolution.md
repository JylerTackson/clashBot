# Evolved Mortar

> *In-game description:* "The New & Improved Mortar uses patented Goblin Delivery Technology to rain noisy green destruction upon the Arena!"

**Base card:** [mortar.md](../cards/mortar.md)

## Overview

The Mortar's Evolution is a Common card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Mortar with a hit speed 1 second faster than the original. Every attack that it does will also spawn a Goblin. It is a metallic cylindrical tube with a golden base, a wooden gimbal on each side with three purple studs on them which are held by purple and black metal stakes, gold chains and a Card Evolution symbol on the side, and a large, double-layered team-colored band near the muzzle, which is surrounded by two gold rings, with a Goblin located near the top. The Mortar's Evolution costs 4 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
