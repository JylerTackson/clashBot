# Evolved Tesla

> *In-game description:* "This Evolution is quite shocking! Bad jokes aside, the evolved Tesla sends out an intermittent Electro Pulse, dealing Damage to any enemies caught in it."

**Base card:** [tesla.md](../cards/tesla.md)

## Overview

The Tesla's Evolution is a Common card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Tesla with identical stats to the original. After surfacing and deployment, the Tesla spawns an expanding electric pulse effect, dealing high damage while stunning all enemies in its radius. The stun lasts 0.5 seconds and covers a 6-tile radius. It appears to be a wooden lattice structure with a violet coil, cone and sphere with golden details placed on top which is surrounded by team-colored supports, a dark violet wire coiled around one of its pillar, and a dark violet wooden trapdoor with golden plates and hinges. The Tesla's Evolution costs 4 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.

## What changes mechanically

- Base stats are identical to the regular Tesla: 4 Elixir, 1.1 sec hit speed, 0.5 sec first hit, 1 sec deploy, 25 sec lifetime, 5.5 range, hits air and ground.
- New ability (Pulsating Voltage): on deployment and every time it surfaces after hiding, it releases an expanding electric pulse.
- The pulse covers a 6-tile radius, deals its own Pulse Damage (e.g. 174 at Level 11) and stuns every enemy inside for 0.5 seconds.
- Unlike the base Tesla, which does not stun at all, the evolved version's pulse does stun — and it repeats every surface until the Tesla is destroyed.
- The underground mechanic is unchanged: while hidden it is immune to all damage except Earthquake and cannot be targeted, and Freeze locks it in whichever state it is in.
- Requires 6 Evolution Shards and 2 cycles to deploy.

## Notes

- With correct timing the pulse can be used twice: place it as the enemy push crosses the bridge so the spawn pulse hits, then let it close and reopen for a second pulse. Risky, but it can hold off a big attack.
- One-shots any unit with less health than Minions at the same Level — Goblins, Spear Goblins, Evolved Bats, Skeletons — so it shreds small low-hitpoint hordes.
- Unlike the regular Tesla it is a somewhat reliable answer to heavy swarms like Evolved Barbarians, Royal Hogs and Evolved Royal Recruits, because the pulse damages and stuns everything inside.
- Graveyard backup counter: fast attacks, enough hitpoints to survive Skeletons, immune to Tornado pulls, and the death pulse can finish surviving Skeletons.
- Tower troop synergy: the Cannoneer covers the swarms he struggles with via the pulse and gets more time on tanks the Tesla distracts; the Dagger Duchess likewise gets swarm cover while high-hitpoint troops are pulled toward the other Duchess with full daggers.
- With good placement plus the Cannoneer it fully counters Royal Giant and Evolved Royal Giant for a +2 Elixir trade, with similar results against Electro Giant, Giant Skeleton, P.E.K.K.A and Bowler.
- With Skeletons it fully counters a Golem for a +3 Elixir trade; Spear Goblins or Dart Goblin instead give a counterpush and chip damage.
- Siege synergy: with X-Bow, the pulse instantly kills light swarms like Skeleton Army and stuns other distractions while adding DPS — but the pair is very weak to Earthquake. With Mortar the same applies for 2 less Elixir total.
- It is also good against an enemy X-Bow or Mortar: it tanks shots, deals heavy damage, and a late-placed pulse can force the siege building to retarget onto it.
- Placement rules match the base Tesla: 3 tiles from the bridge to pull everything, 1 tile further to pull only building-targeters, 1 tile from the river against Earthquake/Lightning/Poison decks, 5 from river and 1 from Crown Tower for Lava Hound, 3 from river and 2 from Crown Tower to split a LavaLoon push (also works on a lone Balloon or Skeleton Barrel), and beside the King's Tower 4 tiles from the top of the Arena after losing a Tower.
- Reactively placed 4 tiles from the defending Crown Tower and 4 tiles from the river it separates centre-staggered tanks from their support; this works on a bridge-placed Giant but not a Golem, whose sight range is lower.
- Still weak to Freeze (locks it above or below ground, letting a Hog Rider walk past), to Earthquake underground, and to fast troops like Spear Goblins that hit it before the retreat completes.
- Offensively, long-range cards — Dart Goblin, Princess, Evolved Musketeer, Flying Machine, Evolved Archers, Magic Archer — can snipe a poorly placed Evolved Tesla from across the river outside pulse range, provided something like an Ice Golem or Goblin Giant keeps it from going underground.
- Good answer to a Princess at the bridge, since it cannot be spelled while hidden.
- Choose it over Cannon (1 Elixir cheaper, ground only, less health) or Inferno Tower (1 more Elixir, more health, great on tanks but swarm-vulnerable) based on deck needs and existing Evolutions.
- Opponents may wait out its 25 second lifetime — be ready for the attack that follows its expiry.
