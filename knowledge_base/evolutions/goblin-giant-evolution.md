# Evolved Goblin Giant

> *In-game description:* "The bigger the sack, the bigger the... amount of Goblins spawned! At low health, this Evolution will spawn a stream of Goblins. Take him out quickly!"

**Base card:** [goblin-giant.md](../cards/goblin-giant.md)

## Overview

The Goblin Giant's Evolution is an Epic card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Goblin Giant with identical stats to the original. When his hitpoints are lowered below 50%, every 2.2 seconds, the Goblin Giant will passively summon a Goblin. The Goblin Giant resembles a colossal, aquamarine-skinned Goblin with a deep pink nose, purple eyes, sideburns and goatee, wearing a purple aviator hat with a Card Evolution symbol and pants with gold stitches and golden linings on both of them, along with a purple backpack with ropes tied on the sides and team-colored suspenders, carries a dagger with a purple handle, and has two ropes tied on his arms and white bands tied on his legs. The Goblin Giant's Evolution costs 6 Elixir and requires 1 cycle to deploy.
