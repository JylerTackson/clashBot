# Evolved Bomber

> *In-game description:* "Bomber has discovered the art of bomb bouncing. It’s like skipping stones, but much more exciting. With his new skill, Bomber will be able to hit multiple targets in a row with one attack!"

**Base card:** [bomber.md](../cards/bomber.md)

## Overview

The Bomber's Evolution is a Common card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Bomber with identical stats to the original. After the initial throw, the bomb will bounce twice, 2.5 tiles apart in a straight line, dealing the same damage as the initial hit and hitting troops in an area as large as that of the initial hit. He appears as a Wall Breaker wearing a purple aviator hat with a gold lining and gold-rimmed team-colored aviation goggles with purple lens and gloves with golden cuffs and carries a purple-spiked black bomb. The Bomber's Evolution costs 2 Elixir and requires 2 cycle to deploy.
