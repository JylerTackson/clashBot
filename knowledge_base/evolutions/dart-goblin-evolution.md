# Evolved Dart Goblin

> *In-game description:* "It's time to blow darts and chew gum, and he's all out of gum. Evolved Dart Goblin's dart Poison the area around his target until they're destroyed. The more he attacks a target, the deadlier the Poison gets."

**Base card:** [dart-goblin.md](../cards/dart-goblin.md)

## Overview

The Dart Goblin Evolution is a Rare card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution shards. It spawns a Dart Goblin with identical stats to the original. When attacking a target, he will leave an area of effect poison, becoming stronger after multiple attacks. He resembles a Goblin, but wears a voodoo mask with purple eyes, team-colored face paint and a rope behind, leaves with circles and purple tips, and a Card Evolution symbol on top, purple pants, team-colored bands on his arm, and brown shoes, carries a purple potion, and holds a blowgun with purple paint. The Dart Goblin's Evolution costs 3 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
