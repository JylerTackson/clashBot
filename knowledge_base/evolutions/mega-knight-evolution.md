# Evolved Mega Knight

> *In-game description:* "Mega Knight's other great love, besides his mustache, is boxing. With each attack, this Evolution uppercuts his target and sends them flying toward the nearest enemy Crown Tower."

**Base card:** [mega-knight.md](../cards/mega-knight.md)

## Overview

The Mega Knight's Evolution is a Legendary card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Mega Knight with identical stats to the original. Every time he attacks, the Evolved Mega Knight will do an uppercut, launching the targeted troop back 4 tiles towards the nearest enemy Crown Tower. He resembles a Knight with magenta eyes, black eyebrows, and glowing pink sclerae, clothed in heavy, massive amethyst armor, a helmet with a team-colored feather on top, large, purple-spiked maces with golden frames and handles as their base, purple chainmail with gold linings, and a team-colored belt with golden spikes and a Card Evolution symbol in the middle. The Mega Knight’s Evolution costs 7 Elixir and requires 1 cycle to deploy.

## What changes mechanically

- Costs 7 Elixir and requires 1 cycle to evolve (6 Evolution Shards to unlock); it spawns a Mega Knight with stats identical to the original.
- New ability, Mega Uppercut: when he attacks, the targeted troop is launched back 4 tiles toward the nearest enemy Crown Tower (the Evolution Attributes table lists a Throwback Range of 4).
- Only the troop he is currently targeting is thrown back, even though the uppercut itself still deals area damage.
- The pushback always launches the target in a straight line toward the enemy Crown Tower regardless of where it was standing.
- The throwback conveniently drops the target inside his 3.5 to 5 tile jump range, so he skips the charging time and jumps immediately when nothing is in the way — an uppercut-jump loop.
- Everything else is unchanged from the base card: 1.7 sec hit speed, 1.3 splash radius, 0.9 sec jump time, 3.5-5 jump range, 2.2 jump splash radius, and identical hitpoints, area damage, spawn damage and jump damage tables.

## Notes

- An extremely powerful defensive wall: the uppercut hurls bulky win conditions far behind their support, often destroying the foundation of the push for a positive Elixir trade.
- Especially brutal against a single tank like a Royal Giant — he repeatedly uppercuts and jumps on it all the way to the enemy Crown Tower.
- He can force any troop off a Crown Tower with the pushback, which is very valuable against Giant Skeleton (death damage), Golem (health) and P.E.K.K.A (DPS).
- He should essentially never be ignored — doing so costs a Crown Tower and notable King Tower damage; only a tower trade justifies it.
- Heavy ground swarms are the answer: Barbarians or Royal Recruits surrounding him only get one unit thrown back at a time while the rest keep attacking, denying his jump and killing him with Crown Tower help.
- Unlike the base card, no mini tank fully counters him except Mini P.E.K.K.A. — the 4 tile pushback keeps interrupting their attacks and feeding his jump.
- With a Cannoneer, all the Champions except Mighty Miner can just barely fully counter him when deployed properly.
- Inferno Tower is the best single-card counter: as a building it cannot be pushed back or jumped, and its full-charge DPS kills him for a +2 Elixir trade — but watch for Zap, Lightning, Rocket and Earthquake in the opponent's deck.
- Inferno Dragon works similarly and more cheaply, but has shorter range and does not distract him, so pair it with something like an Evolved Ice Spirit.
- Mighty Miner is a poor counter — the constant pushback keeps resetting his drill at stage one.
- Never pair the Evolved Mega Knight with Inferno Dragon or Mighty Miner: he shoves their targets away and resets their charge (an exception is the Inferno Dragon on air troops he cannot hit).
- Piercing attackers (Magic Archer, Executioner, Bowler, Firecracker, Evolved Bomber) exploit the straight-line pushback to hit the Crown Tower from the opposite side.
- He is helpless against air troops — pair him with Archers, Spear Goblins, Musketeer, Wizard, Princess, Mega Minion, Skeleton Dragons, Flying Machine, Magic Archer, Archer Queen, Hunter, Dart Goblin, Witch, Baby Dragon, Firecracker, Zappies, Phoenix, Electro Dragon or Executioner, or keep Arrows or Zap in hand.
- Bats plus a Cannon or other defensive building counter him well once he is damaged; conversely Bats synergize with him, giving DPS and air cover while he clears swarms and tanks Tower shots.
- Excellent with Ram Rider: he instantly deletes the stun cards that stop her (Zappies, Electro Wizard, Goblinstein, Evolved Tesla), her snares drag defenders into his jump threshold, and his pushback crushes her counters like Bowler and Monk. Hog Rider and Battle Ram work similarly but are easier to counter without the snare.
- Spawn damage is slightly below a Royal Delivery, killing Musketeer, Electro Wizard, Ice Wizard, Evolved Barbarians or Wizard in the spawn plus two hits; against Zappies, Magic Archer or Mother Witch, deploy further away so the stronger jump lands instead.
- He can counter a Goblin Barrel with precise timing (deployed behind the Tower as the shadow crosses the river), or be dropped between the top and bottom Goblins to prevent most of the damage.
- Against Sparky: timed spawn damage at the bridge plus Tower support kills a weakened Sparky; place him behind a Sparky-and-Giant Skeleton combo, or to the side of a Sparky behind a building-targeting tank with the Sparky nearest.
- Countering a reactive pack of Royal Recruits, watch the other lane's half; an Ice Golem can group them so he spawns on top of all of them.
- King Tower activation setups still work: Ice Spirit four tiles from the King's Tower and one tile into his lane to redirect his jump, then Skeletons one tile from the King's Tower and one tile off his lane; or a Tombstone at the 7-2 placement (or 5 tiles from the river and 2 from the Crown Tower) followed by a troop his jump one-shots.
- Against a suspected Evolved Mega Knight, do not clump troops proactively — split cards like Royal Hogs down the middle of the Arena.
