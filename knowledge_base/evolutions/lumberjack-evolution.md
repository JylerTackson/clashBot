# Evolved Lumberjack

> *In-game description:* "When Evolved Lumberjack is destroyed, his invisible Ghost seeks vengeance as long as it's in a pool of Lumberjack's Rage. Who you gonna call?"

**Base card:** [lumberjack.md](../cards/lumberjack.md)

## Overview

The Lumberjack's Evolution is a Legendary card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Lumberjack with identical stats to the original. When the Lumberjack dies, his ghost will spawn. The ghost has unlimited health, and can't be targeted by troops, buildings and towers, but can be affected by spells, and will disappear after a short time outside of the initial Rage Spell. The Lumberjack resembles a ginger-haired Barbarian with a beard, purple eyes, and a scarlet nose, wearing a brown belt, purple ushanka with the Card Evolution symbol in the center and kilt with gold linings, and team-colored wristbands and sandals, wielding an axe with a purple-tipped blade, a darker handle, and a golden knob on the bottom, and carries a Rage Spell on his shoulder. His ghost appears identical to him, but has pale skin and tongue, white hair, beard, mustache and eyebrows, and his axe's blade is cyan. The Lumberjack's Evolution costs 4 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
