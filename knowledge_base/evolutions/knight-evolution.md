# Evolved Knight

> *In-game description:* "Not only has his facial hair got an upgrade, the Knight’s armor has too! Evolved Knight has a special Shield that reduces Damage unless he is attacking."

**Base card:** [knight.md](../cards/knight.md)

## Overview

The Knight's Evolution is a Common card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Knight with identical stats to the original. When he isn't attacking, he receives 60% less damage from all sources, staying active until the Evolved Knight deals damage to something. The Knight takes the appearance of a man who has purple eyes, a blond-colored mustache, beard and eyebrows, and wears chain mail and a chestplate, along with a team-colored sash and a belt, purple gloves with a golden stripe on them, and metal shoes with purple plates on them that have a golden knob on top of them, while holding a saber, that now has a golden handle. The chainmail in his head is now purple with a golden stripe on it, and he also has a big, purple shield with the Card Evolution symbol covering his right shoulder, acting as an armlet. The Knight's Evolution costs 3 Elixir and requires 2 Cycles to deploy.

## What changes mechanically

- Unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards; still 3 Elixir and requires 2 Cycles to deploy.
- All base stats are identical to the regular Knight — same hitpoints, 78-325 damage by level, 1.2 sec hit speed, Medium speed, melee range, ground-only targeting.
- New ability **Elbow Reflection** shield: while he is not attacking he takes 60% less damage from every damage source.
- The damage reduction is conditional — it switches off as soon as the Evolved Knight deals damage to something, and comes back once he stops attacking, so his effective bulk depends on whether he is walking or swinging.
- Practical consequence: the first hit an enemy lands on him is usually reduced (including big one-shot hits like Prince/Dark Prince charges and Mega Knight's jump), while extended melee exchanges strip the shield.

## Notes

- On defense, place him away from the attacking troop so he walks in under the shield, letting Crown Towers and support chip the enemy — excellent for absorbing Musketeer, Bomber, Wizard, Flying Machine, Dart Goblin, Inferno Dragon, and Sparky damage.
- Exception: against Evolved Bomber, Evolved Firecracker, Bowler, Magic Archer, and Executioner, their long range can hit the Crown Tower or support behind him, so drop him on top of them or angle the placement instead.
- Especially good against Prince, Dark Prince, and Mega Knight, whose charge/jump damage is heavily reduced, letting him plus the Crown Tower kill them for a positive Elixir trade.
- Placed on top of a lone Sparky past the bridge and letting her hit first, he absorbs most of the blast and kills her with the Crown Tower for a +3 Elixir trade, staying healthy for a counterpush.
- With cheap support (Archers, Minions, Goblins, Spear Goblins, Bats) he counters Three Musketeers for up to a +4 Elixir trade — place him away from them but within 6 tiles of the farthest one.
- Very effective against Mortar and X-Bow since the shield tanks most of their shots; if he ends up in the Mortar's blind spot, add distraction troops until its lifetime expires.
- Conversely he is a great tank for your own Mortar or X-Bow, and the Evolved Mortar benefits further because he shields its spawned Goblins.
- Ignoring him only costs slightly more than half a Tower Princess', Cannoneer's, or Royal Chef's health — but not against Dagger Duchess, whose 8 initial daggers barely hurt him while moving, so he can kill her outright.
- Spells are a poor answer: he is only 3 Elixir and takes little damage while moving, so only spell him when spell-vulnerable troops are nearby.
- Best counters are large swarms that keep him attacking — Tombstone, Barbarians, Skeleton Army, Witch, Royal Recruits, Goblin Gang — because he cannot regenerate the shield while swinging; this requires the opponent's spells to be out of cycle.
- Kiting with an Ice Golem works differently: instead of pulling him away, place the Ice Golem so he keeps hitting it (shield off) and then burn him down with Archers, Musketeer, Wizard, Dart Goblin, or Electro Wizard.
- Goblins paired with the Cannoneer can fully counter him by luring him to the center of the defender's territory.
- Minions and Bats take a very long time to kill him because they never force the shield off — the defender must add Goblins or Skeletons to stall him, or use a different counter.
- Conversely Minions/Bats are strong partners; the Evolved Bats can safely activate their overheal behind him and force the opponent to spend spells or anti-air.
- His bulk plus damage reduction protects low-health defenders from Lightning — Musketeer, Goblin Cage, Barbarians, Battle Ram, Mega Minion, Wizard, Flying Machine, Witch, Royal Hogs, Berserker, Hunter, Night Witch, Zappies, Electro Wizard, Electro Dragon, Ice Wizard, Phoenix, Magic Archer, Bandit, Fisherman, Mother Witch, Archer Queen, Goblinstein Doctor, Little Prince — and he keeps a big chunk of health for the push afterwards.
- In front of a Giant or Golem push he distracts Inferno Tower / Inferno Dragon for a long time; even better with a follow-up Zap or Lightning, an Electro Giant win condition, or Electro Wizard / Electro Dragon stuns.
- Goblin Barrel decks: his shield makes him far harder to answer than the base Knight, so Barbarian Barrel or The Log on the Goblins still leaves him pressuring the tower (often a bad trade for the opponent). With the Evolved Goblin Barrel he can tank in the decoy lane, though the visual tell usually gives it away.
- He one-shots Goblins, so he is a passable Goblin Barrel counter — place him on the arena-edge side of the Crown Tower to kill the bottommost Goblin the tower targets last, then counterpush.
- Graveyard pairing (8 Elixir, better in double Elixir or with a lead): his shield forces heavy Elixir into him while the Skeletons chip, and if the opponent focuses the Graveyard he gets tower damage instead.
- With the Evolved Goblin Drill he keeps the spawned Goblins and the drill alive longer, since the drill submerges and cannot tank for them.
- With fast building-targeting cards he is pushed forward and clears their defenders: Battle Ram (Barbarians add damage; behind an Inferno-Tower-targeted Ram he then tanks for the Barbarians; Evolved Battle Ram is deadlier still — best countered by Electro Wizard/Electro Dragon stuns or an emergency Lightning), Hog Rider (his damage clears buildings, but swarms punish the combo), Ram Rider (costliest at 8 Elixir, weak damage output but big tower/building damage while tanked), and Wall Breakers (cheapest at 5 Elixir — he tanks Crown Tower shots so they connect through Zap/Snowball/Barbarian Barrel/The Log, Cannoneer or Dagger Duchess; Royal Delivery is the best answer, Skeleton Army works only if the opponent has no spells, and Inferno Tower or a Bowler in front of the tower handles the Evolved Wall Breakers version).
- For quick pressure, pair him with Archers (especially Evolved Archers), Musketeer, Wizard, or Electro Wizard — those supports survive an equal-level Fireball and he keeps pressuring even if they are spelled.
- On counterpushes, Evolved Bomber or Evolved Firecracker (or Magic Archer / Executioner) clear swarms in front of him and chip the tower — but the long-range ones can activate the King's Tower, so avoid them in Graveyard decks.
- With spells out of the opponent's cycle he is a great tank for Evolved Skeletons, Evolved Bomber, Evolved Dart Goblin, or Evolved Firecracker while they stack their evolution effects; splash units shut this down, particularly against Evolved Skeletons.
- Spawner pairings: Witch, Night Witch, or Mother Witch — play the Evolved Knight at the back and the witch at the bridge to dodge spells; Witch handles swarms, Night Witch adds single-target damage and Bats, and Mother Witch gains most value when the opponent uses Spear Goblins, Skeleton Army, or Goblin Gang to distract him.
- Alternative: Evolved Valkyrie costs 1 more with slower hit speed but more health, more damage, and a pulling whirlwind — better for swarms and win conditions, while the Evolved Knight is preferred for defense, counterpushes with support troops, and letting win conditions connect while he absorbs damage.
