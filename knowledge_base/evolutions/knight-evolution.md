# Evolved Knight

> *In-game description:* "Not only has his facial hair got an upgrade, the Knight’s armor has too! Evolved Knight has a special Shield that reduces Damage unless he is attacking."

**Base card:** [knight.md](../cards/knight.md)

## Overview

The Knight's Evolution is a Common card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Knight with identical stats to the original. When he isn't attacking, he receives 60% less damage from all sources, staying active until the Evolved Knight deals damage to something. The Knight takes the appearance of a man who has purple eyes, a blond-colored mustache, beard and eyebrows, and wears chain mail and a chestplate, along with a team-colored sash and a belt, purple gloves with a golden stripe on them, and metal shoes with purple plates on them that have a golden knob on top of them, while holding a saber, that now has a golden handle. The chainmail in his head is now purple with a golden stripe on it, and he also has a big, purple shield with the Card Evolution symbol covering his right shoulder, acting as an armlet. The Knight's Evolution costs 3 Elixir and requires 2 Cycles to deploy.
