# Evolved Musketeer

> *In-game description:* "She can hit a Royal Hog from thirty tiles away! Evolved Musketeer snipes targets across the Arena in a straight line ahead of her. She has limited Sniper Ammo with increased damage. She cannot snipe Crown Towers."

**Base card:** [musketeer.md](../cards/musketeer.md)

## Overview

The Musketeer's Evolution is a Rare card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Musketeer, with identical stats to the original. After deployment, she will gain 3 infinite-range attacks that will target the closest troop or building from anywhere in the Arena in front of her, dealing 1.8 times the damage of her normal attacks. The Musketeer takes the appearance of a woman who has purple eyes, coiffed curly hair with a ponytail, eyebrows and lipstick, and wears a royal blue tunic with a purple cloth on it, a purple cape with a Card Evolution symbol in the center and gloves, a metal helmet with a team-colored feather and a tiny crater on it, a dark brown belt and boots with dark purple straps, while carrying a blunderbuss-styled musket as her weapon with dark gray spheres as her ammunition and a scope on top. The Musketeer's Evolution costs 4 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.

## What changes mechanically

- Unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards; still 4 Elixir and requires 2 Cycles to deploy.
- Base stats are identical to the original Musketeer — same hitpoints, damage, 1 sec hit speed, 6 tile range and Medium speed.
- New ability: after deployment she gains 3 Sniper Shots that target the closest troop or building anywhere in the Arena in front of her.
- Sniper Shots deal 1.8 times her normal attack damage (e.g. 182 x3 at Level 3, 546 total).
- Sniper Ammo is limited to 3, with a 1 sec sniper hit speed, a 6-30 tile sniper range, a 2 tile width and a 2650 projectile speed.
- Sniper Shots cannot target Crown Towers, so she can shoot troops and buildings behind them, such as an Elixir Collector.
- She only spends Sniper Shots when the target is outside her normal 6 tile range — place her far from the target to make her use them.
- Sniper thresholds: 3 shots kill any troop up to Baby Dragon hitpoints, 2 kill anything up to Elixir Golemite hitpoints, and 1 kills anything up to Elixir Blob hitpoints; they also one-shot shields.

## Notes

- Hard counter to the Evolved Wizard: the first sniper shot destroys his shield and the other two kill him, a +1 Elixir trade with her at full health.
- Strong against ranged troops — Executioner, Electro Wizard, Archer Queen, Inferno Dragon, Witch, Bowler, Sparky, Cannon Cart, Mother Witch, Phoenix, Magic Archer, Ice Wizard, Baby Dragon, Hunter, Mega Minion, Flying Machine, Electro Dragon and Wizard — and the sniper shots let her avoid much of their return damage.
- Good against most air troops at a distance, and she instantly kills anything with Lava Pup hitpoints or less, making her excellent against Lava Hound.
- Against a Balloon, either place her near the bridge to deny all Tower damage (exposing her to its support) or spend the sniper shots on it from safety.
- Attacking a Crown Tower keeps her out of the King's Tower's range.
- She outranges the Cannon outright, and her sniper shots let her outrange Bomb Tower, Inferno Tower, X-Bow and Mortar — place her just beyond their maximum range so she can finish them quickly afterwards.
- Tesla hides underground, so use a cheap distraction like Skeletons to make it pop up before she uses her range advantage.
- Building placement: she bypasses an attacking building at 4 tiles from the river and 3 from the Crown Tower, and a 5-2 placement; 1 tile closer to the Tower stops that. Against passive buildings she bypasses a 5-2 placement unless she is on the inner side of the Arena.
- Do not send her at the second Crown Tower once the first is destroyed — her sniper shots are completely wasted there.
- Wasting her sniper shots is the main counterplay: cheap swarms (Skeletons, Goblins, Minions), bulkier swarms (Barbarians, Royal Recruits, Rascals, Royal Hogs), and spawners (Witch, Night Witch) all bleed the shots away.
- Guards and Royal Recruits soak shots efficiently with shields — only one Royal Recruit dies to the sniper shots.
- Evolved Knight takes 60% less sniper damage, losing only about half his hitpoints, provided he is not distracted elsewhere.
- Royal Ghost gets the first hit while invisible and three-shots her without ever taking a sniper shot.
- Monk's ability deflects her sniper shots back, making her kill herself.
- Mighty Miner can activate his ability to change lanes and dodge her sniper shots entirely, staying at full health.
- Tornado negates her sniper range advantage by pulling her close, forcing regular attacks — useful when she is defending against melee threats like P.E.K.K.A, Mighty Miner, Valkyrie or Giant Skeleton.
- Mini-tanks and high damage troops (Knight, Valkyrie, Dark Prince, Royal Ghost, Executioner, Lumberjack, Prince, Mini P.E.K.K.A.) counter her and survive to counterpush; Mini P.E.K.K.A. one-shots her.
- Dashers and jumpers — Bandit, Boss Bandit, Mega Knight, Fisherman — negate her range; Mega Knight can also land on top of her, and the Fisherman pulls and slows her.
- Skeletons or Goblins surrounding her, or an Ice Spirit/Electro Spirit, limit her to a single Tower shot; Electro Wizard's spawn damage plus stun does the same and leaves him alive.
- Minion Horde, Hunter and Mega Minion kill her quickly when placed on top of her.
- Spells: Fireball one-shots her at a Level difference (otherwise leaves her too weak to hit the Tower), Zap/Giant Snowball/The Log finish her, and Poison, Lightning and Rocket fully destroy an equal-level Musketeer.
- Giant Snowball knocks back and slows her out of connecting, and Barbarian Barrel or Royal Delivery plus the Crown Tower stop her for a positive Elixir trade.
- Void kills her in 2 hits if she is alone inside it — deploying Goblins or another 4+ troop card inside can save her.
- Against Cannoneer and Dagger Duchess she only lands two hits before dying.
- Trifecta with Hog Rider and Valkyrie is stronger than the base version, because she can hang back away from the other two and deny a Lightning that would hit all three — though a P.E.K.K.A still hard counters it for a 5 Elixir advantage.
- Her small mass lets a Hog Rider placed behind her push her to the Tower faster, at the cost of more damage taken and a weaker Hog Rider.
- Spending spells on her frees the opponent to play a card that thrives on their absence, such as Barbarians.
- Good pick for the Dragon Hunt sub-mode of Treasure Hunt thanks to her matchup against Baby Dragon, Phoenix, Mega Minion, Inferno Dragon and Electro Dragon.
