# Evolved Witch

> *In-game description:* "Your Skeletons are willing to sacrifice themselves for their beloved Witch, lending their souls and healing her when they're destroyed."

**Base card:** [witch.md](../cards/witch.md)

## Overview

The Witch's Evolution is an Epic card unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Witch with identical stats to the original. Once a Skeleton that the Evolved Witch has spawned is defeated, she is healed. This healing can overheal, allowing her to achieve 24% more hitpoints at its maximum capacity. The Evolved Witch takes an appearance as a woman who has glowing pink eyes and violet eyebrows, wears a violet cloak with gold linings, a ram face on top and two horns on the sides and dress, a dark gray top, team-colored armlets, darker purple lipstick, and a belt with the Card Evolution symbol in the middle, and wields a staff with a skull on top. The Witch's Evolution costs 5 Elixir and requires 1 cycle to deploy.
