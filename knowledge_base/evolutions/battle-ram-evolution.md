# Evolved Battle Ram

> *In-game description:* "These Evolved Barbarians upgraded their hardware! Evolved Battle Ram deals damage and pushes enemies back during its charge. Once it connects to a building, it won't stop charging!"

**Base card:** [battle-ram.md](../cards/battle-ram.md)

## Overview

The Battle Ram's Evolution is a Rare card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Battle Ram with identical stats to the original. Once it starts charging, it deals damage to any troop on its way to its target and inflicts knockback. Once it reaches a building, it will bounce back and repeatedly deal damage to its target until it dies. Once destroyed, it will break and reveal 2 Evolved Barbarians underneath. It appears to be a battering ram consisting of a large, thick, heavy log with an attached metal ram head at the front that has purple eyes, horn tips and spikes on its neck, along with a metal chain at the back, carried by two Barbarians underneath. The Battle Ram's Evolution costs 4 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
