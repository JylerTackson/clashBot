# Evolved P.E.K.K.A

> *In-game description:* "Floats like a butterfly, stings like a bee. Evolved P.E.K.K.A has a giant soul-sucking sword that restores health after slaying her enemies."

**Base card:** [p-e-k-k-a.md](../cards/p-e-k-k-a.md)

## Overview

The P.E.K.K.A's Evolution is an Epic card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a P.E.K.K.A with identical stats to the original. Once the Evolved P.E.K.K.A takes out a troop or building, a butterfly will spawn around her sword, healing 3.5%-15% of her original hitpoints, the magnitude of the heal being determined by the defeated unit's hitpoints. This healing can overheal, allowing her to achieve 50% more hitpoints at its maximum capacity. She appears as a robot-looking troop larger than the Mini P.E.K.K.A. clothed in heavy dark gray armor with gold linings, dark-colored stabilizers and two large magenta spikes on her shoulders, wears team-colored straps with the Card Evolution symbol in the center, has magenta eyes and horns, and wields a large, magenta-glowing metal sword with a golden handle. The P.E.K.K.A's Evolution costs 7 Elixir and requires 1 cycle to deploy.

## What changes mechanically

- New ability "Butter-Heal": when the Evolved P.E.K.K.A lands the killing blow on a troop or building, a butterfly spawns around her sword and heals her.
- Each heal is worth 3.5%-15% of her original hitpoints, scaled by the defeated unit's hitpoints — the tougher the kill, the bigger the heal.
- The heal is tiered by the victim's hitpoints: three stages with their own heal amounts and hitpoint thresholds (at level 13: 194 heal below 1198 HP, 369 heal below 2409 HP, 698 heal above that).
- Healing can overheal up to 50% above her normal maximum (e.g. 4550 -> 6824 at level 13, 6056 -> 9083 at level 16).
- Heal is applied in pulses: 1 pulse every 0.55 sec, 0.5 sec between pulses.
- All other stats are identical to the base P.E.K.K.A. — 7 Elixir, 1.8 sec hit speed, Slow (45), melee medium (1.2) range, ground-only targeting.
- Crucially she only heals on the finishing blow — unlike Evolved Bats, which recover hitpoints on every attack. If a Dart Goblin, Minions, Spear Goblins, Bats, Skeletons, Guards, Goblins or Archers steals the kill on a tank she was fighting, she gets nothing.
- Requires 1 cycle to evolve.

## Notes

- She is an incredibly powerful wall on offense and defense: she shuts down massive ground pushes for a positive Elixir trade and heals off the kills, staying healthy enough to counterattack.
- Never ignore her with a tower troop — unanswered she takes a Crown Tower and nearly the King's Tower too.
- Do not defend her with small-to-medium ground swarms like Goblin Gang or Guards as you would the regular P.E.K.K.A.: each kill heals her and likely leaves her healthy enough to take a tower.
- She is one of the only single-card hard counters to the Evolved Mega Knight — high DPS drains him while she tanks the pushback-jump combo, and the finishing blow heals her for a neutral Elixir trade with pressure left over.
- Evolved Skeletons and Skeleton Army still counter her through multiplication and sheer DPS, though her healing buys the opponent more time to Zap or Giant Snowball the swarm away.
- Best defensive approach: keep her busy with a high-DPS, high-hitpoint troop (Mighty Miner, Monk, Prince, Goblin Machine, Boss Bandit) so she cannot land kills, while a cheap ground swarm finishes her.
- High-DPS units that survive one hit are decent stallers — Guards and Royal Recruits always survive a hit while shielded thanks to her slow attack rate; Elite Barbarians deal real damage while occupying her.
- She still cannot hit air, so Minion Horde, Phoenix, Mega Minion and Inferno Dragon counter her well — though Arrows/Evolved Zap and Wizard/Musketeer/Electro Wizard neutralize those in turn.
- Situationally she can distract enemy air troops, tanking many shots from Baby Dragon, Electro Dragon, Skeleton Dragons or Flying Machine.
- Long-ranged troops (Musketeer, Dart Goblin, Flying Machine, Magic Archer, Princess, Evolved Archers) plus a mini tank (Ice Golem, Valkyrie, Knight) fully counter her — the range denies her any kills to heal from while the tank kites.
- She is one of the best counters to the Evolved Goblin Giant: she destroys him quickly while the spawned Goblins and Spear Goblins refill her health, creating a high-pressure counterattack.
- She one-shots Barbarians up to 3 levels higher than her.
- Ice Golem kiting, more expensive building chasers (especially Goblin Giant), Inferno Tower, Tombstone, Zappies, Sparky, Miner lane-luring and Fisherman pulls all counter her the same way they counter the base P.E.K.K.A.
- Spells remain essential: Poison keeps large swarms off her and beats hard counters like the Witch; Tornado pulls unreachable ranged units into her sword (letting her OHKO a Witch and kill her Skeletons); Rage only pays off on a counterpush that already has support.
- Pairs well with Fisherman (pulls ranged troops in), Mighty Miner (deploy together and activate his ability for two-lane tension), and Electro Wizard against Electro Giant (stuns timed to her swings mean no reflection damage).
- Double Prince + Evolved P.E.K.K.A. amplifies both strengths and weaknesses; Rocket or Minion Horde punish it. Mega Knight + Evolved P.E.K.K.A. covers each other's weaknesses but costs 14 Elixir and is even weaker to air — use Mini P.E.K.K.A., Mega Minion, Mighty Miner or Prince instead.
- Strong against the Hog Rider + Valkyrie + Musketeer combo (OHKOs the Musketeer, shrugs off the Valkyrie) for a +5 Elixir trade; bodyblocking a lone Hog Rider or Ram Rider stops it entirely at full health.
- Put a distraction unit (Skeletons, Goblins, Barbarians, Guards) in front of her against heavy hitters like Prince, Mighty Miner, Hunter, Sparky or Mini P.E.K.K.A. to preserve her hitpoints.
- Her low speed lets fast charging units (Dark Prince, Prince, Battle Ram, Ram Rider, Golden Knight) push her forward onto a more valuable target.
