# Evolved Princess

> *In-game description:* "There’s nothing like the frozen tundra of the deep North to awaken the Evolution within a worthy royal."

**Base card:** [princess.md](../cards/princess.md)

## Overview

The Princess's Evolution is a Legendary card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns an evolved Princess with area-damage, air-targeting, very long-ranged, ground troop with low hitpoints and moderate damage. She resembles a woman with purple eyes, long black hair with a gray stripe and eyebrows, wearing a blue robe with team-colored sleeves and a woolen base, a cape with a Card Evolution symbol on the front, an ice crown with a golden circlet, a purple belt with a crown symbol, gloves and leggings, and dark brown boots, and wields a bow with a team-colored handle, dark purple stabilizers with golden edges and woolen details, and arrows with team-colored fletchings and icy points. The Princess's Evolution costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

## Abilities (wiki sections)

### Ability

Once the Evolved Princess spawns, her first shot will slow down enemies within a 3-tile radius (by 30%, just like the Ice Wizard) for 7 seconds. After this first shot, she will fire two normal shots, and the third one will slow them down. Additionally, when the Evolved Princess dies, she leaves behind a 3-tile area-damaging slowdown zone that also lasts 7 seconds.
