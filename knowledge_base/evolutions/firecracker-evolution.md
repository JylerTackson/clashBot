# Evolved Firecracker

> *In-game description:* "Some Firecrackers just want to watch the world burn. Especially this one! The Firecracker Evolution shoots a supercharged rocket that deals Damage per second to any enemies caught in her sparks."

**Base card:** [firecracker.md](../cards/firecracker.md)

## Overview

The Firecracker's Evolution is a Common card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Firecracker with identical stats to the original. She shoots 1 projectile that when it ends, breaks into 5 shrapnel, which will continue travelling. Both the initial and small shrapnel will leave sparks behind, which will deal damage every 0.25 seconds and slow down enemy troops on them, similar to a Poison. The Firecracker resembles an Archer, except that she wears a team-colored headband, a navy and gray-colored tunic with a Card Evolution symbol in the middle, an indigo hood, gloves, belt with a gold buckle and buttons and boots with a rope tied to each of them, and carries a wooden rocket launcher with an indigo striped golden rocket inside it that has a golden fuse. The Firecracker's Evolution costs 3 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
