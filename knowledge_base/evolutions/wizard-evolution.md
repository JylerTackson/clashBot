# Evolved Wizard

> *In-game description:* "Is it hot in here or is it just him? Wizard is now outfitted with a fancy fiery shield. Upon breaking, the shield explodes, dealing Damage and pushing nearby enemies back."

**Base card:** [wizard.md](../cards/wizard.md)

## Overview

The Wizard's Evolution is a Rare card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Wizard with identical stats to the original. Once deployed, a Fire Shield will appear around the Wizard, preventing him from dying in 1 attack. Once the shield is destroyed, all pushback capable troops within 3 tiles of the Wizard will be pushed back by 3 tiles and take high damage. He takes the appearance of a man who has glowing pink eyes, brown eyebrows, and a goatee, and wears a violet cloak with brass-colored linings and boots, a team-colored shirt, pants and wristbands, and a golden belt chain with the Card Evolution symbol in the center. The Wizard's Evolution costs 5 Elixir and requires 1 cycle to deploy.

## What changes mechanically

- Unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards; still 5 Elixir and requires only 1 cycle to deploy.
- Spawns a Wizard with stats identical to the original — same 1.4 sec hit speed, 0.4 sec first hit speed, 5.5 range, 1.5 splash radius, Air & Ground targeting and the same hitpoints/damage per level.
- New ability, the Fire Shield: a shield appears on deploy that prevents him from dying in 1 attack, with its own hitpoints pool (90 at level 3 up to 309 at level 16).
- When the shield is destroyed it explodes: all pushback-capable troops within 3 tiles are pushed back 3 tiles and take shield death damage equal to his area damage (131 at level 3 up to 453 at level 16).
- The Evolution Attributes list a Shield Damage Splash Radius of 3 tiles.
- The shield explosion alone does enough damage to destroy Minions and Goblins, which the regular Wizard's deploy cannot do.
- Because of the shield he cannot be one-shot by anything, unlike the base Wizard.

## Notes

- Great defensive troop against swarm-heavy decks thanks to his splash; he can be dropped directly into the center of a large group of Minions or Goblins so the shield explosion instantly wipes them.
- Rocket, Lightning and Fireball are almost entirely ineffective while the shield is up; small spells like The Log, Giant Snowball and Zap pop the shield, so a small spell or a pushback-immune troop is the ideal way to open him up and kill him.
- He can be situationally ignored depending on the tower troop: left alone he takes roughly half a Crown Tower against a Tower Princess, slightly less than half against a Cannoneer, and only chip damage against a Dagger Duchess.
- Strong synergy with Giant, Lava Hound and Golem — his damage cripples or kills tank killers (Hunter, Lumberjack, Inferno Dragon, Three Musketeers, Elite Barbarians, Prince, Mini P.E.K.K.A) and his splash wipes Goblin Gang, Skeleton Army, Royal Recruits, Minion Horde, Evolved Bats and Barbarians, while the Giant tanks for him.
- With any tower troop plus Goblins or Skeletons he fully counters the Evolved Royal Recruits for a +1 Elixir trade and survives for a counterattack.
- Pairs very well with Tornado and Evolved Valkyrie — the Tornado pulls troops into his splash radius for more targets and more damage.
- Very effective against the Lava Hound: high damage on the Hound and splash that clears the Lava Pups, usually holding Crown Tower damage to a minimum.
- With Crown Tower help, Royal Delivery fully counters him for a +2 Elixir trade — drop it on top of him so the Royal Recruit distracts and finishes him after being knocked back by the Fire Shield.
- King Tower activation trick: with a Miner on the inside corner of a Tower Princess, placing the Evolved Wizard can knock the Miner into the King Tower with the shield explosion.
- With a Crown Tower and placed in the center of your side, he fully counters Elite Barbarians for a +1 Elixir trade, which makes him good against decks that run Elite Barbarians inside a bigger push such as Elixir Golem.
- Conversely he synergizes with the Elixir Golem: his shield and ranged damage discourage the opponent from placing units next to the Golem, forcing them to deal with the Wizard first while the Golem chips the tower.
- He can be swapped for other consistent splash Evolutions like Evolved Firecracker or Evolved Bomber depending on deck needs.
- Mega Knight, when the opponent is up Elixir, easily shuts down a small push containing him: the spawn damage instantly destroys the shield and weakens the whole push.
