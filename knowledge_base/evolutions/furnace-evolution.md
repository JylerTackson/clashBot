# Evolved Furnace

> *In-game description:* "All fired up! When it’s hot and attacking, Evolved Furnace summons Fire Spirits even faster from hat to hearth."

**Base card:** [furnace.md](../cards/furnace.md)

## Overview

The Furnace's Evolution is a Rare card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Furnace, with identical stats to the original. When the Furnace attacks, the spawn speed is decreased to 2.4sec, as well as spawn Fire Spirits to the side, instead of the front. It appears to be an amethyst cast-iron steam locomotive armor with golden spikes, limbs, and a Card Evolution symbol in the center, wearing purple gloves ane cape with golden linings, team-colored chainmail, a cracked metal witch-like hat on top with a golden band in the middle, and a golden grate in the center. It also holds a wooden staff with a cauldron placed on top that has a golden handle filled with bubbling magenta fluid in it, which resembles Elixir, along with a large amethyst knob on the bottom with golden spikes on it. The Furnace's Evolution costs 4 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.

## What changes mechanically

- Unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards; costs 4 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
- Spawns a Furnace with stats identical to the original — hitpoints, damage, hit speed (1.8 sec), 6 tile range and the Fire Spirit stats are unchanged.
- New ability "Hot Spawn": while the Furnace is attacking, its spawn speed drops from 7 sec to 2.4 sec, producing Fire Spirits far more frequently.
- The faster spawning only applies when it is attacking; when idle it keeps the base 7 sec spawn cycle.
- Fire Spirits now appear to the side of the Furnace instead of in front of it.
- The side spawns alternate: first to the left, then to the right, then left again, and so on.

## Notes

- Keep the Furnace attacking as much as possible — the Hot Spawn rate only applies while it is attacking, so more attacks means more Fire Spirits.
- The alternating left/right spawn pattern can be used deliberately to lure units from the other side of the arena.
- Rage works really well on it, spawning even more Fire Spirits on top of the faster attacks.
