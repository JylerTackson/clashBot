# Evolved Royal Recruits

> *In-game description:* "Royal Recruits have been taking lessons from Prince. Once their shields have been broken, they will begin an epic charge!"

**Base card:** [royal-recruits.md](../cards/royal-recruits.md)

## Overview

The Royal Recruits' Evolution is a Common card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns 6 Royal Recruits with identical stats to the originals. After their shield is destroyed, they gain the ability to charge towards their target after traveling 2 tiles, dealing 2x damage if they hit the target whilst charging. They appear as men with large, round noses, wear wooden buckets with team-colored feathers, which cover their faces, metal gloves, an armor, a chainmail, a purple shirt, cape, pants and sandals, and a team-colored band on their right arm and belt with purple linings and a Card Evolution symbol in the center, and wield spears and wooden shields. The Royal Recruits' Evolution costs 7 Elixir and requires 1 cycle to deploy.
