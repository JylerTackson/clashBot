# Evolved Cannon

> *In-game description:* "When this Evolution is deployed, it barrages both sides of the Arena, dealing damage and knocking enemies back. Who doesn't love the smell of gunpowder in the morning?"

**Base card:** [cannon.md](../cards/cannon.md)

## Overview

The Cannon's Evolution is a Common card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Cannon with identical stats to the original. After deployment, the Cannon shoots 9 cannonballs in 2 rows, 5 on the top row and 4 on the bottom, which deals area damage and inflicts knockback. It appears as a large dark metal barrel mounted on a four-legged wooden mount with a purple metal plate, legs and supports reinforced with gold chains, with a purple muzzle, two team-colored ridges, one midway and the other at the rear, along with a Card Evolution symbol in between, and a metal wing attached to both sides. The Cannon's Evolution costs 3 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
