# Evolved Royal Hogs

> *In-game description:* "These guys are hogging the spotlight! Evolved Royal Hogs take flight towards the nearest building and will fall to the ground once they reach their destination or, if attacked, doing damage on landing."

**Base card:** [royal-hogs.md](../cards/royal-hogs.md)

## Overview

The Royal Hogs' Evolution is a Rare card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns four Royal Hogs with stats identical to their originals. They deploy as flying troops rather than on the ground, unable to be attacked by ground-targeting troops. Upon attacking, or getting hurt, the hogs will fall to the ground, dealing low area damage on impact. They appear to be smaller versions of the Hog Rider's hog wearing team-colored knight-like helmets with the Card Evolution symbol on top and have wings. The Royal Hogs' Evolution costs 5 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
