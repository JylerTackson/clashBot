# Evolved Goblin Barrel

> *In-game description:* "Evolved Goblin Barrel is like a box of chocolates. You never know what you're going to get. Whenever Evolved Goblin Barrel is cast, a Decoy Barrel - filled with Decoy Goblins! - is thrown in the opposite lane."

**Base card:** [goblin-barrel.md](../cards/goblin-barrel.md)

## Overview

The Goblin Barrel’s Evolution is an Epic card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It is a Goblin Barrel with identical stats to the original. When launched, a second Goblin Barrel is launched alongside the first one, which lands on its mirroring tile in the other lane. Once the second Goblin Barrel has hit the ground, it spawns three Decoy Goblins. which are single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troops with very low hitpoints and high damage. It is a purple-spiked wooden barrel with a purple Goblin emblem on it. The Decoy Goblins appear as dolls with light blonde hair, an "X" mark on their forehead, purple eyes with an "X" on them, and a wind-up key behind them, wear team-colored overalls, and wield a shorter version of the Goblins' daggers. The Goblin Barrel’s Evolution costs 3 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.

## What changes mechanically

- Identical stats to the regular Goblin Barrel: 3 Elixir, 1.5 tile radius, three Goblins with 1.1 second hit speed, Very Fast (120) movement, 1.1 second deploy time and Melee: Short (0.5) range.
- Decoy Barrel ability: a second, visually near-identical Barrel is launched at the same time and lands on the mirroring tile in the opposite lane (+1 Barrel over the base card).
- The second Barrel spawns 3 Decoy Goblins - single-target, ground-targeting, melee ground troops with very low hitpoints and high damage.
- Decoy Goblins are weaker than the real ones at every Level: e.g. at Level 16, 130 hitpoints and 143 damage (130 DPS) versus 325 hitpoints and 193 damage (175 DPS) for the real Goblins.
- The fake Barrel is distinguishable only by a purple glow, so the opponent must guess which lane holds the real Goblins.
- Unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards; requires 2 cycles to deploy.
- Both Barrels remain vulnerable to the same counters as the regular Goblin Barrel.

## Notes

- Devastating on offense in both lanes if not answered properly - the identical appearance tricks the opponent into spending their counter on the wrong Barrel.
- With Crown Tower help and a center-of-arena placement it fully counters a P.E.K.K.A. for a +4 Elixir trade: the Decoy Goblins land close to the real ones, adding damage and distracting her longer, leaving her helpless without a small spell like The Log behind her.
- Evolved Royal Recruits are an excellent partner - three Recruits in each lane tank for both the Decoy and real Goblins, and if the opponent uses The Log or Arrows on the Barrels the Recruits lose their shields, charge and add pressure. It is a hefty 10 Elixir push, so only do it after the Recruits have defended.
- Royal Hogs, especially split, also pair very well - two Hogs per side bait weak spells and tank for the Barrels. Unlike Royal Recruits they do not attack troops, so Executioner, Bowler, Valkyrie, Bomber, Goblin Demolisher, Witch and Wizard mitigate the damage much better.
- Clone + Mirror gimmick: with the opponent's counters out of cycle, an Evolved Goblin Barrel on the King Tower plus a Mirrored Barrel and Clone can produce enough Goblins to destroy the King Tower outright and win instantly - but it is extremely predictable after earlier Barrels.
- Countering it: Goblins or Guards plus an Electro Spirit, each placed one tile below their lane's Crown Tower, fully counter it for a neutral Elixir trade; the Electro Spirit clears the Decoys while the Goblins/Guards distract and kill the real ones and survive for a counterpush.
- Dagger Duchess alone fully counters the Decoy Goblins regardless of placement, letting the defender aim every other counter at the real Goblins; Cannoneer and Tower Princess cannot do this.
- An Evolved Cannon prevents all but one hit on either Crown Tower with a Tower Princess, but only if placed in front of the King Tower, which stops it from distracting anything else.
- If unsure which lane holds the real Barrel, a cheap ranged card such as Dart Goblin, Spear Goblins or Archers directly in front of the King Tower reduces damage from both.
- Activating the King Tower makes both Barrels far less effective, so the defender should prioritize finding a way to do that.
