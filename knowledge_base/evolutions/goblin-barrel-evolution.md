# Evolved Goblin Barrel

> *In-game description:* "Evolved Goblin Barrel is like a box of chocolates. You never know what you're going to get. Whenever Evolved Goblin Barrel is cast, a Decoy Barrel - filled with Decoy Goblins! - is thrown in the opposite lane."

**Base card:** [goblin-barrel.md](../cards/goblin-barrel.md)

## Overview

The Goblin Barrel’s Evolution is an Epic card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It is a Goblin Barrel with identical stats to the original. When launched, a second Goblin Barrel is launched alongside the first one, which lands on its mirroring tile in the other lane. Once the second Goblin Barrel has hit the ground, it spawns three Decoy Goblins. which are single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troops with very low hitpoints and high damage. It is a purple-spiked wooden barrel with a purple Goblin emblem on it. The Decoy Goblins appear as dolls with light blonde hair, an "X" mark on their forehead, purple eyes with an "X" on them, and a wind-up key behind them, wear team-colored overalls, and wield a shorter version of the Goblins' daggers. The Goblin Barrel’s Evolution costs 3 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
