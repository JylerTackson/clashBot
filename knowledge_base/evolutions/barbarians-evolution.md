# Evolved Barbarians

> *In-game description:* "These Barbarians have grown stronger through Evolution! Not only do they now have glorious hair, but they also gain extra Speed and Hitspeed with each attack"

**Base card:** [barbarians.md](../cards/barbarians.md)

## Overview

The Barbarians' Evolution is a Common card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns 5 Barbarians with hitpoints 10% higher than the originals. Every attack, their attack speed and movement speed increases by 30% for 3 seconds. They appear as bare-chested men who wield longer swords with a purple handle, have purple eyes, longer, brighter blond eyebrows, close-cropped hair and horseshoe moustaches, and wear three purple straps with golden buttons on them, which are held by a golden ring in the center, headband with a Card Evolution symbol in the middle, kilt, golden-spiked wristbands and sandals, along with a team-colored belt. The Barbarians' Evolution costs 5 Elixir and requires 1 cycle to deploy.

## What changes mechanically

- Unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards; still 5 Elixir but only requires 1 Cycle to deploy — the cheapest cycle requirement of the Common evolutions.
- Still spawns 5 single-target ground melee Barbarians in a star formation, with 10% more hitpoints than the originals.
- New ability **Blade Rage**: every attack raises their attack speed and movement speed by 30% (lead text says the boost lasts 3 seconds; the Evolution Attributes table lists a 5 sec boost duration).
- The self-rage stacks up over a fight, so their sustained DPS ends up roughly equal to the Elite Barbarians'.
- The rage does not stack with the Rage spell or the Lumberjack's dropped Rage, so those cards should not be paired with them.
- The +10% hitpoints do not change their spell breakpoints much: Lightning still kills them instantly, but they now block 3 Lightning strikes and shield more units.

## Notes

- With their hit speed stacked they shred tanks on defense — Mega Knight, Giant Skeleton, Boss Bandit, P.E.K.K.A.
- They can substitute for Mini P.E.K.K.A., Monk, Lumberjack, Prince, Mighty Miner, and Elite Barbarians thanks to their very high DPS and offensive/defensive pressure.
- As a Lightning shield they now also protect Musketeer, Ice Wizard, Electro Wizard, Goblinstein, and Little Prince, on top of everything regular Barbarians could save.
- Very effective against Hog Rider, Battle Ram, and Ram Rider: heavy damage plus their mass and spread force those units to path around, so they die without touching the Crown Tower.
- Strong tank answer generally — numbers, combined damage, and enough bulk to shrug off most spells; against a ground splash card plus melee tank, wait for the river crossing then drop them onto the splash unit, surround it, and clean up the tank for a big positive trade (fails if there is more than one splasher).
- Effective against Electro Giant for a +2 Elixir trade, surviving his reflected damage — but they end nearly dead, so account for his support troops.
- Placed early they can surround the Crown Tower against a Goblin Barrel with minimal damage taken, though it is a 5-Elixir investment.
- Splitting them at the back discourages attacks on both lanes and reduces spell value; the downside is their slow arrival.
- Do not send them unsupported in one lane — most counters are a positive Elixir trade for the opponent and the other lane is left open. They are best against an opponent low on Elixir or out of counters, since they will destroy a Crown Tower by themselves regardless of tower troop.
- Counters: Fireball or Poison plus a 2-Elixir spell such as Giant Snowball or Rage fully clears them on defense; offensively an early Fireball with Tower Princess or Dagger Duchess support stops them (not with the Cannoneer); Rocket works but is a negative trade unless they have support.
- They bait Fireball and Poison for other spell-weak cards — Zappies, Royal Recruits, Magic Archer, Flying Machine, Ice Wizard, Mother Witch, Skeleton Dragons; if they survive the spell, add a tank in front for value.
- Do not drop a troop on top of them near your Crown Tower — the kill feeds their rage and speeds them up. Use a spell like Fireball or The Log, or an Ice Spirit / Electro Spirit that stuns without triggering the rage.
- Bowler stops them for a neutral Elixir trade by continuously knocking them back so they never attack and never stack the boost.
- A mini-tank (Knight, Valkyrie, Golden Knight, Mighty Miner, Dark Prince, Royal Ghost, Lumberjack, Monk, Skeleton King, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Prince) in the top center of the defender's side fully stops bridge-placed Evolved Barbarians with Cannoneer support.
- Area damage cripples or stops them for a neutral-to-positive trade — Bomber, Wizard, Bowler, Goblin Demolisher, Evolved Ice Spirit, Skeleton King, Sparky, Bomb Tower, Evolved Tesla, Dark Prince, Firecracker, Witch, Valkyrie, Baby Dragon, Skeleton Dragons, Mega Knight, Executioner — but a Tornado or Freeze on that splash unit lets the Barbarians through to devastate the tower.
