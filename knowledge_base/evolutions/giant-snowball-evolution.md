# Evolved Giant Snowball

> *In-game description:* "A rolling snowball gathers no Troops...unless it's Evolved! Evolved Giant Snowball gathers enemy Troops and rolls forward before breaking, which freeze and slows the Troops down."

**Base card:** [giant-snowball.md](../cards/giant-snowball.md)

## Overview

The Giant Snowball's Evolution is a Common card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It is a Giant Snowball with a slowdown duration 1 sec longer than the original. Once the initial hit is unleashed, the affected troops get pulled into it and the Giant Snowball rolls on the ground for 4.5 tiles similarly to the Barbarian Barrel. When it finishes its roll, the troops are freed. It is an oversized snowball surrounded with indigo shards, and has a sword on top and a spear on the bottom, along with a hand and a foot sticking out of it. The Giant Snowball's Evolution costs 2 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.

## What changes mechanically

- Slow duration is 1 second longer than the base card: 4 seconds instead of 3 (still -30% slowdown, 2.5 tile radius, 2 Elixir, air and ground).
- Replaces the knockback with Snow Roll: troops hit are pulled into the snowball and carried along as it rolls 4.5 tiles across the ground at speed 300, similar to a Barbarian Barrel, and are freed when the roll ends.
- Troops inside the rolling snowball cannot be targeted while they are being carried.
- Carried troops keep their collision while rolling, so they can shove other troops around.
- Unlike the regular Snowball, it can stop the charge of heavy troops such as Prince and Dark Prince, and the ramp-up attack of the Mighty Miner.
- Unlike the regular Snowball, it cannot counter a Princess by pushing her closer to your own Crown Tower (the roll moves her the other way).
- Damage values are unchanged from the base Giant Snowball.
- If a Monk reflects it, only the ground-rolling portion is reflected, not the on-air travel.
- Unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards; requires 2 cycles.

## Notes

- Because carried troops are untargetable, it forces your own cards to re-focus - very good with X-Bow, which can then re-target the Crown Tower.
- The roll lines enemies up in a row, ideal for line/splash damage from Magic Archer, Evolved Bomber, Firecracker, Executioner, Wizard or Bowler.
- Re-aiming tool: if you misplaced a building against a structure-targeting troop, throw the Snowball to the left or right of that troop so it ends up nearer the building and locks onto it. Offensively, the same trick redirects a mid-lane defender to the side so it stops attacking your troops.
- Sparky synergy: pull enemy troops closer to a Crown Tower so Sparky's shot hits both the troops and the tower, while the slow buys Sparky reload time.
- With good placement plus a Tower Princess or Cannoneer, it fully counters a Balloon for a +3 or better Elixir trade.
- Rolls a Giant Skeleton away from your Crown Tower, avoiding its massive death bomb damage.
- If an Evolved Valkyrie is placed near your tank at the river, roll her closer to the opponent's Crown Tower - your tank reaches the tower faster and her counter is nullified.
- Adds to the existing Graveyard synergy by rolling the opponent's Graveyard counters further away from the Skeletons.
- Since carried troops keep collision, you can throw it at a tanky enemy behind your card to push your own card forward - useful for reaching the enemy Crown Tower.
- Hits both the bush and the Goblins of a Suspicious Bush for an even Elixir trade; the Goblins survive at about two thirds HP, easily cleaned up by the Crown Towers or most cards.
- Evolved Electro Dragon synergy: freed troops end up grouped together, so the Evolved Electro Dragon's chained bolts hit them all repeatedly.
- Protects a Goblin Drill by rolling nearby counters away and slowing them, letting the Drill do heavy tower damage. Less effective with the Evolved Goblin Drill, which moves toward the pushed card as it takes damage.
- With an Evolved Lumberjack's Ghost near the enemy Crown Tower, roll away the distraction troops so the Ghost locks onto the tower for guaranteed damage.
- Pair with Bandit, Boss Bandit or Golden Knight - rolling enemies far enough away triggers their dash, adding damage and letting them tank hits mid-dash.
