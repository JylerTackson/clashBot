# Evolved Bats

> *In-game description:* "What's cooler than Bats? VAMPIRE BATS. These creatures of the night heal with each attack and can increase their Hitpoints past their maximum!"

**Base card:** [bats.md](../cards/bats.md)

## Overview

The Bats' Evolution is a Common card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns 5 Bats with hitpoints 50% higher than the original. Every time they attack, they heal themselves, having two healing pulses with a 0.5 second interval. The healing can overheal them, allowing their hitpoints to reach up to double of their initial ones. They resemble small, indigo bats with glowing pink eyes, fangs, brighter pink ears, snouts and lower wings, and purple underbellies and upper wings. The Bats' Evolution costs 2 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
