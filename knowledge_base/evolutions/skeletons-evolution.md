# Evolved Skeletons

> *In-game description:* "When there's no room in Spooky Town, the dead will walk the Arena! This Evolution is a never-ending horde with each Skeleton summoning another with every attack!"

**Base card:** [skeletons.md](../cards/skeletons.md)

## Overview

The Skeletons' Evolution is a Common card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns 3 Skeletons, with identical stats to the originals, in a triangle formation. Every time they attack, an additional Evolved Skeleton will spawn, for a maximum total of 8 Skeletons. They resemble small undead creatures with a skull that lacks a lower jaw, have purple eyes and a nose and a scar on their left eye, wield short purple swords, and wear a purple ushanka and a belt around their chests. The Skeletons' Evolution costs 1 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
