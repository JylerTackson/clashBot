# Evolved Goblin Cage

> *In-game description:* "If you smell what the Goblin Brawler is cooking! Enemies that get close to the Evolved Goblin Cage are pulled inside and take damage over time until the enemy or the cage is destroyed."

**Base card:** [goblin-cage.md](../cards/goblin-cage.md)

## Overview

The Goblin Cage's Evolution is a Rare card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Goblin Cage with identical stats to the original. Once a ground troop comes within 3 tiles of the building, they get pulled into the cage and they are attacked directly by the Goblin Cage, and any troop pulled will be freed. When defeated, it spawns a Goblin Brawler with identical stats to the original. It appears to be a wooden cage with four dark violet metal supports with a golden spike on them, a lock on the front with the Card Evolution symbol on it, and a pair of bars at each side, while the Goblin Brawler appears as a larger, buff Goblin with violet eyes, mohawk and unibrow with a golden piercing, has tattoos printed on his back and arms, and sports a striped white shirt and a gold chain with the Card Evolution symbol, along with violet pants and bands tied on his hands which extends till his lower arms. The Goblin Cage’s Evolution costs 4 Elixir and requires 2 cycle to deploy.
