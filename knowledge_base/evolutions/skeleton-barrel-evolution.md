# Evolved Skeleton Barrel

> *In-game description:* "Is it a bird? Is it a plane? Is it... two Barrels full of Skeletons? When Evolved Skeleton Barrel drops its Barrels, it deals Death Damage and spawns Skeletons. Upon taking enough Damage, a Barrel will drop early."

**Base card:** [skeleton-barrel.md](../cards/skeleton-barrel.md)

## Overview

The Skeleton Barrel's Evolution is a Common card unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Skeleton Barrel with hitpoints 25% higher and death damage 64% higher than the original. It spawns with 2 barrels, each with 7 Skeletons: one dropped when it reaches 75% hitpoints, and another dropped when it is defeated. It is a golden-spiked, dark brown, double barrel with a Card Evolution symbol on both sides chained to a pair of team-colored balloons with a violet skull printed on them and a small spike on top of them. The Skeleton Barrel's Evolution costs 3 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.
