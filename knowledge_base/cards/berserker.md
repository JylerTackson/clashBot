# Berserker

> *In-game description:* "It's surprising how often she needs her axes sharpened, but then again, if you liked hitting things as fast as her, so would you."

**Related:** [Hero variant: berserker-hero](../heroes/berserker-hero.md)

## Overview

The Berserker is a Common card that is unlocked from the Jungle Arena (Arena 9) It spawns a fast, single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troop with moderate hitpoints and low damage. She appears as a kid who wears a brown dress made of bear skin and wristbands and a team-colored belt, has orange hair with two buns, eyes and eyebrows, a loose tooth, and a scar on her left chin, arms and legs, and wields a pair of cleavers. A Berserker card costs 2 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Her name is exactly what you're thinking! She won't hesitate to tear you apart for only 2 Elixir! She attacks very fast and is resistant to spells, though her damage is low, so she'll have to be supported accordingly in order to go berserk mode. Beware though, if her mom, Rune Giant, is around, the Berserker will be even more dangerous!

If her **Hero** is active, you can spend 3 Elixir to use her **Savage Survival** ability. creating a strong connection with her spiritual bear. This will increase her movement speed, attack speed, and damage, while making her undestroyable! Her invincibility allows her to shred any and all units in her path, no matter if big or small!
