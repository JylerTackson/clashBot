# Goblin Curse

> *In-game description:* "One does not simply reverse a curse! Enemies within the curse's reach will take damage over time and be turned into Goblins after they are defeated."

## Overview

The Goblin Curse is an Epic card that is unlocked from Serenity Peak (Arena 14). It is an area-damage, air-targeting spell with a medium radius and very low damage that is dealt every second for 6 seconds. If those troops are defeated, they would convert into Goblins. It is a lime-colored liquid encased in a large vial, which is secured by a thin rope, with a large, rectangular wooden cap with three golden hairs, a circular mouth, two large ears, and two green, gem-shaped eyes on the front, resembling a Baby Goblin. A Goblin Curse card costs 2 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Who is the Witch who sent a green, Goblin-turning spell to the Arena? For 2 Elixir, this spell deals low damage in its duration, but makes up for this by making enemy troops in its cursed ring walk slower and turned into Goblins once defeated. You better not become a Goblin yourself if you don't want to face bureaucracy, however.
