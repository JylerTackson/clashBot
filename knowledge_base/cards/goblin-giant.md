# Goblin Giant

> *In-game description:* "This jolly green Goblin Giant stomps towards enemy buildings. He carries two Spear Goblins everywhere he goes. It's a weird but functional arrangement."

**Related:** [Evolution: goblin-giant-evolution](../evolutions/goblin-giant-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Goblin Giant is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Jungle Arena (Arena 9). It spawns a single-target, building-targeting, melee, ground troop with very high hitpoints and moderate damage. He also carries two Spear Goblins on his back, that can attack independently on the Goblin Giant. When he is defeated, the Spear Goblins spawn and continue attacking. He appears to be a colossal, aquamarine-skinned Goblin wearing a brown aviator hat and pants with stitches, along with a backpack with ropes tied on the sides and team-colored suspenders, and has two ropes tied on his wrists, teal eyebrows, and a deep pink nose. A Goblin Giant card costs 6 Elixir to deploy.

## Strong against

- Frail swarms like Bats and Skeletons — the Spear Goblins on his back pick them off quickly, so a mistimed swarm deploy is wasted Elixir.
- Ranged glass cannons: Musketeer, Mega Minion, Electro Wizard, Ice Wizard, Witch, Magic Archer, Flying Machine, Night Witch, Electro Dragon, Baby Dragon, Phoenix and Wizard are all less reliable against him than against a Giant, because the Spear Goblins kill them before they can counterpush.
- Graveyard — the Spear Goblins hit 2 Skeletons at a time (and the Tower Princess) while he tanks; a negative Elixir trade but it sets up a counterpush.
- X-Bow — he tanks its shots while the Spear Goblins damage the X-Bow or its defenders.
- Lava Pups — placed in the middle of them once the Lava Hound is nearly dead, he tanks their damage and the Spear Goblins whittle them down, then counterpushes with most of his health.
- Balloon — with an Ice Spirit or Electro Spirit he prevents all damage except the death bomb and keeps full hitpoints for a counterattack.
- An early Golem placement behind the King Tower — his medium speed, high health and decent damage punish it, forcing a bad choice between defending and losing a Crown Tower.
- P.E.K.K.A. — used as a kiting building chaser he outpaces her and denies her the kill while the Spear Goblins chip her.

## Weak against

- High-DPS mini tanks: Prince, Lumberjack, Monk, Mighty Miner, Inferno Dragon and Mini P.E.K.K.A. are the best single-card answers — the Spear Goblins' chip damage does not threaten them and they kill him before he connects.
- Rascals — the trio's combined damage stops him for a +1 Elixir trade, with the Rascal Boy tanking the Spear Goblins.
- Buildings — his sight range matches the Giant's, so a 5-2 or 4-3 placement lures him, and most building counters are positive Elixir trades.
- Executioner — his axe finishes off the Spear Goblins on the return throw, unlike most splash answers.
- Spells that outdamage him plus a defender — Lightning in particular is used against the Inferno Dragon, Mini P.E.K.K.A. or Lumberjack answering him.
- Skeleton Army, Goblin Gang and Minion Horde — large swarms deal hefty damage to a Goblin Giant push unless the player has spell support.
- Fisherman pulls — he drags away the single-target damage dealers a Goblin Giant relies on the Spear Goblins to outlast (works both directions).

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 6 Elixir building-targeting tank with very high hitpoints and two Spear Goblins riding on his back that attack independently and are untargetable until he dies.
- When he is defeated the Spear Goblins spawn and keep fighting; they are untargetable for a short time after spawning, so the Crown Tower locks onto another unit in range.
- Tank for the Spear Goblins after his death to keep them alive longer — the Miner is ideal because you control exactly when he enters Crown Tower range.
- Their spawn delay is longer than the Battle Ram's Barbarians, so The Log cannot hit both him and the Spear Goblins, and a Bowler will not immediately kill them.
- The Spear Goblins can bait spells like Zap, which you then punish with a follow-up card weak to that spell, such as a Skeleton Barrel.
- He can move and attack in separate patterns, letting him kite units while still damaging them — less effective against troops slower than him (like P.E.K.K.A.), who simply follow him into the other lane and not across the bridge. Better still with Dagger Duchess or Cannoneer.
- Zap and Giant Snowball synergy: their damage lowers defending Minions or Goblins enough for the Spear Goblins to OHKO them.
- Rage does similar damage to Zap for the same OHKO threshold, and buffs both the Goblin Giant and his Spear Goblins without the risk of raging frail swarms.
- Fireball synergy: the Spear Goblins finish off softened defenders like Musketeer, Goblinstein, Little Prince or Electro Wizard.
- Earthquake covers his weakness to buildings by destroying them so he can keep walking, and the Spear Goblins finish troops that survive it, such as a Dart Goblin.
- Lightning is reserved for the high-damage mini tanks used against him — Inferno Dragon, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Lumberjack.
- Sparky pairs well: he moves slightly faster than her so defenders get less area damage value, and he can kite opposite-lane punishes into her; the Spear Goblins also help spells clear the swarms that counter Sparky.
- Fast troops (Dark Prince, Prince, Lumberjack) stay close to him for longer than with other tanks and handle threats; Dark Prince and Prince also shove him forward to connect earlier.
- P.E.K.K.A. + Goblin Giant is expensive but potent — he clears Bats and Skeletons while she wins duels against Prince, Mighty Miner and Mini P.E.K.K.A. Add spell support for larger swarms, and Rage to speed up the Spear Goblins.
- Elite Barbarians are a cheaper alternative to the P.E.K.K.A. in that push — less bulky and more splash-vulnerable, but faster, harder to distract and quicker at clearing small swarms.
- Fisherman pairs with him to pull away single-target counters like Mini P.E.K.K.A., Prince and Mighty Miner.
- He can also tank for a Graveyard, attacking the defenders while the Graveyard damages the Crown Tower.
- Do not use a Tombstone against him — the Spear Goblins OHKO the spawned Skeletons.
- When defending against him with frail swarms, wait until the Spear Goblins lock onto the Crown Tower or a mini tank before deploying, or the swarm is wasted.
