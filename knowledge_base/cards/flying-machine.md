# Flying Machine

> *In-game description:* "The Master Builder has sent his first contraption to the Arena! It's a fast and fun flying machine, but fragile!"

## Overview

The Flying Machine is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Builder's Workshop (Arena 5). It spawns a fast, single-target, air-targeting, long-ranged, air troop with moderate hitpoints and moderate damage. It appears to be a small turret in a barrel with a rotor with team-colored blades on top. A Flying Machine card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A 4 Elixir flying card with moderately-low hitpoints. Its long range and moderate damage allow it to attack from afar, often out-ranging its target, making it a great support card. Although it is quite frail, it can easily pressure the opponent and force a bad trade due to being decently afar from foes!

## Abilities and special mechanics

#### Epic: Beam Me Up, Barb
> "Inferno beam attack"
Uses the Inferno Tower's beam instead of its default attack.

## Strong against

- Spawners and most buildings — its 6 tile range lets it pick off buildings near the middle of the Arena without ever entering Crown Tower range.
- Passive buildings placed 6 tiles from the river, and active buildings placed 5 tiles from the river (including 5-2 and 4-3 lure placements) — it snipes them safely.
- Runners and anything with comparable hitpoints — one shot kills them outright.
- Ram Rider — placed by the river and 2 tiles into the opposite lane it attacks from about 7 tiles, outside her 5.5 tile sight range, so she never fights back.
- Lone Balloon, Knight or Valkyrie — with Crown Tower support it kills them before they connect (the Balloon still lands its death damage).
- Crown Towers under pressure — its long range and 0.5 sec first hit speed mean it lands a shot before the Tower Princess's arrow arrives, so even a nearly dead Flying Machine chips the Tower.
- Air troops threatening a ground push, which is why it covers Royal Recruits well.

## Weak against

- Fireball and Poison — either completely destroys it up to 1 level higher for a neutral Elixir trade, better still when the spell catches other targets.
- Lightning and Rocket — kill it, but at a negative Elixir trade (Rocket especially, given its high movement speed).
- Void — 2 hits destroy it if it is the only troop inside, a slight positive Elixir trade.
- Ranged air-capable troops — Executioner, Musketeer, Wizard and Electro Wizard need only a few hits; with Crown Tower support they survive healthy for a counterattack, and the Electro Wizard should use his spawn Zap to cut incoming damage.
- Hunter — placed on top of it, he does enough damage for a neutral Elixir trade.
- Mega Minion and Inferno Dragon — counters that destroy it completely rather than letting it get a parting shot in.
- Ground blockers plus tower — Ice Golem, Knight or Skeletons soak its shots while the Crown Tower kills it.
- Cannoneer and Dagger Duchess towers — they cut it down to only 2 hits landed, so it can sometimes be ignored.
- Ground melee threats it cannot stop alone — Mini P.E.K.K.A., Prince, Elite Barbarians and Lumberjack keep walking because it cannot distract them.
- Tesla — invisible underground, forcing it to fly into range before attacking despite the range advantage.

## Notes / synergies

- Fragile 4 Elixir air support: 6 tile range, Fast (90) speed, 1.1 sec hit speed, 0.5 sec first hit speed, hits Air & Ground.
- A reasonable Musketeer substitute — airborne, faster, but with less health and less damage, which makes it better at joining a push immediately at the bridge.
- Needs a tank offensively: Ice Golem, Knight, Giant, or best of all a Lava Hound since it is also an air unit.
- Supports a Balloon like other fast air troops, nudging it closer to the Tower and helping bypass building placements, though its long range makes it worse at this than shorter-ranged units.
- Pairs with a cheap Ice Spirit or Heal Spirit for a chunk of Crown Tower damage or a quick defensive combo, but the pair is Fireball-vulnerable.
- Pair it with Zap to finish sturdier swarms such as Minions and Lava Pups, which survive a single shot.
- Synergizes with Royal Recruits — they tank in both lanes while it handles air threats; place it in the center of the Arena rather than committing it to one lane.
- Use it as Fireball bait alongside other Fireball-vulnerable cards (Barbarians, Royal Recruits, Zappies, Ice Wizard, Mother Witch, Magic Archer, Skeleton Dragons) so whichever survives gets free value.
- Place a decoy such as Skeletons, Goblins or Guards to hold ground threats while the Flying Machine finishes them off.
- It cannot be kited: attack range equals sight range, so it never chases; when tanking for your Tower against an enemy Flying Machine, put the ground unit in the same lane so it does not walk out of range.
- Provoke a buried Tesla with a cheap distraction like Skeletons so the Flying Machine can use its range advantage.
- Deny a Hog Rider edge bypass by hovering over the right spot in advance and pushing the Hog toward your central building as it jumps the river — stagger the placement one tile right if the Hog is on the left side, and vice versa.
- Second-tower placements after a Tower Princess falls: 4 tiles from the river connects fastest and is unreactable but sits in King Tower range, allowing only 2 hits; 2 tiles from the river starts out of range of both towers and must float in, giving the opponent more time to react but delivering full damage.
- Interchangeable with Skeleton Dragons depending on whether you want single-target or area damage; both have similar hitpoints, survive weak spells and die to Fireball, and they can bait for each other in the same deck.
- Party modifiers: Bada Bing, Bada Boom! (+100% hit speed), Paragob Pursuit (spawns a Goblin when attacking), Beam Me Up, Barb (Inferno Tower beam attack).
