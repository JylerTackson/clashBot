# Goblin Gang

> *In-game description:* "Spawns Six Goblins - three with knives, three with spears - at a discounted Elixir cost. It's like a Goblin Value Pack!"

## Overview

The Goblin Gang is a Common card that is unlocked from the Jungle Arena (Arena 9). It spawns three Goblins and three Spear Goblins in a hexagon formation. The Goblins will spawn before the Spear Goblins. A Goblin Gang card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Six fast though weak Goblins for 3 Elixir. Three have knives for powerful ground melee combat deployed in front, and three have spears for ranged attacks on both ground and air at the rear. Together, they can clear a multitude of units, but be wary of splash cards when using this Goblin Value Pack!
