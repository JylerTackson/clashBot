# Knight

> *In-game description:* "A tough melee fighter. The Barbarian's handsome, cultured cousin. Rumor has it that he was knighted based on the sheer awesomeness of his mustache alone."

**Related:** [Evolution: knight-evolution](../evolutions/knight-evolution.md) · [Hero variant: knight-hero](../heroes/knight-hero.md)

## Overview

The Knight is a Common card that is unlocked in the Training Camp. It spawns one single-target, ground-targeting, melee, and ground troop with high hitpoints and moderate damage. The Knight takes the appearance of a man who has brown eyes, a blond-colored mustache and eyebrows, and wears chain mail and a chestplate, a team-colored sash and a belt, and brown shoes with metal plates on them, while wielding a saber as his weapon. A Knight card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A moderate-damage single-target melee troop. The classic mini-tank. Despite only targeting ground, this jack-of-most-trades card can act as a great mini-tank and a fair defense against small troops with his moderate-high hitpoints and decent damage output. All for only 3 Elixir; good stats for his cost if you ask anyone!

For 2 Cycles, if his **Evolution** is active, his face receives a glorious and shining mustache! But not only that, he learns how to use his **Elbow Reflection** shield! While he's not attacking, he receives 60% less damage from EVERY damage source, so make sure to tackle him when he is still distracted!

If his **Hero Form** is active, his mustache becomes so stunning that it attracts everyone, because for 2 Elixir, you can use his **Triumphant Taunt** ability, which makes everyone (except building targeting troops) in a 6.5 tiles radius focus on him, even when the affected troops were attacking another unit, while gaining a tough shield for extra protection against paparazzi. Although the taunt lasts for less than a second, taunted troops and buildings will continue to reach or attack him, until he’s defeated!

## Strong against

- Low-hitpoint or low-damage ground support troops — Musketeer, Wizard, Witch, Electro Wizard, Ice Wizard, Magic Archer, Executioner, Cannon Cart, Mother Witch, Archer Queen — his bulk and damage beat them for 3 Elixir.
- Mini P.E.K.K.A., Dark Prince, Prince, Giant Skeleton, Lumberjack, Goblin Machine, Golden Knight, Skeleton King — winnable one-on-one with Crown Tower support.
- Lone Mega Knight — let him cross and travel a few tiles, then place the Knight right next to him so he cannot jump; the Knight plus Crown Tower kill him with no tower damage.
- Lone Sparky past the bridge — he survives one blast at equal level and, with the Crown Tower, kills her before she fires again, for a positive Elixir trade.
- Goblins and Goblin Barrel Goblins — his damage per hit one-shots Goblins; place him on the arena-edge side of the Crown Tower to take out the bottommost Goblin the tower targets last.
- Princess played at the bridge — a good answer, especially when saving spells for something deadlier like Goblin Barrel.
- P.E.K.K.A., Mega Knight, Bowler, Boss Bandit, Mighty Miner, Monk, or Musketeer + Valkyrie — beatable when he is backed by Archers, Spear Goblins, or Dart Goblin (the support survives for a counterpush even if the Knight dies).
- Ronin — his high health and low damage waste Ronin's deflections while his moderate attack speed chips Ronin down.
- Evolved Elite Barbarians — he tanks their spears so support troops can attack unscathed.
- Hero Wizard and Hero Bowler abilities — countered for a -1 Elixir trade with no tower damage, or a +4 Elixir trade if they are allowed to cross the bridge (best if the Knight is in the same lane).
- Hero Valkyrie and Hero Berserker abilities — stopped for a neutral or -1 Elixir trade, and they are left weak enough for the Crown Tower to finish, making it +2 or +4.
- Damaging spells generally — he survives one hit from every damaging spell at equal level, including a Lightning strike or a Vines vine.

## Weak against

- Air troops of every kind — he cannot target air and moves slowly; Minions and Bats defend against him for very little Elixir.
- Swarms — average hit speed and single-target attacks mean large swarms like Skeleton Army overwhelm him (though he one-shots Goblins).
- Goblins or Spear Goblins placed in the center of the defender's side — kill him for a +1 Elixir trade (works for any tower troop).
- Splash troops — Bomber, Wizard, Princess, Firecracker, Goblin Demolisher, Bowler, Magic Archer, Executioner hit him and everything behind him at once; Evolved Wizard, Bowler and Evolved Executioner add knockback for extra survivability.
- Kiting units — Ice Golem, Valkyrie or Skeletons pull a lone Knight toward the center and negate most or all of his tower damage.
- High-damage killers left to clean him up after he is distracted — Mini P.E.K.K.A., Prince, Lumberjack, Mighty Miner.
- Rocket and Lightning against a Knight + spawner push — they kill the support troop and severely cripple him.
- Barbarian Barrel and The Log still counter a Goblin Barrel even while the Knight tanks for it, so avoid that combo when they are in the opponent's hand.
- The opposing Crown Tower — an unsupported Knight sent alone dies fairly easily on the opponent's side, and against a defended X-Bow he takes extra tower damage if pushed too far forward.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: the classic 3-Elixir mini-tank — best stat-per-Elixir bulk with a decent hit speed, used to tank for weaker troops on defense and offense.
- He can shield Musketeer, Bomber, Mega Minion, Wizard, Flying Machine, Witch, Dart Goblin, Hunter, Princess, Electro Wizard, Firecracker, Electro Dragon, Ice Wizard, Phoenix, Bandit, Magic Archer, Night Witch, Cannon Cart, Fisherman, Mother Witch, Archer Queen, or Little Prince.
- Electro Wizard pairing: his stun resets attackers and makes them retarget onto the Knight — but the combo is weak to big swarms like Skeleton Army, so add spells or splash.
- Goblin Barrel decks: he tanks Crown Tower hits so the spawned Goblins are much harder to kill when Zap or Giant Snowball is the opponent's only small spell, and he is the deck's main defensive tank. The same idea works with Graveyard for a pricier 8-Elixir combo.
- Great Goblin Drill tank for 3 Elixir — the opponent must answer both, and it is even stronger with the Evolved Goblin Drill, which cannot tank for its own Goblins because it submerges.
- With fast building-targeting units (Battle Ram, Hog Rider, Ram Rider, Wall Breakers) he gets pushed forward, reaching defenders sooner and tanking for them; the counter is to kite him away with an Ice Golem and answer the win condition with a Cannon or Tesla.
- Against splash troops, deploy the Knight well away from them so support can be dropped on top safely; place him on top of the splasher instead when the support is ranged. Linear AoE or spawner troops need extra help.
- Against low-count swarms, place them farther from him so fewer die during deployment (his hit one-shots Goblins).
- Use him to bait swarm and tank counters so a bigger tank like Giant or Golem has an easier push.
- Supporting him with Goblins, Spear Goblins, Skeletons, or Guards gives a cheap, high-damage package; the low total cost lets the player reinforce whichever half the opponent answers.
- Sent alone he takes about half a Crown Tower's health before dying — usually not recommended, but cheap enough to work as a deck-scouting play.
- Siege interactions: he is a cheap tank for an enemy X-Bow if the opponent cannot block in time (otherwise stay farther back), and a similar play works on Mortar — better against the Evolved Mortar since he also tanks for its spawned Goblins.
- On the player's own side he tanks for Mortar or X-Bow, shielding the building while damaging its attackers; the same surprise play protects a Princess placed at the bridge (not from spells or splash, though).
- Cheap tank for spawners — Witch, Furnace, Night Witch, Mother Witch — letting them put out multiple waves; the drawback is he does not walk straight at the Crown Tower, and Rocket/Lightning punish the push.
- Substitutes: Mini P.E.K.K.A. or Prince (faster, more burst, weaker to swarms, costlier), Valkyrie / Dark Prince / Royal Ghost / Skeleton King (splash mini-tanks), Ice Golem (1 Elixir cheaper, passive, better for kiting lone troops), Knight + Archers to replicate Rascals, Battle Healer (longevity but low damage, and useless healing in X-Bow/Mortar decks), or the Champions Golden Knight, Skeleton King, Mighty Miner, and Monk.
