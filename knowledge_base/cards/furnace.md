# Furnace

> *In-game description:* "Furnace spawns one Fire Spirit at a time while dealing damage with its special cauldron brew. It also makes great brick-oven pancakes."

**Related:** [Evolution: furnace-evolution](../evolutions/furnace-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Furnace is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Hog Mountain (Arena 10). It spawns a single-target, air-targeting, long-ranged, ground troop with moderate hitpoints and moderate damage, but slow hit speed. Every 7 seconds, the Furnace will passively summon a Fire Spirit in front. It appears to be a cast-iron steam locomotive armor with limbs wearing team-colored gloves and pants, chainmail, and a metal witch-like hat on top and a grate in the center. It also holds a wooden staff with a cauldron placed on top filled with bubbling lava in it, along with a large metal knob on the bottom with black spikes on it. A Furnace card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
This 4 Elixir metallic oven has magically grown feet and arms, making him being able to slowly move and attack at long range. But the oven is not for some Show-And-Tell presentation, as he will spawn Fire Spirits out of it every 5 seconds, efficiently countering swarms and chipping down other troops. However, beware of spells, splash damage, and tanks, as they will put out the ongoing fire!

For 2 Cycles, if his **Evolution** is active, he will turn up the heat with his **Hot Spawn** ability! When attacking, this hot-headed kitchen furniture spawns a Fire Spirit from either side (alternating between them) in an even shorter period, so make sure to defeat him quickly, or he'll cause third-degree burn and cook you!

## Abilities and special mechanics

### Modifiers

#### Common: Never Put Out Your Flame
> "+400% Fire Spirit range"
The spawned Fire Spirit's range is increased by 400%, for a total of 12.5 tiles.
#### Rare: So Many Spirits!
> "Spawn every Spirit"
Instead of Fire Spirits, the Furnace spawns Electro Spirits, Ice Spirits, or Heal Spirits. Whichever spirit it will spawn is completely random. Its attacks also deal area damage, identically to a Fire Spirit.
#### Epic: Semifunctional Furnacee
> "Furnace doesn't move"
Furnace's movement speed is decreased to 0, preventing it from moving.
