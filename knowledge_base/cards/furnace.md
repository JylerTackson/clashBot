# Furnace

> *In-game description:* "Furnace spawns one Fire Spirit at a time while dealing damage with its special cauldron brew. It also makes great brick-oven pancakes."

**Related:** [Evolution: furnace-evolution](../evolutions/furnace-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Furnace is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Hog Mountain (Arena 10). It spawns a single-target, air-targeting, long-ranged, ground troop with moderate hitpoints and moderate damage, but slow hit speed. Every 7 seconds, the Furnace will passively summon a Fire Spirit in front. It appears to be a cast-iron steam locomotive armor with limbs wearing team-colored gloves and pants, chainmail, and a metal witch-like hat on top and a grate in the center. It also holds a wooden staff with a cauldron placed on top filled with bubbling lava in it, along with a large metal knob on the bottom with black spikes on it. A Furnace card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
This 4 Elixir metallic oven has magically grown feet and arms, making him being able to slowly move and attack at long range. But the oven is not for some Show-And-Tell presentation, as he will spawn Fire Spirits out of it every 5 seconds, efficiently countering swarms and chipping down other troops. However, beware of spells, splash damage, and tanks, as they will put out the ongoing fire!

For 2 Cycles, if his **Evolution** is active, he will turn up the heat with his **Hot Spawn** ability! When attacking, this hot-headed kitchen furniture spawns a Fire Spirit from either side (alternating between them) in an even shorter period, so make sure to defeat him quickly, or he'll cause third-degree burn and cook you!

## Abilities and special mechanics

### Modifiers

#### Common: Never Put Out Your Flame
> "+400% Fire Spirit range"
The spawned Fire Spirit's range is increased by 400%, for a total of 12.5 tiles.
#### Rare: So Many Spirits!
> "Spawn every Spirit"
Instead of Fire Spirits, the Furnace spawns Electro Spirits, Ice Spirits, or Heal Spirits. Whichever spirit it will spawn is completely random. Its attacks also deal area damage, identically to a Fire Spirit.
#### Epic: Semifunctional Furnacee
> "Furnace doesn't move"
Furnace's movement speed is decreased to 0, preventing it from moving.

## Strong against

- Cheap ground and air swarms — Minions, Bats, Skeletons, Spear Goblins, Goblins, Skeleton Army and Guards are wiped by the splash of the spawned Fire Spirits.
- Enemy spawner cards — the Fire Spirits' splash kills the spawned units and takes out spawners easily, preventing them from building momentum.
- Barbarians and other bulkier swarms — repeated Fire Spirits slowly weaken them enough that they provide no counterpush value; at a safe distance with tower support it can counter them outright without taking damage.
- Center-placed Goblin Barrel — a Furnace placed directly behind the Crown Tower puts the first Fire Spirit close enough to hit all three Goblins (a negative Elixir trade, so follow up with a counterpush).
- Air troops — the Furnace itself targets air and ground at 6 tile range, and the spawned Fire Spirits also hit air.
- Cheap cards dropped to stop a tank — behind a Giant, Golem, Lava Hound, Royal Giant or Hog Rider it eliminates nearly any cheap counter the opponent plays.
- Crown Towers indirectly — Fire Spirits can reach the Tower Princess for valuable chip damage if left unchecked.

## Weak against

- Ranged splash troops that one-shot Fire Spirits — Bowler, Executioner, Bomber, Firecracker, Hunter and Wizard kill the spirits without taking any damage, and the constant spawning keeps them engaged so the opponent accumulates Elixir for a counterpush.
- Tesla — longer duration and enough damage to OHKO the Fire Spirits make it a neutral Elixir trade counter.
- Mother Witch — her range and attack speed defeat Fire Spirits before they jump on her, and the Cursed Hogs she creates distract further spirits.
- Evolved Knight — his shield boost reduces Fire Spirit damage, letting him reach the Furnace and take it out.
- Battle Healer — takes full spirit damage but her passive healing quickly restores it, so she survives the waves.
- Goblin Cage — passively tanks the Fire Spirits (it cannot attack the Furnace) while the Goblin Brawler kills it.
- Lightning and Rocket — kill the Furnace, though at a -2 Elixir trade unless they also hit a Crown Tower or other units; Lightning can additionally remove a spawned Fire Spirit if timed right.
- Void — destroys the Furnace in just 2 strikes if aimed to hit only the Furnace, though Skeletons, Guards, Minions or Goblins can delay or even prevent that.
- Electro Spirit — for 1 Elixir it denies a few waves if placed so it jumps onto a Fire Spirit and chains onto the Furnace.
- Cannoneer and Dagger Duchess tower troops — they defeat spawned Fire Spirits before they connect, so the chip damage can be ignored unless a tank supports them.
- Tombstone — its Skeletons distract the Fire Spirits, which explode on impact, soaking almost all the waves.
- Split Minions — a single Minion absorbs 2 Fire Spirits, saving other troops while attacking the other lane.
- Enemy Skeleton King — the constant, unpreventable Fire Spirit deaths charge his ability and spawn more Skeletons.
- Spells, splash damage and tanks in general, as the card overview warns.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: a 4 Elixir spawning support troop that doubles as defense, thanks to the area damage of its Fire Spirits.
- Great behind a tank — Hog Rider, Giant, Golem, Lava Hound or Royal Giant plus Furnace; if the spirits go unchecked behind the tank they can decimate the Tower with their splash.
- Can be pre-placed for a tank push started from the King Tower, discouraging the opponent from attacking.
- Strong counterattack support: played in advance, the spirits handle Minions, Bats, Skeletons, Spear Goblins and Goblins that an Elixir-starved opponent drops.
- Pairs with fast melee troops — Mini P.E.K.K.A., Prince, Elite Barbarians or Lumberjack — since the spirits clear Goblins and other frail swarms (Skeleton Army excepted) so they reach the Tower quickly; this pairing is vulnerable to P.E.K.K.A., Mega Knight, Mighty Miner or Giant Skeleton.
- Works well with your own Skeleton King: the constant spirit deaths charge his ability, and the Furnace covers his weakness to air troops.
- Affected by Rage — faster attack speed plus faster waves of Fire Spirits.
- Mirroring it spawns two Fire Spirits at a time for fast, heavy area support for tanks, but two Furnaces placed close together hand the opponent a large positive spell trade.
  - Instead stagger them: one at the back and one near the centre of the map slightly off to the side, so both still deal considerable chip damage.
  - Alternatively mirror into the other lane to pressure both lanes and deter attacks with the spirits' defensive presence.
- Bomb Tower and Cannon are poorer answers to it than Tesla — their lower damage lets Fire Spirits accumulate damage and destroy the building.
