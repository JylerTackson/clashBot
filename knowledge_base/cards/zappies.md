# Zappies

> *In-game description:* "Spawns a pack of miniature Zap machines. Who controls them...? Only the Master Builder knows."

## Overview

The Zappies is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Hog Mountain (Arena 10). It spawns three single-target, air-targeting, medium-ranged, ground troops with moderate hitpoints and low damage. Their deployment is in a triangle formation. Each of their attacks stun the target for 0.5 seconds. They appear as small machines having an upwards coil on a wooden chassis and wheels, with a spiked steel ram at the front and team-colored flags at the rear. A Zappies card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
If it moves, then it won’t for much longer. This 4 Elixir trio of menaces will stun a target until it is destroyed, making them exceptionally effective on defense or as support. However, be sure to protect them against swarms and splash damage due to their slow hit speed and moderately-low hitpoints. They also cannot perma-stun their target without support.

## Strong against

- Lone single-target attackers whose attack the 0.5 sec stun resets or delays — Fisherman, Ram Rider, Mighty Miner, Sparky, Prince, Dark Prince, Battle Ram, Inferno Tower and Inferno Dragon.
- Low hit speed troops — Hunter, Baby Dragon, Skeleton Dragons and Bowler are crippled by constant stuns and become almost unable to attack (do not rely on Zappies alone, since these hit multiple troops).
- Slow single-target units generally — all three Zappies focus the same target, and placed beside the Tower they line up vertically and stagger their stuns, leaving the enemy troop nearly immobile.
- Small swarms — Skeletons, Archers, Minions, Spear Goblins, Bats, Guards and Goblins.
- Larger swarms with Crown Tower help — Skeleton Army, Minion Horde and Goblin Gang.
- Balloon — the stalling makes it much harder for the Balloon to reach its target, and Zappies survive Arrows, Zap and Giant Snowball, so the opponent needs Fireball.
- Buildings when supporting a tank, especially the Inferno Tower, and their stuns leave the Crown Tower barely able to shoot.
- Sparky at close range — if you wait for her to cross the bridge and surround her, one or two Zappies survive and keep her permanently stunned until death (the same works on area damage troops without a 360 degree effect).

## Weak against

- Fireball and Poison — their low hitpoints mean either spell destroys the trio for a neutral Elixir trade.
- Ranged splash support — Executioner, Wizard, Firecracker, Bomber, Witch or spell combos stump them easily, so bait those out first.
- Barbarian Barrel and Royal Delivery — both counter Zappies crossing the bridge fairly well even when a tank supports them.
- Mega Knight — his Spawn Damage leaves them low enough that one regular attack finishes them, and he gains extra value hitting the troops usually paired with them.
- Sparky approached from a distance — her range lets her one-shot the whole trio before they can reset her attack.
- Swarms used defensively — Skeletons, Minions, Goblins, Skeleton Army and similar can surround and overwhelm them.
- Splash damage in general, given their slow 2.2 sec hit speed and moderately low hitpoints.
- Any target with enough support that they cannot perma-stun it alone.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: a 4 Elixir defensive card and support troop; three units deploy in a triangle formation, each attack stunning the target for 0.5 seconds.
- Most effective on defense against single-target units, especially slow ones, since all three focus the same target.
- Place them beside the Tower so they line up vertically and stagger their stun timing, leaving the enemy troop nearly immobile.
- They have a medium first hit (0.8 sec), so dropping them on top of a unit to distract is generally a bad idea — place them behind the Crown Tower to give them time to charge their shots and stun approaching units.
- Split two Zappies onto the lane with the healthier Crown Tower to minimise spell value against them.
- Spell bait: pair them with other spell-vulnerable cards such as Royal Hogs to bait the opponent's Fireball/Poison, or use the Zappies themselves as the bait in matchups where they are otherwise weak.
- Strong Graveyard synergy — they bait Poison, the Graveyard's hardest counter; even a single surviving Zappy stuns the Tower once, making it retarget onto the Skeletons, which keeps the Zappy alive, slows the Tower's shots and lets more Skeletons spawn for extra damage.
- Decent behind a tank: they cripple defending buildings, most notably the Inferno Tower.
- Do not push them across the bridge while Barbarian Barrel or Royal Delivery are in the opponent's cycle unless you have support to clear the follow-up defenders.
- Against Sparky, do not engage at range — wait until she crosses the bridge, then surround her so survivors keep her permanently stunned.
- Emergency spell block: if a Crown Tower is low enough that Lightning would finish it, dropping Zappies in the expected area can soak the bolt, since they likely have more hitpoints than the Tower — useful in Sudden Death.
