# Giant Snowball

> *In-game description:* "It’s HUGE! Once it began rolling down Frozen Peak, there was no stopping it. Enemies hit are knocked back and slowed down. Reduced damage to Crown Towers."

**Related:** [Evolution: giant-snowball-evolution](../evolutions/giant-snowball-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Giant Snowball is a Common card that is unlocked from Frozen Peak (Arena 8). It is an area-damage, air-targeting spell with a medium radius and low damage. When used, it slows enemies in its radius for 3 seconds and inflict knockback. It is an oversized snowball. A Giant Snowball card costs 2 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Is a push too hot to handle? A giant, 2 Elixir snowball will make them slow down for 3 seconds and chill out. As a side note, squishier troops tend to go splat while all but the heaviest of troops get knocked back. The Giant Snowball excels as a cheap, versatile disrupter.

For 2 Cycles, if its **Evolution** is active, it learns a new power: **Snow Roll**. It exchanges the knock back for a really cool snowball effect, forcing ALL troops in a 4 tile range to move with it (while being untargetable), making it great for either temporarily clear the path for your push, or push an opposing push back to buy extra time.
