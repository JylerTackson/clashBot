# Night Witch

> *In-game description:* "Summons Bats to do her bidding! If you get too close, she's not afraid of pitching in with her mean-looking battle staff."

## Overview

The Night Witch is a Legendary card that is unlocked from the Serenity Peak (Arena 14). It spawns a single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troop with moderate hitpoints and high damage. Every 5 seconds, the Night Witch will passively summon a group of two Bats surrounding her. However, her first wave of Bats will spawn 1 second after she is deployed. When defeated, she will also spawn a single Bat. She appears similar to the Witch, but has glowing blue eyes, wears a black cloak, top and gloves with silver armlets, a silver belt with an axe-shaped buckle and a glowing blue crystal in the center, and wields a long, darker axe-shaped staff with a glowing blue crystal on the top, which appears similar to her belt. A Night Witch card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
The mean sister of the Witch, she has moderate hitpoints, moderately-high melee single-target damage and the ability to spawn supporting flying Bats for only 4 Elixir, including one after death! She can quickly swarm units and towers with her Bats, but is defenseless against splash damage that can hit air troops.

## Strong against

- Cards that cannot target air — Sparky, Lumberjack, Prince, P.E.K.K.A, Mega Knight, Barbarians and similar are all beaten by her Bats.
- High-hitpoint units on offense or defense, thanks to her high damage per hit plus continuous Bat spawns.
- Frail troops with Archers- or Firecracker-level hitpoints, which she one-shots — drop her on top of a lone squishy troop to preserve her health.
- Mega Knight — with Tower Princess support and a placement that avoids his jump, her Bats plus her death Bat defeat him before he lands on the Princess, leaving Bats for a counterattack.
- Lava Hound — placed in the back, she spawns enough Bats to be distracted by the Hound and kill it (bring a cheap swarm too, since Lava Pups one-shot Bats).
- Graveyard — her Bats quickly clear the Skeletons.
- Goblin Barrel — place her behind the bottom Goblin so she attacks it while her first Bat wave takes the other two.
- Cannoneer — her Bats distract him for a long time (though not indefinitely, unlike the Witch).
- Distracted Valkyrie — her staff slightly outranges some troops (like Prince, Guards, Royal Recruits), letting her poke without taking damage.

## Weak against

- Ranged air-hitting splash: Wizard and Witch kill her and her Bats quickly without taking much damage.
- Ice Wizard — cheap at 1 Elixir less than her, one-shots Bats and his slow delays her next spawn wave.
- Area-denial units Executioner, Magic Archer and Firecracker kill Bats as they spawn, and their killing blow also instantly kills the death Bat.
- Electro Dragon — its chain attack hits her and the Bats and stuns her, delaying spawns; Electro Spirit does the same once for 1 Elixir.
- Evolved Valkyrie — regular hits plus tornado damage 3-shot her while the tornado one-shots Bats (the death Bat survives, so add an Electro Wizard, Dart Goblin, Musketeer or Hunter).
- Air-targeting troops generally, since she cannot attack air herself: Minions, Bats, Electro Dragon, Mega Minion, Flying Machine, Phoenix, Skeleton Dragons, Baby Dragon.
- Ranged air-targeting cards used to pull her to the centre — Archers, Musketeer, Hunter, Mother Witch, Archer Queen, Electro Wizard, Dart Goblin, Zappies, Little Prince, Spear Goblins — but not while she has a tank, since they will target the tank instead.
- Poison is the most effective spell: it cripples her and wipes the Bats, though it needs a follow-up like Zap or Giant Snowball to finish her. Fireball plus Zap or Rage is not enough; Fireball plus Barbarian Barrel or The Log works but misses the Bats.
- Ice Golem kiting into the other lane — her melee Bats follow it while the tower shoots, and its death damage wipes Bats and slows her.
- Mother Witch — one-shots each Bat into Cursed Hogs that distract her while the Mother Witch curses her down.
- Balloon — she cannot stop it connecting even with tower help; her first Bat wave is too slow.

## Notes / synergies

- Spawns two Bats every 5 seconds, with the first wave only 1 second after deploy, plus one Bat on death; 4 Elixir.
- Placed directly behind the King's Tower, one Bat flies into the other lane, giving light coverage there.
- Compared with the Witch: no splash, fewer spawns at a time, and no air targeting, but higher melee damage, more health and faster Bats make her stronger on the counterpush; she also cannot distract ground-targeting troops with her Bats.
- Pairs with most large building-targeting tanks — the tank soaks damage while the Bats attack or distract threats like Inferno Tower.
- Giant: cheap high-hitpoint tank, works either as a back-of-court setup or a bridge rush with her.
- Golem: more hitpoints and damage, and Golemites keep tanking after he dies. Placing the Golem right in front of her as she reaches the Crown Tower blocks her, letting you bank Elixir for support.
- Lava Hound: her Bats help distract counters while she advances, and after she dies she tanks for the Lava Pups while adding Bats.
- Goblin Giant: slightly faster, so Bats stay close to him and she stays back out of splash range.
- Elixir Golem: the cheapest tank option, and his smaller forms keep tanking for her.
- Situational support for fast attackers like Battle Ram, Lumberjack, Berserker and Ram Rider — the Bats catch up and punish a Fisherman used to activate the King Tower while she stays back to deny spell value.
- Graveyard synergy: she tanks while Skeletons and Bats spread damage across the tower.
- Miner synergy: he tanks for her and the Bats for solid tower damage, or distracts ranged area attackers in larger pushes. Weak to swarms and spells, since she targets the tower and ignores everything around her.
- Rage affects her spawned Bats and makes her spawn Bats faster.
- Clone doubles her and her Bats, often overwhelming opponents without a spell or splash unit.
- Skeleton King synergy: her Bats feed his ability charge while covering him against air.
- Strong spell bait: she baits Zap and Giant Snowball for the Bats or Lightning and Poison for herself, opening the way for Skeleton Barrel or Flying Machine.
- Very strong in Touchdown and Heist, where there are no Crown Towers to shoot the Bats and each one must be answered individually.
