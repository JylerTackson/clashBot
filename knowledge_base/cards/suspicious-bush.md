# Suspicious Bush

> *In-game description:* "This invisible bush skulks towards the nearest building. Sus. If Suspicious Bush reaches its target or gets destroyed by a spell, it surprises the enemy with Bush Goblins!"

## Overview

The Suspicious Bush is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Rascal's Hideout (Arena 13). It spawns a medium speed, building-targeting, ground troop with very low hitpoints. Upon deployment, it spawns invisible as it moves towards the nearest building. Once the Suspicious Bush either reaches a building or is defeated, it will spawn two Bush Goblins, which are single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troops with low hitpoints and moderate damage. It is a tiny bush with team-colored flowers on it and a periscope mounted on top that has a white flower on it, while the Bush Goblins appear as Goblins that wear leaves on their heads and waists held by a rope, have team-colored body markings on their skin, and wield stone hammers. A Suspicious Bush card costs 2 Elixir to deploy.
