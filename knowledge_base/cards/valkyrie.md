# Valkyrie

> *In-game description:* "Tough melee fighter, deals area damage around her. Swarm or horde, no problem! She can take them all out with a few spins."

**Related:** [Evolution: valkyrie-evolution](../evolutions/valkyrie-evolution.md) · [Hero variant: valkyrie-hero](../heroes/valkyrie-hero.md)

## Overview

The Valkyrie is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Bone Pit (Arena 2). It spawns an area-damage, ground-targeting, melee, ground troop with high hitpoints and high damage. The Valkyrie attacks by spinning with her axe, damaging all the ground troops surrounding her in a 360° area of effect. She resembles a woman who bears orange hair and eyebrows, have blue eyes, wields a double-sided axe, wears a team-colored suit, wristbands and boots, and attacks with a powerful whirl of her axe. A Valkyrie card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A 4 Elixir powerhouse with high health. This tough mini-tank swings her axe 360º to take out swarms and support troops alike. Despite this, she can't reach air troops and her slow movement speed leaves her susceptible to ranged attacks.

For 2 Cycles, if her **Evolution** is active, she forges her weapon into one that has the **Whirlwind Axe** ability: every time she attacks, her axe spins summon a whirlwind, pulling both ground and air units alike, dealing an additional damage tick. You can't run, don't even hide!

Similarly, if her **Hero** is active, stay away and seek shelter, because for 3 Elixir, you can use her **Wild Whirlwind** ability, which speeds her up and causes her to repeatedly spin her axe once she finds a target, dealing damage over time and traveling between all nearby units! She can't be frozen and takes reduced damage during spinning, but she can still be defeated during her ability.

## Strong against

- Ranged low- and medium-hitpoint troops: Musketeer, Witch, Archers, Mother Witch, Ice Wizard, Little Prince, Princess, Goblinstein, Electro Wizard, Firecracker, Bomber, Zappies, Archer Queen, Magic Archer, Dart Goblin, Spear Goblins and Wizard — a few swings kill them if she is dropped right on top.
- Ground swarms — her 360° splash stops her being surrounded by most of them.
- Melee units in a 1-on-1: Monk, Dark Prince, Prince, Mini P.E.K.K.A, Boss Bandit and even Mega Knight, especially if placed so their charge does not activate.
- Royal Recruits and Guards when she is dropped directly on top of them (she struggles if they approach her instead).
- Graveyard — her 360° attack clears Skeletons fast and she survives every offensive spell in the game.
- Goblin Barrel — placed one tile up from the bottom-outer corner of the Crown Tower she catches two Goblins, or all three if placed slightly early thanks to her fast first attack.
- Skeleton Barrel — let it pop on the Tower, then drop her among the Skeletons to wipe them in one spin.
- Lone Sparky across the bridge — at equal Level she survives one blast and kills her with Tower help for a positive Elixir trade.
- X-Bow pushes — she tanks and clears the frail support cards around it.
- Almost all non-building-targeting troops, with Crown Tower assistance, thanks to her hitpoints-per-Elixir.

## Weak against

- All air troops — she cannot hit them at all; Bats or Minions fully counter her if placed early and she has no support.
- Cheap ranged chip placed at a distance — Spear Goblins, Guards, Dart Goblin, Archers and Skeletons stall her slow movement while the Crown Towers work.
- Inferno Tower (placed far away so she must walk) and Inferno Dragon (placed in front of her, untargetable) when she is unsupported.
- Mini-tanks that pull her to the center — Knight, Lumberjack, Royal Ghost, Battle Healer, Prince, Skeleton King, Golden Knight, Monk or Mini P.E.K.K.A negate most of her Tower damage.
- Building-targeting kiters — Ice Golem, Elixir Golem, Goblin Giant or Battle Ram placed on the fourth tile from the bridge, 1 tile into the other lane, drag her across (Tornado or Fisherman can undo this).
- Cannoneer tower troop — the best tower troop against her, taking only 4 hits before she dies.
- Long-range chip units, especially against a Valkyrie placed behind the King Tower: Magic Archer, Firecracker and Evolved Bomber hit her and the Crown Tower at once.
- Fisherman — can pull her and force her to activate your King Tower.
- A Knight-style tank blocking her while an X-Bow chips her down.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: a 4 Elixir mini-tank and defensive splash unit — high hitpoints, moderate 360° area damage, slow movement, no air targeting.
- Play her reactively: wait for support troops to cross the bridge, then drop her on top of the whole push to save her hitpoints for a counterattack.
- Against melee ground swarms, place her a short distance away rather than on top so she is not hit while deploying — but drop her inside larger pushes to clear them faster.
- Against ranged troops always place her directly on top to nullify their range advantage; on offense a Tornado near her drags ranged units in for her to kill.
- Units with long melee range can hit her without taking splash while she is busy with another target.
- Do not underestimate a lone Valkyrie — even alone she deals heavy Crown Tower damage for 4 Elixir.
- She can be used to bait the opponent's tank counter so a Giant or Golem has an easier time afterwards.
- Pair her with air-targeting support to cover her one hard weakness: Wizard, Musketeer, Electro Wizard, Witch, Executioner, Princess, Goblinstein, Flying Machine, Dart Goblin, Hunter, Little Prince, Firecracker, Archers, Skeleton Dragons, Inferno Dragon, Spear Goblins, Electro Dragon, Zappies, Archer Queen, Magic Archer, Mega Minion, Ice Wizard or Baby Dragon.
- Valkyrie + Mini P.E.K.K.A. covers each other's weaknesses: put her in front and the faster Mini P.E.K.K.A. behind so he pushes her toward the Tower — she clears swarms, he handles tanks. Very weak to air.
- The same push with a Prince is stronger (more hitpoints, charge speed, knockback immunity to break a Bowler) but costs more and has lower DPS without the charge.
- Valkyrie + Elite Barbarians hits harder still, but costs more and their larger sight range separates them from her.
- Valkyrie + Goblins is the cheap version — the Goblins push her and add damage, but die to area damage.
- With fast building-targeting cards (Hog Rider, Ram Rider, Battle Ram, Wall Breakers) she protects them from swarms while they push her forward; Ram Rider also covers air.
- Witch pairs well: both splash, the Witch covers air, and her Skeletons distract P.E.K.K.A, Giant Skeleton, Prince, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Boss Bandit and Mighty Miner.
- Freeze + Valkyrie is a strong defensive combo — freeze the ranged support behind a tank and let her splash wipe them out.
- Valkyrie in front of a Balloon soaks Crown Tower fire and clears the small units sent at it, but the combo dies to air troops and to a building + tank answer.
- Trifecta (Hog Rider + Musketeer + Valkyrie): she tanks for the Hog on offense and for the Musketeer on defense; place the Hog Rider behind her so she stays in front. It loses to Lightning, to Minion Horde (unless Zapped), hard to a P.E.K.K.A for +5 Elixir, to Rocket for up to +6 (Tornado then Rocket for +3), and to a Bowler's knockback.
- When she locks onto your Crown Tower, do not place ranged defenders too close to the Tower or her splash chips both at once.
- Against a Fisherman placed behind the King Tower, put her at the bridge — his hook will pull her in and let her damage the Crown Tower unexpectedly.
