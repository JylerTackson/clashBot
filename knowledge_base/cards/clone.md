# Clone

> *In-game description:* "Duplicates all friendly troops in the target area. Cloned troops are fragile, but pack the same punch as the original! Doesn't affect buildings."

## Overview

The Clone is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Miner's Mine (Arena 15). It is an air-targeting spell with a medium radius that spawns fragile duplicates of all troops within its area of effect behind the originals. It is a translucent, teal-colored liquid encased in a small oblong vial, and appears to be duplicating itself. It is also the same as that spawned by the Santa Hog Rider. A Clone card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
The Clone is an incredibly volatile spell that clones every troop within its radius. For only 3 Elixir, it doubles the damage and can overwhelm opponents as Clones still retain the abilities of their normal counterparts. Despite this, each cloned troop only has 1 hitpoint, so beware of area damage! Clone can affect Champions, but only the original will use its ability. Also, it doesn't affect buildings (except the Cannon Cart before its building state) nor Skeletons summoned by the almighty Skeleton King.

## Strong against

- Crown Towers already locked onto a tank or other high-hitpoint unit — cloning the attackers doubles the damage before the Tower can retarget.
- Crown Towers against cloned Wall Breakers — the clones spawn right next to the Tower, which usually cannot react in time.
- Opponents whose splash and cheap spell answers are out of rotation — cloned Skeleton Army or other numerous low-hitpoint troops can then deal heavy damage.
- Defending troops in front of a death-damage unit — a cloned Giant Skeleton sets off its bomb so the original can push through much more easily.
- Defensive building placements — the forward displacement when a troop is cloned can shove a Balloon or Battle Ram past a building's pull toward the Crown Tower.
- Single-lane defences, via split push — cloning a set of Three Musketeers leaves 2 on one side and 4 on the other.
- Spells aimed at your push — cloning in anticipation of a spell displaces the original forward and can make a slightly off-target spell miss it.

## Weak against

- Any splash or damage-over-time spell — Zap, The Log, Arrows, Giant Snowball, Earthquake, Poison and Tornado all wipe the 1 hitpoint clones for an Elixir advantage.
- Poison, Arrows, Vines and Goblin Curse specifically — they tick multiple times and clear even cloned Lava Hound pups or shielded Guards.
- Ice Golem — sacrificing it into the clones triggers its death damage and wipes them all out.
- Royal Delivery — listed alongside Zap, Arrows and Poison as an easy answer to a cloned Lava Hound.
- Mass-spawn cards that spread out — units outside the relatively small radius simply do not get cloned.
- Threshold/special-mode troops — a clone cannot reach its low-health special mode, so cloning them is a wasted play.
- Elixir Golem — never clone it; the clones hand the opponent up to 8 extra Elixir.
- Electro Giant — pointless to clone, since a 1 hitpoint copy only counterattacks once.
- Melee troops about to die to a ranged troop — cloning them accomplishes nothing (cloning a ranged troop against a melee one is safer).
- Buildings, and Skeleton King Skeletons from Soul Summoning — cannot be cloned at all (the Cannon Cart before its building state is the exception).

## Notes / synergies

- Role: a 3 Elixir offensive multiplier — spawns 1 hitpoint duplicates of every troop in its radius, placed behind the originals.
- Unlike Mirror it duplicates every troop in range and can cost less, but every copy has a single hitpoint.
- Clones keep most unique effects: shields (with 1 hitpoint, still negating one hit's excess damage), periodic spawns (Witch, Night Witch) and death spawns (Skeleton Barrel, Golem, Elixir Golem, Goblin Giant, Lava Hound) which themselves come out cloned.
- Death effects carry over — a cloned Lumberjack drops a cloned Rage, a cloned Elixir Golem still gives Elixir in every form, and Mother Witch/Goblin Curse produce cloned Cursed Hogs or Goblins from cloned victims.
- Dropped items (Giant Skeleton's or Balloon's bomb) cannot themselves be cloned, though the clone still drops a cloned item.
- Charge/dash/jump/grab cards (Dark Prince, Prince, Battle Ram, Ram Rider, Evolved Royal Recruits, Bandit, Boss Bandit, Mega Knight, Fisherman, and partly Sparky) keep their abilities; cloning does not interrupt the original, and the clone does not inherit the current charge state.
- Sparky is immune to stuns while being cloned — wait for the opponent to clone her before dropping an Electro Wizard on top.
- Cloning a Golem during his 3 second deploy makes the clone start moving immediately.
- Spirit Empress clones match the original's form regardless of Elixir; cloned Ice Wizards, Electro Wizards and Mega Knights deal no spawn damage, and a cloned Battle Healer gives no spawn healing.
- Best used on low-hitpoint units that come in numbers (Skeleton Army being the classic), where the 1 hitpoint drawback barely matters.
- Cloning a Lava Hound is very effective: when the clone dies its Pups spring out and the Tower snaps back to the real Hound while the Pups chip it.
- Clone + Mirror multiplies units exponentially; mirroring first then cloning is pricier but leaves half at full health, cloning then mirroring is cheaper with fewer units and only a third at full health. Mirroring a 3 Elixir card and then cloning gives 4 copies for 10 Elixir.
- Champions and Heroes can be cloned but only the original activates its ability; Goblinstein's Lightning Link always targets the non-cloned Monster and never retargets to the clone.
- Evolved troops usually clone into their unevolved version (Evolved Archers give regular Archers), though troops they spawn — like the Evolved Mortar's Goblins — clone normally.
- Exceptions to that rule: General Gerry stays cloned (his Skeletons lose the immortality effect), Evolved Goblin Barrel Decoy Goblins function the same, cloning the second Evolved Skeleton Barrel gives a third normal Skeleton Barrel, and cloned Evolved Royal Ghost becomes a regular Royal Ghost with normally-cloned Souldiers.
- Evolved Wall Breakers cannot be cloned but their Runners can — still, cloning the Evolved Wall Breakers themselves deals more damage.
- Deck risk: Clone can show up in the starting hand and effectively leave the player with only 3 usable cards; it is safer in 2v2 and Troop Rush where it can be cast on allied or Arena-generated troops.
- Arrows are not recommended against a cloned Skeleton Barrel because of the 0.5 second gap between the Skeletons spawning and the Barrel dying — Poison and Goblin Curse still work.
