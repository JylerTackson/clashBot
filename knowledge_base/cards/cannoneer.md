# Cannoneer

> *In-game description:* "Slow but powerful. What could be better than a giant cannon on your Tower?"

## Overview

The Cannoneer is an Epic card that is unlocked from Frozen Peak (Arena 8). It is a single-target, air-targeting, long-ranged tower troop with moderate hitpoints and high damage. The Cannoneer resembles a short and stout man wearing goggles, a team-colored cloak, brown gloves and sandals, has a loose tooth, carries a sponge rammer and a backpack with a crown emblem in the center filled with cannonballs, and wields a large cannon with team-colored crown paint and a control lever.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Blast Away! The Cannoneer is a tower troop that deals a lot more damage but at a lower hit speed. This makes him great against low health, low count units and tanks, but susceptible to swarms. Also, you might want use noise reducers if your ears cannot handle the blasts.
