# Poison

> *In-game description:* "Covers the area in a deadly toxin, damaging enemy troops and buildings over time. Yet somehow leaves the grass green and healthy. Go figure! Reduced damage to Crown Towers."

## Overview

The Poison is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Jungle Arena (Arena 9). It is an area-damage, air-targeting spell with a medium radius and low damage that is dealt every second for 8 seconds. It also decreases the movement speed of enemy troops by 15%. It is a deep red liquid in a thin oblong vial. A Poison card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A 4 Elixir area-denial spell that maintains a powerful presence. It is capable of disrupting defenses, crippling pushes, and preventing supports being played by destroying any swarms and many glass cannons that pass through an area in a matter of seconds – but only if they stay inside it! Speaking of staying inside, in its ring of toxicity, troops walk slightly slower, aggravating the hurt put on them.

## Abilities and special mechanics

### Modifiers

#### Common: The Poison & the Hare
> "+150% enemy slowdown"
Increases slowdown of troops inside the Poison.
#### Rare: Bigger & Better
> "Increasing radius & damage"
Poison sequentially gets bigger and deals more damage with every tick.
#### Epic: No Escape
> "Permanent Poison"
Poison never goes away until the match ends or is dropped in a different location.

## Strong against

- Musketeer, Electro Wizard, Goblinstein and Little Prince — unlike Fireball, Poison's total damage fully defeats them if they stay in the radius for the whole duration.
- Swarm cards — the 8 second duration denies the area, killing swarms that pass through and forcing a less practical counter out of the opponent.
- Spawner buildings such as Goblin Hut and Furnace, plus the Elixir Collector — it drains the lifetimer while also killing the units they spawn.
- Witch and Night Witch — limits their damage output and leaves them at critical health along with their Skeletons/Bats.
- Clone — a hard counter: the 8 second duration wipes cloned troops and their death spawns, damages the originals, and denies the Clone for its whole duration.
- Graveyard — a consistent counter, matching its duration and covering most of its radius (a few Skeletons still get a hit in).
- Ranged support parked behind slow tanks (P.E.K.K.A., Elixir Golem, Ice Golem, Rune Giant, Giant Skeleton, Valkyrie, Mighty Miner, Battle Healer, Skeleton King, Giant, Lava Hound, Golem) — they stand still long enough to take full damage.
- X-Bow and Mortar setups — weakens the building and quickly kills the swarm defenders around it, discouraging reinforcements.
- Three Musketeers placed in the back — a quick Poison can be a 5 Elixir advantage if you can afford to activate the enemy King's Tower.
- Ranged glass cannons (Princess, Dart Goblin, Zappies, Firecracker, Bomber, Mother Witch, Archers, Spear Goblins, Magic Archer) when combined with a Miner feint.

## Weak against

- Barbarians, Zappies and Royal Recruits — high enough health to do heavy damage before the Poison strips them, though it is still a positive Elixir trade.
- Valkyrie and other higher-health defenders — survive it, so the Graveyard + Poison plan does not work against them.
- Knight and other cheap tanks — blunt the Miner + Poison combo since they simply outlast the spell.
- Fast troops that leave the radius (and anything in Rage mode) — Poison cannot land its full damage before they walk out, so it is a poor choice in Rage mode.
- Skeletons and Bats two levels above the Poison — it cannot one-shot them, so the spell needs to be kept upgraded.
- Troops that survive a lone Poison — Witch, Fisherman, Hunter, Berserker, Mega Minion, Night Witch, Bandit, Electro Dragon, Wizard and Cannon Cart or Baby Dragon all need a follow-up.
- Defences played on the fly — it is generally bad on defence because the damage is too slow to save a Tower on its own.
- Poison bait cards like Goblin Hut or Ice Wizard — they pull the Poison out before it can answer the real threat.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: a 4 Elixir area-denial spell — low damage every second for 8 seconds over a medium radius, plus a 15% movement slow.
- The 15% slow does not stack with the Ice Wizard, Ice Golem or Giant Snowball slow, and lingers briefly after a troop leaves the radius (most visible on fast cards like Lumberjack, Hog Rider, Elite Barbarians, Bandit, Royal Ghost, Berserker, Flying Machine and Mini P.E.K.K.A.).
- Interchangeable with Fireball when you want area denial over burst: bigger crippling radius, but no immediate damage and no knockback.
- Damage is not instantaneous on spawn, so swarms surviving the first tick still land a hit or two.
- Strong on offence behind a building-targeting troop or a single-target troop, since it punishes the swarm answers.
- Pairs with Graveyard: it handles the enemy counters and answers Minions or other small defenders.
- Pairs with Miner: the swarm denial lets the Miner chip uninterrupted; deploy the Poison at the exact moment the Miner resurfaces, or offset the two placements to disguise where the Miner is.
- With a Miner, feigning an attack on a passive building (aiming at the Crown Tower instead) can bait Elixir and let the Poison hit the troop, the Tower and the building at once.
- Combine with Zap, The Log or Tornado to hold troops inside the radius longer and finish off survivors; a mini tank like the Ice Golem does the same by distracting them.
- If a poisoned troop survives near your Tower, wait for it to reach the bridge before Zapping so the Zap also catches its meatshields — but let the Tower finish it if it has already crossed.
- Against X-Bow/Mortar you can instead place the Poison further back, between the building and the Crown Tower, to prioritise Tower damage.
- Best in decks where the opponent defends close to their Tower with units that stay put for a long time.
- Modifier **The Poison & the Hare** (Common): +150% enemy slowdown inside the Poison.
- Modifier **Bigger & Better** (Rare): the Poison sequentially grows in radius and damage with every tick.
- Modifier **No Escape** (Epic): the Poison is permanent until the match ends or it is dropped in a different location.
