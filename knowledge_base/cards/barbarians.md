# Barbarians

> *In-game description:* "A horde of melee attackers with mean mustaches and even meaner tempers."

**Related:** [Evolution: barbarians-evolution](../evolutions/barbarians-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Barbarians is a Common card that is unlocked from the Barbarian Bowl (Arena 3). It spawns five single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troops with moderate hitpoints and damage. Their deployment is in a star formation. They appear as bare-chested men who wield swords, have blue eyes, blonde eyebrows, close-cropped hair and horseshoe moustaches, and wear a brown kilt and sandals, a team-colored belt with a shield buckle and spiked wristbands. They are also the same as those spawned by the Barbarian Hut, Battle Ram, Barbarian Barrel, and the Barbarian Launcher. A Barbarians card costs 5 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Five single-target melee troops with thoroughly average stats. Despite only targeting ground, they are a mighty collective force and can swiftly deal with a great variety of threats for 5 Elixir, but keep them away from powerful splash cards.

For 1 Cycle, if their **Evolution** is active, their hair and moustache will drastically increase in length. And you better wish their enhanced swords never touch you, because if they do, their **Blade Rage** skill will allow them to enrage themselves and attack even faster! Troop destroying service incoming!

## Strong against

- Single-target ground troops — Prince, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Golden Knight, Mighty Miner, Lumberjack, Monk, Boss Bandit, Giant Skeleton — five bodies surround and kill them fast.
- Lone glass cannons on the player's side — Prince, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Bandit, Lumberjack, Hunter, Musketeer — dropped directly on top to surround them.
- Enemy tanks — their numbers, high combined damage and ability to soak most spells make them a strong tank answer, and support troops struggle against their combined hitpoints.
- Electro Giant — high DPS and enough hitpoints to survive his reflected damage, countering him for a +2 Elixir trade (though they usually die or end nearly dead).
- Hog Rider and Ram Rider — heavy damage plus their mass and spread force the rider to path around them, so they die without touching the Crown Tower.
- Graveyard — their unit count covers most of the spawn area and their hitpoints survive Arrows, Zap, The Log, and Giant Snowball.
- Goblin Barrel — placed early enough they surround the Crown Tower and absorb most of the damage (a 5-Elixir investment, so only when better counters are missing).
- Ground splash + melee tank pushes — wait for them to cross the river, then drop the Barbarians on the splash card to surround and destroy it, then finish the unsupported tank for a big positive trade.
- Weak spells generally — Arrows, Zap, The Log, and Giant Snowball do not kill them.

## Weak against

- Air troops of every kind — Minions, Bats, Baby Dragon, Electro Dragon, Flying Machine, Mega Minion, Phoenix, Skeleton Dragons beat them unharmed, several at a positive Elixir trade.
- Ranged splash troops at a distance — Bomber, Bowler, Wizard, Witch, Goblin Demolisher, Sparky, Firecracker, Executioner.
- Fireball — its damage and radius OHKO them (unless the Fireball is a level lower).
- Poison — the damage over time wipes them and any support (unless they out-level the Poison).
- Rocket — kills them, though it is a negative Elixir trade unless they are paired with something like Sparky, Inferno Dragon, Lumberjack, or Elite Barbarians.
- Swarms such as Skeleton Army — their slow attack speed leaves them vulnerable.
- 360-degree splash — a charging Dark Prince, a Valkyrie, or a jumping Mega Knight cannot be beaten by surrounding them.
- P.E.K.K.A. — she OHKOs Barbarians up to 2 levels higher, so the surround play fails.
- Multiple splash units in one push — the Barbarians get wiped immediately.
- Their slow move speed generally — an unsupported group in one lane is easy to counter positively and leaves the other lane open.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 5-Elixir defensive ground swarm and tank-killer that converts into a strong counterpush.
- A successful defense converts well: tank for the Barbarians, or use them to tank for a glass cannon, for a threatening push.
- They can completely destroy a Crown Tower by themselves, so they punish an opponent who is very low on Elixir or whose counters are out of rotation.
- Rage + Barbarians can severely damage or take a Crown Tower, since Rage boosts both movement and attack speed and the combo is hard to stop — but Poison still handles it.
- They work as Fireball/Poison bait, letting cards weak to those spells (Zappies, Magic Archer, Mother Witch, Skeleton Dragons, Flying Machine) operate more safely; if they survive the spell, add a tank in front to get value.
- With 5 units they block three Lightning strikes for fragile cards like Magic Archer, Mother Witch, Firecracker, Princess, Zappies, Bomber, Dart Goblin, Skeleton Dragons, or Flying Machine, and 2 Barbarians survive to keep protecting them.
- They add troop count for a Void, splitting its damage enough to save another troop.
- Splitting them in the back discourages attacks on either lane and reduces spell value, at the cost of slow arrival letting some damage through.
- Situationally they can distract air troops on defense, since they survive several hits while the Crown Tower chips the flyer down.
- When surrounding a splash unit, make sure the surround actually closes — a partial surround means taking far more damage than necessary.
- Watch for Tornado or Freeze used on your splash defender: it frees the Barbarians to wreck the Crown Tower.
- Alternative: Guards cost 3 Elixir with slightly longer range, faster movement, and a spell-resistant shield (except vs Poison), but fewer units, less health and much less damage.
