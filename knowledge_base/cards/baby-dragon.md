# Baby Dragon

> *In-game description:* "Burps fireballs from the sky that deal area damage. Baby dragons hatch cute, hungry and ready for a barbeque."

**Related:** [Evolution: baby-dragon-evolution](../evolutions/baby-dragon-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Baby Dragon is an Epic card that is unlocked from P.E.K.K.A's Playhouse (Arena 6). It spawns a fast, area-damage, air-targeting, short-ranged, air troop with high hitpoints and moderate damage. The Baby Dragon is a small, spring green dragon with short, stubby, team-colored wings, ears, spikes, tongue, hands and feet, brown eyes, and yellow sclera. A Baby Dragon card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A 4 Elixir troop with moderately high hitpoints and fast movement speed that deals low splash damage making him effective as a support troop, killing swarms while chipping at ground troops and towers. He's also a decent mini-tank, but weak against high damage units and takes a while to defeat enemy units.

For 2 Cycles, if his **Evolution** is active, his wings toughen up to practice the **Wind Gust** ability! This wind gust slows down the movement of enemy troops, while also speeding up your own troops. Talk about usefulness! However, movement speed is maintained, which only means that this cute little baby is not actually enraged.

## Abilities and special mechanics

### Modifiers

#### Common: Baby Fly, Fly, Fly
> "+200% movement speed"
Baby Dragon's movement speed is increased by 200%, for a total of 270.
#### Rare: Gargantuan Flameballs
> "Fireball attacks"
Baby Dragon spawns a Fireball when it attacks.
#### Epic: Baby's Got a Baby!
> "Egg spawns Evo Dragon"
When defeated, Baby Dragon spawns an Egg, which will spawn an Evolved Baby Dragon, Evolved Electro Dragon, or Evolved Inferno Dragon when it hatches. Whichever evolved dragon will spawn is completely random.
