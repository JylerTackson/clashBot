# Barbarian Barrel

> *In-game description:* "It rolls over and damages anything in its path, then breaks open and out pops a Barbarian! How did he get inside?!"

**Related:** [Hero variant: barbarian-barrel-hero](../heroes/barbarian-barrel-hero.md)

## Overview

The Barbarian Barrel is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Jungle Arena (Arena 9). It is an area-damage, ground-targeting spell with a short rectangular area and moderate damage. It can only be deployed on the player's own side. Once the spell reaches its destination, it spawns a single Barbarian. It is a spiked barrel with a Barbarian inside it. A Barbarian Barrel card costs 2 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
An angry Barbarian in a barrel rolls a moderate distance, dealing moderate damage to troops and buildings, then breaks open and goes to town with his sword. Despite falling short of reaching the Crown Towers, for 2 Elixir this card can protect, attack, and distract all at the same time.

 If his **Hero Form** is active and the Barbarian is released, 1 Elixir can be spent to use the **Rowdy Roll** ability, which makes the Barbarian simply roll once again with a shorter distance, and then heal the Barbarian quite a bit. The ability can be used to clean more troops, dodge attacks, stall for longer, or simply do some more Crown Tower chip.
