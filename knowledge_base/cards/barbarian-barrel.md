# Barbarian Barrel

> *In-game description:* "It rolls over and damages anything in its path, then breaks open and out pops a Barbarian! How did he get inside?!"

**Related:** [Hero variant: barbarian-barrel-hero](../heroes/barbarian-barrel-hero.md)

## Overview

The Barbarian Barrel is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Jungle Arena (Arena 9). It is an area-damage, ground-targeting spell with a short rectangular area and moderate damage. It can only be deployed on the player's own side. Once the spell reaches its destination, it spawns a single Barbarian. It is a spiked barrel with a Barbarian inside it. A Barbarian Barrel card costs 2 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
An angry Barbarian in a barrel rolls a moderate distance, dealing moderate damage to troops and buildings, then breaks open and goes to town with his sword. Despite falling short of reaching the Crown Towers, for 2 Elixir this card can protect, attack, and distract all at the same time.

 If his **Hero Form** is active and the Barbarian is released, 1 Elixir can be spent to use the **Rowdy Roll** ability, which makes the Barbarian simply roll once again with a shorter distance, and then heal the Barbarian quite a bit. The ability can be used to clean more troops, dodge attacks, stall for longer, or simply do some more Crown Tower chip.

## Strong against

- Goblin Barrel — kills the spawned Goblins up to one level higher than the spell, and the spawned Barbarian distracts a tank in a Goblin Barrel + tank combo.
- Ground swarms in general — clears them so a Battle Ram, Hog Rider or Ram Rider can connect, and the Barbarian handles bulkier counters.
- Lone support troops (Wizard, Witch, Ice Wizard, Bandit, Electro Wizard, Night Witch, Berserker, Fisherman, Musketeer) — fully countered with Crown Tower help, or by the Barbarian alone if the troop is distracted.
- Hunter — spawn the Barbarian at a distance so he survives the first shot and distracts the Hunter while the Crown Tower finishes him.
- Lumberjack and Mini P.E.K.K.A. — place so they must walk backwards to kill the Barbarian, buying the Tower Princess time.
- Tombstone — destroys it like The Log, and also kills the death Skeletons if it is under 50% health.
- Goblin Drill — decent soft counter, since the area of effect hits both the Drill and the Goblins spawned on its destruction.
- Skeleton Barrel — destroys all the death Skeletons for a positive Elixir trade (its death damage is low).
- Evolved Wizard's shield — the Barrel's damage removes it.
- Air troops near the centre (Baby Dragon, Electro Dragon, Skeleton Dragons, Mega Minion, Flying Machine, Phoenix, Minions) — spawn the Barbarian in the opposite lane to kite them while both Towers chip them down.
- Bulky support (Executioner, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Cannon Cart, Goblin Demolisher, Lumberjack) when paired with Fireball — the Barrel finishes what the Fireball leaves alive.

## Weak against

- Prince — almost useless against him: little damage dealt, and the Barbarian is one-shot by the charge.
- Any support unit or the Tower Princess — they fully counter the spawned Barbarian; Skeletons, Electro Spirit and Ice Spirit make it a +1 Elixir trade.
- Cannoneer and Dagger Duchess — the Barbarian never connects at all, so they can ignore it entirely.
- Guards, Dark Prince and Royal Recruits — their shields survive the Barrel's damage, unlike the Evolved Wizard's.
- Equal-level Princess, Dart Goblin and Rascal Girls — survive the roll and need an extra Barbarian swing; only a Barrel one level higher kills outright.
- Crown Towers — its range cannot reach them, so it needs a partner spell (Arrows, Zap, Fireball, Giant Snowball, Poison) that can.
- Tombstone or other targets placed deep in enemy territory — out of the Barrel's reach since it can only be deployed on your own side.
- Staggered Goblin Barrels — a Goblin Barrel offset 1 tile from centre makes a centred Barbarian Barrel miss a Goblin, letting it stab twice.
- Skeleton Barrel's 0.5 second death animation — casting during it affects neither the Barrel nor the Skeletons.
- Monk — a Barrel cast at an ability-active Monk in the centre is deflected back and spawns an enemy Barbarian that walks into and activates your King's Tower.
- Graveyard Skeletons — the Barrel's radius is thinner than The Log's, so Skeletons that look in range often are not.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: a 2 Elixir ground-targeting rolling spell plus a follow-up Barbarian — it can protect, attack and distract at once, but stops short of the Crown Tower.
- Played on the bridge, the Barbarian gets exactly one hit on the Crown Tower.
- Placed at most 2 tiles from the river, the Barbarian spawns on the opponent's side; play it as close to the river as possible when you want him to reach something like an Inferno Tower.
- Its range lets the Barbarian spawn across the river, which adds defensive value against ranged troops.
- Battle Ram synergy is strong: the Barrel clears swarms and adds a third Barbarian; Hog Rider and Ram Rider work the same way.
- Fireball + Barbarian Barrel is a strong combo for bulky support troops that survive a lone Fireball.
- Graveyard: cast the Graveyard first, then the Barrel at the bridge so the Barbarian spawns as the Skeletons do, tanking for them and chipping the Tower.
- The Barbarian can tank for a Miner (with the Tower attacking him), or the Miner can tank for the Barbarian; Goblin Drill works similarly.
- Barbarian Barrel + Goblin Barrel is an even cheaper push — the Barbarian tanks while the Goblins hit the Tower, and Zap does not hurt him; only run it when The Log or an enemy Barbarian Barrel is out of rotation.
- Works as a situational Graveyard counter: wait for Skeletons to build up, then roll it so the Barbarian both kills and tanks some of them.
- Pairs with Evolved Bats and other cheap swarms (Goblin Gang, Guards, Evolved Skeletons, Skeleton Army) — the Barbarian tanks while they attack and, for Evolved Bats, heal up.
- Pairs with Royal Giant: it kills swarms placed on him and the Barbarian handles single defenders, while the Royal Giant tanks so the Barbarian may connect.
- Against the Witch, cast as soon as her Skeletons spawn (so they cannot shield her) and aim so the Barbarian lands beside her.
- Because it spawns a troop, it can split a building-targeter from its support — e.g. luring a Giant Skeleton away from a Balloon so air defence can focus the Balloon.
