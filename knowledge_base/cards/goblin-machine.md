# Goblin Machine

> *In-game description:* "Goblin Machine has two arms for melee and one big rocket for faraway targets. Who let a baby drive this thing?"

## Overview

The Goblin Machine is a Legendary card that is unlocked from the Miner's Mine (Arena 15). It spawns a single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troop with high hitpoints and moderate damage. It also carries a rocket launcher on its back that can attack independently from the Goblin Machine. The rocket launcher is an area-damage, air-targeting, medium-ranged troop with very high damage. It has a blind spot, preventing it from attacking enemies inside it. It is a wood and metal mech with team-colored zig zag lines, spikes on the edges, a Goblin emblem in the center, and a team-colored rocket launcher on its back that has a striped team-colored rocket, all controlled by a Baby Goblin who has a magenta mohawk, carries a small hammer on his back, and wears light brown gloves. A Goblin Machine card costs 5 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
We don't know if something so puny as a Goblin built this mech, but we do know that this machine is anything but puny! For 5 Elixir, a massive mech made of wood and metal is deployed into the arena, having high health and low damage with every punch. Though, the true star of the show is the Rocket, dealing high damage to both ground and air units after a long 5 second charge time, but only a third of its damage is dealt to Crown Towers. Additionally, this Rocket has a dead zone to where any troop or building less than 2.5 tiles in front of the machine isn’t targeted, helping the machine deal with those pesky ranged troops much easier.
