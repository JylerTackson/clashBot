# Prince

> *In-game description:* "Don't let the little pony fool you. Once the Prince gets a running start, you WILL be trampled. Deals double damage once he gets charging."

## Overview

The Prince is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Royal Arena (Arena 7). It spawns a single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troop with high hitpoints and very high damage. After he travels 2 tiles, he will begin to charge towards his target, and deals double damage if he hits the target whilst charging. He is able to jump over the river. He resembles a man in heavy, golden armor, who wields a striped team-colored lance tied with a team-colored ribbon on top, wears a helmet with a visor and a team-colored feather on it, a team-colored belt and brown shoes with golden plates on them, has a brown goatee, mustache, hair, eyebrows and eyes, and rides a pony with team-colored reins and a saddle on its back. A Prince card costs 5 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
With very high damage and good hitpoints for 5 Elixir, the Prince is powerful on offense and defense. His charge ability gives him extra speed and allows him to deal double damage to a target when he crashes, allowing him to devastate targets or speed up slow units. His trusty pony can also jump over the river, just like a hog. However, his slow attack speed means swarms in particular make it difficult for him to break through.
