# Golem

> *In-game description:* "Slow but durable, only attacks buildings. When destroyed, explosively splits into two Golemites and deals area damage!"

## Overview

The Golem is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Hog Mountain (Arena 10). It spawns a slow, single-target, building-targeting, melee, ground troop with extremely high hitpoints and high damage. When defeated, the Golem explodes, dealing damage, inflicting knockback, while spawning two Golemites which have moderate hitpoints and low damage. Like the Golem, the Golemites also explode, dealing damage and inflicting knockback. The Golem is a colossal, menacing rock brute with team-colored eyes and crystals on its back, while its Golemites appear similarly, but are smaller with only one eye. A Golem card costs 8 Elixir to deploy.

## Strong against

- X-Bow and Mortar — the Golem is a very effective tank against them; place him at the building's maximum range so Elixir can be accumulated for support instead of placing him directly on it.
- Low-hitpoint ranged troops (Archers, Princess, Dart Goblin, Bomber, Firecracker, Minions) — the Golem's death damage kills anything with as much or less hitpoints than Lava Pups, especially when a Tornado reels poorly spaced defenders into the explosion.
- Skeletons and Bats — the Golemites' own death damage one-shots them up to 4 levels higher, often letting a Golemite survive to chip the Tower.
- Elixir Blobs and similar chip troops — Golem death damage plus Golemite death damage fully defeats them.
- Enemy pushes when used as a defensive damage sponge — Golem tanks for the Crown Tower while damage dealers (e.g. Mega Minion against a bridge Mega Knight) clean up, then leaves a sizable counterpush.
- Hog Rider placed on your side after a Tower falls — Golem's high mass and 3 second deploy let it bodyblock, forcing the Hog Rider to walk around and retarget the King's Tower.
- Non-spawner defensive buildings without troop support — they can handle a lone Golem but struggle once the Golem is properly supported.

## Weak against

- P.E.K.K.A — enough damage per second to defeat the Golem before it hits a Crown Tower and enough health to survive support fire; a positive trade at 7 Elixir against 8.
- Mini P.E.K.K.A — similar result to P.E.K.K.A, but the support troops must be taken out first as he cannot withstand much damage.
- Lumberjack — kills the Golem in similar time thanks to his speed, and his dropped Rage helps the rest of the defense if he dies.
- Inferno Dragon and Mighty Miner — low initial damage, but after 4 seconds they reach maximum damage and melt the Golem.
- Inferno Tower — destroys a Golem for a positive Elixir trade if allowed to reach its maximum damage stage.
- Sparky — combines high damage with range, hitting the Golem from a safe distance.
- Skeleton Army — 3 Elixir of damage per second demolishes the Golem, and the death explosion is not large enough to kill every Skeleton, so survivors finish the Golemites.
- Goblin Gang and Rascals — placed at a distance so the Spear Goblins survive the death damage and then defend against the Golemites.
- Guards — their shields survive the Golem's death damage and they quickly destroy the Golemites, reliably finishing a weakened Golem.
- Barbarians, Elite Barbarians, Royal Recruits and Three Musketeers — bulky high-damage swarms that survive the death damage (Royal Recruits even on their shields), though expensive and, for Three Musketeers, a negative trade.
- Witch and Night Witch — continuously spawned troops accumulate damage until the Golem dies, though their initial damage is low and death damage clears the spawns.
- Barbarian Hut — the most effective spawner against the Golem; its Barbarians stop him short of the Tower and survivors counterpush.
- Fisherman — pulls the Golem far from its support, can force a lane switch, or drags him in front of the King's Tower to activate it.
- Ice Golem — kites the Golem's support troops into the other lane, separating them from the tank.
- Bomb Tower — poor against the Golem himself but strong against a large ground Golem push, especially once the Golemites appear (useless against air support).
- Tesla placed 4 tiles from the bridge and 4 tiles from the Crown Tower — pulls a Golem placed behind the King Tower away from its support (does not work if the Golem was placed at the bridge).
- Opposite-lane pressure — Dark Prince or Goblin Giant punish an early Golem placement, forcing the Golem player to abandon the push or take heavy damage.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 8 Elixir beatdown win condition — a building-targeting tank meant to be supported, not to deal the damage itself.
- Best placement is behind the King's Tower: the 3 second deploy time and slow movement let Elixir regenerate and support troops line up behind him.
- Placing the Golem at the bridge or on the opponent's side is situational and usually bad — the deploy time gives the opponent free damage and reaction time, and a building placed away from his target can even change which Crown Tower he heads for.
- Do not open a match with Golem; 8 Elixir leaves too little to defend an early attack. Start the push once the opponent's main threat is out of rotation, at double Elixir, or off an Elixir Collector advantage.
- Against building-heavy decks, place the Golem in the corner of the Arena — his sight range is lower than a Giant's, so he can bypass some building placements.
- When the Golem dies, buildings and troops retarget the support behind him for a split second before the Golemites spawn; Zap, Lightning or Freeze stuns the Tower and flips its target to the Golemites (Ice Spirit or Electro Wizard also work but are blockable).
- Splash support is strongly recommended: Baby Dragon, Firecracker, Bowler, Executioner, Bomber, Goblin Demolisher and Wizard clear swarms from safely behind the Golem.
- Stun support pushes through charging defenders — Inferno Tower, Inferno Dragon, Mighty Miner, Prince, Little Prince and Sparky. Zap is the cheapest reset; Lightning costs more but also one-shots many glass cannons (Musketeer, Mega Minion, Electro Dragon, Hunter, Archer Queen, Fisherman, Electro Wizard, Witch, Bandit, Night Witch, Berserker, Little Prince, Wizard).
- Against an Inferno Tower placed at the bridge and into the other lane (to dodge a double-value Lightning), Electro Wizard or Electro Dragon are best — they stun continuously until it dies and then join the counterpush.
- Other Inferno answers: fast swarms (Bats, Spear Goblins, Minion Horde) at mid-river to abuse its slow first targeting; Night Witch's Bats; shielded troops like Guards or Dark Prince to force a reset; sniping an early Inferno Tower with Dart Goblin or Magic Archer; or baiting it with a Skeleton Barrel. A lone Earthquake is not enough.
- Long-range support (Musketeer, Magic Archer) and river-bypassing troops (Royal Ghost, Bandit, Prince) soften defensive buildings before the Golem gets in range.
- Fisherman as support combines long-range building damage with a pull that drags Anti-Tank cards off the Golem; Evolved Valkyrie does this to a lesser extent.
- Witch and Night Witch spawn distractions safely behind the Golem, but a splash troop plus Tornado combo shuts them down.
- Battle Healer keeps the support troops alive, though her healing on the Golem itself is negligible.
- Tornado or Fisherman cancel an opponent's Ice Golem kiting attempt — a negative Elixir trade but better than losing all support.
- Heavy hitters in the push (Prince, Mighty Miner, Sparky, Hunter, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Monk, Lumberjack) answer tanky blockers like Giant Skeleton, Mega Knight and Evolved Goblin Giant.
- Never attack in the same lane as your Golem; the opponent's defenders would double as Golem defense. Attack the opposite lane instead.
- Cheap distraction cards (Skeletons, Tombstone, Berserker) cover opposite-lane pressure; avoid Spirits, which are sacrificed on contact and cannot counterpush. Most buildings are poor in Golem decks except troop spawners like Goblin Cage and Goblin Hut.
- Golem can bodyblock your own troops — e.g. placing him in front of a Night Witch that has reached the Crown Tower holds her there while you build Elixir.
- Cloning the Golem during his 3 second deploy produces a clone that starts moving immediately, an unexpected way to start the push early.
