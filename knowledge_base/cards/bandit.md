# Bandit

> *In-game description:* "The Bandit dashes to her target and delivers an extra big hit! While dashing, she can't be touched. The mask keeps her identity safe, and gives her bonus cool points!"

## Overview

The Bandit is a Legendary card that is unlocked from the Rascal's Hideout (Arena 13). It spawns a fast, single target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troop with moderate hitpoints and moderate damage. If there are ground units between 3.5 and 6 tiles of her, she will stop moving and begin charging a dash attack which takes 0.8 seconds to execute, and will deal double damage. She is immune to damage during her dash. She appears similar to an Archer, except that she has white hair, wears a mask, a forest green cloak with a team-colored patch, a brown belt and gloves, and a team-colored tunic, and wields a blackjack. A Bandit card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

## Strong against

- Squishy ranged troops near the river — her dash crosses the river and one-shots equal-level Dart Goblin, Archers, Bomber, Wall Breakers, Firecracker, Rascal Girls and Princess.
- Medium-health glass cannons — dash takes Musketeer, Electro Wizard or Wizard to roughly half health, needing only two more regular hits.
- Prince and Dark Prince — she dashes onto them, dealing damage while absorbing their charge thanks to her dash invulnerability.
- Spread-out formations — the dash easily covers the gap between separated troops and lands double damage.
- Inferno Tower and Inferno Dragon — dashing resets their damage ramp-up, making her an effective (if ultimately doomed) distraction.
- Undefended Crown Towers — ignoring her is often fatal; a lone Bandit takes a tower to 50% health for only 3 Elixir.
- Overcommitted pushes — her long dash range and fast dash initiation make her a strong reactive punish card.

## Weak against

- Cheap swarms placed in front of stronger troops — Skeletons or Goblins soak the dash; this must be done at the latest right before she dashes.
- Goblins — without the dash she cannot one-shot mini-swarm units at or above Goblin hitpoints, so they shut her down for a positive Elixir trade.
- Skeletons alone can fully counter a lone Bandit: while she charges, place them 2 tiles from the centre toward her attack side and 3 tiles from the King Tower so she dashes into both towers' range and dies before hitting the Crown Tower.
- Melee troops placed directly in front of her (within 3 tiles) — she cannot dash at targets that close, leaving her with low DPS.
- Melee mini-tanks deployed close to her, denying the dash and the extra damage.
- Ice Spirit — the cheapest but riskiest counter: release it on the fourth tile and one tile into the other lane 1.3 seconds before it enters her aggro range so it escapes the dash and freezes her.
- Mini P.E.K.K.A. and Lumberjack — the usual high-damage answers to her.
- Inferno Tower / Inferno Dragon still incinerate her even though the dash resets their ramp-up.
- Miner — forces the Bandit to walk instead of dash, leaving her user helpless.
- Swarms and high-damage units in general, since her hitpoints are only moderate.

## Notes / synergies

- Primarily an offensive card: cheap punishment or dual-lane push, since her dash mechanic and fast speed make her an immediate threat.
- Defensively, place her at a distance from the enemy troop like a ranged troop so she gets the dash bonus and preserves hitpoints for a counterattack; up close she loses most one-on-one trades.
- Dash range is 3.5-6 tiles, takes 0.8 seconds to execute, deals double damage, and she is immune to damage throughout.
- If a single troop that her dash one-shots stands between the bridge and the Crown Tower, place her so she dashes onto that troop and then onto the tower — she counters it and reaches the tower faster.
- Pairs well with Dark Prince: they keep pace without colliding much, his splash clears Goblin Gang and Skeleton Army, and combined damage kills glass cannons like Lumberjack. The pair is vulnerable to air, e.g. Minion Horde, so bring support.
- Rage speeds up her dash, so she pairs well with Lumberjack — he softens swarms and mini-tanks for her, and his death Rage makes her dash to the next target faster.
- Excellent bait card: she pulls out Mini P.E.K.K.A. or Lumberjack, after which a Hog Rider can go in the other lane unopposed. Her mobility and 3 Elixir cost make her the best card for this tactic.
- Risky opener: deploying her at the start of the battle can land a full dash on the tower if the opponent reacts slowly, and otherwise scouts their deck.
- When defending against her, two precisely placed low-hitpoint cards such as Ice Spirit and Dart Goblin can activate your King Tower.
- Use cheap swarm units to soak her dash and protect key support troops like Dart Goblin or Princess.
