# Tower Princess

> *In-game description:* "The gold standard for Tower Troops. A Tower Princess can defend against anything, one arrow at a time."

## Overview

The Tower Princess is a Common card that is unlocked in the Training Camp. It places a single-target, air-targeting, and long-ranged tower troop on the player's two towers with high hitpoints and low damage. The Tower Princess takes the appearance of a Princess, except that her crown is silver, she wears a team-colored cape, and the quiver on her back is darker with a metal plate on it.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
The King decides to guard his secondary towers with his trusted daughters, because family always comes first. High hitpoints and quick but low damage is always nice as a secondary means of defense.

## Strong against

- Siege decks — Mortar, Royal Giant and X-Bow all need more uses to break her, since she has the highest hitpoints of any Tower Troop and survives long enough for your troops to kill the building.
- Spell-cycle decks — Fireball, Rocket, Lightning and Poison chip her down more slowly than other Tower Troops, which matters late against defensive decks.
- Outranging troops like Magic Archer and Firecracker — her health buys extra time to answer them even while she cannot reach back.
- Swarms and tanks alike, thanks to a moderate hit speed and consistent damage; she is the all-rounder among Tower Troops.
- Spirit cards — a Tower Princess one level above them 2-hit-kills every Spirit before it can connect.
- Melee attackers on the King's Tower after your other Tower falls — Knight, Giant, Valkyrie, Hog Rider, Golem, Miner and Royal Ghost come within her range, so she helps the King finish them.
- Close one-on-one defensive fights she tips in your favor — for example Goblin Cage plus her damage fully counters a Mini P.E.K.K.A. that the Goblin Brawler would otherwise lose to.

## Weak against

- Bulky high-hitpoint troops, where the Cannoneer's higher damage would do better.
- Fast-paced decks, where the Dagger Duchess' quick damage is the stronger answer.
- Long-range melee troops (1.6 tiles or more) attacking the King's Tower from the far side — Prince, Night Witch and Mighty Miner stay out of the surviving Tower Princess' range.
- High-DPS ranged units that stay outside her reach — Wizard, Royal Giant, Three Musketeers, Inferno Dragon, Sparky, Bowler and Executioner.
- Heavy hitters that reach her — Mini P.E.K.K.A., P.E.K.K.A., Balloon and Sparky delete large chunks of her health per hit.
- Rapid-hit-speed units — Evolved Barbarians, Royal Recruits, Three Musketeers, Elite Barbarians, Inferno Dragon, Phoenix, Lumberjack, Goblin Machine, Golden Knight, Mighty Miner and Monk drain or destroy her quickly if not stopped.
- Evolved Royal Recruits, which have the range to threaten the King's Tower while staying clear of the second Tower Princess.
- Her own low damage output — she needs distraction troops, mini tanks or buildings to actually kill anything substantial.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: default all-around Tower Troop, 7.5-tile range, 0.8-second attack period, hits air and ground, single target; highest hitpoints of any Tower Troop.
- Compared with the King's Tower she has 0.5 tiles more range and a 0.2-second faster hit speed, but slightly slower projectiles.
- Place defensive units in the middle of your side so attackers walk the longest path in her range, maximizing her damage and possibly pulling the second Tower Princess in too.
- Kiting cards — Ice Golem, Battle Ram, Goblin Giant, Elixir Golem — let both Tower Princesses hit the same target, good against slow melee units like Valkyrie, P.E.K.K.A., Battle Healer, Giant Skeleton, Mega Knight, Skeleton King and Mighty Miner without taking Tower damage.
- Against big building-targeting tanks (Giant, Golem, Electro Giant), place a 3x3 defensive building at the 3-4 position so the tank is lured off the Towers while both Tower Princesses can reach it; 2x2 buildings like Tesla go at 3-3 (a 3-4 placement usually will not pull them), which does not let the second Tower Princess help.
- Lava Hound can bypass certain building placements if played at the far back or at the leftmost part of the middle of the Arena.
- To keep long-range King's Tower attackers in her range, play your defending troop closer to the Princess Tower so she still targets the enemy unit.
- Leveling note: at equal level every Spirit connects for chip damage; leveling her past them stops that but also removes the Electro Spirit chain interaction used to activate your King's Tower, so level with care.
- Last-resort saves on a unit already hitting her: stun, slow, pushback or pull effects — but every one of them needs a follow-up distraction unit so the attacker retargets away from her.
- Rocket, Freeze and Lightning are emergency-only: Freeze does very little damage for a 4-second stun, and Rocket and Lightning cost 6 Elixir with only a 0.5-second stun in Lightning's case.
- Fireball, Rocket and Giant Snowball cannot push back heavy cards, so avoid using them for that purpose against P.E.K.K.A., Prince, Dark Prince, Bowler, Sparky or Mighty Miner.
- Evolved Giant Snowball does not knock back like the normal version but shoves troops a few tiles horizontally, slows for longer and clumps them together — a follow-up Rocket usually finishes the group.
- Electro Wizard and Zappies lack the health to hold a target off her for long; back them with mini tanks like Knight, Valkyrie, Prince, Battle Healer, Goblin Machine, Skeleton King or Golden Knight.
- Electro Dragon cannot distract ground troops that do not target air, so pair it with a ground unit that also benefits from its stun.
- Musketeer, Evolved Barbarians, Wizard, Electro Wizard, Goblinstein and Little Prince survive a Fireball on a sliver, and the Tower Princess can usually finish them off — though a supporting tank may still let chip damage through.
- Compared with the other Tower Troops: less burst than Cannoneer, less speed than Dagger Duchess, no level boost like Royal Chef, but the most health and the most consistent output.
