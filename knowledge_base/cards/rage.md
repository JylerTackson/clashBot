# Rage

> *In-game description:* "Increases troop movement and attack speed. Buildings attack faster and summon troops quicker, too. Chaaaarge!"

## Overview

The Rage is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Serenity Peak (Arena 14). It is an area-damage, air-targeting spell with a medium radius and low damage. It increases the movement speed and attack speed of allied troops and buildings by 30%. It is a deep purple potion packed in a large bottle. It is also the same as that spawned by the Lumberjack and the Santa Hog Rider. A Rage card costs 2 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Pump up your troops with a 2 Elixir boost! The Rage increases the attack speed and movement speed of all units in its effective radius, greatly multiplying their strength and creating savage beasts that can take out towers in seconds. It also deals some damage for that extra support! However, when used in the wrong place or time, it can just be a waste of Elixir.

### Modifiers

#### Common: Raging Rage
> "+200% radius"
Rage's area radius is increased by 200%, for a total of 9 tiles. Its damage is also increased.
#### Rare: Just Superhero Stuff
> "Super Rage buff"
The Rage's boost is increased.
#### Epic: Keep Calm & Rage On
> "Infinite Rage buff"
Rage's duration after leaving the radius is increased to infinite seconds.
