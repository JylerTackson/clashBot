# Wall Breakers

> *In-game description:* "A daring duo of dangerous dive bombers. Nothing warms a Wall Breaker's cold and undead heart like blowing up buildings."

**Related:** [Evolution: wall-breakers-evolution](../evolutions/wall-breakers-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Wall Breakers is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Spooky Town (Arena 12). It spawns two very fast, area-damage, building-targeting, melee, ground troop with low hitpoints and very high damage. They drop their barrel at their target when attacking, destroying themselves in the process on impact. Their deployment is in a horizontal formation. They resemble Skeletons donning team-colored bandannas and carry explosive barrels. A Wall Breakers card costs 2 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Those guys have broke the Arena's walls and inserted their way into the King's deck! After realizing walls don't exist gameplay-wise in Clash Royale, the daring duo runs straight for the nearest building and causes damage with their barrels of gunpowder, which explode on impact with a tower for 2 Elixir! Reacting just a second too slow often means giving the user both the damage advantage and Elixir advantage, though spells can make quick work of them.

For 2 Cycles, if their **Evolution** is active, they'll decide to roll on their **Powdered Barrelcade** while holding dynamites in their now free hands. When the barrels they're running on explodes before reaching a building, they'll go off with a bang! And the best part? The Wall Breakers themselves survive, ready to explode buildings with their dynamites for half of their barrel's damage! If the Runners die too soon, no explosion is dealt, and if the Barrel connects to a building, the Runners won't deal extra damage.
