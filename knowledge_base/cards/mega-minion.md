# Mega Minion

> *In-game description:* "Flying, armored and powerful. What could be its weakness?! Cupcakes."

**Related:** [Hero variant: mega-minion-hero](../heroes/mega-minion-hero.md)

## Overview

The Mega Minion is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Barbarian Bowl (Arena 3). It spawns a single-target, air-targeting, melee, air troop with moderate hitpoints and high damage. It appears as an enlarged Minion wearing a set of armor with a mask, chainmail, and a team-colored belt. A Mega Minion card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A 3 Elixir flying armored gargoyle with below-average health and high damage. What makes him different from other aerial units? He is spell resistant, so not even a lone Fireball can take him out! Plus, his high damage output will allow him to intimidate anyone with his mighty and scary swipes! His only weaknesses? Swarms, ranged units, and cupcakes.

If his **Hero Form** is active, you can spend 2 Elixir to use his **Wounding Warp** ability, which makes him swiftly fly towards the lowest-hitpoint enemy, regardless of distance, and dealing high damage, but with a cost of him dealing significantly less damage to Crown Towers.
