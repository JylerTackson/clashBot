# Bomb Tower

> *In-game description:* "Defensive building that houses a Bomber. Deals area damage to anything dumb enough to stand near it."

## Overview

The Bomb Tower is a Rare card that is unlocked at Spell Valley (Arena 4). It spawns an area-damage, ground-targeting, long-ranged, building with high hitpoints and moderate damage. When defeated, it drops a bomb which explodes after 3 seconds, dealing damage. The building is made of wood, surrounded by steel rams, held together by ropes on the sides, and has a team-colored stripe of paint on it, with a Bomber located at the top. A Bomb Tower card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Bombs away! This ground-only building throws bombs dealing moderate splash damage at a decent rate, making it perfect against swarms. Combined with its medium hitpoints, its cost of 4 Elixir, and a death bomb, this building is one of the most effective against ground units in the game. It attacks quite slowly for its damage though, so tankier units will only take a few scratches from the explosives.
