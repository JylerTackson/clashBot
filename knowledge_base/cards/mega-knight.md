# Mega Knight

> *In-game description:* "He lands with the force of 1,000 mustaches, then jumps from one foe to the next dealing huge area damage. Stand aside!"

**Related:** [Evolution: mega-knight-evolution](../evolutions/mega-knight-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Mega Knight is a Legendary card that is unlocked from the Electro Valley (Arena 11). It spawns an area-damage, ground-targeting, melee, ground troop with very high hitpoints and high damage. Upon deployment, he will deal damage to enemy units around him in a 360º area around him, and inflict knockback. If there are ground units between 3.5 and 5 tiles of him, he will stop moving and begin charging a jump attack which takes 0.9 seconds to execute, and will deal double damage, and inflict knockback. He appears as a Knight with blue eyes and black eyebrows, wearing a helmet with a team-colored feather on top, maces on his arms, and huge dark armor. A Mega Knight card costs 7 Elixir to deploy.

Ladies and gentlemen, prepare to get slammed by a thousand mustaches worth of power: Mega Knight! This super soldier will land with a BOOM, dealing massive area damage to all ground opponents around him. Then he will jump to the next target in range, landing with another powerful BOOM! And if he's not jumping, he'll do a powerful smack, dealing splash damage to melee units! He has very high hitpoints for 7 Elixir, but can still be defeated by high damage units due to his slow attack.

For 1 Cycle, if his **Evolution** is active, his mustache grows big, bad and strong (though you cannot see it) and he learns his signature technique called the **Mega Uppercut**! This technique will make every other attack knock back ANY opponent (one at a time) much closer to the Crown Tower, making units like P.E.K.K.A., Giant, Giant Skeleton, or Golem a jump in the park.

## Strong against

- Glass cannons and support troops — his spawn damage (slightly less than a Royal Delivery) plus two normal hits kills Musketeer, Evolved Barbarians, Little Prince, Ice Wizard, Electro Wizard, Goblinstein and Wizard.
- Zappies, Mother Witch and Magic Archer — they survive the spawn impact but not the jump, so deploy him further away to jump on them instead of on top of them.
- Equal-level Elite Barbarians — high spawn damage and knockback beat them, ideally with the jump and with both knocked in the same direction.
- Clumped-up pushes — very high hitpoints plus 360-degree spawn damage means a big grouped push gives him enormous value.
- X-Bow and Mortar — his jump is hard to react to at the bridge and removes most of their health, and he then cleans up their weak support (they keep locking on mid-air, but he survives it).
- Sparky — timed at the bridge his spawn damage plus a Crown Tower can finish a weakened Sparky while he absorbs her shot; placed behind a Sparky-plus-Giant Skeleton combo he lands on the Sparky and drags the tank into his splash.
- Goblin Barrel — precisely timed spawn damage (deployed behind the Tower as the Barrel's shadow crosses the river) kills all three Goblins.
- Prince — with Crown Tower help his spawn damage counteracts the charge and he survives with plenty of health for a counterattack.
- Skeleton Army — his 1.3 splash radius usually wipes it even while surrounded, unless he is already around half health.
- Royal Recruits — good value, though watch for the half that comes down the other lane; an Ice Golem can bunch them for a perfect spawn.

## Weak against

- All air troops — he is ground-targeting only, so Minions and Bats beat him for free.
- P.E.K.K.A. — arguably a hard counter, beating him one-on-one with no Crown Tower help and surviving with health for a counterpush.
- Tanks in general — Giant, Royal Giant, Giant Skeleton, Goblin Giant and Golem outlast his fairly low sustained DPS.
- Counters placed within 3 tiles of him — Knight, Battle Healer, Golden Knight, Skeleton King, Valkyrie, or glass cannons like Mini P.E.K.K.A., Lumberjack, Royal Ghost, Night Witch and Goblin Brawler beat him efficiently when his jump is denied.
- Inferno Tower, Inferno Dragon and Mighty Miner — they kill him if their ramp-up is not interrupted (add an Ice Golem distraction if it is).
- Prince — with Crown Tower support his high hitpoints and damage beat the Mega Knight.
- Bandit — deployed 4 tiles from the river and 3 tiles from the Crown Tower while he is 2 to 3 tiles from the river, she dashes at the right moment to nullify his jump and lure him into range of both Crown Towers.
- Barbarians and Royal Recruits played reactively with his jump negated — 3 Barbarians survive and demand another answer, and split Royal Recruits threaten the other lane.
- Bats plus a Cannon or other defensive building — the building distracts while the Bats chew him down (best when he is already damaged).
- The Log — the only spell that can stop his jump, since it can kill him mid-air.
- Tornado — pulls him out of jump range; the Fisherman is the only card that can cancel a jump already in progress.
- Skeleton Army placed at the middle of the Tower veering to the opposite side right as he jumps — a few Skeletons distract while the rest converge, killing him with one or two survivors and no Tower damage.
- King Tower activation setups — Ice Spirit plus Skeletons redirecting his jumps, a disposable troop plus Tornado, a Tombstone at the 7-2 placement, or a Fisherman pull all turn his splash into an activation.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 7 Elixir Legendary tank-killer of support troops — more defense-oriented than offense; drop him on a clump of squishy troops and counterpush with what survives.
- Mechanics: 360-degree spawn damage with knockback on deploy, and a jump attack triggered by ground units 3.5 to 5 tiles away that takes 0.9 seconds, deals double damage and knocks back.
- The jump does more damage than the spawn impact, so placement distance is a real decision — on top for spawn damage, further away for the jump.
- Without the jump, his damage per second is low for 7 Elixir; the key to countering him is placing the counter no more than three tiles away so he never charges.
- Stuns and Zap do not stop a jump once it initiates; he can only be stopped by dying mid-air, by The Log, by being pulled out of jump range with Tornado, or, mid-jump, by the Fisherman's drag.
- Stunned in the air he still lands but stays stunned, so a Freeze can buy time to place a troop that denies a second jump — situational and impractical in single Elixir.
- He is not invincible while airborne (unlike the Bandit's dash) — The Log, Earthquake and Bowler cannot hit him mid-air, but X-Bow, Mortar, Sparky and Bomber can.
- He retargets like the Bandit onto any troop placed closer than his current target, so cheap Skeletons or Goblins in front of him can soak an unavoidable jump.
- Cloning quirks: cloned Mega Knights deal no spawn damage, a clone does not inherit the original's jumping state, and cloning does not interrupt the original's jump.
- Always pair him with air coverage — Archers, Electro Dragon, Archer Queen, Musketeer, Wizard, Princess, Flying Machine, Skeleton Dragons, Mega Minion, Magic Archer, Hunter, Dart Goblin, Witch, Baby Dragon, Spear Goblins, Firecracker, Zappies, Phoenix or Executioner — or keep Arrows in hand.
- Excellent with Inferno Dragon: he clears the ranged glass cannons and swarms that beat the Dragon, while the Dragon melts the tanks he cannot. Mighty Miner is an alternative. Bring spells for air swarms.
- Pairs with high-damage troops — Prince, P.E.K.K.A or Mini P.E.K.K.A — that handle tanks while he handles swarms; Mega Knight plus P.E.K.K.A. is devastating but 14 Elixir and very weak to air, so a cheaper Mini P.E.K.K.A. or Prince is usually better.
- Strong odd combo with Ram Rider: he stomps the stun cards (Zappies, Electro Wizard, Goblinstein) and buildings like Tesla that stop her, and the pair cannot both be distracted by one troop.
- Bats synergize both ways — they provide DPS and air cover while he clears swarms and tanks Tower shots.
- Mega Knight plus a Double Prince push amplifies both the strengths and the weaknesses of the push; Rocket, P.E.K.K.A or Minion Horde punish it. On defense he can beat a Double Prince push with cheap distractions like Bats.
- Against a Sparky behind a building-targeting tank, place him to the side with the Sparky nearest so he lands on both and distracts her, freeing your other troops to kill the tank.
- If a Goblin Barrel timing is too tight, dropping him between the top and bottom Goblins prevents most of the Tower damage.
- Against a very high-level pair of Elite Barbarians do not use him — he only removes about 4/5 of their health; use a swarm, Sparky or P.E.K.K.A. instead. Also avoid placing him 3.5 to 5 tiles directly in front of them, which knocks them in two directions.
- If the opponent reveals a P.E.K.K.A., play the Mega Knight in the opposite lane — he is faster and harder to distract than she is.
- Do not defend him with unsupported glass cannons (Musketeer, Ice Wizard, Witch, Electro Wizard, Wizard); add a tank like Knight, Monk, Battle Healer, Golden Knight, Skeleton King or Valkyrie.
- Against a suspected Mega Knight, avoid proactively clumping troops; split cards like Royal Hogs down the middle of the Arena instead.
