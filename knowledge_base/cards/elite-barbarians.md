# Elite Barbarians

> *In-game description:* "Spawns a pair of leveled up Barbarians. They're like regular Barbarians, only harder, better, faster and stronger."

**Related:** [Evolution: elite-barbarians-evolution](../evolutions/elite-barbarians-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Elite Barbarians is a Common card that is unlocked from the Hog Mountain (Arena 10). It spawns two fast, single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troops with high hitpoints and very high damage. Their deployment is in a horizontal formation. They resemble normal Barbarians, except they wear a horned helmet with a team-colored stripe and edges. An Elite Barbarians card costs 6 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A pair of upgraded Barbarians for 6 Elixir, notorious for their sheer combined speed and melee power. Together, they can wreck tanks, evaporate glass cannons and shred down building-targeting units, though they're easily distracted and are especially weak against large swarms. Brain? How about some brawn instead!

For 1 Cycle, if their **Evolution** is active, they will gain and throw their **Elite Spears**at any unit ahead, dealing damage and leaving behind a path of rage, making them even faster and more powerful. And if one isn't enough, they will get a new spear after a while! You can't get rid of their eternal anger!
