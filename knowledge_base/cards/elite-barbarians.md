# Elite Barbarians

> *In-game description:* "Spawns a pair of leveled up Barbarians. They're like regular Barbarians, only harder, better, faster and stronger."

**Related:** [Evolution: elite-barbarians-evolution](../evolutions/elite-barbarians-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Elite Barbarians is a Common card that is unlocked from the Hog Mountain (Arena 10). It spawns two fast, single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troops with high hitpoints and very high damage. Their deployment is in a horizontal formation. They resemble normal Barbarians, except they wear a horned helmet with a team-colored stripe and edges. An Elite Barbarians card costs 6 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A pair of upgraded Barbarians for 6 Elixir, notorious for their sheer combined speed and melee power. Together, they can wreck tanks, evaporate glass cannons and shred down building-targeting units, though they're easily distracted and are especially weak against large swarms. Brain? How about some brawn instead!

For 1 Cycle, if their **Evolution** is active, they will gain and throw their **Elite Spears**at any unit ahead, dealing damage and leaving behind a path of rage, making them even faster and more powerful. And if one isn't enough, they will get a new spear after a while! You can't get rid of their eternal anger!

## Strong against

- Tanks and bulky single-target ground units — their combined melee damage wrecks tanks quickly.
- Glass cannons like Musketeer, Bandit, Ice Wizard, Magic Archer, Electro Wizard, Berserker, Archer Queen, Royal Ghost and Wizard — they shred them and survive for a counterattack.
- Building-targeting units — they are shredded down before reaching their target.
- Elixir Golem + Battle Healer combo — high individual damage and hitpoints kill the Battle Healer easily and destroy every Elixir Golem form fast, one-shotting each Elixir Blob (kill the Battle Healer first).
- Ram Rider — their large mass obstructs her pathing, forcing a longer route, and the Rider's damage is split between the two of them.
- Mega Knight — placed 1 tile ahead of him so they split on top of him before he jumps, they can fully counter him and leave one Elite Barbarian at full health; a similar placement works against Sparky.
- Splash attackers in general, if placed so the two of them surround the troop and minimize the damage taken.
- Treasure Hunt game mode — high damage claims the building quickly and decent health keeps them on the Arena.

## Weak against

- Large swarms — almost all swarms counter them for a positive Elixir trade because their health is mediocre.
- Barbarians — the higher troop count shuts down Elite Barbarians for a positive Elixir trade.
- P.E.K.K.A, Royal Recruits and Mega Knight — reliably chop them down alone and survive (damaged) to counterpush for a negative Elixir trade.
- Sparky — kills them for a neutral Elixir trade, but only one-shots them if they are a Level lower, otherwise needing Crown Tower help.
- Bowler and Evolved Executioner — knockback pushes them away and delays them connecting, countering for a +1 Elixir trade.
- Splash units (Bomber, Valkyrie, Wizard, Executioner, Bowler, Skeleton King, Dark Prince) paired with Skeletons, Goblins, Guards, Heal Spirit, Electro Spirit or Ice Spirit to divert damage.
- Glass cannons (Mini P.E.K.K.A, Night Witch, Executioner, Cannon Cart, Lumberjack), especially protected by an Ice Golem or Elixir Golem.
- Firecracker — her 1.5 tile recoil kites lone Elite Barbarians; with an Ice Spirit or Skeletons to finish them it is a +2 Elixir trade.
- Witch — her Skeletons distract long enough for her to kill them without extra cards; the Skeleton King's ability does the same.
- Ice Golem or Elixir Golem plus a high damage support card such as Minions or Bats.
- Rocket (destroys them up to 1 Level higher for a neutral trade, though hard to land on fast troops) and Lightning (cripples them plus one support card).
- Mini tanks (Knight, Ice Golem, Prince, Valkyrie) that pull them to the center so both Towers whittle them down for a positive trade.
- Tombstone — stalls them with continually spawned Skeletons.
- Bomb Tower — positive Elixir trade, though a distraction card like Skeletons or Goblins is needed if the Elite Barbarians out-level it.
- Ram Rider kiting without jumping the river — stays in range longer and snares them, stopping them for a +1 Elixir trade.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: primarily a defensive card turned into a threatening counterattack, cheaper than pushing them raw at the bridge.
- Versus regular Barbarians: higher stats but only 2 units at 6 Elixir, so less combined damage and weaker defensively, but much less susceptible to splash (an equal-Level Fireball will not one-shot them).
- Comparable to a P.E.K.K.A but 1 Elixir cheaper and split into a pair — splitting one down each lane pressures the opponent to defend both.
- When defending split Elite Barbarians, place a troop or building in the middle so their high sight range pulls both to the center.
- Rushing a lane works when the opponent is low on Elixir or their counter is out of rotation, thanks to their fast movement speed; keep an offensive spell ready for cheap swarms.
- Their fast move speed lets them push slower troops like Valkyrie and Skeleton King — the slow troop clears swarms while the Elite Barbarians kill bulkier threats.
- Rage amplifies their already fast speed and makes them very hard to react to, but leaves a large Elixir deficit and they are still swarm-vulnerable.
- Lumberjack is an alternative: his fast hit speed handles small swarms and his death Rage supercharges them (and can finish off a Skeleton Army), at the cost of 2 more Elixir.
- Fire Spirit combo: deters and clears swarms and adds Tower damage; place it behind the Elite Barbarians (it is faster) so it targets the same troops. Still beaten by mini tank + swarm, and gives spell value.
- Electro Spirit: similar swarm help and can hit swarms placed to dodge others; less damage than Fire Spirit (worse vs Goblins and Spear Goblins) but the stun helps against bulkier troops and lets the pair instantly kill 2-hit survivors like a Witch.
- Ice Spirit: weaker against swarms but a longer stun for bulkier opponents; the Evolved Ice Spirit lets them break through mini tanks like Valkyrie and Bowler.
- Heal Spirit: a decent replacement when they are critically damaged, though it leaves them weak to swarms bulkier than Skeletons or Bats.
- Mobile area attackers like Royal Ghost and Dark Prince cover their swarm weakness while they kill bulky threats — but such pushes die to P.E.K.K.A and Mega Knight.
- Split Skeleton Dragons pair well, covering the swarm weakness; placing the swarm at the center of the Arena brings the Elite Barbarians back together, and their higher hitpoints/damage make this combo better than other Skeleton Dragons pairings when unsplit.
- Their mediocre hitpoints and high damage still make them decent tanks for Archers, Firecracker, Bomber and Spear Goblins.
- They are not knockback-immune (unlike Prince and Dark Prince), which also makes them poor counters to the Evolved Royal Giant and Evolved Battle Ram — use ranged units instead.
- Against a defending Tombstone: hover a small spell like Zap over it and cast right as it dies to remove the four spawned Skeletons so they head straight for the Tower.
- Goblin Cage survives 2 of their hits, but life decay can make it die instantly if placed too early.
- Rocketing them is risky — it frees the opponent to play a card that thrives on the Rocket's absence, such as an Elixir Collector.
- Fisherman is risky: he pulls one in for a hard hit while the other closes fast, so bring a distraction unit.
- Elite Barbarians are unlocked in a higher Arena than Barbarians, so Barbarians are easier to obtain and level for ladder.
