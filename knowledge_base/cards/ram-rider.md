# Ram Rider

> *In-game description:* "Together they charge through the Arena; snaring enemies, knocking down towers... and chewing grass!?"

## Overview

The Ram Rider is a Legendary card that is unlocked from the Electro Valley (Arena 11). It spawns a single-target, building-targeting, melee, ground troop with high hitpoints and high damage. After she travels 2 tiles, she will begin to charge towards her target, and deals double damage if she hits the target whilst charging. She is able to jump over the river. She carries a Rider on her back that can attack independently of the Ram. The Rider's attacks only affect troops, but she can snare them, slowing down their movement speed by 70%. The Ram Rider appears as a dark-skinned woman who has light brown eyes, black hair and eyebrows, and white spots on her face, wears a sandal dress, a team-colored belt, and a golden band on her neck and right arm, wield bolas, and rides a ram that wears a team-colored headband, has a golden piercing in its left ear, and carries a team-colored scroll on its back. A Ram Rider card costs 5 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
She lands into the arena on her trusty ram, dashing to the nearest building while the rider snares and damages any nearby troops, slowing their travelling speed significantly. The ram deals massive damage when it collides with the tower. Like her brother, the Hog Rider, the ram is able to jump over the river, all for 5 Elixir. Nothing will stop this inseparable duo!
