# Lightning

> *In-game description:* "Bolts of lightning damage and stun up to three enemy troops or buildings with the most hitpoints in the target area. Reduced damage to Crown Towers."

## Overview

The Lightning is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Frozen Peak (Arena 8). It is a multi-damage, air-targeting spell with a medium radius and very high damage. When used, it can stun enemies in its radius for 0.5 seconds. The three troops that are targeted are always the three that have the highest hitpoints within its radius. It is a deep blue liquid encased in a large vial. A Lightning card costs 6 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
For 6 Elixir, Lightning strikes the three units with the highest hitpoints in a large area, either crippling or completely annihilating them. As a bonus, there's an extra half-second stun at the end! It's great when taking out support units in a huge push, but is utterly worthless against small units and swarms.

## Strong against

- Support troops in a big push - two of its three strikes can cripple or obliterate them.
- Any troop with hitpoints at or below a Phoenix - one-shot.
- Wizard, Electro Dragon, Archer Queen and Witch - especially next to the enemy Crown Tower, where it also chips the tower for only 1 extra Elixir and still has a third strike left.
- Three Musketeers - a clean counter worth a 3 Elixir advantage (avoid hitting the Tower if you want all 3 struck).
- Archer Queen at equal Level - destroyed immediately, and since it is a spell it hits her even while her ability is active (a neutral trade in that case).
- Evolved Witch - killed even at full overheal hitpoints, plus 2 of her Skeletons.
- Sparky - resets her charge and heavily damages her and her support (though an equal-Level Sparky survives).
- Inferno Tower, Inferno Dragon and Mighty Miner - heavy damage plus a target-lock reset that delays them badly.
- Charging troops - Prince, Dark Prince, Evolved Royal Recruits and Ram Rider all have their charge reset by the 0.5 second stun.
- Cannon Cart - reduced to a useless defensive building that despawns shortly after; the same tactic works on the Goblin Demolisher.
- Buildings generally - it cripples them so building-targeting troops can push on, and unlike Rocket its radius is big enough to also catch a Crown Tower.
- Elixir Collector - denies all its Elixir (it still gives 1 on death), and unlike Rocket, placing it in front of the King Tower does not stop Lightning hitting both the Collector and the Crown Tower.
- Low-health Crown Towers - a good finisher, cast at the Tower's corner.
- Mortar and X-Bow setups - crippled, and the stun may make them re-target if a distraction arrives late.

## Weak against

- Small units and swarms - it only strikes 3 targets, so it is essentially worthless against them.
- Extremely high-hitpoint troops - Giant, Lava Hound, Mega Knight, Giant Skeleton, Golem, Evolved Knight and P.E.K.K.A. shrug it off.
- Shielded units - only the shield is destroyed, since shield and unit count as one entity (Battle Ram is the exception, as the ram and Barbarians are separate entities it can re-target).
- Dark Prince and Evolved Royal Recruits with shields intact - their shields cancel most of the damage, so break the shields first.
- Mother Witch - her summoned Cursed Hogs have more hitpoints, so they soak the strikes and she can survive.
- Goblin Cage used as a lightning rod - it absorbs 2 of the 3 strikes, protecting Musketeer, Zappies, Electro Wizard, Little Prince, Ice Wizard, Wizard, Magic Archer, Goblinstein, Flying Machine, Princess, Firecracker, Bomber and Mother Witch.
- Any high-hitpoint troop parked next to a fragile one - Lightning always picks the healthiest targets, so the small unit is shielded from it.
- Thin target counts - with only 1 or 2 targets in the radius it strikes fewer times and is usually a negative Elixir trade.
- Goblin Barrel - impractical, a negative trade that may not hit all the Goblins (only worth it to save a low tower in the closing seconds).
- Late-game tower snipes with troops nearby - a nearly dead Crown Tower can have less health than a Skeleton, so the strikes go to the troops instead.
- Evolved Barbarians - it can only kill 3 of the 5, whereas Fireball + Zap clears all of them.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 6 Elixir heavy spell - it strikes the three highest-hitpoint troops or buildings in its radius for very high damage and stuns them for 0.5 seconds.
- Only cast it when there are enough targets in the radius; with fewer than three it strikes fewer times and wastes value.
- Hold it back and do not reveal it until the moment with the highest possible Elixir advantage arises, and consciously plan which three targets it should hit.
- The Elixir trade is usually negative, so hitting only 1 or 2 troops or a lone building should be avoided.
- Excellent in Lava Hound decks, where it one-shots ranged threats like Wizard, Electro Dragon and Archer Queen that would shred the Hound and its support. It can do the same to cheaper units like Hunter, Electro Wizard or Night Witch, at the cost of a large Elixir deficit.
- Placement tip for finishing a Crown Tower: aim at the corner of the Tower while it is still inside the radius, so nearby troops are not chosen as targets instead.
- Defending against a Lightning tower-snipe: watch your Crown Tower's health and place a swarm card; Battle Ram and Zappies are especially good since they are cheap across the widest range of tower health.
- Against Inferno Tower it is a negative trade on its own (the building costs 1 less Elixir and can still hurt your push), so try to catch something else too.
- Substitutes: Rocket (same cost, longer deploy plus travel time and a smaller area, but massive area damage that hits far more targets), Void (half the cost, slightly less damage, kills the same troops except Phoenix, but poor against swarms like Barbarians and Zappies), Earthquake (3 Elixir cheaper and far better against buildings, with a snare instead of a stun that cannot reset charges), and Freeze (cheaper with a much longer stun but far lower damage and a smaller area).
- Lightning vs the Fireball + Zap combo: that combo adds about 10 equal-Level kills over a lone Fireball, while Lightning adds at least 8 more (Hunter, Night Witch, Electro Dragon, Bandit, Phoenix, Berserker, Archer Queen, Battle Ram) plus everything the combo kills, in one less deck slot - but the combo is better against Evolved Barbarians.
