# Wizard

> *In-game description:* "The most awesome man to ever set foot in the Arena, the Wizard will blow you away with his handsomeness... and/or fireballs."

**Related:** [Evolution: wizard-evolution](../evolutions/wizard-evolution.md) · [Hero variant: wizard-hero](../heroes/wizard-hero.md)

## Overview

The Wizard is a Rare card that is unlocked from Spell Valley (Arena 4). It spawns an area-damage, air-targeting, medium-ranged, ground troop with moderate hitpoints and high damage. He appears as a man who wears a team-colored cloak and pants, a dark brown shirt, wristbands, belt and boots, and have brown eyes, eyebrows, hair, and a goatee. A Wizard card costs 5 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
For 5 Elixir, the Wizard burns foes on the ground and in the air with heavy-hitting fireballs that deal splash damage. He only has moderate health so protect him to maximize his damage potential. The Wizard excels against groups of enemies, but not so much against tanks, and poetically a Fireball.

For 1 Cycle, if his **Evolution** is active, the Wizard exchanges his stylish hair for the **Fiery Field** ability, giving him extra protection from all sources of damage. This fire shield also explodes when destroyed, dealing splash damage and knocking back foes by a lot!

If his **Hero** is active, you can use his **Fiery Flight** ability for 1 Elixir, which grants cool looking wings for flight, ground unit evasion, and extra movement speed, as he conjures fearsome fireballs that suck nearby troops into the attack point with a fiery whirlwind. Watch close and witness true magic!

## Strong against

- Clumped groups of troops — 1.5 tile splash from 5.5 range clears units that funnel together as they cross the bridge.
- Minions, Goblins and Guards' shields — one shot each per attack.
- Skeleton Dragons (two shots), Barbarians (two shots) and Royal Hogs (three shots).
- Balloon — his damage per second stops it landing anything more than its death damage on a Crown Tower.
- Lava Hound — high DPS on the Hound and one-shots the Lava Pups; a perfectly timed fireball can wipe the pups before they spread (though the timing is rarely achievable).
- Swarms in general — Minion Horde, Goblin Gang, Bats, Barbarians, Royal Recruits and Skeleton Army all fold to his splash, which is why he pairs with a Prince.
- Dual-lane pushes such as Royal Recruits — his range lets him cover both lanes.
- Three Musketeers — with a mini-tank in front he can eliminate them in a few hits and survive for a counterpush.

## Weak against

- Fireball and Poison — leave him at critical health so any extra attacker finishes him; at a higher level than the Wizard either kills him outright.
- Barbarian Barrel — placed so the barrel hits him and the Barbarian pops out behind to distract, the Barbarian plus Crown Tower kills him for a 3 Elixir profit; Royal Delivery works similarly.
- Lightning and Rocket — instantly defeat him, though it is a negative Elixir trade unless they also hit something else such as a Crown Tower.
- Void — kills a lone Wizard in 2 hits for a slight positive Elixir trade if he is the only troop inside it.
- Moderate to high hitpoint troops — Mini P.E.K.K.A (one-shots him while taking little damage), Knight, Prince, Cannon Cart and Valkyrie.
- Musketeer — fast hit speed and similar damage give an easy 1 Elixir profit (she is left under half health at equal level); Ice Wizard works similarly.
- Ice Golem — tanks his attacks while the Crown Tower kills him, a +3 Elixir trade and the cheapest check.
- Archers with Crown Tower help — a +2 Elixir trade, though they die in the process.
- Bandit — placed far away so she dashes, taking no damage during the dash and hitting him hard, leaving her healthy for a counterattack.
- Electro Wizard — spawn damage plus stun weakens the Wizard enough for the Crown Tower to finish him and wins the 1v1.
- Swarms once he has crossed the bridge, provided another troop tanks for him; Barbarians are the only swarm bulky and spread out enough to do it alone.
- Tanks — his single-target damage output is poor against high hitpoint units.

## Notes / synergies

- Support/splash role at 5 Elixir: 1.4 sec hit speed, 0.4 sec first hit speed, 5.5 range, 1.5 splash radius, hits Air & Ground.
- Excellent behind high-hitpoint tanks, clearing clumped defenders in their path; equally good defensively because pushes funnel together at the bridge.
- Against big swarms place him at long range — his fire rate is slightly slow, and the distance preserves his hitpoints for a counterpush.
- Pairs excellently with Tornado, which drags troops into the middle so his splash hits everything at the center.
- Pair with Ice Golem against dual-lane pushes to lure the units together for extra splash value.
- Wizard plus Prince is high-risk, high-reward: the Prince kills mini tanks like Mini P.E.K.K.A while the Wizard clears swarms; the combo is beaten by separating them, as they have different movement speeds and ranges.
- When defending against him with a mini-tank, place the tank before any Musketeer or other unit locks onto the Wizard so he takes no damage.
- If an enemy Wizard has already connected to your Crown Tower, do not place the counter unit near the Tower — it will take unnecessary damage; equally, countering very late close to your own Tower gives away easily avoidable Tower damage.
- Goblins or any card spawning at least 4 troops, timed right, can save him from a Void.
- Beware that spending your spell on the Wizard lets the opponent follow up with a card that thrives without it, such as Barbarians.
- Compared to the Bomber he hits air, has more hitpoints, deals more damage, attacks faster and from further, but costs more Elixir and is therefore less flexible (5 Elixir committed to one push versus 2 Elixir plus 3 spare).
- His damage per attack outclasses other air-and-ground splash options like Baby Dragon, Witch, Ice Wizard or a single Skeleton Dragon when the goal is killing Minions, Goblins, Guards, Skeleton Dragons, Barbarians or Royal Hogs quickly.
- Party modifiers: Fury o' Flames (bigger splash radius), INCOMING! (attacks with Fireballs), Zap! Ouch! Brrr! (spawns an Ice Wizard, Wizard and Electro Wizard trio).
