# Vines

> *In-game description:* "Entangle the three highest hitpoint troops or buildings in the area, stunning them while doing damage. If the troop is in the air, they'll ground them. Don't worry, they'll grow on you!"

## Overview

The Vines is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Frozen Peak (Arena 8). It is a multi-damage, air-targeting spell with a small radius and high damage per second. When used, it will freeze the troop in place and render it unable to move or attack, and allow ground units to hit the trapped unit for 2.5 seconds. The three troops that are targeted are always the three that have the highest hitpoints within its radius. It appears as an aquamarine-colored potion encased in a large, diamond-shaped vial, that is strangled with a curling vine with thorns. It also has a lid which is made of wood with a thick, large thorn on top of it. A Vines card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
For 3 Elixir, you will get 3 crazy overgrown vines. These vines will target the healthiest targets in their radius, and in 2 seconds they will do good damage, entangle enemies so they can neither move nor attack, and drag air units to the ground. However, they are not so numerous, as they can only entangle up to 3 targets.
