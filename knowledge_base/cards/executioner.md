# Executioner

> *In-game description:* "He throws his axe like a boomerang, striking all enemies on the way out AND back. It's a miracle he doesn't lose an arm."

**Related:** [Evolution: executioner-evolution](../evolutions/executioner-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Executioner is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Serenity Peak (Arena 14). It spawns an area damage, air-targeting, medium-ranged, ground troop with high hitpoints and high damage. The Executioner launches an axe that travels up to its maximum range, and returns back to him after some time, while piercing through enemies in both ways. He appears as a shirtless man who has blue eyes, a large red nose, a five-o'clock shadow, carries a double-sided axe with a chip on one of its sides, and wears a black mask, gloves and boots, a belt, and a team-colored kilt and pants. An Executioner card costs 5 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
For 5 Elixir, he provides insane value with moderately high hitpoints and a sharp piercing axe that damages everything, ground or air, in its path for 4 tiles. On top of that, his axe returns, dealing another swing of damage on the way back to him, dealing much more! He can obliterate tightly packed units and deal decent damage, but can still be surrounded due to his slow attack rate. Speaking about coming back, his foes can't say the same.

For 1 Cycle, if his **Evolution** is active, the Executioner's biceps get bigger and stronger while gaining a nice, magical tattoo on his arm. He is also granted with a new technique called the **Axe Smash**. This makes it so anything within 3 tiles take extra damage while knocking back anything but big units, making him extra threatening on defense.
