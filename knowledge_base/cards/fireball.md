# Fireball

> *In-game description:* "Annnnnd... Fireball. Incinerates a small area, dealing high damage. Reduced damage to Crown Towers."

## Overview

The Fireball is a Rare card that is unlocked in the Training Camp. It is an area-damage, air-targeting spell with a medium radius and high damage that also inflicts knockback. It is a large ball of fire. A Fireball card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
It's a bird! It's a plane! It's a… meteor? No! A Fireball! A large fiery rock pummels the Arena and anything else in its small radius, dealing significant damage to all enemy troops and buildings, air or ground, all for 4 Elixir. It can even knock back some ground troops! It easily destroys low hitpoint troops and cripples medium hitpoint units.

## Strong against

- Groups of small troops - Minion Horde, Goblin Gang, Skeleton Army, Skeleton Dragons, Zappies and Archers are wiped out.
- Weakened units of any kind - it is a reliable finisher.
- Zappies, Skeleton Dragons, Mother Witch and Magic Archer up to 2 Levels higher, and Flying Machine up to 1 Level higher, for a neutral Elixir trade.
- Ice Wizard of equal Level - one-shot, opening the way for an offensive push (a slight negative Elixir trade).
- Barbarians, Wizards, Electro Wizards, Little Princes, Furnaces, Goblinsteins and Musketeers when the Fireball is a higher Level than they are - completely destroyed.
- Goblin Demolisher - pushes him below the 50% hitpoint threshold so his own decay kills him (keep him far from the tower).
- Rascals - one-shots the Rascal Girls and damages the Rascal Boy, who is also knocked back and may re-target.
- Skeleton Barrel and Evolved Skeleton Barrel - fully destroyed on its own, with the Skeletons landing too far away to reach the Tower Princess.
- Inferno Dragon - the knockback resets its ramp-up attack, buying time for a tank plus anti-air push.
- Buildings - Elixir Collector (denying a lot of Elixir), Tombstone (destroyed regardless of hitpoints), spawner buildings like Barbarian Hut (shortening lifespan and denying waves), and Cannons or Bomb Towers so a Hog Rider can break through.
- Low-health enemy Crown Towers in Overtime - aim for the bottom corner nearest your King's Tower to cut travel time.
- Three Musketeers placed in the back - a quick Fireball is a huge 5 Elixir advantage if you can afford to activate the enemy King's Tower.

## Weak against

- Lone high-hitpoint troops - Giant, Golem, Mega Knight, Royal Giant, Giant Skeleton, Lava Hound, Evolved Knight, Electro Giant, Goblin Giant and P.E.K.K.A. barely notice it, a clear negative Elixir trade.
- Equal-Level medium troops that survive it - Barbarians, Wizards, Furnaces, Musketeers, Elite Musketeers, Hunters, Electro Wizards, Witches, Little Princes, Goblinsteins and Mega Minions live through it and can be disastrous behind a tank.
- Shielded troops - shields waste most of its damage; especially futile against Evolved Royal Recruits, since removing shields lets them charge and possibly dodge the spell.
- Inferno Tower and Mighty Miner - immune to the knockback, so their charge-up is not reset. The Evolved Inferno Dragon also does not reset when knocked back.
- Fast or very fast troops - the travel delay lets them leave the radius unless you aim ahead of their path.
- Goblin Barrel - a negative Elixir trade even hitting all Goblins (only worth it to save a low tower in the final seconds).
- Royal Hogs - left with a sliver of health rather than destroyed, so it cannot be relied upon.
- Lone X-Bow or Mortar - not enough damage to stop significant tower damage; wait until defenders are placed alongside it.
- Tesla while underground - immune until revealed.
- Monk - a predicted Fireball can be reflected back at your own Crown Tower by his ability.
- Fireball-bait swarms - Minion Horde, Skeleton Dragons and Zappies bait it out easily, so pack an area-damage troop too.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 4 Elixir generalist strong spell - decent damage, reasonable travel time, wide radius, works even as a deck's only spell.
- Be patient with it: waiting until another unit is deployed next to the target yields a bigger Elixir advantage than casting early.
- Account for travel time - troops at the edge of the radius when you cast may be gone when it lands; place it slightly ahead along the troop's path.
- Its knockback is a true stun that resets the attack animations of small to medium troops, unlike Zap's stun which only stalls animations.
- Well-timed knockback can let your troops kill high-damage survivors like Executioner, Wizard, Mega Minion or Hunter before they land a hit.
- Fireball + Zap or Rage replaces a Rocket for finishing medium troops: less damage and one more card slot, but far less travel time and more flexible costs. Land the Zap or Rage first, or the Fireball's knockback makes it miss.
- Fireball + The Log is an alternative that kills things the Zap/Rage version cannot, such as a Night Witch, but cannot hit air.
- Fireball + Barbarian Barrel handles troops like the Executioner that survive two spells, since the spawned Barbarian finishes them.
- Fireball + Giant Snowball is risky - the knockback from one can make the other miss; keep both in hand and cast them well apart.
- Fireball + Spirit cards is cheaper than two spells: with Electro Spirit, Ice Spirit or Heal Spirit it beats anything up to Elixir Golemites; with Fire Spirit, anything up to a Hunter.
- Fireball plus a Champion ability works too - e.g. Fireball + the Golden Knight's Dashing Dash eliminates anything up to an Archer Queen.
- Tornado synergy: launch the Fireball first, then Tornado so its center overlaps the blast radius, dragging groups in. Aim well ahead of targets or at their shadow. Costs 7 Elixir and fails on Tornado-resistant units (Prince, Dark Prince, Giant, Lava Hound, Golem); best when targets move at different speeds, e.g. Lumberjack plus Balloon.
- Synergizes with Goblin Giant and Ram Rider - the Spear Goblins or the Rider finish off Fireball survivors like Musketeer, Goblinstein, Little Prince or Electro Wizard.
- With a Tower Princess, a Fireball aimed at the bottom of a Balloon's shadow knocks it away and just barely prevents tower damage (except the death bomb); fails if the Fireball or Tower Princess is lower Level than the Balloon.
- Remember to kill the tank only after its support is gone, or it can heavily damage or destroy a Crown Tower.
- Against spawner buildings, hit a spawned wave along with the building - hitting only the building is usually a negative trade. The same logic applies to siege buildings and Tombstone.
- Defensive placement to dodge enemy Fireballs: put your building 3 tiles from the river and 4 from the Crown Tower (for Tesla, 3 and 3) so both cannot be hit at once.
- A well-placed Fireball can knock a small central troop such as a Wizard into the other lane - useful for steering it toward your better-defended side, though Tornado does this far more reliably.
- In a pinch it resets a Ram Rider or Battle Ram charge, though a cheaper knockback spell like The Log is more efficient.
- Substitutes: Poison (higher damage, larger area, lasting area denial, better vs spawners, but delayed and no knockback), Royal Delivery (1 Elixir cheaper and spawns a Royal Recruit, but own-side only and usually needs a second spell), Void (higher damage, kills Electro Dragon, 1 Elixir cheaper, but poor vs swarms like Barbarians and Zappies), and Earthquake (1 Elixir cheaper, far better against buildings, with a snare instead of knockback that cannot reset charges).
- Modifier - Common: Scorched Earth, leaves burning residue on the ground.
- Modifier - Rare: Brighter Than the Sun!, sends a second Fireball mirrored on the middle y-axis.
- Modifier - Epic: Fanning the Flames, throws 3 Fireballs alongside a Tornado.
