# Ice Golem

> *In-game description:* "He's tough, targets buildings and explodes when destroyed, slowing nearby enemies. Made entirely out of ice... or is he?! Yes."

**Related:** [Hero variant: ice-golem-hero](../heroes/ice-golem-hero.md)

## Overview

The Ice Golem is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Frozen Peak (Arena 8). It spawns a slow, single-target, building-targeting, melee, ground troop with high hitpoints and very low damage. When defeated, the Ice Golem explodes, dealing damage, while additionally slowing down anything nearby for 2 seconds. The Ice Golem takes the appearance of a large creature made out of snow with glowing team-colored eyes and ice shards on his head, with his hands being mostly made of ice, and his nose and rocks on his spine being in the form of coal. An Ice Golem card costs 2 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A cheap yet versatile mini-tank, the Ice Golem provides a wide range of uses for only 2 Elixir. He's slow enough for a non-committal opening in the back, useful for kiting & distracting, flexible in a push, and deals slowing death damage that can destroy low-health enemy units.

If his **Hero** is active, you can use his **Snowstorm** ability for 2 Elixir, which conjures a freezing blizzard around him that blasts for 3 times, each dealing small damage and applying a slowing effect. Don’t worry about premature death, as the blizzard will continue to blast until it fades away. Blingo Blizzard.

## Strong against

- Skeletons and Bats — his death damage one-shots them outright.
- Goblins, Spear Goblins, Minions, Princess, Dart Goblin and Rascal Girls — death damage plus a Zap or Giant Snowball fully clears them.
- Slow melee troops — P.E.K.K.A, Giant Skeleton, Valkyrie, Skeleton King and Mighty Miner take a long time to catch him and get kited into the other lane while the Crown Towers chip them.
- Long-range troops with big sight ranges — Dart Goblin, Firecracker and Magic Archer can be kited and stopped for a positive Elixir trade.
- Ranged support such as Musketeer, Wizard, Ice Wizard, Mega Minion, Archer Queen, Witch, Hunter and Electro Wizard — dropped directly on top of them, his death slow lets the Tower finish them.
- Clone — he distracts the clones and one-shots them with his death area damage.
- Skeleton Barrel — played right as the barrel drops so most Skeletons target him and get caught by the death damage.
- Dual-lane attacks — placed in the center he forces one lane's troops to merge with the other, setting up area attackers like Mega Knight, Skeleton King, Valkyrie or Dark Prince.
- Lightning — his high hitpoints absorb one strike, potentially saving a Musketeer, Witch, Hunter, Fisherman, Wizard, Mega Minion, Electro Dragon, Berserker, Night Witch, Phoenix, Archer Queen, Battle Ram or Electro Wizard.

## Weak against

- Crown Towers and buildings on his own — his damage is very low (54 DPS at level 16), so he should never attack solo.
- Fast troops that outrun the kite — he is Slow (45), so the kiting trick works far better on slow units.
- Earthquake and Ram Rider — their movement-speed debuffs are more severe and overwrite his slow entirely.
- Giant Snowball and Ice Wizard slows — his death slow does not stack with theirs, so pairing them wastes the effect.
- Anything that must be killed rather than delayed — he is a delaying mini-tank, not a damage dealer.
- The Golem role — he is not a replacement for a Golem: much cheaper, far fewer hitpoints, lower death damage, no Golemites.

## Notes / synergies

- Cheapest mini-tank in the game at 2 Elixir: Slow (45), 2.5 sec hit speed, Melee: Short (0.75), targets buildings only, high hitpoints, very low damage.
- Death Nova on death: 2 tile radius, -30% movement speed for 2 seconds, hitting Air & Ground, with death damage equal to his hit damage (135 at level 16).
- Classic offensive use: place him in front of a Hog Rider, Battle Ram, Dark Prince or Ram Rider so Crown Towers, troops and buildings target him instead, then slow everything when he dies — usually worth 1-2 extra Hog hits.
- His death damage also handles the Skeleton Army that commonly counters a Hog Rider.
- Place him at the bridge with a unit behind him so he tanks first and slows on death.
- Place him in front of leftover low-health defenders to start a mini counter-push or force a response.
- Kiting: play him 2 tiles from the river in the opposite lane and melee troops will chase him across, buying your Towers time.
- Ice Golem then Balloon is a strong, simple combo — he tanks cheaply, distracts poorly placed anti-air, and his death damage kills Bats.
- Pair him with ground damage dealers like Prince, Mini P.E.K.K.A, P.E.K.K.A, Mighty Miner or Elite Barbarians to clear the swarms sent to kill them; add a Zap for Minions, Minion Horde or Evolved Bats.
- With cheap fast troops such as Goblins he enables real Crown Tower damage.
- He fits cycle decks well since they usually lack any high-hitpoint unit, and his low cost makes him disposable on both offense and defense.
- He is a cheap absorb for spirit cards like Fire Spirit and Ice Spirit — place him away from the intended target so the Spirit does not hit both.
