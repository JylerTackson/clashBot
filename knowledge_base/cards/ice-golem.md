# Ice Golem

> *In-game description:* "He's tough, targets buildings and explodes when destroyed, slowing nearby enemies. Made entirely out of ice... or is he?! Yes."

**Related:** [Hero variant: ice-golem-hero](../heroes/ice-golem-hero.md)

## Overview

The Ice Golem is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Frozen Peak (Arena 8). It spawns a slow, single-target, building-targeting, melee, ground troop with high hitpoints and very low damage. When defeated, the Ice Golem explodes, dealing damage, while additionally slowing down anything nearby for 2 seconds. The Ice Golem takes the appearance of a large creature made out of snow with glowing team-colored eyes and ice shards on his head, with his hands being mostly made of ice, and his nose and rocks on his spine being in the form of coal. An Ice Golem card costs 2 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A cheap yet versatile mini-tank, the Ice Golem provides a wide range of uses for only 2 Elixir. He's slow enough for a non-committal opening in the back, useful for kiting & distracting, flexible in a push, and deals slowing death damage that can destroy low-health enemy units.

If his **Hero** is active, you can use his **Snowstorm** ability for 2 Elixir, which conjures a freezing blizzard around him that blasts for 3 times, each dealing small damage and applying a slowing effect. Don’t worry about premature death, as the blizzard will continue to blast until it fades away. Blingo Blizzard.
