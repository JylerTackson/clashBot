# Minions

> *In-game description:* "Three fast, unarmored flying attackers. Roses are red, minions are blue, they can fly, and will crush you!"

## Overview

The Minions is a Common card that is unlocked in the Training Camp. It spawns three single-target, air-targeting, short-ranged, and air troops with low hitpoints and low damage, deployed in a triangular formation. The Minions take the appearance of small, light blue gargoyles with team-colored eyes, an underbite, two large, team-colored horns, two stubby silver wings on their backs, and large hands and legs with vicious-looking claws covered in Dark Elixir, while wearing two team-colored bands on their arms, and producing blackish purple orbs of Dark Elixir as their ammunition. They are also the same as the Minions spawned by the Minion Horde card. A Minions card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Three fast, moderate-damage single-target ranged air troops. Unarmored but targeting both ground and air, they can be a high-damage support card or a quick defense against glass cannons at the cost of 3 Elixir. A bit vulnerable to splash damage, but it'll do the trick.

## Strong against

- Slow-moving mini-tanks — Knight, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Valkyrie, Hog Rider, Battle Healer, Balloon, Bowler, Lumberjack, Goblin Machine, Golden Knight, Skeleton King, Goblinstein, Mighty Miner — their high hit speed and 2.5-tile range chips these down quickly while the ground units cannot hit back.
- Balloon — Minions obstruct its path and kill it outright with their high DPS, and the Balloon can never target them since it only attacks ground/buildings.
- Golem — chip him down alongside the Crown Tower for a +5 Elixir trade; placed correctly they even avoid the Golem's death damage and get pushed clear of the Golemites.
- Giant Skeleton, Mega Knight, and Boss Bandit — if distracted first (e.g. with an Ice Golem), Minions take them out before they connect to the Crown Tower.
- Prince — if his charge is interrupted by a 1-Elixir troop, Minions kill him for a +1 Elixir trade; Dark Prince works too with Electro Spirit or Ice Spirit as the distraction.
- Single-target ranged glass cannons when dropped on top of them — Musketeer, Flying Machine, Dart Goblin, Electro Wizard, Archer Queen (and distracted Three Musketeers); Minions survive one hit and burst them down.
- Skeleton Barrel — place on top; after it dies the Minions drift out of the death damage and come back for the Skeletons, a neutral Elixir trade.
- Battle Ram (and Evolved Battle Ram) — placed in front of a lone Ram they kill it; add a cheap distraction for the spawned Barbarians for a neutral trade and healthy Minions.
- Defensive buildings near the river — Cannon, Tesla, Mortar, X-Bow — as air troops they cross the river freely and their three-Minion DPS melts buildings fast.
- Inferno Tower / Inferno Dragon — used as distraction so slow tanks (Valkyrie, P.E.K.K.A., Battle Healer, Giant Skeleton, Sparky, Bowler, Goblin Machine, Skeleton King, Mighty Miner) can push through.
- Graveyard — quick hit speed and immunity to the Crown Tower's hitbox lets them negate a large portion of its damage.
- Big tanks when supported — Giant, P.E.K.K.A., Royal Giant, Giant Skeleton, Goblin Giant, Golem, Mega Knight, Electro Giant, Lava Hound, Elixir Golem.
- Baby Dragon — with correct placement (6 tiles from the bridge, 4 from the Crown Tower, when the Baby Dragon is 2 tiles from the bridge) one Minion dodges an attack and they stop it for a +1 Elixir trade.

## Weak against

- Arrows — kill all three in 2 of the 3 waves for a neutral Elixir trade.
- Royal Delivery — neutral Elixir trade and leaves a Royal Recruit behind; best used when something is tanking for the Minions.
- Vines — neutral Elixir trade, though healthier friendly troops in the area can steal targeting priority and save some Minions.
- Zap, Giant Snowball, and Rage — weaken them enough for the Tower Princess to OHKO, or let anti-air troops finish them in fewer hits; pair with an Ice Golem or a Spirit to finish the remaining hitpoints.
- Void — destroys all three within 2 hits if they are the only troops inside it.
- Cheap distractions — Skeletons or any Spirit in the center stalls them for the Crown Towers; Fire Spirit placed close enough lets the tower OHKO them.
- Ice Golem — distracts them for the Crown Tower, and its death nova further damages and slows them.
- Multi-troop ranged units — Archers, Spear Goblins, Rascals distract long enough for the Crown Tower to finish; Spear Goblins are a +1 Elixir trade, and Evolved Archers 2HKO them from longer range.
- Cheap anti-air ranged units — Dart Goblin (fast hit and retarget speed, 2HKO), Princess (splash, 2HKO), Firecracker (shrapnel kills all three with correct alignment).
- Splash anti-air — Skeleton Dragons, Wizard, Witch, Magic Archer, Hunter, Executioner; Wizard kills all three in one attack and Executioner does so with a full axe throw and survives Lightning.
- Witch and Mother Witch — their spawned Skeletons/Cursed Hogs distract the Minions long enough to be finished off.
- Furnace — the Fire Spirits leave them weak enough for the Furnace and Crown Tower to OHKO, then the Furnace feeds a counterpush.
- Electro Dragon — chains onto all three with one attack and 2HKOs them (-2 Elixir trade but very healthy afterwards); Evolved Electro Dragon may finish them in one attack.
- Evolved Musketeer — her three sniper shots OHKO them.
- Evolved Tesla pulses and Evolved Cannon barrage — weaken or finish off Minions, especially ones already damaged.
- Barbarian Barrel — cannot hit them, but the spawned Barbarian distracts them for the Crown Tower (+1 Elixir trade, best when it also distracts something else).
- Poison — commonly paired with Graveyard, it kills them quickly, so do not over-rely on Minions there.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: cheap 3-Elixir air support / defensive swarm; best conserved for defense because they easily outrun their support and are simple to counter on offense.
- On offense they are only viable for counterpushes when the opponent lacks AoE anti-air (Skeleton Dragons, Wizard, Witch, Magic Archer, Princess, Firecracker, Executioner).
- Unscathed Minions after a defense become threatening if a tank is dropped in front of them for a counterpush.
- Miner synergy: he reaches the Crown Tower directly and tanks for the Minions without being spell- or splash-vulnerable, forcing the opponent to answer both; good punish when ahead on Elixir. Goblin Drill works similarly, but Evolved Goblin Drill will not tank for them due to its threshold mechanic.
- Freeze can disable the Crown Tower and nearby cards for 4 seconds during a Minion attack — punishing against a Zap-reliant opponent, but a heavy 7-Elixir investment and risky once the surprise is gone.
- Leveling Minions is a high priority: a Tower Princess or Royal Chef one level higher 2HKOs them, gutting offensive use (same principle as Archers).
- Mini-tank + Minions is a strong pairing: the mini-tank soaks splash and distracts ground troops while the Minions supply damage and free placement.
- Air support for Lava Hound: they chip its counters, reach buildings faster, and help a paired Balloon move up more quickly.
- Balloon synergy: they deny Mega Minion, Bats, and Phoenix defenses, and their mass speeds the Balloon toward the tower; either can be positioned to tank for the other (e.g. against a Musketeer).
- With Goblin Giant, the backpack Spear Goblins chip Minions when defending — distract them first; on offense the Minions complement that chip damage. The same complementary effect applies to an allied Electro Giant's Zap Pack.
- Against Electro Giant their range lets them bypass the Zap Pack when deployed to the side; a poor placement loses only one Minion.
- Against Royal Ghost, distract him with another troop so he becomes visible and targetable; the same trick works on Evolved Knight's damage-reduction shield (though Minions can still target the Knight).
- Placement against air troops: short distance from melee units so the deploy timer finishes, directly on top of ranged units to negate range, and surround (or wait for a lock-on) against air splashers like Skeleton Dragons and Baby Dragon.
- Against Firecracker, Minions can be placed so she triggers a King Tower activation and still dies to the Crown Tower before firing again — not possible against Cannoneer and stricter timing against Dagger Duchess.
- Do not drop them on an Evolved Skeleton Barrel — its two drops kill them while the second drop's Skeletons survive.
- Supported Minions help stop dual-lane pressure cards like Royal Recruits, Royal Hogs, and Elite Barbarians, and equally support those cards on offense.
- Being air, they bypass the river and force awkward defensive building placements or extra Elixir from the opponent.
- Getting Arrows out of them can be a bait play, but it also lets the opponent bait Arrows before a Minion Horde.
- Substitutes: Bats (cheaper, more bodies, but far frailer and OHKO'd by more cards), Mega Minion (tankier and higher single hit, lower total DPS and much slower), Phoenix (similar for 1 more Elixir), Minion Horde (double the units for 2 more Elixir but less flexible and more spell-vulnerable), ground anti-air (Archers, Musketeer, Spear Goblins, Dart Goblin, Princess, Firecracker), other air cards (Skeleton Dragons, Flying Machine, Baby Dragon, Electro Dragon), or cheap swarms (Goblins, Skeletons, Guards) if the deck has other anti-air.
