# Lumberjack

> *In-game description:* "He chops trees by day and hunts The Log by night. His bottle of Rage spills everywhere when he's defeated."

**Related:** [Evolution: lumberjack-evolution](../evolutions/lumberjack-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Lumberjack is a Legendary card that is unlocked from the Serenity Peak (Arena 14). It spawns a very fast, single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troop with high hitpoints and high damage. When defeated, the Lumberjack spawns a Rage. The Lumberjack appears as a ginger-haired Barbarian with a beard, purple eyes, and a scarlet nose, wearing a brown belt and a team-colored ushanka, wristbands and sandals. He wields an axe and carries a Rage Spell on his shoulder. A Lumberjack card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A fast and powerful Barbarian for 4 Elixir, the Lumberjack provides high damage and speed with moderate hitpoints. He shreds tanks, towers, and even small swarms with his extremely fast attack speed and high damage, making him formidable whether on defense or offense. He also drops a bottle of Rage when he falls, boosting any friendly units nearby!

For 2 Cycles, if his **Evolution** is active, his rage is enough to resuscitate him thanks to the **Raged Revenge** ability. After dropping his Rage, he will rise from the dead in the form of a spooky ghost. This ghost has UNLIMITED health and is ALWAYS invisible, but he disappears shortly after, either when not affected by Rage or after his own Rage runs out. Who you gonna call?
