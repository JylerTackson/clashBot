# Sparky

> *In-game description:* "Sparky slowly charges up, then unloads MASSIVE area damage. Overkill isn't in her vocabulary."

## Overview

The Sparky is a Legendary card that is unlocked from the Electro Valley (Arena 11). It spawns a slow, area-damage, ground-targeting, medium-ranged, ground troop with high hitpoints and extremely high damage. Sparky will charge up over time, releasing her attack when it is full and when an enemy is within her range. However, this charge can be reset by a stun effect. A common example is the Zap which can reset Sparky's charge. She takes the appearance of a large machine with a front-facing golden tesla coil with an attached steel ram and a plate at the front and rear, a wooden base with 4 wheels and golden coil springs, and team-colored body encased in black metallic frame. A Sparky card costs 6 Elixir to deploy.

## Strong against

- High-hitpoint ground troops - Giant, Royal Giant, Giant Skeleton, Goblin Giant, Goblin Machine, Rune Giant, Mega Knight, P.E.K.K.A., Golem, Prince, Knight, Valkyrie, Golden Knight, Skeleton King, Monk, Mighty Miner, Bowler and Elite Barbarians all die in just a few blasts.
- Whole pushes on defence - her large area damage and radius let her decimate clumped counterattacks for large Elixir trades.
- Buildings - she one-shots most of them from range; the few that survive a blast are left so low that health decay finishes them.
- Battle Ram - one blast kills the Ram itself (though the spawned Barbarians then attack her).
- Cannoneer - destroyed in 2 blasts; Dagger Duchess and Tower Princess survive one more but still must not ignore her.
- Double Prince pushes when paired with The Log - Log first to strip the Dark Prince's shield and reset the Prince's charge, then Sparky finishes them.
- Electro Giant - he only stuns her after she has already hit him, so he troubles her far less than other stun cards.
- Zappies approached from a distance - she one-shots all three before they can reset her.
- The Floor is Healing sub-mode - she one-shots most troops, negating the global Heal entirely.

## Weak against

- Air troops - she cannot target air at all, so Minions, Bats and Inferno Dragon kill her unharmed.
- Electro Wizard and Electro Dragon - they permanently stun her until she dies, for a positive Elixir trade.
- Zap, Freeze and Lightning - reset her charge; paired with a troop that would otherwise be one-shot, they save it for a counterattack.
- Rocket - hard counter, destroying an equal-level Sparky in one hit; her slow speed also clumps her support, making Rocket or Giant Skeleton value easy.
- Ground swarms - Barbarians, Skeleton Army, Guards, Skeletons, Goblins, Royal Recruits or Goblin Gang surrounding her only lose a fraction to each blast (avoid this if she has splash support).
- Skeletons plus Bats - the Skeletons stall while the Bats chip her down, unless the opponent has Arrows.
- Tombstone with a tower troop - the spawned Skeletons distract her indefinitely because of her slow attack.
- Graveyard - distracts her for up to 7 seconds with a high chance of killing her, and the scattered Skeletons blunt her splash support; the Skeleton King's ability works similarly.
- Cheap chaff - her damage is wasted on Ice Spirit, Skeletons, Goblins, or on shielded Guards.
- Mini P.E.K.K.A. - survives one blast and kills her in 2 hits (unless he is a Level lower than her).
- P.E.K.K.A. and Mega Knight - a negative Elixir trade, but they stop her while taking only one blast, keeping most hitpoints for a counterattack.
- Bandit - her dash is damage-immune, so she avoids the blast entirely and, with a Tower Princess, stops Sparky without taking damage.
- Golden Knight - his ability absorbs one hit and his hitpoints survive a second; with a tower troop that is a positive Elixir trade.
- Monk - his ability deflects her blast back at her, leaving her on a sliver of health (something else must finish her off).
- Evolved Knight - placed 1 tile into the opposite lane, he tanks two blasts and stays high on health for a counterpush.

## Notes / synergies

- Typical role: 6 Elixir slow, area-damage ground troop; strongest on counterattack, weak and expensive when committed alone on offence.
- Best used as support behind a building-targeting tank like Golem or Giant - the tank separates from her when defenders appear, so one splash card cannot hit both, and her threat can bait an overcommitment that lets the tank through.
- Goblin Giant is a good partner: he tanks while his Spear Goblins handle the air troops she cannot touch, though both are fairly fragile.
- P.E.K.K.A. helps finish units she cannot one-shot; Mega Knight helps clear the swarms around her - both combos are expensive and helpless against air.
- Ice Golem is a cheap mini tank whose death damage kills Skeletons and Bats and softens other troops for her.
- Be careful pairing her with Royal Giant - her large hitbox and mass shove him closer to the Tower; keep a slight gap so she can shoot threats from behind.
- Always run splash support (Bomber, Executioner, Wizard, Bowler, Witch, Goblin Demolisher, Fire Spirit) so her blast is not wasted on chaff.
- Mini P.E.K.K.A. supports her well, finishing units that survive a blast and instantly killing an Electro Wizard dropped on her (limiting him to a single stun).
- Fisherman pairs excellently - he can pull an opposite-lane punish into her range to be blasted (ineffective against swarms or air).
- Tornado is a strong counter-partner against her: it drags her and her splash support together, ideally onto the Crown Tower.
- Heal Spirit's few hundred hitpoints can be enough for her to land one deadly connection on a Crown Tower.
- Rage disguises her firing timing and can land a surprise Tower blast, best once the opponent's distractions are spent.
- Recoil: she is pushed back 0.75 tiles after firing. Do not defend with her in the middle or she may recoil into the other lane or out of range; the recoil does help her stay away from slow troops, and it makes a Miner on a distracted Sparky ineffective since she may retarget and kill him.
- Cloning retains her current charge and keeps charging from where it left off, but the clone itself starts from empty. She is immune to stun while cloning - wait for the opponent's Clone before dropping an Electro Wizard on her.
- If an Electro Wizard is walking up to your fully charged Sparky, an interrupting spell buys her the shot before he stuns her.
- She is not the best anti-tank card despite her damage - Mini P.E.K.K.A. or Elite Barbarians have higher DPS against a lone Royal Giant or Golem; Sparky is better against big multi-card pushes.
- Do not attack in the same lane as an enemy Sparky - push the opposite lane to force the opponent to abandon her; an Ice Golem can kite an opposite-lane rush into her lane so she nullifies it.
- Place her in the middle rather than behind your Crown Tower if the opponent has Rocket, and bait the Rocket with other expensive cards such as Elite Barbarians or Elixir Collector.
- In Sparky mirror matches, Zap the enemy Sparky so yours fires first and kills her for free.
- Keep her at a distance from an Electro Giant so she avoids his reflected damage and extra stuns.
- Beware baiting out Zap/Freeze/Lightning too readily - their absence lets cards like Skeleton Barrel thrive.
