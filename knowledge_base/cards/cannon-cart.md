# Cannon Cart

> *In-game description:* "A Cannon on wheels?! Bet they won't see that coming! At low health, it becomes a Cannon not on wheels."

## Overview

The Cannon Cart is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Miner's Mine (Arena 15). It spawns a single-target, ground-targeting, medium-ranged, ground troop with high hitpoints and moderate damage. When its hitpoints are lowered below 50%, it becomes a building with a 15-second lifetime. It appears as a Cannon with a wooden base atop a wooden cart, with a small team-colored ridge at the rear and a large bolted ridge in the center. A Cannon Cart card costs 5 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
This upgraded Cannon now moves and has more than double the hp! It's slick and quick too, being able to sneakily lock onto a Princess Tower in the blink of an eye and deal surprising amounts of damage if not reacted quickly. Once it reaches 50%, it activates its building form, which acts the same as a Cannon (though with different stats). The Cartless Cannon stays intact for 15 seconds to deter any incoming attacks. However, it's weak to swarms, air troops, and has a heftier 5 Elixir cost.
