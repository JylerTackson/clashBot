# Mother Witch

> *In-game description:* "Places a curse on enemy troops with each attack. When a cursed troop is destroyed, it turns into a building-targeting Hog that fights alongside the Mother Witch. She also bakes great cookies."

## Overview

The Mother Witch is a Legendary card that is unlocked from the Miner's Mine (Arena 15). It spawns a single-target, air-targeting, medium-ranged, ground troop with moderate hitpoints and moderate damage. When the Mother Witch attacks a troop, that troop will have a curse placed on it for 5 seconds. If the troop dies during the curse's duration, a Cursed Hog will spawn, which has moderate hitpoints and very low damage. She appears to be an elderly Witch wearing a lighter-colored cloak with a pink outline and a team-colored patch on its front and back, shorter purple gloves with team-colored cuffs, and a belt with a skull face in the middle, has glowing blue eyes and purple nail paint, wields a staff with a cauldron placed on top, and carries a wooden spoon. The Cursed Hog resembles a Royal Hog without its helmet and has a team-colored band on its tusks and a skull logo on the side of its back. A Mother Witch card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
For 4 Elixir, this witch cackles wildly as she curses her foes, causing them to turn into Cursed Hogs if they fall during the curse's effect. Against a large swarm of units, she can quickly create an army to assault opposing buildings! However, her damage and hitpoints are low, so bulky units will shrug off her curse and make quick work of her, and the curse lasts for only 5 seconds. Save her for an opportune moment in order to start cooking up a hog stew!
