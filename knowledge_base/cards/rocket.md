# Rocket

> *In-game description:* "Deals high damage to a small area. Looks really awesome doing it. Reduced damage to Crown Towers."

## Overview

The Rocket is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Builder's Workshop (Arena 5). It is an area-damage, air-targeting spell with a small radius and very high damage that also inflicts knockback. It is a large rocket with a nose cone, an iron hull, fins, and a skull printed on it. A Rocket card costs 6 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
For a hefty 6 Elixir you can destroy a push, a building, or... your Elixir. The ridiculously high damage comes with a ridiculously small area of effect and a long delay, so be sure not to miss. Recommended targets include support troops, expensive buildings, and Crown Towers — bingo if you hit all three!

## Strong against

- Enemy Crown Towers - it has the highest damage per Elixir against them and is the best endgame finisher; aim at the bottom corner nearest your own King's Tower to cut travel time.
- Medium-health troops - Musketeer, Archer Queen, Night Witch, Wizard, Mega Minion, Hunter, Phoenix, Electro Dragon, Royal Hogs, Bandit, Baby Dragon and Witch all die to it.
- Elite Barbarians up to 1 Level higher - a neutral Elixir trade if aimed well (and even higher-Level ones get finished by the Crown Tower).
- Equally-leveled Mini P.E.K.K.A. - killed, though a negative trade unless his support is hit too.
- Sparky at equal Level - completely destroyed for a neutral Elixir trade, plus tower damage if she was placed in the back.
- Evolved Witch - killed even at full overheal hitpoints, with no risk of leaving her Skeleton wave behind.
- Evolved Barbarians - destroyed without needing a second spell (a slight negative trade), which Fireball cannot do.
- X-Bow - heavily damaged, or destroyed outright if the Rocket is at least 1 Level higher, while also wiping its support.
- Buildings that cannot dodge - Barbarian Hut (a neutral Elixir trade), Inferno Tower (crippled or destroyed), Elixir Collector (denies all Elixir up to 4 Levels higher, though it still gives 1 on death), Mortar and Tesla.
- Cannon Cart - reduced to a useless defensive building that despawns shortly after.
- Big clumped pushes when paired with Tornado - it nukes the whole group.
- 18+ equal-Level cards that a lone Fireball cannot kill, including Hunter, Executioner, Night Witch, Sparky, Baby Dragon, Electro Dragon, Mini P.E.K.K.A, Bandit, Royal Ghost, Phoenix, Goblin Demolisher, Ice Golem, Miner, Elite Barbarians, Lumberjack, Berserker, Archer Queen and Inferno Dragon.

## Weak against

- Swarms of low-hitpoint troops - the radius is only 2 tiles, so Arrows, The Log, Giant Snowball or Zap are usually the better answer.
- Fast-moving troops - the long cast time plus small radius make it very hard to land.
- Shielded troops - shields waste most of its damage; especially futile against Evolved Royal Recruits, since stripping shields lets them charge and dodge it.
- Goblin Barrel - a negative Elixir trade that may not even hit all the Goblins (only worth it to save a low tower in the closing seconds).
- Lone Balloon, Hog Rider or Ram Rider - killing them alone is a negative trade; save it for when they have support (it does reset the Ram Rider's charge in a pinch).
- Battle Healer - she survives it and heals back up by attacking anything you place afterwards.
- Monk - a predicted Rocket can be deflected back at your own Crown Tower.
- Troops under Rage or Clone - Rage lets Three Musketeers, Baby Dragon or Balloon dodge it, and a well-timed Clone can shift a troop out of a slightly off-target Rocket.
- Single targets in general - hitting only 1 or 2 troops is usually a negative Elixir trade.
- The Rage sub-mode of the Global Spell game mode - the extra speed makes it miss entirely.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 6 Elixir heavy nuke / endgame finisher - very high damage with knockback, but a small 2 tile radius and long travel time, so missing is expensive.
- Recommended target priority: support troops, expensive buildings and Crown Towers, ideally several at once.
- General rule: if units are or will be near the intended target, wait to hit them all together, but do not abandon the primary target unless something more important appears.
- Do not Rocket a lone back-placed medium troop early - explore the opponent's deck first; countering a Lumberjack + Balloon push is far better value than Rocketing a back Witch.
- Against the Archer Queen, wait until she activates her ability so the Elixir trade evens out.
- Tower Princess takes 9 Rocket casts to destroy; every other tower troop takes one fewer. Plan accordingly when Rocket is your only tower damage.
- Tornado synergy: launch the Rocket first, then Tornado with its center overlapping the blast radius. Costs 9 Elixir and leaves you very defenseless, and Tornado-resistant units (Prince, Dark Prince, Giant, Lava Hound, Golem) can break it. Best when targets move at different speeds, e.g. Lumberjack plus Balloon. Aim well in front of targets or at their shadow.
- Lightning is an alternative to Rocket in the Tornado combo - larger range, instant damage, up to three targets, but much less damage and it picks targets by hitpoints.
- Pairs with knockback cards - The Log, Giant Snowball or an incoming Skeleton Barrel - placed so they shove troops into the Rocket's radius.
- Rocket vs the Fireball + Zap combo: Fireball + Zap only adds about 10 equal-Level kills over a lone Fireball, while Rocket kills at least 18 more plus everything that combo does, and uses one less deck slot.
- Aim at the very edge of a Barbarian Hut to catch both the hut and its Barbarians - precise timing is needed given the small radius.
- Against a Tesla + X-Bow or Mortar setup, place a distracting unit so the Tesla surfaces and gets caught by the Rocket - the Mortar dies outright and the X-Bow is left low and distracted.
- Even against an equal-Level X-Bow expect around 15% Crown Tower health lost, so add a cheap distraction unit.
- Elixir Collector placed behind a Crown Tower lets you hit both; good opponents will move it in front of their King Tower to deny this.
- When the opponent spends their Rocket, punish it - they drop to about 4 Elixir, so attack in either lane cheaply or drop cards that thrive without a Rocket, like an Elixir Collector.
- Rocket + Goblin Barrel is a threatening last-resort finisher: the Rocket's body hides the Barrel so the Goblins land unnoticed. Alternatively cast the Rocket slightly earlier to kill Barrel counters like Hunter and Witch, or slightly later to deter the opponent from placing them.
- If you only want to break buildings, Earthquake does it for half the Elixir.
- Very effective in increased-Elixir game modes and 2v2, where more troops are on the field (especially with a Tornado to clump them).
- Effective in The Floor is Healing sub-mode, since one-shotting most troops negates the Arena's healing.
- Modifier - Common: Knows No Bounds, +200% radius (triples the Rocket's radius).
- Modifier - Rare: Party On, Goblin!, sends out a Party Rocket.
- Modifier - Epic: Bouncing Bomb, the Rocket bounces and explodes multiple times.
