# Guards

> *In-game description:* "Three ruthless bone brothers with shields. Knock off their shields and all that's left are three ruthless bone brothers."

## Overview

The Guards is an Epic card that is unlocked from the P.E.K.K.A's Playhouse (Arena 6). It spawns three fast single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troops with low hitpoints and low damage with shields in addition to their normal hitpoints. Their deployment is in a triangle formation. They appear to be Skeletons, but are somewhat taller, wear black helmets with team-colored feathers, and carry spears and wooden shields with a crown emblem on them. A Guards card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

## Strong against

- Single-target and slow-attacking ground troops — Prince, P.E.K.K.A., Giant Skeleton, Boss Bandit and Mini P.E.K.K.A. are all easily countered.
- Troops that cannot break a shield in one hit — Knight, Musketeer, Bandit, Battle Healer, Archer Queen, Electro Wizard, Golden Knight and Fisherman are especially poor against them.
- Lone Sparky — a shield absorbs one blast, and dropped directly on her so only one Guard is hit, they destroy her with Crown Tower help, leaving two shielded Guards plus one without.
- Mega Knight — placed directly on top of him before his jump animation so he only hits one at a time, though the placement must be exact or the counter fails.
- Graveyard — more durable than Skeletons and with longer 1.6 tile spears, they defend it well.
- Miner — they tank multiple hits from him for the same reasons.
- Charge-up damage dealers — Inferno Tower, Inferno Dragon and Mighty Miner reset their charge each time they break a shield, so the Guards distract them far longer.
- Ground swarms such as groups of Skeletons, and they can distract air troops like Minions even though they cannot hit them.
- A weakened Golem — their shields survive its death damage and they quickly destroy the Golemites (they will die soon after, and this fails if the Golem is supported).
- Rocket and Lightning, in the sense that the worst those spells can do is strip the shields, since shield and unit count as one entity.

## Weak against

- The Log — knocks the shields off and leaves them weak enough for the Crown Tower to finish; Evolved Zap works the same way.
- Poison — its 8 ticks strip the shields in about 3 seconds and kill the Guards about a second later, a full counter.
- Void — destroys them entirely for a neutral Elixir trade if they are alone (2 strikes for the shields, the third for the Guards); any card spawning 2+ troops protects them from it.
- Arrows and Royal Delivery — same 3 Elixir cost and enough damage to strip all three shields, with Royal Delivery also leaving a Royal Recruit to help.
- Barbarian Barrel — 1 Elixir cheaper than Royal Delivery, but the barrel alone cannot break the shields and needs the Barbarian to finish.
- Area damage troops — Bomber, Royal Ghost, Ice Wizard, Firecracker and Princess are the most efficient; Wizard, Valkyrie, Executioner, Dark Prince, Witch, Magic Archer, Bowler, Mega Knight, Goblin Demolisher, Skeleton King and Bomb Tower also work but cost more.
- Electro Dragon — its chain hits all three at once and kills them in 3 attacks; a negative Elixir trade, but it keeps full hitpoints for a counterattack.
- Hunter — his attack bypasses the shields: the first shot fully destroys 2 Guards and the next finishes the third, leaving him ready to counterattack.
- Air troops — Guards cannot target air, so Bats and Minions kill them quickly, and Baby Dragon and Skeleton Dragons are especially good by hitting several at once.
- Spirit cards paired with The Log — Fire, Ice, Heal and Electro Spirit cannot break the shields but can finish the Guards themselves, so with precise timing that combination is a neutral Elixir trade; Ice Spirit alone can freeze them for the Crown Tower to clean up.
- Tanky troops on offense — with low damage for 3 Elixir they can even fail to take down a Royal Giant quickly.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: a 3 Elixir defensive card — three fast, shielded, ground-only melee troops in a triangle formation; low-risk, high-reward for gaining positive Elixir trades against a push.
- They work like Skeletons at triple the cost but are far more reliable defensively, because a shield negates all excess damage from a single hit.
- Best used defensively and then folded into a counterpush behind something that can tank, such as a Miner, Ice Golem or Hog Rider.
- Rarely worth playing on offense by themselves — they lack damage and pushing power for 3 Elixir.
- Their 1.6 tile spears slightly outrange some troops (like the Prince's), so they can poke a distracted Valkyrie without taking damage and set up a counterpush.
- Great support for X-Bow or Mortar — the shields let them distract long enough for the siege building to keep firing.
- Against unsupported Three Musketeers, once your Crown Tower has locked on, quick-drop Guards then Ice Spirit: the first Guard absorbs three simultaneous hits and the 1.2 second freeze lets the other two Guards plus the Tower finish them.
- Very strong paired with Ice Wizard — his slow and chip damage massively improve their survivability while they distract and damage; add high-DPS ranged units like Cannon or Musketeer to exploit the free time.
- Deck substitutions: a cheaper alternative to Barbarians (2 Elixir less, but lower stats across the board except speed).
- Skeletons and Goblins are cheaper replacements (Goblins better offensively thanks to speed), but both lack shields and are much worse against spells.
- Skeleton Army and Goblin Gang give far more damage for the same 3 Elixir, again at the cost of spell vulnerability.
- Tombstone is a same-cost alternative if you do not need a counterpush, and it also distracts building-targeting troops like Giant, Battle Ram, Ram Rider, Wall Breakers, Elixir Golem and Hog Rider.
