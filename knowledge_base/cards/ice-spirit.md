# Ice Spirit

> *In-game description:* "Spawns one lively little Ice Spirit to freeze a group of enemies. Stay frosty."

**Related:** [Evolution: ice-spirit-evolution](../evolutions/ice-spirit-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Ice Spirit is a Common card that is unlocked from the Frozen Peak (Arena 8). It spawns a very fast, area-damage, air-targeting, short-ranged, ground troop with low hitpoints and low damage. It launches itself at its target when attacking, destroying itself on impact. It will stun affected troops for 1.1 seconds. It resembles a small snowball with cyan eyes and a mouth, along with its hands, legs, teeth and crystals on its head being in the form of ice. An Ice Spirit card costs 1 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
This fast, 1 Elixir little snowball has excellent defensive and offensive capabilities due to his Ice Blast, which can reset channeled abilities, force enemies to retarget, or even freeze the opposition as potent support in a push. His cheap cost also makes him great to quickly shuffle through a player's deck.

But what if 1 isn't enough? Well, for 2 Cycles, if his **Evolution** is active, he comes up with a new chilling ability: the **Frost Blast**! He will blast his targets one more time, dealing more damage, freezing and stalling them for even longer!

## Abilities and special mechanics

### Modifiers

#### Rare: To Thaw or Not to Thaw

> "Blizzard attack"
Ice Spirit's attacks spawn a Snowstorm when it attacks.

## Strong against

- Charge and ramp-up units — its 1.1 second stun resets Sparky, Mighty Miner, Inferno Tower, Inferno Dragon, Little Prince, and the Prince's and Dark Prince's charges.
- Sparky specifically — wait for her to fire (distract with Skeletons if needed), then freeze her right before her next shot.
- Skeletons and Bats — its area damage clears them.
- Spear Goblins — placed on the frontmost tile on either side of the Crown Tower it jumps on the trio just before the third spear lands, freezing all three and leaving them low enough for the Crown Tower to finish, a positive Elixir trade.
- Goblin Gang — deployed right before the Spear Goblins lock onto the Crown Tower, the whole swarm gets splashed and stunned for a +2 Elixir trade.
- Magic Archer — placed on the fifth tile from the bridge and staggered into the opposite lane as he walks into tower range, it kites and fully counters him.
- Skeleton Barrel — dropped behind the Crown Tower when the barrel's shadow is one tile away, it lands on every Skeleton and prevents all damage except the death damage.
- Balloon — 1 Elixir buys a 1.1 second stun, which can deny another high-damage hit even after it has locked on.
- Bandit and Boss Bandit dashes and the Mega Knight jump — placed near their target it absorbs the dash/jump.
- Medium-sized units that need more than one hit to kill it — Musketeer, Executioner, Electro Dragon, Fisherman, Baby Dragon, Witch, Archer Queen, Zappies, Firecracker, Minions, Princess, Ice Wizard, Miner, Magic Archer, Phoenix, Goblin Demolisher, Bomber, Dart Goblin and Bandit — giving it time to freeze them.
- Guards' bodies (not their shields) — Ice Spirit + The Log counters them for a neutral Elixir trade with precise timing.

## Weak against

- Cannoneer and Dagger Duchess — they kill it before it can connect, so the tank-and-freeze trick on the Crown Tower fails.
- A Tower Princess of higher level than the Ice Spirit, which also stops it from surviving to freeze the tower.
- Spear Goblins when it is dropped straight on top of them — all three spears fly immediately and kill it before it jumps.
- Guards — its damage cannot break their shields.
- Its low damage generally: even Ice Spirit + Zap leaves Archers, Wall Breakers, Bomber and Firecracker alive with a sliver of health.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 1 Elixir utility and cycle card; its value is the 1.1 second freeze plus small splash, used on defense and to speed the deck back to the win condition.
- It has enough hitpoints to reach an enemy Tower Princess, so it can tank and freeze the tower while Goblin cards, Graveyard, Archers, Minions, Minion Horde, Skeleton Army, Evolved Bats or Evolved Skeletons connect unscathed.
- Ice Spirit + Goblins for 3 Elixir deals heavy Crown Tower damage if ignored.
- The freeze forces retargets: against an X-Bow locked on your Crown Tower, an Ice Spirit followed by a tank makes the X-Bow switch to the tank.
- On offense it extends a push, and freezing the Crown Tower is especially strong with Hog Rider and Balloon, buying the Balloon time for another hit.
- Pairs with Poison on defense — the stall keeps troops in the Poison, and the chip damage finishes targets like Witch and Musketeer that would otherwise survive.
- Supports ranged defenders (Archers, Musketeer) by distracting and freezing, giving them extra damage time; same idea against ranged splash like Bowler or Executioner while swarms close in.
- Ice Spirit + Skeletons is the classic Cycle deck core — both pull troops across the map, the Skeletons supply surprising DPS, the Ice Spirit buys them time, and both cycle cheaply.
- Ice Spirit + Bats is a lesser-known pairing: the Ice Spirit distracts ground-targeting troops (and air-targeters like Dart Goblin) that the Bats cannot.
- Ice Spirit + Zap kills Goblins, Minions, Princess and Dart Goblin and strips the Guards' shields; Ice Spirit + Fire Spirit hits slightly harder and also kills Archers, Bomber, Wall Breakers and Firecracker.
- Ice Spirit + Tornado clumps a big push so it damages and freezes everything at once; add ranged AoE like Executioner or Wizard. Rocket makes it lethal — Rocket first, then the Ice Spirit at a distance, then the Tornado — but the 10 Elixir cost limits it to big pushes.
- Strong with ramp-up defenders — Inferno Tower, Little Prince and Mighty Miner get safe time to reach full damage; Mini P.E.K.K.A. gains one or two extra high-damage hits.
- Works with defensive buildings like Cannon and Tesla, stalling non-building-targeting troops and resetting the charges of Battle Ram and Ram Rider.
- Sent ahead of a Hog Rider it freezes buildings or, with a Zap, removes Goblins, Minions, Dart Goblin, Princess or Rascal Girls; the same applies ahead of Lumberjack, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Wizard and Musketeer.
- Pairs with a mini tank (Knight, Valkyrie, Skeleton King, Monk) to beat both Princes, since the freeze resets their charge.
- The stun holds troops in place for piercing splash units like Magic Archer, Executioner and Firecracker, which is how they handle a Miner + Minion Horde push.
- Substitutes: Giant Snowball (pricier, no distraction unit, but knockback plus slow stalls better and it is more useful on offense), Royal Delivery (expensive, but OHKOs Minions, Archers, Bomber, Firecracker, Wall Breakers, Elixir Blobs and leaves a shielded Recruit), Zap (more damage and placeable on the opponent's side, but half the stun duration at double the cost), Electro Spirit (same resets, chain hits spaced-out units like Bats and Graveyard, but less damage, shorter stun, and it can activate the enemy King Tower — bad in Graveyard decks).
- Other 1 Elixir alternatives: Skeletons distract better and have high combined DPS, Fire Spirit trades the stun for much more damage against swarms and better Goblin Drill defense, and Heal Spirit suits heavier offensive decks.
