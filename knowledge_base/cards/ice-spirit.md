# Ice Spirit

> *In-game description:* "Spawns one lively little Ice Spirit to freeze a group of enemies. Stay frosty."

**Related:** [Evolution: ice-spirit-evolution](../evolutions/ice-spirit-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Ice Spirit is a Common card that is unlocked from the Frozen Peak (Arena 8). It spawns a very fast, area-damage, air-targeting, short-ranged, ground troop with low hitpoints and low damage. It launches itself at its target when attacking, destroying itself on impact. It will stun affected troops for 1.1 seconds. It resembles a small snowball with cyan eyes and a mouth, along with its hands, legs, teeth and crystals on its head being in the form of ice. An Ice Spirit card costs 1 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
This fast, 1 Elixir little snowball has excellent defensive and offensive capabilities due to his Ice Blast, which can reset channeled abilities, force enemies to retarget, or even freeze the opposition as potent support in a push. His cheap cost also makes him great to quickly shuffle through a player's deck.

But what if 1 isn't enough? Well, for 2 Cycles, if his **Evolution** is active, he comes up with a new chilling ability: the **Frost Blast**! He will blast his targets one more time, dealing more damage, freezing and stalling them for even longer!
