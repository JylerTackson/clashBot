# Royal Chef

> *In-game description:* "Royal Chef cooks a mean Pancake and throws it to your Troop with the most Hitpoints, increasing their Level by 1. Pancakes take longer to cook when he's attacking, and he serves Troops who haven't eaten yet"

## Overview

The Royal Chef is a Legendary card that is unlocked from Rascal's Hideout (Arena 13). It is a single-target, air-targeting, long-ranged tower troop with moderate hitpoints and low damage. Around every 23 seconds, he will throw a pancake at an allied troop on the Arena, increasing their Level by 1. The Royal Chef appears to be a man with aqua eyes, a gold tooth, blue hair and moustache, wears a white toque which covers his eyes and uniform with team-colored cuffs, a brown apron with gold buttons, a belt with a crown emblem, and team-colored pants, carries a pan and a spatula, and has a peg leg.

## Strong against

- Opposing spell counters — a pancake pushes your troops over key spell thresholds, so Barbarian Barrel and Earthquake no longer break a fed Dark Prince's shield.
- Arrows — a fed Elixir Golem's Elixir Blobs survive them.
- Fireball and Poison — a fed Ice Wizard survives either, and a fed Musketeer, Electro Wizard, Little Prince or Goblinstein survives Poison.
- Void — a fed Electro Dragon survives it.
- Lightning — a fed Archer Queen, Battle Ram or Phoenix survives it.
- Rocket — a fed Mini P.E.K.K.A. or Sparky survives it.
- Otherwise he shares the Tower Princess' damage output, so the same defensive matchups apply, just more slowly.

## Weak against

- Chip-damage troops — he has less health and a slower hit speed than the Tower Princess, so ignoring chip is much less affordable.
- Aggression aimed at his Towers: the more he is attacking, the slower he cooks, so pressure directly delays the pancakes.
- Losing a Crown Tower slows cooking as if he were attacking; losing both stops cooking entirely.
- Troops dropped below 33% of maximum health, which he refuses to feed — so heavy chip on your own tank can waste the pancake.
- Buildings, which he cannot feed at all, making him a poor fit alongside building win conditions.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: support Tower Troop, 7.5-tile range, 1-second hit speed, single target, hits air and ground; damage equal to the Tower Princess but slower and with fewer hitpoints.
- Pancake mechanic: after a 7-second start-up he throws a pancake roughly every 23 seconds (23-38 sec depending on how much he is attacking), raising an allied troop's Level by 1.
- Targeting rules: prioritizes higher-hitpoint troops, prefers troops that have not been fed yet, ignores anything below 33% of maximum health, and ignores buildings.
- He spreads pancakes across a push — in a Goblin Giant + Sparky push, if the Goblin Giant already ate, the Sparky gets the next one.
- Best in decks built around large tanks — Giant, Golem, Electro Giant, Goblin Giant, Lava Hound and Royal Giant.
- The level boost carries into most special effects: shields and overheal hitpoints scale with the extra level.
- Threshold-based modes (troops that switch behavior at a health percentage) keep the same threshold but trigger at higher absolute hitpoints, and the alternate mode's inherited stats are boosted too.
- Spawner units pass the boost on: Witch and Night Witch spawns, and death spawns from Skeleton Barrel, Goblin Giant, Golem, Elixir Golem, Battle Ram and Lava Hound, all come out leveled up — as do Champion ability spawns from Skeleton King and Little Prince.
- A fed Goblin Giant boosts both him and his backpack Spear Goblins, including the death spawns; Ram Rider and Goblin Machine work the same way.
- Death effects scale as well: a fed Lumberjack drops a higher-level Rage (Evolved Lumberjack also leaves a leveled-up Lumberjack Ghost), and a fed Skeleton Barrel drops a leveled-up container with more death damage and higher-level Skeletons.
- Mother Witch's Cursed Hogs match her own level when she is fed, regardless of the cursed troop's level.
- Items dropped by Giant Skeleton, Balloon and Mighty Miner cannot themselves be fed, but a fed parent still drops a leveled-up item.
- Spawn-time effects already happened, so a fed Ice Wizard, Electro Wizard or Mega Knight gets no extra spawn damage and a fed Battle Healer no extra spawn healing.
- He can push troops past the normal Level cap, and a single troop can be fed more than once per match — easiest if the rest of your deck is buildings and spells that he cannot target, and if that troop stays above 33% health.
- Because of the pancake, placing support troops in the back is safer with him than with other Tower Troops.
- Watch out with Barbarian Barrel and Royal Delivery: the spawned Barbarian or Royal Recruit can steal the pancake from a more valuable troop — although feeding them is sometimes the better defensive outcome.
