# Elixir Collector

> *In-game description:* "You gotta spend Elixir to make Elixir! This building makes 8 Elixir over its Lifetime. Does not appear in your starting hand."

## Overview

The Elixir Collector is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Miner's Mine (Arena 15). It spawns a building with high hitpoints. Every 12 seconds, the Elixir Collector will passively generate 1 Elixir. When defeated, it will also generate 1 Elixir. It appears as a wooden building reinforced with team-colored seals on top and bases on the bottom, with a vertical glass vat in between two wooden slabs, in which Elixir keeps pumping up. A metal pipe, a hose, and a shutoff value with a metal axle are fixed behind it, with a small puddle of Elixir back and to the left of the collector tube, which are mounted on top of a metal base. An Elixir Collector card costs 6 Elixir to deploy.

## Strong against

- Electro Giant — surprisingly effective, since the building takes no reflection damage and stalls him long enough for the Crown Towers to kill him.
- Building-targeting troops as a last-resort distraction — Giant, Golem, Lava Hound and Wall Breakers can be pulled onto it, though its hitpoints are low for the cost.
- Opponents without a big spell or a Miner — with no way to address the Collector they fall into an Elixir disadvantage and must play hyper-aggressively instead.
- Spell-bait decks — paired with cards like Wizard and Sparky it forces the opponent to choose between spelling the Collector or saving the spell for your troops.

## Weak against

- Fireball — a Fireball 2 or more levels higher destroys the Collector outright for a 1 Elixir profit, and denies all production if cast fast enough.
- Poison — same idea as Fireball; damage shortens the lifetime and cuts production, and it becomes clearly positive if it also catches troops or a Crown Tower.
- Rocket — instantly eliminates the Collector; cast it as early as possible, before any Elixir is generated, for a -1 Elixir trade.
- Earthquake — another spell that chews through the building's lifetime, and it punishes a Collector placed behind the Crown Tower less well than in the middle.
- Miner — reliably chips the Collector down and beats the spell-bait plan, since it does not need the opponent's big spell.
- Goblin Barrel or Miner sent at a near-full-health Collector next to the King's Tower — heavy damage, and the Crown Towers usually kill the unit before the King activates.
- Graveyard — against a Collector placed behind a Crown Tower, a Knight or Baby Dragon at the bridge plus Graveyard placed to spill Skeletons onto the Collector threatens both at once.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 6 Elixir passive economy building, 1 Elixir every 12 seconds plus 1 Elixir on death, up to 8 Elixir total, for a 2 Elixir profit if never damaged.
- Does not appear in your starting hand, so it shrinks the opening draw pool to 7 cards (6 if also running Mirror) — usable to manipulate your opening hand.
- Best played at full Elixir so nothing is wasted, but accept that 6 Elixir leaves you vulnerable for a while.
- Ideally play it right after the opponent has just spent their Fireball, Poison, Rocket or other high-damage spell.
- Even a spelled Collector is not a disaster: at equal levels versus Fireball you still get 2 Elixir plus the death Elixir, a 3 Elixir loss for you against the opponent's 4.
- Remaining production can be spent quickly on cards the opponent's spell would otherwise punish — Three Musketeers or Barbarians are game-changing while the Fireball is out of rotation.
- Mirroring Collectors costs 13 Elixir for 16 Elixir returned (3 profit) but leaves you defenseless; only attempt with a big Elixir lead and cheap cards in hand. A mirrored Collector's profit is just the death Elixir.
- Still run a real defensive building (Cannon, Goblin Cage, Tesla) alongside it — the Collector will not stop Hog Rider, Battle Ram or Ram Rider from hitting your Tower.
- At 10 Elixir the Collector holds its Elixir instead of granting it, so an opponent sitting at 10 can dump 11 Elixir of cards at once; do not sit on it, and play a card at about 0.5 seconds (allowing for the 1-second server delay) to avoid losing the held Elixir.
- Read the next Elixir tick off the model: the central channel fills with pink Elixir, then empties as it dispenses.
- Placement behind the Crown Tower: bad against Rocket (the spell hits tower and Collector), good against Poison and Earthquake because it leaves room to place defenders.
- Placement in the middle: good against Miner and Goblin Drill (both Crown Towers hit them, and Rocket cannot also damage a tower), bad against Poison and Earthquake since those catch your defenders. Especially strong with Dagger Duchess, which nearly instantly deletes a Miner or Goblin Drill.
- These placement rules apply to passive buildings; defensive buildings still belong between the Towers.
- Defending it: the Collector's collision box can block units like Valkyrie from reaching Skeletons, and Tornado can drag an attacking Miner or Goblin Barrel onto the King's Tower to save the Collector's health.
- Rage interaction: Rage produces no extra Elixir in practice — 3 Rage spells are needed for a single extra Elixir, a 5 Elixir deficit, so it is not worth it.
- In the Rage Challenge, building lifetimes are unaffected but production speed is boosted, giving up to 9 Elixir instead of the usual 7.
