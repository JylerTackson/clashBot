# Elixir Collector

> *In-game description:* "You gotta spend Elixir to make Elixir! This building makes 8 Elixir over its Lifetime. Does not appear in your starting hand."

## Overview

The Elixir Collector is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Miner's Mine (Arena 15). It spawns a building with high hitpoints. Every 12 seconds, the Elixir Collector will passively generate 1 Elixir. When defeated, it will also generate 1 Elixir. It appears as a wooden building reinforced with team-colored seals on top and bases on the bottom, with a vertical glass vat in between two wooden slabs, in which Elixir keeps pumping up. A metal pipe, a hose, and a shutoff value with a metal axle are fixed behind it, with a small puddle of Elixir back and to the left of the collector tube, which are mounted on top of a metal base. An Elixir Collector card costs 6 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
You gotta spend Elixir to make Elixir! For 6 Elixir, this passive building has moderate health, the ability to produce bonus Elixir while active, and gives 1 Elixir when destroyed, granting a maximum of 8 Elixir if left untouched. This will make large pushes much easier to play, although this building comes with significant risk due to its high cost and how dealing damage to it will result in less elixir generated (although its normally a negative Elixir trade for the opponent if a spell is used on it). Elixir Collector will not appear in your starting hand.
