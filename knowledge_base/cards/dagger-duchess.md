# Dagger Duchess

> *In-game description:* "This Royal stockpiles her daggers when enemies are far away. When they dare to draw near, she unleashes her daggers in a rapid flurry! Oh, one more thing. Don't call her Princess."

## Overview

The Dagger Duchess is a Legendary card that is unlocked from Electro Valley (Arena 11). It is a single-target, air-targeting, long-ranged tower troop with moderate hitpoints and low damage. She attacks up to 8 times with a very fast hit speed by throwing daggers, until she depletes all of them, after which she reloads one dagger at a time with a slower reload speed. She resembles a woman who has cyan eyes, blonde eyebrows, a small mole on her left cheek, and blonde hair in a bun, wields daggers, wears a team-colored headband, a team-colored dress trimmed with white, white leggings, team-colored shoes, and a gilded breastplate, and carries a dagger case.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
This fiery royal isn't one for dainty lobs. She pelts enemies with quick, stinging daggers, building up to a ferocious finale. She charges up a "dagger bar" over time. Once an enemy gets close, she depletes the dagger bar, unleashing a sharp flurry of up to 8 blades, one enemy at a time, dealing great damage. She's good at cleaning swarms and weak counter-pushes, but can be baited to throw away all of her daggers and end up nearly defenseless. A massive push can also overwhelm her with ease. And make sure to call her a "Duchess". She envies the Princess.
