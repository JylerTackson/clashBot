# Inferno Dragon

> *In-game description:* "Shoots a focused beam of fire that increases in damage over time. Wears a helmet because flying can be dangerous."

**Related:** [Evolution: inferno-dragon-evolution](../evolutions/inferno-dragon-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Inferno Dragon is a Legendary card that is unlocked from the Electro Valley (Arena 11). It spawns a single-target, air-targeting, short-ranged, air troop with high hitpoints and various damage stages. The Inferno Dragon's damage increases over time as it remains focused on a single target specifically every two seconds it goes up to a stage until stage 3. It resembles a Baby Dragon with a helmet, a yellow hose and a fuel tank on its back. An Inferno Dragon card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
For 4 Elixir, this dragon has moderately high health and high damage. With his infernal blast, he can make crisps out of tanks and towers alike, and can even survive a Lightning! But beware of swarms and stunning electrickery, as he is shockingly hopeless against them.

For 2 Cycles, if his **Evolution** is active, he will gain a nice pair of goggles alongside a transparent fuel tank and unlock a new ability: the **Nine-Circled Beam**. This tank will store the damage of his infernal blasts, carrying over to the next unit. He can even go overdrive and unlock the 4th stage, dealing twice as much damage of his 3rd stage after 20 seconds of attacking, easily melting any opponent that dares to oppose him. However, the tank leaks after 7 seconds, resetting him, and he's still not immune to stun and reset attacks. Holy reference!
