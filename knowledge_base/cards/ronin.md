# Ronin

> *In-game description:* "A wandering warrior with nowhere to be and no one he can’t defeat. Ronin parries one melee attack every few seconds, reflecting even more damage than he blocked. 1v1 him, we triple hog dare you!"

## Overview

The Ronin is a Legendary card that is unlocked from the Serenity Peak (Arena 14). It spawns one single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troop with high hit points and high damage. The Ronin can block the attack of opposing melee troops and deal double the damage to them every 3.5 seconds. He appears as a man wearing a viridian-colored robe with a beige trim and patterns, a straw hat with a team-colored tag on the side supported by a gold ring, which covers his eyes, a team-colored sash around his waist, and flip flops, has dark blue hair and a clean-shaved beard, carries two long scabbards attached to his hips, and wields two katanas. A Ronin card costs 5 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A wanderer from afar, a past marred by pain and vengeance... Ronin lost everything in his life, so he vowed he would never lose again! For 5 Elixir, Ronin is a fast mini-tank with dual-wielding katanas to slice his opponents two-fold! When his Parry ability is ready, the next melee attack he takes will be blocked, and the damage deflected back at his assailant – making him an exceptional choice against slow heavy-hitters. That said, he is susceptible to being outranged and swarmed, and cannot swat away aerial pests.
