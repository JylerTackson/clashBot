# Elixir Golem

> *In-game description:* "Splits into two Elixir Golemites when destroyed, which split into two sentient Blobs when defeated. Each part of the Elixir Golem gives your opponent some Elixir when destroyed!"

## Overview

The Elixir Golem is a Rare card that is unlocked from Serenity Peak (Arena 14). It spawns a slow, single-target, building-targeting, melee, ground troop with high hitpoints and high damage. When defeated, Elixir Golem splits into two faster Elixir Golemites which have moderate hitpoints and low damage. When the Elixir Golemites are defeated, each one produces two faster Elixir Blobs which have low hitpoints and very low damage. Each part of the Elixir Golem awards the opponent Elixir when destroyed; the main Elixir Golem gives 1 Elixir, and both Elixir Golemites and Elixir Blobs give 0.5 Elixir. The Elixir Golem appears as a massive brute made of Elixir, wearing two team-colored collars on its arms, with its Elixir Golemites being smaller versions with the team-colored collar on their waist, and their Elixir Blobs resembling a plain lump of Elixir. An Elixir Golem card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

A hefty mound of Elixir, dividing into two Elixir Golemites with half his stats that, in turn, divide into two sentient Elixir Blobs with half of their stats each, the Elixir Golem provides formidable tanking and damaging ability for the very low price of 3 Elixir. Do be warned - when your opponent manages to divide him into his lesser stages, they get 1 Elixir from the Golem and 0.5 Elixir per Golemite/Blob destroyed -  totalling 4 Elixir! This kind gift is enough to turn the tides against you, so make sure this 4 Elixir is spent in the worst way possible!

## Abilities and special mechanics

### Modifiers

#### Common: Gooey Golemites
> "+2 Elixir Golemite"
When the Elixir Golem is defeated, it splits into 4 Elixir Golemites instead of 2.
#### Rare: Give Some, Get Some
> "Get Elixir when defeated"
When any form of the Elixir Golem is defeated, both the player and the opponent will gain the Elixir death spawn instead of only the opponent.
#### Epic: From Blob to Glob
> "Evolving Blobs"
Every few seconds, an Elixir Blob spawns behind the Elixir Golem. This Elixir Blob will change to an Elixir Golemite if it remains alive.

## Strong against

- Buildings — unlike the Golem and Ice Golem it does devastating damage to buildings, and even more as it divides into more units.
- Ramp-up damage dealers — Inferno Dragon and Mighty Miner are far less effective, since they must recharge their damage on every sub-unit.
- Most defensive buildings other than Barbarian Hut, Bomb Tower and a well-placed Tesla, which handle only a lone or unsupported Elixir Golem.
- Crown Towers when ignored — with high health, three waves of units and good damage for 3 Elixir it is a strong chip card that should not be left alone.
- Non-piercing single-target defenders in general, which have to chew through the Golem, then 2 Golemites, then 4 Blobs.

## Weak against

- Piercing splash cards — Bowler, Executioner, Barbarian Barrel and Firecracker hit multiple forms at once and can even carry damage into the next form; Bowler and Evolved Executioner also knock Golemites and Blobs back off their target, and Evolved Firecracker slows them with her sparks.
- Arrows — enough damage to destroy the Elixir Blobs outright (though that immediately hands over 2 Elixir); Poison also kills Blobs, needing at least 4 hits each.
- The Log — brings the Blobs low enough for a single Crown Tower arrow to finish them.
- Barbarian Hut — the most effective spawner against it; the Barbarians deal high damage, stop it reaching the Crown Tower and some survive for a counterpush.
- Bomb Tower — its splash punishes the multiplying forms, especially once the Golemites appear (but not against pushes with air support such as Skeleton Dragons, Flying Machine, Baby Dragon, Minion Horde or Electro Dragon).
- A reactive Tesla placed 4 tiles from the bridge and 4 tiles from the Crown Tower, which pulls a Golem played behind the King Tower away from its support (does not work if it was played at the bridge).
- Cheap anti-tank cards — Mini P.E.K.K.A., Hunter, Lumberjack, Mega Minion, Evolved Skeletons and Skeleton Army can even turn a profit given the Elixir it hands back.
- Any defense at all, in Elixir terms — 3 Elixir spent gives the opponent 4 Elixir back, a 7 Elixir swing before their defensive spend.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: a 3 Elixir building-targeting tank that splits into 2 Elixir Golemites (half stats), which each split into 2 Elixir Blobs (half again); each form speeds up (Slow, Medium, Fast).
- Death Elixir: 1 from the Golem plus 0.5 per Golemite and Blob, 4 Elixir total to the opponent when fully destroyed.
- Its cheap upfront cost is the point: it can lead a counterpush more easily than Giant, Lava Hound, Giant Skeleton, P.E.K.K.A., Mega Knight, Goblin Giant, Electro Giant or Golem, cycles faster and leaves up to 7 Elixir in hand.
- That also allows more expensive support — Golem plus Sparky costs 14 Elixir, Elixir Golem plus Sparky only 9.
- Offset the Elixir gift: start the push right after a positive trade or a good defense, so the opponent ends up even rather than ahead.
- Or protect it so it never dies — a mini tank in front works, and Elixir Golem with Battle Healer or Valkyrie are cheap pushes that are devastating if mishandled.
- If the opponent is at full Elixir the parts give nothing, and they may be forced to play cards just to avoid leaking, weakening their counterpush.
- An Elixir Collector on the field neutralises the trade: its net +1 Elixir cancels the extra Elixir the Golem hands over.
- Against building decks, place it in the corner of the arena — its sight range is lower than a Giant's, so it can bypass some building placements.
- When defending against it, save spells for the last two phases for maximum area value; when attacking with it, note that towers and buildings briefly retarget the troops behind it in the split second before Golemites spawn — fix that with Zap, Lightning or Freeze (Ice Spirit or Electro Wizard also work but are blockable).
- All healing affects every sub-unit, making healers unusually strong with it.
- Its first form is too heavy for a Tornado to drag to the King Tower even when locked on a Crown Tower, though Golemites and Blobs can be pulled.
- Do not Clone or Mirror it when a push stalls — more units simply means more Elixir handed to the defender, and Clone is especially bad since the clones die easily.
- Not a replacement for Ice Golem: the Ice Golem is cheaper, frailer, has death damage, slows troops and works reactively on both sides, while the Elixir Golem is a big-push tank.
- Slightly better in modes with faster Elixir generation, where the defender is more likely to leak the Elixir it gives.
- In 2v2 only the player who lands the killing blow gets the Elixir, so aim it at the lane of the opponent more likely to leak; it is worse if both teammates run it.
- Excellent in Boat Battles — huge total hitpoints for the cost, and the defender's cards are not tied to Elixir, so the drawback does not apply.
