# P.E.K.K.A.

> *In-game description:* "A heavily armored, slow melee fighter. Swings from the hip, but packs a huge punch."

**Related:** [Evolution: p-e-k-k-a-evolution](../evolutions/p-e-k-k-a-evolution.md)

## Overview

The P.E.K.K.A is an Epic card that is unlocked from the P.E.K.K.A's Playhouse (Arena 6). It spawns a slow, single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troop with very high hitpoints and extremely high damage. She appears as a robot-looking troop larger than the Mini P.E.K.K.A. wearing a heavy dark steel blue armor, has team-colored horns and eyes, and wields a large metal sword. A P.E.K.K.A card costs 7 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A very tender robot from the inside, unless you decide to be mean to her or her butterflies! She has extremely high damage and hitpoints but is very slow and easy to distract with swarms, often requiring splash support. For 7 Elixir and her very high stats, she’s very reliable for defense & tanking, and can devastate towers — if she gets there.

For 1 Cycle, if her **Evolution** is active, P.E.K.K.A. will become the warrior of the butterflies and learn the **Butter-Heal** skill. This causes a butterfly to spawn after she deals the final blow to an enemy troop or building, healing some of her health depending on the targeted unit's max health! This can even go past her regular health, making her the tankiest unit in the entire game (theoretically)!

## Strong against

- Large ground pushes — her massive damage and very high hitpoints make her nearly impossible to defeat head-on, so she gets enormous value against big commitments.
- Dangerous single-target ground threats: Evolved Mega Knight, Elite Barbarians, Prince, Mighty Miner, Giant Skeleton, Sparky, Golem, Monk, Boss Bandit and Electro Giant — she destroys most ground units in a few hits and keeps enough health to counterattack.
- Barbarians — she one-shots them even up to 3 levels higher than her.
- Musketeer — she OHKOs her; against the Hog Rider + Valkyrie + Musketeer combo she takes little damage from the Valkyrie and can stop most of it for a +5 Elixir trade.
- Lone Hog Rider — placed in front to bodyblock, she stops him entirely and keeps full hitpoints for a counterattack (negative Elixir trade). Works the same against Ram Rider.
- Double Prince pushes — overwhelmed alone, but with cheap distractions like Bats or Minions she stops the push and retains hitpoints.
- Witch — with Tornado support the Tornado damage lets her OHKO the Witch while the spell clears the Skeletons.
- Multi-card ground offenses combining different roles — her damage and hitpoints handle several roles at once.
- Air units as a tank target (situational) — her hitpoints let her soak many shots from Baby Dragon, Electro Dragon, Phoenix or Flying Machine as a distraction.

## Weak against

- Ground swarms — Skeleton Army or Evolved Skeletons distract and defeat a lone P.E.K.K.A for a positive Elixir trade (though a quick Zap or The Log undoes this).
- Air troops of any kind — she cannot attack air, so Minion Horde or Inferno Dragon chip her down completely unharmed and set up a counterattack.
- Hunter — an effective counter when protected by a tank (Knight, Giant, Ice Golem, Valkyrie, Prince) or a swarm (Guards, Skeletons, Goblins) that keeps him safe next to her.
- Royal Recruits — one side plus the Crown Tower handles her while the other side builds a counterpush; their shields and hitpoints survive any spell costing 3 Elixir or less.
- Ice Golem — the cheapest and most effective kite, dragging her away from her support and leaving her helpless.
- Costlier building chasers, especially Goblin Giant — its faster move speed keeps it out of her reach during the kite while its Spear Goblins chip her down.
- Inferno Tower — stops her alone and keeps most of its hitpoints (but is itself weak to stuns from Electro Wizard, Electro Dragon, Goblinstein or Zappies).
- Tombstone — constant Skeleton spawns, plus death Skeletons, distract her for a very long time.
- Zappies — placed beside the Crown Tower they line up vertically and stagger their stuns, leaving her nearly immobile (weak to Fireball and Poison).
- Electro Dragon and Electro Wizard — slow her down from a distance and are fairly spell resistant; decisive in a P.E.K.K.A. vs P.E.K.K.A. duel.
- Fisherman — pulls her away from an offensive tank, separating her from the push.
- Miner — lures her into the other lane; she obliterates him but your tank continues unharmed.
- Sparky — a viable counter, but risky if the P.E.K.K.A is 2+ levels higher, since she survives 3 blasts and then kills Sparky.
- Skeleton King's ability — the summoned Skeletons distract her; Poison is the P.E.K.K.A. player's answer to this.
- Mini tank + swarm combos placed together — the mini tank soaks the P.E.K.K.A.'s support while the swarm handles her.
- Spells and distraction units generally are the most effective methods against a P.E.K.K.A push.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: "high risk, high reward" 7 Elixir win condition/heavy defender; excellent on defense because she attacks all ground units, but easily distracted on offense — never send her alone.
- Unlike other big tanks she targets all units rather than buildings only, which makes her weaker on offense but a hard counter on defense.
- As an offensive meat shield she limits the opponent's placements and can bait an overcommitment, letting quick troops (Lumberjack, Golden Knight, Little Prince, Dart Goblin, Monk, Berserker) slip past unharmed.
- Splash ranged support covers her weakness: Wizard, Bowler, Bomber, Firecracker, Baby Dragon, Magic Archer, Ice Wizard, Witch, Goblin Demolisher, Princess, Skeleton Dragons, Executioner. Such combos are still vulnerable to spells or mini tanks that pick off the support first.
- Executioner is a particularly good partner — area damage against swarms, tanky enough to act as a mini tank, and survives a strike from an enemy P.E.K.K.A.
- Valkyrie, Executioner, Bowler, Wizard or Baby Dragon support all handle swarms and survive most spells; with Valkyrie or Bowler bring an extra answer to air.
- Air swarms (Bats, Minions) pair well — they clear ground swarms while she tanks, but cheap spells wipe them and leave her alone.
- Electro Dragon partner: its chain lightning denies small swarms and the Inferno cards she is weak to; Electro Wizard is similar but easier to block, only hits 2 targets and dies to glass cannons.
- Spells are essential in P.E.K.K.A. decks as a stand-in when splash troops are out of rotation.
- Poison: long duration keeps large swarms off her and beats hard counters like Witch and the Skeleton King's ability, usually assuring at least one connection.
- Tornado: pulls ranged units she cannot reach into her sword and clears some swarms.
- Rage: good on a counterpush that already has support troops; raging a lone P.E.K.K.A wastes Elixir since it fixes neither swarms nor kiting.
- Fisherman: pulls in ranged troops she struggles to reach and adds damage — still bring a spell or splash unit for swarms and air.
- Goblin Giant + P.E.K.K.A. is expensive but potent — the Goblin Giant clears Bats and Skeletons while she wins duels against Prince, Mighty Miner and Mini P.E.K.K.A.; add spell support for Skeleton Army, Goblin Gang and Minion Horde, and Rage to amplify the push and the Spear Goblins.
- Mighty Miner + P.E.K.K.A.: deploy together and activate the Mighty Miner's ability on the opponent's side to create pressure in both lanes.
- Double Prince + P.E.K.K.A. amplifies both the push's strengths and its weaknesses; high-damage answers like Rocket or Minion Horde punish it.
- Mega Knight + P.E.K.K.A. covers each other's weaknesses but costs 14 Elixir, is even weaker to air, and usually needs one of them used on defense first; don't run both in one deck — use Mini P.E.K.K.A., Mighty Miner, Mega Minion or Prince instead.
- Against Electro Giant, pair her with Electro Wizard: timed right, the stun coincides with her swings so she takes no reflection damage.
- Place a distraction unit like Skeletons or Goblins in front of her when defending a heavy hitter such as Prince or Mini P.E.K.K.A. to preserve hitpoints for the counterattack.
- Her slow speed lets fast charging units (Prince, Dark Prince, Ram Rider, Battle Ram, Golden Knight) push her forward toward a more valuable target.
- Playing her behind the King's Tower is risky at 7 Elixir; only do it with an Elixir lead, when you must build a bigger push, when the opponent's punish would be inefficient, when they have no punish card, or when they must attack her lane.
- Card substitutes: Mega Knight (same cost, more hitpoints and splash but far less damage — pick P.E.K.K.A. against bulky threats, Mega Knight against swarms), Mini P.E.K.K.A. (cheaper, faster, slightly less damage), Elite Barbarians (dual-lane splitting but much weaker alone), Sparky (higher damage and area damage from range, but slower, frailer and stun-vulnerable).
