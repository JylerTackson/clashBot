# Skeleton Barrel

> *In-game description:* "It's a Skeleton party in the sky, until all the balloons pop... then it's a Skeleton party on the ground!"

**Related:** [Evolution: skeleton-barrel-evolution](../evolutions/skeleton-barrel-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Skeleton Barrel is a Common card that is unlocked from the Jungle Arena (Arena 9). It spawns a fast, single-target, building-targeting, melee, air troop with moderate hitpoints. Once it reaches a building or it is destroyed, the barrel drops, dealing area damage, inflicting knockback, and spawning seven Skeletons in a circle. It is a spiked black barrel with a white skull printed on the front tied to three team-colored balloons. A Skeleton Barrel card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A 3 Elixir barrel packed with bones that drifts through the air looking for towers and trouble. It's a bit on the fragile side, but it soars quickly through the arena. When it makes contact with a building, or when the balloons get popped, it explodes and drops seven sword-swinging Skeletons ready to party!

For 2 Cycles, if its **Evolution** is active, it will benefit from the **Double Trouble** Barrel and drop an extra barrel at 75% of its hitpoints (each Barrel has naturally more health)! Double the trouble, double the Skeletons!

## Strong against

- Crown Towers left unanswered — an equally levelled Skeleton Barrel brings a full-health Crown Tower below half for only 3 Elixir, so it should almost never be ignored.
- Inferno Tower and Inferno Dragon — it targets buildings and drops seven Skeletons they cannot handle alone, distracting them for a tank and forcing out a card like Valkyrie or Zap; against the Inferno Dragon the barrel's knockback also causes a retarget.
- Cheap troops with Spear Goblin-level hitpoints or less pre-placed at its landing point — the death damage one-shots them.
- Splash defenders when paired with Freeze — freezing the Wizard, Bomber, Dark Prince, Ice Wizard, Goblin Demolisher, Skeleton King or Valkyrie as it pops lets the Skeletons hurt both the troop and the tower.
- Opponents caught off guard at the start of a match — a bridge-side Skeleton Barrel often connects and, even when answered, scouts the opponent's deck.

## Weak against

- Cheap small spells — The Log, Zap, Barbarian Barrel, Giant Snowball and Rage all wipe the seven Skeletons for a positive Elixir trade (Arrows works too but is only neutral).
- Electro Spirit — chains onto all 7 Skeletons for a +2 Elixir trade; Fire Spirit does the same with the right timing and placement.
- Area damage troops — Magic Archer, Skeleton Dragons, Executioner, Wizard, Firecracker, Princess, Witch, Baby Dragon and Bowler clear most or all Skeletons in one or two attacks.
- A precisely timed Magic Archer — deployed behind the King Tower in the same lane when the barrel's shadow is 5 tiles from the Crown Tower, his arrow pierces nearly every Skeleton.
- Minions placed directly in its path, which stop it from connecting and blunt the Skeletons' damage.
- Cannon or Tombstone — the most Elixir-efficient buildings, neutralizing it for an even trade with Crown Tower help; Bomb Tower, Goblin Hut, Tesla and Furnace also work but trade negatively on their own.
- Tornado — it can still displace the barrel during the 0.5 second animation, even dragging it near the King Tower to activate it (a few Skeletons will still get up to a tenth of the tower's health).
- Rocket or Lightning against a Balloon + Skeleton Barrel push — Lightning is the safer answer since the Rocket's slow travel can miss both airborne units.
- Bomb Tower specifically, whose health, area damage and ground-only targeting make it a dedicated Skeleton Barrel answer while another card handles a Balloon.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 3 Elixir building-targeting air win condition / spell bait — the barrel flies at buildings and, on death or contact, deals area damage, knocks back, and drops 7 Skeletons.
- Timing quirk: for 0.5 seconds while the barrel is falling, neither the barrel nor the Skeletons exist as entities, so a spell cast in that window does nothing.
- Because small spells clean it up cheaply, run other bait cards such as Goblin Gang, Bats or Skeletons to stretch the opponent's spell answers thin.
- The death area damage one-shots anything with Spear Goblin hitpoints or less, which stops preemptive cheap troops at the landing point.
- Miner synergy: the barrel tanks tower hits while the Miner chips, and during the 0.5 second gap the tower locks onto the Miner instead; the two also attack from the front and back at once. Ice Golem, Elixir Golem and Knight can tank similarly but with less guaranteed chip.
- Goblin Barrel synergy: used simultaneously, one spell cannot cover both sets of spawns, and the Skeleton Barrel can tank a few shots for the Goblins.
- Ice Spirit or Zap alongside it turns the death damage lethal — with Ice Spirit it kills Goblins and Minions, with Zap it kills Archers, Firecracker and Dart Goblin, protecting cards like Hog Rider, Battle Ram and Lava Hound.
- Clone is especially strong on it: the displaced clone makes both Skeleton groups nearly impossible to hit with one spell, and it nudges the original barrel forward to connect sooner and bypass some building placements.
- A heavier version: Skeleton Barrel plus Mirror, then Clone and Rage the Skeletons at the tower; splash cards still stop it.
- Balloon push: tap the Balloon while hovering the Skeleton Barrel and double tap to deploy, so the barrel's melee range and Fast speed shove the Balloon toward the tower; knockback spells spent on the pair leave nothing for the Skeletons.
- If the Balloon is only next in the queue, deploy the barrel further back and drop the Balloon in front of it near the bridge; the combo is weaker at the Arena edges, where the barrel drifts past the Balloon.
- Balloon and Skeleton Barrel share counters, so one can bait for the other in the opposite lane — a building or Tornado brings them back together.
- The counter to that push is a defensive building (e.g. Tesla) to pull both, then spells on the Skeletons as they land over the building.
- Electro Giant + Skeleton Barrel is a risky but strong pairing: the Giant tanks and clears swarms while the barrel reaches the tower.
