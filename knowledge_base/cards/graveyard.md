# Graveyard

> *In-game description:* "Surprise! It's a party. A Skeleton party, anywhere in the Arena. Yay!"

## Overview

The Graveyard is a Legendary card that is unlocked from the Spooky Town (Arena 12). It is spell with a large radius. After 2.2 seconds, it spawns a single Skeleton that appears to be rising from a tombstone with the letters "XX" on it every 0.5 seconds for 9 seconds on the edge of the spell's radius. A Graveyard card costs 5 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
This portable 5 Elixir ritual starts a Skeleton party anywhere in the Arena! With its timed release of an entire Skeleton Army, the Graveyard can easily overrun towers if not countered with small units or area damage, and can also act as a last-second distraction later in the game.
