# Graveyard

> *In-game description:* "Surprise! It's a party. A Skeleton party, anywhere in the Arena. Yay!"

## Overview

The Graveyard is a Legendary card that is unlocked from the Spooky Town (Arena 12). It is spell with a large radius. After 2.2 seconds, it spawns a single Skeleton that appears to be rising from a tombstone with the letters "XX" on it every 0.5 seconds for 9 seconds on the edge of the spell's radius. A Graveyard card costs 5 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
This portable 5 Elixir ritual starts a Skeleton party anywhere in the Arena! With its timed release of an entire Skeleton Army, the Graveyard can easily overrun towers if not countered with small units or area damage, and can also act as a last-second distraction later in the game.

## Strong against

- Cannoneer — his slow attack speed is badly exploited by the stream of Skeletons; a Graveyard + tank push is very effective against him.
- Spawner buildings such as Barbarian Hut and Elixir Collector — the volume and spawn pattern of Skeletons destroys them before the opponent can defend (avoid if the building is near the King's Tower).
- Buildings generally, especially Inferno Tower — its slow retargeting is overwhelmed by the staggered Skeletons, particularly when paired with a building-targeting troop like Giant.
- Single-target and slow-attacking units on defense — the staggered spawn stalls them, which is why it pairs with Inferno Tower against big pushes.
- Crown Towers under a tank — with a Giant or Golem tanking, the Skeletons chew through the Tower.
- Sudden Death / racing situations — good at stalling enemy troops while both players race for a Tower.
- Frail ranged defenders — Tornado can drag them into the Skeletons, or weaken them enough for a spell to finish.
- Dagger Duchess — forces her to spend all 8 daggers on Skeletons rather than the tank.

## Weak against

- Area-damage troops — Valkyrie (hits Skeletons behind her, too tanky to spell), Wizard, Bowler, Bomber, Goblin Demolisher, Ice Wizard, Executioner, and especially Evolved Valkyrie whose tornado also pulls in out-of-range Skeletons.
- Cheap fast swarms — Goblins, Bats and Minions; Bats and Minions can also avoid being attacked at all.
- Guards — fast enough to clear Skeletons, longer range so they do not clump, and their shields resist every spell except Poison.
- Archers and Spear Goblins — range lets them defend while staying out of prediction-spell range.
- Skeleton Army and Goblin Gang — cover a wide area and kill new Skeletons instantly for a 2+ Elixir advantage (but die to Zap, Giant Snowball, Arrows or Poison).
- Barbarians, Rascals and Minion Horde — work for a neutral Elixir trade.
- Skeleton Dragons — area damage kills several Skeletons per attack without being overwhelmed.
- Witch and Night Witch — spawned units distract while the Witch splashes and the Bats attack.
- Golden Knight, Lumberjack, Berserker, Monk and Dart Goblin — fast attack speed keeps pace with the Tower Princess and they survive most spells.
- Mother Witch — one-shots each Skeleton and turns the whole Graveyard into a huge counterpush for a positive Elixir trade.
- Magic Archer — rapid shots hit multiple Skeletons at once (but he is very Poison-vulnerable).
- Poison — the most reliable spell counter; its radius covers most of the Graveyard and each pulse one-shots Skeletons.
- Goblin Curse — the single best spell counter, a +3 Elixir trade that negates nearly all damage and creates a large Goblin counterpush.
- Electro Spirit (up to 9 Skeletons for 1 Elixir), Evolved Electro Dragon, Evolved Tesla (one pulse clears a big accumulation) and Barbarian Barrel.
- An activated King's Tower — its combined targeting with the Crown Tower kills far more Skeletons and severely weakens the push.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 5 Elixir win-condition spell placeable anywhere on the map; 2.2 second delay before the first Skeleton, then one Skeleton every 0.5 sec for 9 seconds.
- Core push: a high-hitpoint tank (Giant, Golem) at the Crown Tower plus the Graveyard cast on the Tower — the tank absorbs Tower damage while the Skeletons kill it.
- Bulky ranged tanks — Bowler, Executioner and Baby Dragon are great Graveyard tanks because their range keeps them away from the Skeletons and splits the opponent's defense.
- Barbarian Barrel is a cheap tank: cast the Graveyard first, then Barrel at the bridge so the Barbarian spawns as the Skeletons begin.
- Miner synergy: the Miner can be placed to tank instantly for the Skeletons; the combo still loses to splash + swarm defenses.
- Tornado synergy: pulls defenders away from the Skeletons, or pulls them into your own splash units for extra value — but it is a lot of Elixir.
- Placement: never centre the spell on the Crown Tower or a Skeleton may spawn near the King's Tower and activate it. Place on the side of the Crown Tower furthest from the King's Tower, optimally 1 tile staggered towards the Arena edge.
- Do not pair with Zap — the stun makes the Tower retarget onto the Skeletons and breaks the tank-Graveyard synergy. Electro Giant is a poor tank for the same reason (his Zap Pack retargets him).
- Do pair with troops whose normal attack stuns — Electro Wizard, Electro Dragon, Goblinstein, Zappies — since messing with the Crown Tower's attack speed lets more Skeletons accumulate. Ice Wizard does the same by slowing it, but that is an 8 Elixir push, so only after he has defended.
- Freeze can bypass hard counters like Valkyrie, Skeleton Dragons or Baby Dragon and can briefly disable an activated King's Tower, but the opponent keeps a healthy troop for the counterpush — use it with an Elixir lead or in Overtime. Poison beats the combo for a +5 Elixir trade, and playing Graveyard without a tank telegraphs the Freeze.
- Poison also pairs well with Graveyard, clearing small counters and enemy Minion placements, though not high-health troops like Valkyrie.
- Bait the opponent's Poison with cards like Goblin Hut or Ice Wizard; spell-vulnerable cards like Princess indirectly support Graveyard because the opponent can only spell one of them.
- Avoid King-Tower-activating cards in the deck (Electro Spirit, Evolved Bomber, Magic Archer, Bowler, Firecracker) — an active King's Tower is an enormous problem.
- Healing cards (Heal Spirit, Battle Healer) are wasted on Skeletons that are one-shot by most cards, so do not run them in Graveyard decks.
- Versus Goblin Barrel: Graveyard spawns more troops and near-guarantees damage through unpredictability, but the Skeletons are delayed, individually frail, and the card is harder to obtain and level.
- On defense the spell must be cast preemptively because of the 2.2 second delay before the first Skeleton.
