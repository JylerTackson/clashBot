# Spirit Empress

> *In-game description:* "Behold the Spirit Empress, the First of Her Name, Protector of Spirits, Rider of Dragons. Summon her as a hard-hitting ground troop for 3 Elixir, or atop her majestic spirit dragon for 6 Elixir."

## Overview

The Spirit Empress is a Legendary card that is unlocked from the Miner's Mine (Arena 15). It spawns a single-target, air-targeting, medium-ranged, air troop with high hitpoints and high damage, or a single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troop with moderate hitpoints and high damage, depending on its Elixir cost. She appears as a woman who bears light blonde eyebrows and hair with two buns tied with team-colored knots, has heterochromic eyes, one golden and one blue, wears golden wristbands, an armor similar to the Battle Healer's, a team-colored cape and boots, and wields a scimitar with a light blue glow and a blue handle. She also appears to ride a blue dragon that looks similar to an Electro Dragon, except that it is paler and has glowing eyes. A Spirit Empress card costs 6 Elixir to deploy if the player has 6 or more Elixir stored, and 3 Elixir to deploy otherwise.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
The Spirit Empress comes into the arena obeying your desires. If the player has between 3 and 6 Elixir, then she will come by foot; dealing good damage at close range while walking quickly. But if the player has 6 or more elixir, then she will come on her flying dragon; attacking a bit slower but at longer range. This makes her great at offense, as well as at cheap emergency defense.

## Strong against

- The vast majority of single troops — like the Mega Minion she deals high damage per attack in both forms, but with higher hitpoints in each.
- Fireball and Poison decks, since she survives either spell combined with any 2 Elixir spell.
- Lightning, which does not deal enough damage to one-shot her.
- Air pushes when deployed in her 6 Elixir flying form, thanks to 5 tile range and Air & Ground targeting.
- Ground pushes needing an emergency answer, where the 3 Elixir walking form gives Fast (90) movement and high melee damage cheaply.

## Weak against

- Swarms in general — she only targets one troop at a time.
- Ground form specifically: Goblins, Barbarians, Royal Recruits, Guards and Evolved Skeletons.
- Air form specifically: air-targeting swarms such as Spear Goblins, Minions, Bats and Archers.
- Rocket, which one-shots her in both forms and is a neutral Elixir trade against the flying form.

## Notes / synergies

- Dual-cost card: she costs 6 Elixir and arrives as a ranged air troop (5 range, 1.6 sec hit speed, Medium speed) if you have 6 or more Elixir stored, and 3 Elixir as a fast ground melee troop (Melee: Medium 1.2, 1.2 sec hit speed, Fast) otherwise.
- Play the cheap ground form for emergency defense and the air form for offense.
- Mostly resistant to spells because of her hitpoint pool in both forms.
- Mirror copies whichever form was played last — e.g. mirroring the 3 Elixir version costs 4 Elixir and gives the ground form one level higher, and the mirrored cost does not rise even if you cross the 6 Elixir threshold.
- Works best alongside big win conditions such as Golem, Giant, Royal Giant and Lava Hound.
