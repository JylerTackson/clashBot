# Freeze

> *In-game description:* "Freezes and damages enemy troops and buildings, making them unable to move or attack. Everybody chill. Reduced damage to Crown Towers."

## Overview

The Freeze is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Frozen Peak (Arena 8). It is an area-damage, air-targeting spell with a medium radius and low damage. When used, it can stun enemies in its radius for 4 seconds. It appears as a cyan liquid bottled inside a transparent jar. A Freeze card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Freeze! This 4 Elixir spell incapacitates and slightly damages troops and buildings for 4 seconds. It can catch players off guard, helps your troops win combat unscathed, and can often clutch a victory. You can also use it as a last second defense by freezing enemy troops that are just about to reach your tower. But beware, as it buys time for your opponent to create an even bigger push by amassing new troops on the frozen ones, which will make defending even harder.

## Strong against

- Skeletons, Bats, Suspicious Bushes and Decoy Goblins — Freeze's low area damage alone is enough to fully defeat them.
- Goblins, Spear Goblins, Minions, Rascal Girls, Princesses, Dart Goblins, Archers and Firecrackers — killed when Freeze is paired with another cheap spell such as Zap.
- Inferno Tower, Inferno Dragon, Mighty Miner and Little Prince — the 4 second stun resets their damage/attack-speed ramp-up and disables them.
- Charge and dash cards (Bandit, Prince, Dark Prince, Battle Ram, Evolved Royal Recruits, Boss Bandit, Ram Rider), Sparky's attack charge-up, Mega Knight's jump and Fisherman's grab — all are reset once the Freeze wears off.
- Heavy pushes about to connect — P.E.K.K.A., Prince, Giant Skeleton, Golem, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Mighty Miner, Inferno Dragon, Hog Rider or Elite Barbarians can be stalled defensively before they reach a Tower.
- Electro Giant — a frozen Electro Giant does not deflect damage back, letting defenders (or attackers pushing past him) beat him more easily.
- Archer Queen — Freeze lasts longer than her ability, so it neutralises the invisibility entirely.
- Tesla — uniquely among spells, Freeze affects it even while underground, letting Battle Ram, Wall Breakers, Dart Goblin or Magic Archer bypass or attack it.
- Death-spawn cards (Elixir Golem, Tombstone, Lava Hound, Golem) — units spawned from a unit killed while frozen stay frozen for the remaining duration.
- Ground splashers when combined with Skeleton Army (Valkyrie, Mega Knight, Dark Prince, Skeleton King) or with Valkyrie against clumped ranged support.

## Weak against

- Evolved Bats — they survive a Freeze even without healing, making the spell a -2 Elixir trade against them.
- Poison — a 4 Elixir Poison shuts down the Graveyard + Freeze combo, so the Poison must be baited out first.
- Spread-out defences — troops and buildings placed apart cannot all be caught in the 3 tile radius; a weak counter can also be used to bait the Freeze out.
- Ranged troops placed on the bridge or one tile from it — too far from the Crown Tower for one Freeze to cover both.
- A building placed directly at the river — far enough from the Crown Tower that Freeze cannot hit both, though such placements are snipeable by long-ranged support.
- Staggered death-spawns — Goblin Giant's Spear Goblins and Battle Ram's Barbarians deploy on a delay, so they are not caught by the Freeze.
- Opponents who read the spell — Freeze depends on surprise, so predictable pairings get defended accordingly.
- A full Elixir Collector — it still generates its stored Elixir while frozen, so freezing it wastes the play.
- The Safe in Heist — it cannot attack, so freezing it accomplishes nothing.
- Its own 1 second deploy delay — a mistimed cast wastes 4 Elixir and leaves the player open to a counterattack.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: a 4 Elixir offensive-support / clutch-defence spell that trades damage for a 4 second disable; identical duration at every level.
- Time it to land just before your units would die — an unrevealed Freeze can turn the opponent's clean defence into massive damage.
- Its effect works like Zap or Lightning: affected units pause their attack animation and resume it, rather than restarting it as with The Log.
- Balloon + Freeze: cast as the Balloon approaches, disabling the Tower and its defenders. Conversely, Freeze plus air-targeting damage (Mega Minion, Hunter, Wizard, Executioner, Phoenix, Musketeer) kills an enemy Balloon.
- Graveyard + Freeze works like a Graveyard push without needing a tank, and clears Skeleton Army or Bats defending it; adding a tank keeps the surprise factor.
- Hog Rider + Freeze punishes swarm-reliant opponents — Skeletons and Bats die outright, Spear Goblins/Goblins/Minions are left low enough for a small spell. Against non-swarm counters it invites a big counterpush, so use it with backup or when closing out a match.
- Ram Rider + Freeze plays the same way for 9 Elixir but is safer, since her snare weakens the counterattack.
- Inferno Dragon + Freeze is a strong pairing: the stun lets the beam charge up safely and can play around Electro Wizard and Electro Dragon.
- Evolved Goblin Giant + Freeze gives the Spear Goblins extra attack time and lets more Goblins spawn, overwhelming single-target defenders like Prince, Mighty Miner, P.E.K.K.A., Monk, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Boss Bandit and Lumberjack.
- Freeze + Valkyrie: freeze the support troops behind a tank (Musketeer, Magic Archer, Wizard, Ice Wizard, Electro Wizard, Hunter, Witch, Princess, Archers, Firecracker, Spear Goblins, Bomber) and let the Valkyrie wipe them out.
- Freeze + Skeleton Army (1 Elixir cheaper) shreds ground splashers but is fragile to spells; Freeze + Barbarians is the same idea for 2 more Elixir, still weak to Fireball.
- The radius is big enough to cover a Crown Tower and the King's Tower at once — cast between them after taking the first Tower to disable both.
- After losing a Crown Tower, defend away from the King Tower so the opponent must choose between freezing your defender or the King Tower.
- Avoid casting near the enemy King's Tower: the damage can activate it, and the activation animation continues while frozen, so it attacks immediately on thaw. Freeze can still temporarily mitigate an already-activated King Tower.
- A frozen troop that is Cloned produces an unfrozen clone, which can catch an opponent off guard.
- Building lifetimers keep counting down while frozen, so freezing an Elixir Collector or spawner is usually only worth it alongside other pressure.
- Do not pair Freeze with your own Electro Giant — frozen enemies cannot attack him and so never trigger his Zap Pack (except units outside its range).
- Can pin a troop in place so piercing attackers (Magic Archer, Firecracker, Executioner, Evolved Bomber, Bowler) hit it and the Tower together, though Tornado does this more safely.
- Strong stalling tool in Sudden Death when both players are racing for a Tower, and very strong in the Rage sub-mode of Global Spell.
- Lightning is the alternative if pure damage is preferred — more damage plus a stun and better against Inferno Tower, but 6 Elixir instead of 4.
- Levelling is low priority since duration is level-independent; at equal levels it only one-shots Bats and Skeletons, though level 13 is worth reaching to one-shot Mirrored level 16 Skeletons and Bats.
