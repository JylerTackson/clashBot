# Tornado

> *In-game description:* "Drags enemy troops to its center while dealing damage over time, just like a magnet. A big, swirling, Tornado-y magnet."

## Overview

The Tornado is an Epic card that is unlocked from Miner's Mine (Arena 15). It is an area-damage, air-targeting spell with a very large radius and low damage that is dealt every 0.55 seconds for 1.05 seconds. It also has a singularity effect that pulls nearby troops to the center. A Tornado card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
The weather forecast signals a big tornado coming after you! This windy Tornado pulls any troops in its large effective radius, quickly grouping them all up in an instant (perfect for any incoming splash damage) while also slightly damaging them for only 3 Elixir! When used correctly, this spell can be invaluable. Also useful for dragging that pesky Hog Rider to activate your King Tower.

## Strong against

- Skeletons, Bats, Suspicious Bushes and Decoy Goblins — its low damage alone fully defeats them.
- Goblins, Spear Goblins, Minions, Rascal Girls, Princesses and Dart Goblins — killed when combined with another cheap spell such as Zap or Giant Snowball, useful for clearing spread-out defending swarms.
- Clone — the very large radius wipes clones outright since they all have 1 hitpoint (a cloned Skeleton Barrel's delayed Skeletons still need answering).
- Miner, Goblin Barrel and Graveyard chip attacks — pulling them forces a King's Tower activation and lets all three Towers punish future pushes.
- Slow and stationary troops — pull strength scales inversely with speed and with unit mass, so Musketeers and idle units are dragged much further than a Hog Rider.
- Light units (Hog Rider, Lumberjack, Mini P.E.K.K.A.) — displaced far more than heavy ones like Valkyrie, Giant or Golem.
- Princess — can be pulled into Crown Tower range for a neutral Elixir trade, though she still gets 1-2 shots off.
- Giant Skeleton — can be dragged away from your Crown Tower at the last second so its death bomb deals no Tower damage.
- Clumped defences generally, when paired with splash (Sparky, Executioner, Wizard, Ice Wizard) or with Rocket.
- Cannon Cart — pulling it away from its target can leave the unshielded form out of range of whatever outranges it.

## Weak against

- Raged troops — Rage makes units resist the pull, so avoid Tornado against them and in the Rage sub-mode of Global Spell.
- Buildings — like knockback it cannot displace them, and its damage is too low to be worth using on them (Zap, Arrows or Earthquake are better for finishing a low Crown Tower).
- Charging cards — Prince, Dark Prince, Battle Ram, Ram Rider, Evolved Royal Recruits, Mighty Miner and Inferno Dragon do not have charges reset by the pull, and resist it far more while charging; Sparky's and Evolved Inferno Dragon's stockpiles also survive.
- Ranged troops when dragged away — they keep attacking anything in range, so pulling them backwards achieves little; pull them closer instead.
- Stun troops such as Electro Wizard and Electro Dragon — if they hit units mid-pull, the stagger shortens or cancels the pull entirely.
- Bandit and Mega Knight — pulling them too far can accidentally trigger their charge attacks.
- Enemy Tornado — an opposing Tornado can drag a troop back out of your King's Tower activation setup.
- Its own 1.05 second duration — a misplacement wastes 3 Elixir and can let heavy damage through your own Crown Towers.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: a 3 Elixir area-damage spell with a very large radius and a singularity pull, dealing low damage every 0.55 seconds for 1.05 seconds.
- Units in the Tornado can still attack anything in range, so the pull is about repositioning rather than disabling.
- Splash pairings are the core use — Tornado + Sparky lets her one-shot a clump; Ice Wizard slows the grouped units; Executioner and Wizard wipe hordes in a P.E.K.K.A. Tornado deck.
- Use it to drag defenders away from your win condition, preserving glass cannons — especially good with Graveyard, pulling a Valkyrie off the Skeletons.
- Pull ranged troops toward your heavy hitters to nullify their range, and use a second unit as a distraction (e.g. pulling a Mini P.E.K.K.A. off your Giant and onto your Knight).
- Pull troops in line with the Crown Tower so piercing splash (Magic Archer, Firecracker, Evolved Bomber, Bowler, Executioner) hits both the troops and the Tower.
- Combine with chain attackers (Electro Dragon, Electro Spirit, Golden Knight), pulling in at most as many troops as the chain can hit.
- Pull enemies onto a Giant Skeleton's or Balloon's dropped bomb to finish them, or pull them backwards so the bomb or a P.E.K.K.A. hit lands on the Tower instead.
- Tornado + Poison holds troops inside the Poison longer and adds damage, killing survivors like Witch, Mega Minion, Hunter or Wizard.
- Tornado + Rocket groups scattered support (Witch, Musketeer, Wizard) into one spot; cast the Rocket slightly before the Tornado so they are still clumped on impact.
- Electro Giant synergy: pull ranged attackers (Inferno Dragon, Cannon Cart, Musketeer, Sparky, Flying Machine, Dart Goblin) into his damaging radius; Evolved Royal Giant works similarly.
- Evolved Archers: the pull can either deactivate their Power Shot by dragging targets close, or enable it by pushing targets far enough away.
- Siege trick: pre-emptively pull the distracting unit out of an X-Bow or Mortar's range (ideally backwards and into the other lane) so the building locks onto the Crown Tower; siege buildings are unaffected by the resulting King activation.
- Tornado can also pull units into a Mortar's blind spot to force a retarget onto the Crown Tower, but only when another card is protecting the Mortar.
- Can pull a building-targeting troop toward a misplaced building, and can redirect a split-lane threat (e.g. pulling a Bandit into your P.E.K.K.A.).
- King's Tower activation: place it on the tile directly in front of the King's Tower and two tiles into the defending lane for most melee troops; afterwards pull troops to the centre so all three Towers hit them, using cheap Skeletons to hold them there.
- An Ice Golem is sometimes needed alongside the Tornado to force an activation (e.g. angling a Magic Archer's pierce into the King's Tower).
- Activation techniques depend on your Tower troop — Tower Princess and Royal Chef manage the Wall Breakers pull reliably, Dagger Duchess kills them too fast.
- Do not repeatedly pull heavy hitters (Golem, Balloon, Elite Barbarians, Prince, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Inferno Dragon, Mighty Miner, P.E.K.K.A.) to the King's Tower — they survive all three Towers and can set up a back-door three-crown loss.
- Activating the King's Tower is not always worth the Tower damage: against a Royal Giant, for instance, he never enters King's Tower range.
- Drag rather than tap when deploying so the exact area of effect is visible, or tap and hold before releasing, to avoid misplacement.
