# Electro Dragon

> *In-game description:* "Spits out bolts of electricity hitting up to three targets. Suffers from middle child syndrome to boot."

**Related:** [Evolution: electro-dragon-evolution](../evolutions/electro-dragon-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Electro Dragon is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Spooky Town (Arena 12). It spawns a single-target, air-targeting, short-ranged, air troop with moderate hitpoints and moderate damage. The Electro Dragon launches an attack that hits its target, which will arc and strike up to 2 other targets within 4 tiles of each other. It will stun affected troops for 0.5 seconds. The Electro Dragon resembles a small blue dragon with blue eyes, gray spots and glowing spikes on its back, purple tongue, and team-colored wings and underbelly. An Electro Dragon card costs 5 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
How shocking! This dragon attacks slowly and does low damage per hit for 5 Elixir, but there's more. His attacks will chain on to two secondary targets, gaining value with each unit hit. As if that wasn't enough, he stuns on hit, making him a powerful disruptor. However, alone, he's weak to tanks, glass cannons, and large swarms alike, and when he approaches a Princess Tower, a clever opponent may sacrifice a troop just to get that neat King's Tower activation.

For 1 Cycle, if his **Evolution** is active, his attack will chain through UNLIMITED troops thanks to the **Infini-Shock** skill, dealing with any kind of swarm with ease. His bolts can even bounce between 2 different units, wrecking up massive pushes. However, the chain will shockingly move slower, deal much less damage per hit, and not stun after the first 3 targets. And luckily, your Crown Towers are safe from those bounces (not from the initial 3, however).

## Strong against

- Witch and Night Witch — the chain attack damages the spawner while defeating their Skeletons or Bats, and the stun delays further spawning.
- Charge units that cannot hit air — Prince, Battle Ram, Mighty Miner, Dark Prince, Ram Rider, Bandit and Sparky are all reset by the 0.5 second stun.
- Double Prince pushes — the Princes cannot target it, so paired with a ground mini tank it stops the push with nearly all its hitpoints intact.
- Zappies — stun effects do not reset the Electro Dragon's attack, so it destroys them with plenty of health left for a counterpush.
- Balloon — stops it on its own, preventing everything but the death bomb; add an Ice Spirit or Electro Spirit to stop the death bomb too.
- Inferno Tower, Inferno Dragon and Mighty Miner — the repeated stun resets their charge, rendering them useless much like an Electro Wizard.
- Elixir Golem and Lava Hound — the chain hits their death-spawn units as well, increasing its value (this does not work on Skeleton Barrel, whose Skeletons spawn after a slight delay).
- Goblin Barrel — the attack chains onto each Goblin, though they still get a few stabs in, so it should not be the only counter.
- Support troops hiding behind a tank — e.g. Archers behind a Giant, or Rascals, since the chain reaches past the tank.
- X-Bow and Mortar pushes, combined with a mini tank — the Electro Dragon forces the building to retarget while the chain hits defenders next to it.
- Three Musketeers — with a mini tank it stops two of them (or all three with a Tornado to bunch them up); on defense, Electro Dragon plus Skeletons or an Ice Spirit stops two of the split Musketeers.
- Spawner buildings placed in the center — an Electro Dragon on the river snipes the Goblin Hut along with its Spear Goblins, and chips troops from other spawners.

## Weak against

- Lightning — one-shots an Electro Dragon up to 1 Level higher.
- Rocket — the Electro Dragon moves slower than other dragons, making it easier to hit, and its higher cost makes the trade less negative for the Rocket player.
- Void — a lone Electro Dragon dies within all 3 hits for a positive Elixir trade.
- Single-troop cards such as Musketeer, Electro Wizard, Wizard and Mega Minion — with nothing to chain onto, the Electro Dragon only deals one third of its potential firepower.
- Ice Golem — the cheapest check; it tanks the hits while the Crown Tower shoots the Dragon down, a +3 Elixir trade with a half-health Ice Golem left over.
- Goblin Giant — tanks while the Spear Goblins, which the chain cannot reach, chew through it.
- Minions and Bats — weak to the chain but, placed close and surrounding it, they are just enough to stop it before it connects to the Crown Tower.
- Large swarms like Skeleton Army — the chain hits only three individual units, so a swarm placed in front absorbs it and shields the real target.
- Tanks and glass cannons in general — it is fragile for 5 Elixir with slow attack and move speed, so it loses one-on-one.
- Ground-attacking troops used as bait — a poorly placed Electro Dragon can be kited into the other lane while the Crown Towers shoot it down.
- A sacrificial unit placed between the Crown Tower and King's Tower — the chain bounces to it and then to the King's Tower, activating it.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 5 Elixir defensive support and disruptor — best used as a helper on defense behind other troops, or behind a tank on offense, since it is fragile with slow attack and movement.
- The attack chains from the target to a maximum of 2 other units, each within 4 tiles of the previous one, and stuns each for 0.5 seconds.
- This is chain lightning, not splash: exactly three individual troops are hit, and unspent charges are not stacked onto the original target (unlike the Electro Wizard), so a lone target takes only one third of the damage.
- King Tower activation risk: a lone Electro Dragon attacking a Crown Tower can chain through a unit placed between the Towers into the King's Tower — avoid running it in Graveyard decks for this reason.
- Buildings make the best activation relay (large, close to both Towers, and cannot walk out of chain range); a Princess placed 3 tiles beside the Crown Tower and 1 tile in front of the King Tower also works but dies doing it. Cannoneer and Dagger Duchess can do it too, with more precision required.
- To bait a Lightning off it, run other Lightning-vulnerable cards (Witch, Night Witch, Mega Minion, Hunter, Wizard), place the Electro Dragon away from other units, or place it near a Crown Tower and follow with a higher-hitpoint card like Ice Golem or Miner to absorb the remaining strikes.
- Very strong in Golem and Giant decks — it delays and defeats the support troops and tank killers sent to answer the tank.
- Pairs well with the Miner: the Electro Dragon connects first, then the Miner goes in and the Dragon's stun makes the Tower retarget to the Miner. Counter that push by distracting the Electro Dragon with a single troop away from the Tower and the Miner with another card.
- Tornado synergy: pulling troops together lets the chain reach them. On offense, drag at most 2 troops in so the third bounce lands on the Crown Tower.
- Place it toward the edges of the Arena so opposing ground troops are further away and cannot kite it into the other lane.
- Against an enemy Electro Dragon, a chain-blocking swarm in front of your key unit limits the damage to three bodies.
- Taking a hit with Skeletons or Spear Goblins first minimizes Crown Tower damage while setting up chain tricks.
