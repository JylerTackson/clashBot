# Monk

> *In-game description:* "Monk has spent many isolated years perfecting a new style of combat. He fires off a 3-hit combo, with the last blow dealing extra Damage and pushing enemies back!"

## Overview

The Monk is a Champion card that is unlocked from the Silent Sanctuary (Arena 18). It spawns a single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troop with high hitpoints and moderate damage. The Monk uses a 3-hit combo: the first 2 attacks deal normal damage, while the 3rd strike deals extra damage and knockback, even if the targeted troop is normally immune to knockback. Monk's ability reflects projectiles back to their source, as well as granting himself a large damage reduction for a short time. He appears similar to the Hog Rider, except that he is tan, and his mohawk, beard and eyebrows are white, he has a long horseshoe mustache, and wears a mala on his wrists, a cincture, and a team-colored kilt. A Monk card costs 5 Elixir to deploy and his ability costs an additional 1 Elixir to activate.

Make the opponents wish they've never fought in the first place with this peacemaking powerhouse! With 5 Elixir, a tank is spawned, attacking through foes through melee attacks, with the third one dealing triple the damage of said attack AND pushing the victim a few tiles back.

It can deflect as long as it is travelling in the air with velocity not too high, meaning it cannot deflect stuff like Inferno Beams, stunning electricity, or a sword.

## Abilities and special mechanics

### Ability: Pensive Protection

> "Takes less Damage while deflecting projectiles back at the opponent!"

The Monk has a special ability, accessible from the rightmost side of the screen just above the player's card slots in battle. After a 1-second delay, the Monk activates his “Pensive Protection”, reducing all incoming damage he takes by 65% and reflect all incoming projectile-based ranged attacks back to the said offender. Spells are always reflected to the closest opposing Crown Tower. He cannot protect nearby allies from melee attacks, non-projectile ranged attacks and non-projectile spells. The Monk is impervious to all forms of knockback and the Tornado's pull during Pensive Protection's duration. The ability goes on a 17-second cooldown after the duration ends. The Pensive Protection ability costs 1 Elixir to activate.

## Strong against

- Almost every ranged unit — Pensive Protection reflects projectile attacks back at the sender, making him a hard counter to them.
- Archer Queen — activating the ability against her turns her boosted attack speed against her for a quick demise.
- Hunter — a point-blank shot is fully reflected and instantly kills him regardless of level (he takes nothing if he shoots from a distance, as the projectiles despawn).
- Piercing-projectile troops (Bowler, Executioner, Firecracker, Evolved Bomber, Magic Archer) — reflected for full damage, and the Bowler's knockback is turned on his own troops.
- Executioner specifically — his axe is returned immediately, so he re-throws it faster and takes heavy damage or dies.
- Royal Giant — even though he cannot target the Monk, his projectiles can be reflected back for major self-damage if the Monk sits close to their path.
- Mighty Miner — the third-hit knockback resets his ramp-up every combo while dealing triple damage.
- Ram Rider — her snare is reflected and slows her; combined with the combo knockback he counters her (play him before she covers half the distance to your Tower).
- Ice Wizard — slowed by his own reflected attack, along with any unit next to him.
- Fisherman — his hook is fully stifled for the ability's duration, forcing him to recharge repeatedly.
- Cannon Cart — the Monk and his ability force it into its building phase for a slight negative trade.
- X-Bow and Mortar — the X-Bow absorbs heavy reflected damage and loses most of its synergies; the Mortar is fully defended, its shots either reflected or soaked by his hitpoints.
- Sparky — placed at the bridge against a Sparky behind the King Tower he can reflect her shot onto the Tower for massive damage and kill her (level-dependent).
- Slow, low-damage ground troops in general — the combo's third hit knocks back all ground troops, even ones normally immune to knockback.

## Weak against

- Inferno Dragon and Inferno Tower — their beams are not projectiles, so nothing is reflected and using the ability against them is near-useless; both counter him for a neutral or positive trade.
- Instant-attack electric cards — Tesla, Zappies, Electro Dragon and Electro Wizard take no reflected damage.
- Strong melee troops — Mini P.E.K.K.A., Prince, P.E.K.K.A., Elite Barbarians and Evolved Mega Knight simply out-damage him.
- Bats and Phoenix — they do not attack with projectiles, and since he cannot hit air they defeat him unharmed.
- Spirit cards — their attacks cannot be reflected; Heal Spirit in particular helps other cards fight him.
- Poison — still counters fragile ranged units deployed near a Monk, since it is not reflected.
- Spells and attacks he cannot deflect at all: melee attacks, non-projectile ranged attacks, Inferno beams, stunning electricity, and non-projectile spells.
- Weak spells that get through his shield when protecting allies: Freeze, Zap, Earthquake, Tornado and Royal Delivery; Lightning and Poison still kill moderate-health support behind him.
- Baiting his ability then activating your King Tower — lure him to the center, and when he cloaks use The Log, Magic Archer or Firecracker to activate it.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: high-hitpoint melee tank / anti-ranged defensive Champion; count him plus the ability as 6 Elixir (5 to deploy, 1 for Pensive Protection).
- His attack is a 3-hit combo: the first two are normal, the third deals extra damage plus knockback that works even on knockback-immune troops.
- Killing a troop does not reset the combo, so the third strike still lands on the next target (e.g. against Rascals it finishes the second Rascal Girl or heavily damages the Rascal Boy).
- Pensive Protection: 1 second delay, then 65% damage reduction and reflection of all incoming projectile attacks for the duration; 17 second cooldown, 1 Elixir.
- During the ability he is immune to all knockback and to Tornado's pull.
- Spells are always reflected to the closest opposing Crown Tower — including Rocket and Goblin Barrel if he stands near a Crown Tower.
- Reflected projectiles that splash or do not seek a target deal only 25% damage to Crown Towers, while the original troop takes full damage; deflected Crown Tower shots are likewise reduced to 25%, so pair the ability with real damage dealers when attacking.
- Reflected projectiles keep the damage of their original source and do not scale to the Monk's level.
- Use him proactively rather than reactively against spells — reacting to a Rocket leaves a very small deployment and activation window.
- He works as a spell shield for glass cannons: with a Firecracker he denies every weak spell except Freeze, Zap, Earthquake, Tornado and Royal Delivery, and he protects Electro Wizard, Zappies, Magic Archer, Skeleton Dragons, Mother Witch, Ice Wizard or Musketeer from everything but Lightning and Poison.
- Standing near an Elixir Collector, Sparky, Executioner or your Crown Tower he denies Rocket cycling.
- Arrows are only fully reflected if he is within 2 tiles of the exact center of the cast, otherwise some arrows still hit the troops around him.
- Reflected The Log and Barbarian Barrel turn friendly, keep full range and travel further than normal (the Barbarian Barrel even spawns a friendly Barbarian) — but anything standing in front of the Monk is not saved from them.
- A defeated Mother Witch turns herself into a friendly Cursed Hog when killed by the reflection.
- Firecracker projectiles do no damage to him at all, because the initial shot and its shrapnel are separate phases.
- Predicting a Magic Archer or Firecracker at the bridge and activating the ability makes them damage their own Crown Tower.
- A cloned Monk starts walking with its ability already activated.
- He can be set as a Boat Battles defender but cannot use his ability there, so he is a poor Clan Wars Defense Tower choice.
