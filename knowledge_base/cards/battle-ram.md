# Battle Ram

> *In-game description:* "Two Barbarians holding a big log charge at the nearest building, dealing significant damage if they connect; then they go to town with their swords!"

**Related:** [Evolution: battle-ram-evolution](../evolutions/battle-ram-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Battle Ram is a Rare card that is unlocked from Barbarian Bowl (Arena 3). It spawns a single-target, building-targeting, melee, ground troop with moderate hitpoints and high damage. After it travels 3 tiles, it will begin to charge towards its target, and deals double damage if it hits its target whilst charging. Once it reaches a building or it is destroyed, it will break and reveal the two Barbarians underneath. It appears to be a battering ram consisting of a large, thick, heavy log with an attached metal knob at the front and a chain at the back carried by two Barbarians underneath. A Battle Ram card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A fast but fragile 4 Elixir surprise to smash into buildings and disrupt the defense. Once it gets charging, it must be taken out before it rams into a tower, or the target will take double damage! Two Barbarians also pop out after the ram is destroyed or crashed, making this card reliable for both defense and offense.

For 2 Cycles, if its **Evolution** is active, it adds the ram some nice, shiny details and a scary ram head with massive horns appears on the front of the ram, granting the card a new mighty power: the **Ram-Squared!**. The Battle Ram will smash through all buildings without crashing and breaking a sweat, meaning that it can only be taken down if its hitpoints reach 0! Plus, it will damage all troops that dare to get nearby it, as well as push back weaklings (small and medium-sized troops) aside! The Barbarians will also be Evolved and show up for extra rage-filled madness and destruction!

## Strong against

- Undefended Crown Towers — by itself it does more Crown Tower damage than a Hog Rider or Ram Rider when ignored, and a charged hit lands double damage.
- Buildings — it is a building targeter that crashes into them, and the two Barbarians keep chipping afterwards.
- Single big-hit attackers like Sparky — as a last-resort distraction the Ram soaks the blast and then spawns 2 Barbarians to help defend.
- Mega Knight — takes 3 hits to break the Ram (2 if he jumps) plus another 2-3 for the Barbarians, buying 5-6 seconds to place high-damage mini tanks.
- Non-building-targeting melee units — can be kited to the other lane much like an Ice Golem (only before it starts charging).
- Lightning-bait support such as Musketeer, Fisherman, Electro Wizard, Wizard, Royal Hogs, Berserker, Barbarians, Royal Recruits, Electro Dragon, Ice Wizard, Hunter, Night Witch, Goblin Cage, Mega Minion, Bandit or Witch — the Ram out-healths them and can eat a strike for them.
- Freeze — a Ram destroyed while frozen still spawns unfrozen Barbarians.
- Low-DPS air defenders such as Baby Dragon or Skeleton Dragons — they cannot stop it connecting to the Tower Princess.

## Weak against

- Stun and knockback cards — Bowler, Evolved Executioner, Electro Wizard, Zappies, Goblinstein and Electro Dragon continually disrupt the charge and also clean up the spawned Barbarians while keeping most of their health for a counterpush.
- Ice Wizard — his constant slow delays the charge-up and cuts its speed once charging.
- High-DPS ground swarms — Skeleton Army and Goblin Gang block its path and shut it down before the Crown Tower.
- High-damage mini tanks — Mini P.E.K.K.A, Prince, Mighty Miner, Lumberjack, Monk, Sparky and Elite Barbarians break the ram phase quickly and still survive the Barbarians.
- Defensive buildings — every building except the Elixir Collector shuts it down; Tombstone and Cannon are positive Elixir trades and spawners like Goblin Hut rack up troop value.
- Air troops with enough DPS — Minions, Mega Minion and Bats stop it connecting, though the Barbarians will still land a few hits.
- Piercing splash — The Log, Barbarian Barrel, Bowler, Firecracker, Magic Archer, Evolved Bomber and Executioner carry their fatal hit through onto the spawned Barbarians.
- Lightning — because the Barbarians are separate entities, it can hit both the Ram and the Barbarians.
- Minion Horde — shuts down the popular Battle Ram + Dark Prince pairing, since the Dark Prince cannot hit air.

## Notes / synergies

- Offensive win-condition/pressure card at 4 Elixir: it begins charging after 3 tiles and a charged hit deals double damage, so it must be killed before it connects.
- Its sight range is only 5.5 tiles, shorter than most building targeters, so some defensive placements will not pull it — e.g. a Cannon 4 tiles from the bridge and 3 tiles from the defending Crown Tower — which limits how much of both lanes the opponent can cover.
- The ram works as a shield for the Barbarians: if it is destroyed in one blow the Barbarians take no damage, similar to a Dark Prince, Guard or Royal Recruit shield.
- It cannot fully block Vines, since only one unit exists initially — a waste of 4 Elixir.
- Pairs with Ice Golem: the Ice Golem's death damage slows melee defenders and finishes Skeletons or Bats, raising the chance the Ram connects; a Valkyrie instead handles bulkier swarms.
- The charge mechanic pushes heavier, slower troops toward the Crown Tower faster, and troops with at least Fast movement speed can push the Battle Ram before it starts charging.
- Pairs with Dark Prince — both charge, so they stay aligned, and the Dark Prince clears the swarms that would stop the Ram; bring Arrows or Evolved Zap for Minion Horde.
- Deploy it in the opposite lane as pressure, forcing the opponent to spend Elixir on defense.
- Versus Tombstone, time a cheap spell to defeat the Skeletons spawned on the Tombstone's death, forcing the opponent to spend another card on the Barbarians.
- Against Lightning, if the unit being protected has less health than the Barbarians (Flying Machine, Magic Archer, Mother Witch), the Ram plus its Barbarians can absorb all 3 strikes.
- Defensive kiting caveat: it starts charging after 3 tiles, so slow units such as P.E.K.K.A., Skeleton King, Valkyrie, Giant Skeleton, Mega Minion or Mighty Miner eventually lose sight and are not kited the whole way.
- Only a passable X-Bow distraction: there is a brief gap between the Ram breaking and the Barbarians spawning (like Skeleton Barrel) in which the X-Bow re-locks onto the Crown Tower, so it needs extra support.
- Usable defensively as a last-resort distraction against a high-damage troop when no other counter is in hand.
