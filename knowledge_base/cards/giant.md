# Giant

> *In-game description:* "Slow but durable, only attacks buildings. A real one-man wrecking crew!"

**Related:** [Hero variant: giant-hero](../heroes/giant-hero.md)

## Overview

The Giant is a Rare card that is unlocked in the Training Camp. It spawns a single-target, building-targeting, melee, and ground troop with very high hitpoints and high damage. The Giant takes the appearance of a colossal humanoid creature who has blue eyes, bushy orange eyebrows and mutton chops, and no hair on his head, wearing a large, brown coat made of cloth with a team-colored patch on its front and back, a team-colored belt, and dark brown pants. A Giant card costs 5 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
The "original" tank, the Giant is a 5 Elixir meat shield with high health and moderate melee damage. He ignores all enemy troops and slowly makes his way towards buildings, soaking up damage to protect any troops behind him. Once he gets there, he’ll start breaking it! A real one-man wrecking crew!

If his **Hero** is active, don't get near him, because for 2 Elixir, you can use his **Heroic Hurl** ability, which throws the highest-health troop near the Giant a long distance into the air, causing it to take small impact damage upon landing (which air troops are immune to). Best not to anger the Giant!
