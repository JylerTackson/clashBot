# X-Bow

> *In-game description:* "Nice tower you got there. Would be a shame if this X-Bow whittled it down from this side of the Arena..."

## Overview

The X-Bow is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Hog Mountain (Arena 10). It spawns a single-target, ground-targeting, very long-ranged, building with high hitpoints and very low damage. It appears to be a crossbow with wooden, stone, and metallic features along with a shining steel bolt for ammunition mounted on top of a base with a metal rim and team-colored plates. An X-Bow card costs 6 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
This 6 Elixir automatic crossbow has moderate hitpoints, low damage, a long range and a very fast hit speed. It can quickly whittle down small swarms and moderate-health troops, cripple tanks, and destroy towers. However, it can be very easily distracted by anything that’s not an air troop, and is easily destroyed by spells and heftier swarms.

## Strong against

- Small ground swarms — Goblins, Spear Goblins, Archers, Wall Breakers, Guards and Skeletons are shredded by its 0.3-second hit speed at 11.5 tiles.
- Moderate-health ground troops in general, whittled down long before they reach it thanks to its extreme range.
- Enemy Crown Towers — it outranges them, so an unanswered X-Bow near the river will single-handedly destroy a Tower.
- Tanks, which it cripples while they walk the long approach (though it needs support to actually finish them).
- Mortar in the same role — the X-Bow's much higher DPS and lifetime damage make it the more threatening siege option.
- Melee attackers forced to walk onto your side when it is placed one tile from the river, taking Crown Tower damage on the way in.

## Weak against

- Building-targeting troops — only another building distracts them, so Giant, Goblin Giant, Electro Giant, Golem, Elixir Golem, Hog Rider, Ram Rider and Battle Ram pummel it directly.
- Rocket — same 6 Elixir cost, massive damage to the X-Bow plus any nearby support troops.
- Lightning — nullifies the expensive X-Bow-plus-building setups.
- 6-tile ranged snipers — Musketeer, Firecracker, Flying Machine and Evolved Archers pick it off for a good trade if it is placed 3 tiles from the river.
- Air troops in general, since the X-Bow cannot target air at all — Balloon in particular 2-hit-kills an X-Bow at the bridge.
- Cheap high-DPS mini tanks — Knight, Lumberjack, Miner, Golden Knight, Valkyrie, Monk, Skeleton King, Mini P.E.K.K.A and Dark Prince are Elixir-efficient answers to a lone X-Bow.
- Ranged bruisers with reach and bulk — Cannon Cart, Bowler, Executioner, Sparky and especially Royal Giant, who targets buildings and keeps over half his health to counterpush.
- Heavy hitters — Mega Knight, Giant Skeleton, Mighty Miner, Prince and P.E.K.K.A. decimate the X-Bow and its support.
- Area-damage spells on defense, since the cheap low-hitpoint troops played beside a 6 Elixir X-Bow give big spell value.
- Zap or any stun, which forces a Tower-locked X-Bow to retarget the nearest entity and denies a lot of damage.
- Tornado, which pulls your distraction tank away and lets an enemy X-Bow lock onto the Tower.
- Splash troops dropped directly on top of it on the extra terrain after a Tower falls — a Valkyrie placed on the X-Bow both damages and tanks for itself.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 6 Elixir siege win condition and defensive building, 11.5-tile range, 30-second lifetime, ground-only, 3.5-second deploy time.
- Offensive placement: one tile from the river in front of the Crown Tower maximizes range and forces melee defenders onto your side; staggering one tile left or right is fine, more puts the Tower out of range.
- Defensive placement: behind or between your own Crown Towers, where it is protected but still reaches attackers.
- The 3.5-second deploy time is the defender's window — dropping a distraction at the last second is usually best, though occasionally playing it immediately (an early Inferno Tower, say) catches the X-Bow player off guard.
- Always tank for your X-Bow; cheap tanks like Ice Golem and Knight are the most versatile given the X-Bow's 6 Elixir cost. Frail support belongs behind or beside it, except deliberate distractions like Skeletons and Goblins.
- Rage on an X-Bow speeds its hit rate and buffs supporting troops, but the 8 Elixir commitment is easily countered by a tank for a positive trade.
- Building support: Inferno Tower handles tanks like the Giant; Tesla is cheaper, clears small swarms faster and is spell-immune unless attacking (Earthquake excepted); Bomb Tower is best against bulky swarms like Barbarians, Royal Hogs and Royal Recruits but attacks slowly. These combos cost 10-11 Elixir and are best saved for Double Elixir.
- Cover the swarm weakness with splash — Ice Wizard, Wizard, Executioner, Bomber, Magic Archer, Witch, Goblin Demolisher, Bowler, Baby Dragon, Princess, Firecracker — or spells like Arrows, Evolved Zap, Giant Snowball and The Log.
- The Log is a strong X-Bow spell: cheap, and its knockback buys the X-Bow more time; playing on the bridge means its "own side only" limitation never matters.
- Giant Snowball keeps the knockback and adds a slow; Evolved Giant Snowball can clear defenders entirely so the X-Bow connects to the Tower.
- Tornado can pre-emptively drag a far-back distraction unit backwards and ideally into the other lane so the X-Bow locks the Tower; the X-Bow is unaffected by the King's Tower activation this causes.
- Barbarian Barrel and Royal Delivery clear swarms and leave a Barbarian or Royal Recruit behind, though Royal Delivery cannot reach the X-Bow itself when defending.
- Do not pair it with Heal Spirit, Battle Healer, Royal Chef or Rune Giant — none of their effects apply to a building.
- Defending against an offensive X-Bow with a tank: (1) one tile in front of the targeted Tower is usually best and buys time to add ranged support; (2) at the bridge to pressure immediately, good when the X-Bow player is Elixir-down; (3) center of the arena slightly into the other lane, safe from support but only a temporary distraction. Placements 1 and 3 lose to Tornado.
- LavaLoon is a bad idea into X-Bow setups — the Balloon's shorter range puts it in front of the Lava Hound; clear the buildings first.
- When countering a bridge Balloon with the X-Bow player, note the Balloon takes no X-Bow damage, so add a mini tank like Ice Golem in range to absorb the Tower damage.
- Spending the Rocket on an X-Bow frees the opponent to play cards that thrive without it, such as an Elixir Collector.
- Facing X-Bow, take the first crown and play for 2-1; the extra terrain after a Tower falls lets you place troops directly on the X-Bow.
- If both players have lost opposite-lane Towers, the X-Bow player should attack the Tower opposite their own destroyed one from the middle, limiting placement spots. Attacking the King's Tower is not recommended — all four sides are exposed and it has too many hitpoints.
- A center-bridge X-Bow can be hit by a Magic Archer placed on the center of the bridge one tile from the river, which also lets the shot carry into the Crown Tower; against a staggered X-Bow the Magic Archer must come one tile closer to hit both.
- An area-damage spell in hand while attacking lets the X-Bow clear low and moderate hitpoint defenders and lock onto the Crown Tower.
