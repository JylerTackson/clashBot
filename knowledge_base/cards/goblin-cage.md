# Goblin Cage

> *In-game description:* "When the Goblin Cage is destroyed, a Goblin Brawler is unleashed into the Arena! Goblin Brawler always skips leg day."

**Related:** [Evolution: goblin-cage-evolution](../evolutions/goblin-cage-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Goblin Cage is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Goblin Stadium (Arena 1). It spawns a building with moderate hitpoints. When defeated, it will spawn a Goblin Brawler, which is a fast, single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troop with high hitpoints and high damage. It appears to be a wooden cage with four metal supports, a lock on the front, and a pair of bars at each side, while the Goblin Brawler appears as a larger, buff Goblin with a unibrow, white bands tied on his hands, and tattoos printed on his back and arms. He is also the same as that spawned by the Party Hut. A Goblin Cage card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
For 4 Elixir, you can drop a building containing a Goblin Brawler on the arena, pulling Hogs, Giants, and Golems alike. That cage won't hold for long though, as after a little while if it's not broken, the Goblin Brawler inside does the job himself! When released, he gets his revenge for being captured on every enemy in his sight, dishing out impressive damage while having moderate health!

 For 2 cycles, if his **Evolution** is active, the Goblin Brawler will get so angry from getting imprisoned time after time that he practiced the **Cage Rage** moveset, making himself able to brawl even inside the Cage! When the troop is close to the Cage, the Brawler grabs it and rapidly deals damage to them, making them untargetable and unable to do anything. Power Punch!

## Strong against

- Building-targeting win conditions — Hog Rider, Ram Rider, Giants and Golems are all pulled, and its spawner-sized hitbox pulls them from slightly further away than a defensive building like the Cannon.
- Mega Knight — placed in the centre of the player's side it can completely stop him with both Crown Towers helping, for a large positive Elixir trade.
- Electro Giant — a good counter, since he cannot zap it and must break it with his regular attack, after which the Goblin Brawler cripples or kills him.
- Battle Ram, Skeleton Barrel and Suspicious Bush — it absorbs the self-destructing attack, survives a few follow-up hits, and the Brawler cleans up what spawns.
- Wall Breakers and Evolved Wall Breakers — it lures and takes both hits, saving a more valuable target (a slight negative Elixir trade, and the Brawler is released early).
- Golden Knight — it can absorb his Dashing Dash and then the Brawler helps finish him for a +1 Elixir trade.
- Giant Skeleton — placed behind him, the Cage absorbs a few hits and the Brawler is one of the few units that can escape the death bomb.
- Minion Horde — placed 1 tile from the far side of the Arena it pulls the whole Horde; the Brawler then kites the survivors and gets 2 hits on the enemy Crown Tower.
- Furnace Fire Spirits — a decent passive tank for them, although the Cage expires before the Furnace does.
- Royal Giant and ranged troops — placing the Cage on top of them means the Brawler attacks immediately instead of walking into damage.

## Weak against

- Earthquake — deals enough damage on its own to destroy the Cage and release the Brawler prematurely.
- Dual-lane pushes — Royal Recruits, split Royal Hogs, split Zappies and split Three Musketeers overwhelm the Brawler, who only targets one unit at a time.
- Swarms — the Brawler has only average hitpoints and single-target melee, so swarm cards handle him.
- Skeletons placed in the centre of the Arena — enough of a distraction for the Crown Tower to kill the Brawler before he connects.
- Slowing spells — anything that slows the Brawler can stop him reaching the Crown Tower.
- Heavy hitters — Elite Barbarians take the Cage down in 2 hits, and a Mini P.E.K.K.A. leaves it on very low health after one.
- Lightning — although the Cage absorbs 2 of the 3 strikes, that still destroys it.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 4 Elixir Rare defensive building with a 20 second lifetime that spawns a Goblin Brawler (Fast 90, melee short 0.8, 1.1 sec hit speed) when destroyed or when it expires.
- Its hitbox is spawner-sized (like Goblin Hut) rather than Cannon-sized, so it pulls building-targeters from further away and is a safe passive middle placement that most spells are not worth using on.
- It is not a substitute for a spawner building: the Cage wants to absorb hits as a distraction, while spawners want to avoid damage so they keep producing units.
- Against Hog Rider or Ram Rider, place it directly in front of them rather than in the centre so the Brawler attacks immediately instead of chasing.
- As a last-resort defense, place it as far away as possible but directly in front of the attacker to buy an extra 1.5 seconds.
- Against ranged troops (and the Royal Giant) place it on top of them so the Brawler engages at once.
- Against a Mortar or X-Bow, place it in the same lane so the Brawler fights their defenders; in the other lane the siege building simply retargets after breaking the Cage.
- It absorbs 2 of Lightning's 3 strikes, so it can shield Lightning-vulnerable troops such as Musketeer, Zappies, Electro Wizard, Little Prince, Ice Wizard, Wizard, Magic Archer, Goblinstein, Flying Machine, Princess, Firecracker, Bomber and Mother Witch.
- The Evolved Ice Spirit's Ice Blast stun has no effect on the Cage, making it a valid (if Elixir-negative) answer inside larger pushes.
- Pair the Brawler with Zap, The Log, Arrows, Fireball or Poison to clear the swarms that stop him, letting him advance on the Crown Tower.
- After destroying a Crown Tower, the Cage can be placed on the opponent's side to distract the remaining Crown Tower away from an attacking troop.
- The Goblin Brawler is the same unit spawned by the Party Hut.
