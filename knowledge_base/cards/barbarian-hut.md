# Barbarian Hut

> *In-game description:* "Building that periodically spawns Barbarians to fight the enemy. Time to make the Barbarians!"

## Overview

The Barbarian Hut is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Jungle Arena (Arena 9). It spawns a building with high hitpoints. Every 15 seconds, the Barbarian Hut will passively summon a group of three Barbarians, with a 0.5 seconds delay from each other. When defeated, it will also spawn a Barbarian. It appears as a wooden hut with a team-colored roof and a sword hoisted above the entrance, along with a keg of Elixir for decoration beside it. A Barbarian Hut card costs 6 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
This crude but high-hitpoint barrack spawns a trio of Barbarians every 15 seconds, who deal quite a bit of damage when ignored. These Barbarians can be valuable as support, granting a significant Elixir advantage. Its death results in an additional Barbarian spawned for extra destruction. However, this hut is expensive, costing a whole 6 Elixir, and the Barbarians can be easily taken out if handled correctly.
