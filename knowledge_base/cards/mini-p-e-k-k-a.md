# Mini P.E.K.K.A.

> *In-game description:* "The Arena is a certified butterfly-free zone. No distractions for P.E.K.K.A, only destruction."

**Related:** [Hero variant: mini-p-e-k-k-a-hero](../heroes/mini-p-e-k-k-a-hero.md)

## Overview

The Mini P.E.K.K.A. is a Rare card that is unlocked in the Training Camp. It spawns one single-target, ground-targeting, melee, and ground troop with high hitpoints and extremely high damage. The Mini P.E.K.K.A. takes the appearance of a robot-like troop smaller than the P.E.K.K.A. who wears a dark steel blue armor with two team-colored horns, spikes on his arms, and a single eye, while wielding a smaller version of the P.E.K.K.A.'s metal sword as his weapon. A Mini P.E.K.K.A. card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
The classic glass cannon — a mighty punch packed powerhouse within a moderate health, for a cost of 4 Elixir. He's a fast single-targeting troop who can delete the majority of support troops or devastate tanks and towers alike with only a few strikes, but is weak against swarms.

Deliver him some pancakes, and you'll see his sweet and cute side! If not, then he'll make you. If his **Hero** is active, then all of those months learning from Royal Chef will pay off and he'll begin cooking pancakes for himself! He starts with a pancake, takes 22 seconds to cook more, but attacking a troop fills the bar by about 45%, for a maximum of 5 pancakes (in 3 bars, which means the last bar gives 2 pancakes). Then, for 1 Elixir, you can use his **Breakfast Boost** ability to give him that extra morning energy to get out of the bed, enjoy the day, and level up by how many pancakes he managed to cook! This ability is one-time use only, so make sure to cook pancakes really well, or everything's wasted.
