# Bomber

> *In-game description:* "Small, lightly protected skeleton who throws bombs. Deals area damage that can wipe out a swarm of enemies."

**Related:** [Evolution: bomber-evolution](../evolutions/bomber-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Bomber is a Common card that is unlocked from the Bone Pit (Arena 2). It spawns an area-damage, ground-targeting, medium-ranged, ground troop with low hitpoints and high damage. He appears to be a Wall Breaker that carries a black bomb and wears a team-colored aviator hat with gold-rimmed aviation goggles and gloves. A Bomber card costs 2 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A 2 Elixir moderate-damage and medium-ranged splash troop. While fragile and only targeting ground, he is decent against medium to large swarms and can deal great damage behind a tank for his price.

For 2 Cycles, if his **Evolution** is active, the Bomber learns the **Bouncing Boom** ability, which is exactly what it sounds like! Each of his attacks bounces twice after the initial throw! Piercing through enemies while dealing heavy amounts of splash damage and greatly increasing his chances of hitting the Crown Towers! Talk about value! A bouncing bomb might activate the King Tower and cannot hit the same troop more than once.
