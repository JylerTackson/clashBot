# Goblin Hut

> *In-game description:* "Defensive building that spawns Spear Goblins when enemies are in range. Don't look inside... you don't want to see how they're made."

## Overview

The Goblin Hut is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Goblin Stadium (Arena 1). It spawns a single-target, long-ranged, building with high hitpoints. Every 2.2 seconds, the Goblin Hut will summon a Spear Goblin when an enemy is within range. When defeated, it will also spawn a Spear Goblin. It appears as a wooden hut on a stone foundation with a thatched roof that has a team-colored chimney on top, which is surrounded by wooden planks on each side, two horns protruding from the sides, three spears near the entrance, two on the right and one on the left, with a rope tied to each of them, and a small team-colored skull at the bottom used as a decoration. A Goblin Hut card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Here come the Spear Goblins! For 4 Elixir, the building will take a nap when there is no action, but once a unit gets at least 6 tiles close to this tribal-looking building, a Spear Goblin will come out of it every 2.2 seconds! This can happen for as long as there is a troop in the Goblin Hut's activation range. And just when you think they'll stop coming, another Spear Goblin pops out upon the hut's death! They can be amazing on defense, while easily building up behind a tank to cause even more damage on a counterpush, but are still weak to splash damage. The Goblin Hut lasts 30 seconds, so make half a minute count!
