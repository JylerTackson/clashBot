# Electro Spirit

> *In-game description:* "Jumps on enemies, dealing Area Damage and stunning up to 9 enemy Troops. Locked in an eternal battle with Knight for the best mustache."

## Overview

The Electro Spirit is a Common card that is unlocked from the Spell Valley (Arena 4). It spawns a very fast, single-target, air-targeting, short-ranged, ground troop with low hitpoints and low damage. It launches itself at its target when attacking, destroying itself on impact. If it jumps onto a unit, its attack will arc and strike up to 8 other targets within 4 tiles of each other. It will stun affected troops for 0.5 seconds. Its physical appearance is similar to an Ice Spirit, but its body is violet, and it has a glowing blue spiky moustache. An Electro Spirit card costs 1 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Costing a shockingly low 1 Elixir, the Electro Spirit shows his killer mustache to the table. Although his damage and stun duration don't match that of the Ice Spirit, his attack will chain onto up to nine units within four tiles of each other, allowing for some truly shocking effects that can stretch far across the arena, even all the way to your opponent's King Tower!

## Abilities and special mechanics

### Modifiers

#### Common: Sentient & Spirited
> "3 Electro Spirits"
When placed, it spawns three Electro Spirits instead of one, in a triangle formation.
#### Rare: Zap, Glorious Zap
> "Zaps all units"
Electro Spirit's chain range increases to infinite tiles, and its chain count increases to infinite.
#### Epic: Don't Look Directly at It
> "Chain Lightning"
Electro Spirit spawns a Lightning when it attacks.

## Strong against

- Charge-up attacks — Inferno Tower, Inferno Dragon, Prince, Dark Prince, Ram Rider, Battle Ram, Bandit, Fisherman, Sparky, Mega Knight, Mighty Miner — the 0.5 second stun resets them for 1 Elixir.
- Whole pushes at once — deployed a short distance away so it jumps the frontmost unit, the chain stuns and forces most or all troops to retarget.
- Witch and Night Witch — the chain damages them while clearing their Skeletons or Bats and delays further spawning.
- Most swarms with Crown Tower help, when placed to pull them off to a side before stunning (weaker against Minion Horde, which needs 10 tower shots).
- Medium units that cannot one-shot it — Musketeer, Executioner, Baby Dragon, Witch, Minions, Princess, Goblin Demolisher, Electro Wizard, Magic Archer, Zappies, Firecracker, Dart Goblin, Bomber, Miner, Electro Dragon, Archer Queen, Phoenix, Fisherman, Ice Wizard, Bandit — it survives a hit and gets its stun off.
- Slow flyers like Mega Minion and Hunter — placed near the river as they cross, it lures them and keeps them off the Tower Princess.
- Elixir Golem and Lava Hound — the chain also catches their death spawns for extra value.
- Balloon — a 1-Elixir stun that can prevent an extra high-damage hit when it is already locked on.
- Guards — it cannot break their shields but does finish the Guards themselves; with The Log it counters them for a neutral Elixir trade with precise timing.
- Furnace — for 1 Elixir it denies a few waves if placed so it jumps a Fire Spirit and chains onto the Furnace.
- Goblin Barrel — planted so it attacks the inner Goblin just before the barrel's shadow reaches the river, it prevents all but one hit.

## Weak against

- The Log and Barbarian Barrel — the cheapest answers, both OHKO it, so do not play it behind a push when those are in hand (unless deliberately baiting).
- Spear Goblins placed on top — all three throw spears immediately and kill it before it connects.
- Cannoneer and Dagger Duchess — destroy it before it can connect at the tower.
- Skeleton Barrel — the delay between its death and the Skeletons spawning means the chain misses them.
- Single targets in general — if the attack strikes only one unit, that unit takes just one-ninth of the potential damage, and unspent charges are not stacked onto it like the Electro Wizard's.
- Shielded troops — it deals slightly less damage per hit than the Ice Spirit, so it cannot bring shielded units into a Dart Goblin's one-shot range.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 1-Elixir cycle card and stun/reset tool that buys time for other troops or the Crown Tower.
- Its chain hits up to 9 units within 4 tiles of each other — more chains, more range and less cost than the Electro Dragon, making it hard to answer efficiently.
- Beware of King's Tower activation: if it crosses the bridge at full health, a unit placed between the Tower Princess and King's Tower (even Skeletons acting as a link) can chain into the King Tower — so avoid it in decks like Graveyard that suffer from an activated King, and only send it when the opponent is low on Elixir or answers with troops.
- Stun + Poison: holding troops inside the Poison radius for the extra 0.5 seconds increases the spell's total damage.
- Stun to hold troops in place for piercing splashers like the Magic Archer, which lets that combo handle a Miner + Minion Horde push.
- With Zap or Giant Snowball it lets the spell OHKO frail swarms, and with Fireball it puts Musketeer, Electro Wizard, Goblinstein, Little Prince, or Wizard into finishing range.
- Played ahead of a Hog Rider it stuns buildings or (with a Zap) kills Goblins, Minions, Dart Goblin, Princess, or Rascal Girls — the same applies with Lumberjack, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Prince, and Musketeer.
- Fire Spirit + Electro Spirit for 2 Elixir total kills Princess, Minions, Archers, Bomber, and Firecracker and can fully stop a Goblin Barrel with the Crown Tower; deploy both a short distance from the target so they finish deploying.
- Electro Spirit + Skeletons fully stops a Goblin Barrel for a positive Elixir trade — Electro Spirit on the inner Goblin, Skeletons distracting the other two.
- Against Skeleton Barrel, let it connect first, then place the Electro Spirit behind the Crown Tower so it jumps onto the Skeletons.
- Against Graveyard, wait for a few Skeletons to group up before jumping in.
- It kites a Royal Ghost into the other lane, which is most valuable for separating him from a bigger push.
- Balloon synergy: the 0.5 second stun on a defender gives the Balloon time to keep moving and land a hit on the tower.
- It can substitute for Zap — same stun duration at half the cost — but with noticeably less damage, no area damage, and no ability to be cast on the opponent's side.
- It is not a substitute for Skeletons: it sacrifices itself on attack, so it contributes nothing to building large pushes the way surviving Skeletons do.
