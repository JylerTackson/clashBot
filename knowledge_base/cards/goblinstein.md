# Goblinstein

> *In-game description:* "It’s ALIVE! Monster lumbers towards enemy buildings while Doctor waits to spring the trap. At the press of a button, Doctor electrifies the link between himself and Monster, frying nearby enemies!"

## Overview

The Goblinstein is a Champion card that is unlocked from the Silent Sanctuary (Arena 18). It spawns a single-target, air-targeting, medium-ranged, ground troop with moderate hitpoints and low damage, and a single target, building-targeting, melee, ground troop with high hitpoints and low damage. The Goblinstein’s attacks will stun a targeted unit for 0.5 seconds. Being a Champion card, they will stay out of the player's card cycle, and only return once they are defeated. The Goblinstein's ability has him shoot an electric spark towards an antenna that is typically located on the Monster's head. The Goblinstein Doctor resembles a Goblin with white hair, wearing a silver-rimmed team-colored goggles with glowing orange lens, a satellite dish on his head with a team-colored strap, a white coat, tan gloves, and brown boots, and wields a controller. The Monster is a large, green Goblin with a square head with an antenna on top, with some of its body being darker and red and having gold stitches and bolts on them, and wears team-colored pants. A Goblinstein card costs 5 Elixir to deploy and his ability costs an additional 2 Elixir to activate.

## Abilities and special mechanics

### Ability: Lightning Link

> "Electrifies the link, damaging any enemies that get near. Shocking!"

The Goblinstein has a special ability, accessible from the rightmost side of the screen just above the player's card slots in battle. After a 1-second delay, the Goblinstein activates his “Lightning Link”, creating an electric link between him and Monster. It damages by shocking nearby targets every 0.5 seconds up to 2 tiles away. This ability deals reduced damage to the Crown Tower. If the Monster has already been defeated, it will drop an antenna on the ground which is what the Goblinstein's electric link will target when the ability is activated. The Lightning Link ability costs 2 Elixir to activate.
