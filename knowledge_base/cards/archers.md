# Archers

> *In-game description:* "A pair of lightly armored ranged attackers. They'll help you take down ground and air units, but you're on your own with hair coloring advice."

**Related:** [Evolution: archers-evolution](../evolutions/archers-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Archers is a Common card that is unlocked in the Training Camp. It spawns two single-target, air-targeting, medium-ranged, and ground troops with low hitpoints and low damage, deployed in a horizontal line. The Archers take the appearance of women who have blue eyes, deep pink eyebrows and short hair with side bangs, are barefoot, and wear golden bands on their arms, team-colored capes, lime-colored tunics, and a team-colored belt with a buckle, along with a bag and a quiver around their waists and on their backs respectively, while wielding a recurve bow with team-colored stabilizers as their weapon, and having arrows with team-colored fletchings and a team-colored point as their ammunition. An Archers card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Two low-damage yet medium-ranged single-target troops with knowledge in hair coloring. Targeting both ground and air, and lightly armored, they are inexpensive support troops or a quick defense against small pushes while costing only 3 Elixir. They're weak to decent splash damage, though.

For 2 Cycles, if their **Evolution** is active, they learn how to use the **Power Shot**, a skill that increases their range, sharpens their attacks, and improves their accuracy, showing results from the Archer Queen's training program, dishing out 25% more damage against far away units! They're still weak to the same splash damage, but they're tough!
