# Arrows

> *In-game description:* "Arrows pepper a large area, damaging all enemies hit. Reduced damage to Crown Towers."

## Overview

The Arrows is a Common card that is unlocked in the Training Camp. It is an area-damage air-targeting spell with a large radius, high speed, and low damage that deals damage in 3 sets of volleys. The Arrows take the appearance of objects consisting of a medium-sized shaft, red fletchings, and shiny metal points. An Arrows card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Shoot into the sky with a 3 Elixir rain of Arrows. Its moderately high damage and large radius makes clearing swarms and weak enemies a breeze, but is lackluster against higher health units like the Knight or Giant.

## Strong against

- Large low-health swarms — Bats, Skeleton Army, Goblin Gang and Minion Horde; the Minion Horde is a positive Elixir trade.
- Minions — fully defeated even up to 5 Levels higher than the Arrows.
- Goblin Barrel — the most reliable counter at a neutral Elixir trade; fire right as or slightly before the Goblins spawn, and the 3.5 tile radius denies all realistic fake-out placements.
- Evolved Skeleton Army — ideal counter, since the General's shield is weaker than a Guard's and 2 volleys take out both him and the nearby Skeletons.
- Dart Goblin and Princess up to 2 Levels higher (all 3 volleys); Archers, Bomber, Firecracker and Wall Breakers up to 1 Level higher.
- Minions, Goblins, Spear Goblins and all Spirit cards die to 2 volleys; Skeletons and Bats die to a single volley.
- Evolved Archers, Evolved Bomber, Evolved Dart Goblin, Evolved Firecracker and Evolved Wall Breakers are all kept at bay (Wall Breaker Runners survive and still chip).
- Shields — 2 of 3 waves destroy Evolved Wizard, Dark Prince and Royal Recruit shields (the third chips the troop); Guards need all 3 volleys, and only their shields are removed.
- Clone — a hard counter, wiping cloned units and their death spawns (except delayed spawns like a cloned Skeleton Barrel's Skeletons).
- Spawned sub-units — Phoenix's Egg (preventing respawn), Lava Pups from a Lava Hound, and Elixir Blobs from an Elixir Golem (reclaiming 2 Elixir at once).
- Graveyard — a decent answer when nothing better is in hand; wait for the Skeletons to clump near the Crown Tower.
- Spawner buildings at low health — Goblin Hut, Tombstone or Barbarian Hut, since the volleys also clear their generated waves and death spawn if timed right.

## Weak against

- Higher-health troops such as Knight or Giant — the low damage makes it lackluster against them.
- Fragile-but-not-frail units (Barbarians, Skeleton Dragons, Flying Machine, Skeleton Barrel, Zappies, Magic Archer, Mother Witch) — only crippled, requiring a Zap or the Crown Tower to finish them, and Magic Archer can still land a Tower hit thanks to his range.
- Tombstone at full health — the 3 volleys cannot destroy it plus its death-spawn Skeletons.
- Inferno Dragon — Arrows do little damage themselves, only enough to let the Tower Princess finish it before it ramps up.
- Monk with Pensive Protection — he reflects the spell, though only fully if he is within 2 tiles of the exact center of the cast.
- Buildings in general — finishing one off wastes the spell's swarm-clearing potential unless another vulnerable unit is in the radius.
- Negative trades on cheap targets — Bomber, Bats, Wall Breakers, Goblins and Spear Goblins, so only Arrow them when other troops are hit too or instant removal is essential.
- Opponents who bait it out with a cheap swarm and follow with Goblin Barrel, Goblin Gang, Minion Horde or Princess once it is out of hand.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 3 Elixir area-damage spell hitting Air & Ground in a 3.5 tile radius with high projectile speed (1100) and damage split into 3 volleys.
- Costs 1 more than Zap but has a larger area of effect and more damage; the three separate volleys give it a slight area-denial effect.
- Its damage caps out at roughly Archers and Firecracker without assistance, which still shuts down almost every swarm card in the game.
- The travel time is short and the large radius means it rarely misses entirely, so it can be cast preemptively to erase swarms the moment they land.
- Excellent support for single-target attackers: Knight, Mini P.E.K.K.A., P.E.K.K.A., Prince, Giant Skeleton, Elite Barbarians, Inferno Dragon, Bandit, Lumberjack, Goblin Machine, Golden Knight, Boss Bandit, Mighty Miner and Monk — but a failed prediction leaves the defender with an Elixir advantage.
- Preemptive Arrows on low-health distraction cards (anything with less health than Archers) clear the path for those heavy hitters, at the cost of a negative trade.
- Synergizes with Graveyard on offense — all three waves kill Archers or Minions for 1 less Elixir than Poison, keeping more Skeletons alive; cast reactively, since the waves do not linger like Poison.
- Combines with the Golden Knight's ability to fully eliminate anything with health equal to or lower than the Little Prince, clearing his path to the Crown Tower.
- Use them to deny value from high-value cheap cards: Bomber, Firecracker, Archers, Minions, Bats, Minion Horde, Goblins, Spear Goblins, Goblin Gang, Dart Goblin, Princess and Wall Breakers.
- Baiting works both ways — deploy a cheap swarm to draw out the opponent's Arrows, then commit your real swarm, but check for Fireball, Zap, Wizard, Witch, Poison, Ice Wizard or Executioner first.
- Arrowing a low-health Tombstone lets building-targeting troops keep walking without being pulled or chipped.
- Substitutes: Fireball (1 more Elixir, slower, smaller radius, more damage and knockback), Zap (1 cheaper, instant, resets charge/ramp-up cards), Giant Snowball (Zap damage with knockback/slow and a +1 trade on Goblin Barrel), Vines (same cost, grounds and freezes air, but only 3 targets), The Log (cheaper, knockback, no anti-air), Earthquake (same cost, slower, hits buildings hard, no anti-air), Royal Delivery (same cost, slightly more damage plus a Royal Recruit, but defensive only and smaller radius).
