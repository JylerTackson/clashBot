# Electro Giant

> *In-game description:* "He channels electricity through his Zap Pack, a unique device that stuns and damages any troop attacking him within its range. Don't tell him that his finger guns aren't real! He'll zap you."

## Overview

The Electro Giant is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Rascal's Hideout (Arena 13). It spawns a slow, single-target, building-targeting, melee, ground troop with very high hitpoints and moderate damage. Enemy units who damage the Electro Giant while being within a 3-tile radius of him will be damaged and stunned for 0.5 seconds with each hit. He generally resembles the Giant, but is bare-chested, has spiky eyebrows and a beard, wears gloves with team-colored bolts on top, gold-rimmed goggles, team-colored pants, and a belt with a golden bolt in the center, and carries a generator on his back with two team-colored rings on top called the ‘Zap Pack’, which creates a team-colored electric field around him. An Electro Giant card costs 7 Elixir to deploy.
