# Giant Skeleton

> *In-game description:* "The bigger the skeleton, the bigger the bomb. Carries a bomb that blows up when the Giant Skeleton dies."

## Overview

The Giant Skeleton is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Frozen Peak (Arena 8). It spawns a single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troop with very high hitpoints and high damage. When defeated, he drops a bomb which explodes after 3 seconds, dealing damage, which is double against Crown Towers, and inflicting knockback. He appears to be a colossal Skeleton that wears a team-colored ushanka, has an underbite, and carries a large gunpowder barrel on his back and a black bomb on his hand. A Giant Skeleton card costs 6 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
This oversized skeleton costs 6 Elixir, gets distracted by everything, and struggles heavily against swarms or equally bulky units. These weaknesses are made up for with his bomb that explodes three seconds after his death, dealing high damage in its radius, as well as pushing back lighter foes. Try to bait the opponent into overcommitting on their defense against it to reap massive positive Elixir trades!
