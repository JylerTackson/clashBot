# The Log

> *In-game description:* "A spilt bottle of Rage turned an innocent tree trunk into "The Log". Now, it seeks revenge by crushing anything in its path! Reduced damage to Crown Towers."

## Overview

The “The Log” is a Legendary card that is unlocked from the Electro Valley (Arena 11). It is an area-damage, ground-targeting spell with a very long rectangular area and moderate damage that also inflicts knockback to any troop it hits. It can only be deployed on the player's own side. It appears as a long, spiked piece of wood. A "The Log" card costs 2 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
It rolls. It crushes. It knocks back. It makes those heads roll. It's none other than The Log. In a glorious entrance accompanied by a holy choir chanting "LOG!", a spiked log rolls across the Arena floor, dealing moderate damage and knocking back every enemy ground unit in its path for just 2 Elixir! Recommended targets include low health support troops, charging enemies, and every ground swarm. It can't hit air units, so don't get too comfortable! It seeks the Lumberjack with all its rage, their history go way back…

## Strong against

- Ground swarms — Barbarians, Skeleton Army and Goblin Gang can be damaged, pushed back or wiped out by a single 2 Elixir cast.
- Princess, Rascal Girl and Dart Goblin — one-shot killed at equal Level (but not if they are a higher Level than The Log).
- Goblin Barrel — a great counter; slightly harder to time than Arrows but 1 Elixir cheaper, and it also damages any tank accompanying the barrel.
- Tombstone and Goblin Drill — if the building is around half health, The Log destroys it and the troops it spawns afterwards.
- Battle Ram — deals extra damage to the Barbarians spawned on its death (does not work on Goblin Giant).
- Prince and Dark Prince — disarms their charge attacks, same as Zap, for 2 Elixir.
- Hunter — knockback pushes him further from his target, sharply reducing his damage.
- Magic Archer, Mother Witch, Ice Wizard and Little Prince — fully countered by The Log plus one Cannoneer shot (+2 and +1 Elixir trades respectively).
- Any locked-on ground troop — the knockback fully resets their attack animation (unlike Zap or Lightning, which only pause it).

## Weak against

- All air units — The Log is ground-targeting only, so Minions, Bats and Minion Horde need a separate splash card.
- Wall Breakers, Bomber, Archers, Firecracker and Elixir Blobs — the five ground units Arrows can one-shot that The Log cannot (Crown Tower support is still needed to finish them).
- Sparky — The Log's knockback does not reset her charge; only Zap does.
- Mega Knight charging his jump, and Bandit during her dash — pushback does not stop them from landing the attack.
- Hog Rider, Mega Knight, Ram Rider, Royal Hogs and Cursed Hogs — can jump over The Log and avoid it entirely (rare, do not rely on it).
- Higher-Level Princess, Rascal Girl or Dart Goblin — survive the hit when their Level exceeds The Log's.
- Anything on the opponent's side of the Arena — it can only be deployed on the player's own half.
- Tricky Goblin Barrels placed 3 tiles above a Crown Tower — a Log played too far forward can miss one or more Goblins.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 2 Elixir ground-only area-damage spell with universal knockback; a cheaper alternative to Arrows (similar damage and area, 1 Elixir less) and a same-cost alternative to Zap.
- Deploy restriction: can only be placed on the player's own side, so it cannot save a Miner or Goblin Barrel from enemy swarms — do not run it as the only small spell in those decks.
- Lane switching: place it on the side opposite to where you want an enemy troop to go; the knockback pushes centre-lane troops into the other lane, weakening a defense or a counterpush.
- X-Bow interaction: knocking a Golem or Giant out of range lets your X-Bow connect to the Crown Tower.
- River push: a unit walking on the edge of the river can be pushed back over to the opponent's side, delaying Giant, Rune Giant, Golem, Mighty Miner, Giant Skeleton, Skeleton King, Elixir Golem, Goblin Machine, Valkyrie and P.E.K.K.A.
- Royal Giant in the extra space after a Tower falls can be pushed into the King's Tower lane, protecting the frailer Tower, or made to retarget a late-placed building.
- Miner placed on the Crown Tower corner nearest the King's Tower can be pushed into the King's Tower, activating it.
- Pushing a Bowler or Magic Archer further inside the Arena lets their piercing attacks reach and activate the King Tower.
- Offense: pairs with grounded units — swarms placed on top of The Log are hit immediately and usually wiped; account for the staggered deploy time of multi-unit cards (all except Skeletons and Skeleton Army) so it rolls over them after their hitboxes spawn.
- Spell combos: finishes off units that survive Fireball or Poison, such as Night Witch, Hunter, Berserker, Bandit and Witch; with Poison it also pushes them back into the spell's area. Usually a negative Elixir trade, so aim for extra value.
- Fireball timing: if a troop survives a Fireball near your Crown Tower, wait until it reaches the bridge to Log it and catch the meat shields too — but let the Tower finish it if it has already crossed.
- Spirit combos: The Log + any Spirit defeats up to Elixir Blobs (Zap needs Fire Spirit for that threshold) and also defeats Guards, though for Guards The Log must hit before the Spirit.
- Width: wider than Barbarian Barrel, so a Goblin Barrel placed 1 tile to the side of the Tower still gets hit by all 3 Goblins.
- Evolved Goblin Barrel placed centre can only be fully denied with Dagger Duchess holding at least 3 daggers — Log the Goblins side while the Duchess handles the Decoy Goblins side.
- Three Musketeers: a well-timed Magic Archer + The Log stops 2 of the split Musketeers and chips the Crown Tower, leaving 3 Elixir for the third.
