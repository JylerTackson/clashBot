# Hog Rider

> *In-game description:* "Fast melee troop that targets buildings and can jump over the river. He followed the echoing call of "Hog Riderrrrr" all the way through the Arena doors."

## Overview

The Hog Rider is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Builder's Workshop (Arena 5). It spawns a fast, single-target, building-targeting, melee, ground troop with high hitpoints and high damage. He is able to jump over the river. He appears as a dark-skinned shirtless man with blue eyes, dark brown eyebrows, beard and mohawk, wearing a gold earring in his left ear, a team-colored belt and wristbands, along with a brown loincloth and sandals. He wields a hammer with two brass bands and a team-colored ribbon tied at the bottom, and is riding a hog with a brown bridle attached to its tusks. A Hog Rider card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A reliable card against towers. Zoom past troops with his moderate-high health and blazing fast speed and landing a hit or two on towers while letting out his signature scream "HOG RIDER!" — all for a measly 4 Elixir. Also, his hog will jump over the river! However, good building placements easily draw him away, and he is seriously helpless against swarms and tank busters.

## Strong against

- Crown Towers — his Very Fast (120) speed and river jump let him rush past troops and land hits for only 4 Elixir, which is his whole purpose.
- Ground attackers while he is jumping the river — he cannot be targeted mid-jump, so The Log and Earthquake fail to damage him.
- Mega Knight — deploy him so he jumps as the Mega Knight crosses the river; the jump makes him immune to the Mega Knight's jump and he then distracts him for a few seconds.
- Elixir Collector placed in front of the King's Tower — from the bridge he destroys it for a positive Elixir trade (though he usually dies to both Crown Towers afterwards).
- Very Fast non-building-targeting troops — placed on the fourth tile from the bridge, slightly into the opposite lane, he kites them thanks to his matching speed and building-only targeting.
- Grounded units in a large push — placed right at the bridge he pulls them in, becomes untargetable during the jump, then pulls them back, separating troops from their tank.
- Slow starts — deployed at the bridge at match start or at 10 Elixir he punishes a bad opening hand and scouts how the opponent answers.

## Weak against

- Defensive buildings — the best general answer, since he only targets buildings and gets pulled away.
- Ground swarms — Skeleton Army, Goblin Gang and Barbarians damage him down while blocking his path; Barbarians counter him without strict timing (all of them are spell-vulnerable, though).
- Air swarms — Minion Horde, Skeleton Dragons and Bats work well because his usual support cards do not counter them.
- Hunter — kills him in 2 hits placed right on top of him, though anything placed in front of the Hog transfers the overkill damage.
- Guards or Goblins plus The Log with good placement and timing — fully counters him, cancelling every Hog hit.
- Tornado — placed on the second tile in front of the King's Tower and staggered two tiles toward the Crown Tower it activates the King without taking damage (do not repeat it more than about three times or the King Tower becomes a back-door three-crown risk).
- Fisherman — a perfectly timed pull stops him dealing damage and activates the King's Tower; once activated, place the Fisherman closer to the river so the Hog lands somewhere safer.
- Inferno Tower and Inferno Dragon — they keep ramping their damage even while he is mid-jump.
- Rocket — a reasonable answer when there is no good card in hand or when it clips something else.
- Passive/spawner buildings 3 tiles from the river in the middle — their larger hitbox makes the pull unavoidable.

## Notes / synergies

- Win condition at 4 Elixir: Very Fast (120), 1.6 sec hit speed, 0.6 sec first hit speed, Melee: Short (0.8), targets buildings only, jumps the river.
- Earthquake is his best partner, wiping almost every building and grounded swarms; Lightning does similar building work while crippling Wizard, Witch, Mega Minion, Electro Dragon, Night Witch, Bandit, Electro Wizard, Archer Queen, Berserker, Fisherman, Musketeer, Ice Wizard or Hunter.
- Predictive spells pay off: The Log ahead of a Skeleton Army, Goblin Gang or Tombstone keeps his hitpoints intact, and in Double Elixir a predictive Fireball handles bulkier swarms like Barbarians.
- Hog Rider + Lumberjack is a fast swarm-bait and damage combo; answer the defending swarm with Arrows, and a dead Lumberjack's Rage makes the Hog even more dangerous.
- Hog Rider + Mini P.E.K.K.A is fast and high damage, the Mini P.E.K.K.A handling Executioner, Wizard, Knight, Valkyrie, Royal Ghost, Golden Knight and Musketeer — but Skeleton Army, a Skeletons/Goblins kite, or air cards like Minion Horde and Evolved Electro Dragon break it.
- Hog Rider + Goblin Barrel is awkward to defend; time it so the Hog tanks the Tower shots for the Goblins (Barbarian Barrel shuts it down cheaply if the barrel lands directly on the Tower).
- The Trifecta — Hog Rider, Musketeer and Valkyrie for 12 Elixir — with the Hog placed behind the Valkyrie so she is boosted forward and stays in front to shield him; it loses to Lightning, sometimes Minion Horde, and is hard-countered by P.E.K.K.A for a 5 Elixir advantage.
- Hog Rider + Balloon is devastating, the Hog tanking and clearing buildings, but it is easy to separate by move speed and weak to swarms and anti-air; splitting them across lanes stretches the defense until a building or Tornado re-merges them.
- His speed boosts mini tanks like Ice Golem or Valkyrie forward, and placing a bulky anti-swarm card in front of him both transports it faster and shields him from swarms.
- He can tank for low-hitpoint troops such as Goblins, Skeletons, Spear Goblins, Archers and Guards; most cheap swarms complement him since they nearly match his speed and force multiple answers.
- Hog Rider + Freeze surprises swarm-reliant defenders (Skeletons and Bats die outright, Spear Goblins, Goblins and Minions are left finishable by a small spell); against non-swarm defenses it invites a big counterpush, so only use it with backup or at the end of a match.
- Do not spend him just to kill a building unless you are on a cycle deck fast enough to reach the next Hog before the opponent reaches their next counter — spells, long-range troops or waiting out the lifetime are usually better.
- Building placement matters: defensive buildings have a smaller hitbox, so one placed 3 tiles from the river in the middle can be bypassed by a Hog placed at the very edge of the Arena.
- Defenders can deny that edge bypass by pushing him toward a central building as he jumps — Bats, Skeleton Dragons and Minion Horde right in front of him on deploy; Minions, Skeleton Barrel, Mega Minion, Flying Machine, Electro Dragon, Baby Dragon, Inferno Dragon, Balloon or Lava Hound staggered one tile to the right when he is on the left side and vice versa; or an Ice Golem at his landing spot to force him around it.
