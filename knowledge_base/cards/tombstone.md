# Tombstone

> *In-game description:* "Building that periodically spawns Skeletons to fight the enemy... and when destroyed, spawns 4 more Skeletons! Creepy."

**Related:** [Hero variant: tombstone-hero](../heroes/tombstone-hero.md)

## Overview

The Tombstone is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Bone Pit (Arena 2). It spawns a building with moderate hitpoints. Every 3.5 seconds, the Tombstone will passively summon a group of two Skeletons, with a 0.5 seconds delay from each other. When destroyed, it will also spawn four Skeletons. It appears as a coffin with a mounted gravestone that has a skull and a bone printed on it and a wooden plank located on both sides serving as a trapdoor, and emits a team-colored glow. A Tombstone card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A cheap spawner with moderate hitpoints at the cost of only 3 Elixir that spawns two Skeletons every 3.5 seconds. When destroyed, 4 more spooky scary Skeletons pop up to take down the destroyer. The spawned Skeletons and the death spawn ones can easily distract and take out single-target units, making this building very versatile.

 But what if you want a REALLY big skeleton instead. Well if the **Hero Form** is active, and your tombstone is still alive (or 1.5 sec after its destroyed), you can use the **Regal Revive** for 5 Elixer. This will destroy the tombstone (still spawns the death skeletons) and spawn the royale Tomb Queen. The Tomb Queen will walk towards towers with alot of health and damage. So if you need a win condition, then she is here to help whenever you need her. And as a bonus, 4 skeletons will spawn when she dies, dealing even more damage.

## Abilities and special mechanics

### Modifiers

#### Common: Double Dead
> "x2 Skellies"
Tombstone's spawn speed is doubled, for a total of 1.75 seconds. It also deals damage after death.
#### Rare: Spawned Siege
> "Spawn Wallbreakers"
Instead of Skeletons, the Tombstone spawns Wall Breakers.
#### Epic: Tomb of the King
> "Spawn Guards & Skeleton King"
Instead of Skeletons, the Tombstone spawns Guards when alive and a Skeleton King after death. It also deals damage after death.
