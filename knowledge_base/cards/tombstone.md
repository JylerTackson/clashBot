# Tombstone

> *In-game description:* "Building that periodically spawns Skeletons to fight the enemy... and when destroyed, spawns 4 more Skeletons! Creepy."

**Related:** [Hero variant: tombstone-hero](../heroes/tombstone-hero.md)

## Overview

The Tombstone is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Bone Pit (Arena 2). It spawns a building with moderate hitpoints. Every 3.5 seconds, the Tombstone will passively summon a group of two Skeletons, with a 0.5 seconds delay from each other. When destroyed, it will also spawn four Skeletons. It appears as a coffin with a mounted gravestone that has a skull and a bone printed on it and a wooden plank located on both sides serving as a trapdoor, and emits a team-colored glow. A Tombstone card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
A cheap spawner with moderate hitpoints at the cost of only 3 Elixir that spawns two Skeletons every 3.5 seconds. When destroyed, 4 more spooky scary Skeletons pop up to take down the destroyer. The spawned Skeletons and the death spawn ones can easily distract and take out single-target units, making this building very versatile.

 But what if you want a REALLY big skeleton instead. Well if the **Hero Form** is active, and your tombstone is still alive (or 1.5 sec after its destroyed), you can use the **Regal Revive** for 5 Elixer. This will destroy the tombstone (still spawns the death skeletons) and spawn the royale Tomb Queen. The Tomb Queen will walk towards towers with alot of health and damage. So if you need a win condition, then she is here to help whenever you need her. And as a bonus, 4 skeletons will spawn when she dies, dealing even more damage.

## Abilities and special mechanics

### Modifiers

#### Common: Double Dead
> "x2 Skellies"
Tombstone's spawn speed is doubled, for a total of 1.75 seconds. It also deals damage after death.
#### Rare: Spawned Siege
> "Spawn Wallbreakers"
Instead of Skeletons, the Tombstone spawns Wall Breakers.
#### Epic: Tomb of the King
> "Spawn Guards & Skeleton King"
Instead of Skeletons, the Tombstone spawns Guards when alive and a Skeleton King after death. It also deals damage after death.

## Strong against

- Single-target, slow-attacking troops — P.E.K.K.A., Mini P.E.K.K.A. and Giant Skeleton are distracted indefinitely by the trickle of Skeletons.
- Building-targeting troops — Giant, Hog Rider and Golem get pulled, and the 4 death-spawn Skeletons can heavily damage or destroy them.
- Charging single-target troops — Prince, Bandit, Evolved Royal Recruits and Boss Bandit waste their charge on a Skeleton and cannot re-charge; the same tactic works on the Inferno Dragon.
- Lone Sparky — her slow attack speed means the Skeletons distract her indefinitely while the Tower Princess kills her.
- Slow long-ranged units — Princess and Mortar stay distracted for a long time, buying Elixir to answer them properly.
- Baby Dragon and Skeleton Dragons — distracted long enough for the Crown Tower to finish them.
- Wall Breakers and Evolved Wall Breakers — it soaks their damage; against split Wall Breakers an early centre placement lets the first Skeleton wave kill them with no damage taken at all.
- Battle Ram, Skeleton Barrel and Suspicious Bush — it absorbs the self-destruct hit and the Skeletons handle the death-spawn units.
- Royal Hogs — better than other buildings here, since the Skeletons split damage across several Hogs and cannot be targeted back.
- Mighty Miner — a centre-placed Tombstone counters a proactive one; he struggles to shred the Skeletons and still walks to the Tombstone even after using his ability.
- Miner and Graveyard Skeletons placed next to the Crown Towers — it distracts them effectively.

## Weak against

- Mother Witch — extremely effective against it: she one-shots the Skeletons from range into Cursed Hogs that break the Tombstone, then converts the death spawn too.
- Piercing splash — Bowler, Firecracker, Evolved Bomber, Magic Archer and Executioner destroy the building and the fresh Skeletons together.
- Little Prince placed on top — his ability destroys the Tombstone and its Skeletons.
- The Log — an excellent counter to a centre-placed Tombstone below 50% health, killing the building and its death-spawn Skeletons.
- Barbarian Barrel — same effect as The Log plus a Barbarian, but it cannot reach a Tombstone placed deep.
- Earthquake — stronger than Arrows: 2 hits destroy the Tombstone and the third kills the spawned Skeletons for the same cost.
- Arrows — a neutral trade, but they can only hit the spawner or the death spawn, not both.
- Fireball and Poison — Fireball kills it at any health (usually Elixir-negative and it misses the death spawn); Poison kills it over the full duration and handles the death spawn.
- Zap and Giant Snowball — do not hurt the building but clear the death-spawn Skeletons to open a lane.
- Balloon — the Tombstone is ineffective against it: the Balloon one-shots it whenever it is placed and the Skeletons cannot hit air.
- Goblin Giant — not recommended, since the Spear Goblins one-shot the spawned Skeletons.
- Evolved Royal Giant — do not use the retarget-push trick on him; his recoil destroys the Tombstone faster and wipes the Skeletons.
- Evolved Battle Ram — a charging one instantly destroys the Skeletons, unlike the regular Battle Ram.
- Any troop backed by area damage troops or spells — the Tombstone is eliminated too quickly to be worth it.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 3 Elixir Rare spawner building, 30 second lifetime, spawning a pair of Skeletons at a time (0.5 sec apart) and 4 Skeletons on death.
- General building placement: near the centre of your territory, roughly 4 tiles from the river staggered into the weaker Crown Tower's lane, so troops are drawn away while both Towers can chip in.
- Unlike other spawners, do not place it behind the Crown Towers — the Skeletons are not worth protecting and spells used on it will also chip your Tower.
- Against an edge-placed Hog Rider, shift the Tombstone 1 tile higher to catch him every time, at the cost of being easier to snipe from across the river.
- Against a Ram Rider, place it in front of the Crown Tower so she cannot keep the Skeletons out of range; they will reach and kill her for a positive Elixir trade.
- Retarget trick: its Skeletons have enough hitbox to physically push a ranged troop locked on your Tower so it retargets the Tombstone — no defensive building can do this.
- Tower troop synergy: a well-placed Tombstone lets the Cannoneer and Dagger Duchess focus the tank while the Skeletons distract and add damage.
- King Tower activation: a 7-2 placement right in front of the King's Tower on the Mega Knight's side lures him into activating it; a 5 tiles from river / 2 tiles from Crown Tower placement is more consistent, followed by a troop the Mega Knight's jump one-shots near the King's Tower. A standard 4-2 placement also makes a Firecracker activate the King Tower, though deploying too early makes her lock onto the Skeletons instead.
- Skeleton King synergy: letting 5 waves of Skeletons die fully charges a friendly Skeleton King's ability — and for the same reason, do not play the Tombstone while an enemy Skeleton King is alive.
- Goblinstein's Monster distracted by the Tombstone can use its ability to damage the building and kill nearby Skeletons.
- Do not play it offensively in a lane without support — Crown Towers one-shot the Skeletons and they are poor cover troops.
- Golem note: its death damage kills the Skeletons and the Golemites can still chip the Crown Tower.
- Because the opponent must spend a spell on it, it pairs well with spell-vulnerable cards that thrive once the spell is gone, such as Goblin Barrel or Graveyard.
- Substitutions: Cannon (same cost, attacks directly, better vs ranged and splash; Tombstone is better vs melee and building-targeters), Goblin Cage (1 more Elixir, tankier, better vs area damage; Tombstone excels vs heavy hitters), Barbarian Hut (bulkier and stronger troops but 6 Elixir and slower spawns), Guards (same cost, spell-resistant, but cannot distract building-targeters), Skeleton Army (same cost, 5 fewer Skeletons total but all at once and far more spell-vulnerable).
- In the Rage Challenge, building lifetimes are unaffected but its spawn speed is, producing up to 10.5 waves of Skeletons instead of the usual 8.
