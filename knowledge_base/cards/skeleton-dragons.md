# Skeleton Dragons

> *In-game description:* "This pair of skeletal scorchers deal Area Damage and fly above the Arena. They also play a mean rib cage xylophone duet."

## Overview

The Skeleton Dragons is a Common card that is unlocked from the Spell Valley (Arena 4). It spawns two fast, area-damage, air-targeting, short-ranged, air troops with moderate hitpoints and moderate damage. Their deployment is in a horizontal formation. They appear to be smaller, skeletal versions of the Baby Dragon with team-colored wings, horns and back spikes. A Skeleton Dragons card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
These two smaller, skeletal cousins of the Baby Dragon move just as fast while packing almost double the punch when together for only 4 Elixir! Although  being brittle piles of bones resulted in them having noticeably less health and slower attack speed than the Baby Dragon, because there’s two of them, they can help defend in both lanes, join split lane pushes or play a mean rib cage xylophone duet.
