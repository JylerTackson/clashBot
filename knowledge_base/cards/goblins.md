# Goblins

> *In-game description:* "Four fast, unarmored melee attackers. Small, fast, green and mean!"

**Related:** [Hero variant: goblins-hero](../heroes/goblins-hero.md)

## Overview

The Goblins is a Common card that is unlocked from Goblin Stadium (Arena 1). It spawns four very fast, single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troops with low hitpoints and low damage. Their deployment is in a square formation. The Goblins take the appearance of small, green creatures with pointy ears, green eyes, dark green eyebrows, amber sclerae, and a large peach nose, while carrying daggers and wearing team-colored suspenders and wristbands, along with brown pants and shoes. They are also the same as those spawned by the Goblin Barrel, Goblin Gang, Goblin Drill, Party Hut, Goblin Curse, Evolved Mortar, and the Evolved Goblin Giant. A Goblins card costs 2 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Four very fast, moderate damage, single-target melee troops. Unarmored and only targeting ground, they are nevertheless an excellent defense against glass cannons like the Mini P.E.K.K.A., and not to be ignored with a tank or mini-tank. All for only 2 Elixir! Just watch out for any gold that you might have.

If their **Hero** is active, the last Goblin defeated will leave a banner behind for 5 seconds, and for a dirt-cheap cost of 1 Elixir, you can use their **Banner Brigade** ability to summon 2 Brigade Goblins near the banner! Not only can these Goblins provide extra defense support, they are also threatening on offense as they can behave (nearly) like a mini Goblin Barrel if the banner is right next to your opponent’s Crown Tower, just make sure the Goblins don’t spawn right into splash damage, like a Valkyrie’s axe sweep for example.

## Strong against

- High-damage single-target troops — Prince, Mini P.E.K.K.A., P.E.K.K.A., Mega Minion, Giant Skeleton, Inferno Dragon, Mighty Miner — four bodies overwhelm attackers that kill one at a time.
- Weak, slow single-target troops — Battle Healer, Miner, Fisherman, Archer Queen — they take far too long to kill four Goblins.
- Miner specifically — he cannot one-shot each Goblin, so they defeat him.
- Prince's charge — deployed diagonal to the center in front of him after he starts charging, they absorb the charge and lengthen his walk (place close enough to the tower that he cannot recharge).
- Graveyard — their fast attack and movement speed clear the Skeletons before they act.
- Goblin Barrel — with quick reaction, Goblins placed behind the Crown Tower fully counter most placements; even mistimed, the enemy Goblins only land one or two stabs.
- Princess at the bridge — they survive her first attack and her slow hit speed means she cannot kill them back.
- Single-direction area damage units when surrounded with good timing — Sparky, Dark Prince, Royal Ghost, Bomber, Wizard, Ice Wizard, Skeleton King — killed with Crown Tower help.
- Building-targeting troops like Giant and Hog Rider — cheap, high damage, though rarely a full counter on their own.
- Inferno Tower — they stall it briefly, buying time to answer it or reset its damage ramp-up.

## Weak against

- The Log and Barbarian Barrel — the most reliable counters, killing them for an equal Elixir trade with no tower help.
- Arrows (2 hits) and Earthquake (3 hits) — work anywhere on the map, but are a negative Elixir trade unless used offensively.
- Zap and Giant Snowball with Crown Tower support — neither kills alone, though a Zap one level higher does.
- Void — destroys all four within 2 hits if they are the only troops inside, a slight negative Elixir trade.
- Splash troops — Wizard, Executioner, Bowler, Firecracker, Witch, Princess, Magic Archer, Bomber; Bomber is a great same-cost counter since he hits several at once.
- Fire Spirit — OHKOs Goblins, stopping them even behind a tank; Skeletons or any Spirit in the center stalls them for the Crown Towers.
- Ice Golem — distracts them for the Crown Tower, and its death nova damages and slows them further.
- Multi-troop ranged units placed at a distance — Archers, Spear Goblins — beat lone Goblins.
- Wide-radius area damage — Valkyrie, Mega Knight's jump, Dark Prince's charge — cannot be beaten by the spread-out deploy trick.
- Poison and Arrows when they are used to defend a Graveyard — always keep a second answer ready.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 2-Elixir cheap ground swarm — distraction, high burst damage, and cycle filler.
- Strong behind high-hitpoint tanks (Giant, P.E.K.K.A., Giant Skeleton, Golem) and behind cheap tanks that survive to the tower (Ice Spirit, Electro Spirit, Miner, Ice Golem), where they add large damage for little Elixir.
- Placed behind Valkyrie, Ice Golem, Knight, Battle Healer, Monk, Barbarians, or Barbarian Hut Barbarians they push the slow tank forward, and the combo can take a tower if ignored.
- Pair with fast win conditions like Hog Rider and Battle Ram — matching speeds keeps them together and the Goblins distract single-target melee defenders; splash support is the danger.
- Miner + Goblins is a cheap, repeatable 5-Elixir play where the Miner tanks and the Goblins deal the damage, though The Log mitigates most of it.
- For chip damage, place lone Goblins one tile to either side of the bridge rather than dead center so they land extra hits as the tower kills them — efficient for finishing a low-health tower.
- Deploy trick: drop the Goblins, then a single ground unit on the same tile immediately after, to spread them apart so area attackers (Baby Dragon, Goblin Demolisher, Skeleton Dragons, Ice Wizard, Magic Archer, Witch) hit them one at a time; works best when that unit is a ranged attacker.
- They add troop count for a Void, letting the spell's damage split enough to save another troop.
- Substitutes: Skeletons (Goblins are better in every way but Elixir cost and hit speed, and survive Zap/Giant Snowball) and Guards (Goblins are cheaper and faster).
- Goblins + Spear Goblins replicate the Goblin Gang for slightly more Elixir, with one extra Goblin and the flexibility of two cheap cards instead of a single 3-Elixir commitment.
