# Goblins

> *In-game description:* "Four fast, unarmored melee attackers. Small, fast, green and mean!"

**Related:** [Hero variant: goblins-hero](../heroes/goblins-hero.md)

## Overview

The Goblins is a Common card that is unlocked from Goblin Stadium (Arena 1). It spawns four very fast, single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troops with low hitpoints and low damage. Their deployment is in a square formation. The Goblins take the appearance of small, green creatures with pointy ears, green eyes, dark green eyebrows, amber sclerae, and a large peach nose, while carrying daggers and wearing team-colored suspenders and wristbands, along with brown pants and shoes. They are also the same as those spawned by the Goblin Barrel, Goblin Gang, Goblin Drill, Party Hut, Goblin Curse, Evolved Mortar, and the Evolved Goblin Giant. A Goblins card costs 2 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Four very fast, moderate damage, single-target melee troops. Unarmored and only targeting ground, they are nevertheless an excellent defense against glass cannons like the Mini P.E.K.K.A., and not to be ignored with a tank or mini-tank. All for only 2 Elixir! Just watch out for any gold that you might have.

If their **Hero** is active, the last Goblin defeated will leave a banner behind for 5 seconds, and for a dirt-cheap cost of 1 Elixir, you can use their **Banner Brigade** ability to summon 2 Brigade Goblins near the banner! Not only can these Goblins provide extra defense support, they are also threatening on offense as they can behave (nearly) like a mini Goblin Barrel if the banner is right next to your opponent’s Crown Tower, just make sure the Goblins don’t spawn right into splash damage, like a Valkyrie’s axe sweep for example.
