# Skeleton Army

> *In-game description:* "Spawns an army of Skeletons. Meet Larry and his friends Harry, Gerry, Terry, Mary, etc."

**Related:** [Evolution: skeleton-army-evolution](../evolutions/skeleton-army-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Skeleton Army is an Epic card that is unlocked from P.E.K.K.A's Playhouse (Arena 6). It spawns 15 Skeletons in a scatter formation. A Skeleton Army card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Fifteen skeletons for just 3 Elixir — this massive swarm can overwhelm single-target enemy units in the blink of an eye! However, with their very low hitpoints, they can be easily taken out by area damage or even several ranged units, so they’re best used on defense with caution.

However, you should NOT mock the General of the army, because for 2 Cycles, if their **Evolution** is active, General Gerry is summoned to the army to command them into the path of victory with the **Un-Undead** skill! When the Skeletons from the army are defeated, they don't actually vanish, they just become invisible to enemy troops, while still being able to damage them! However, they truly vanish when the General is taken down, because an army cannot go so long without their General (any alive Skeletons will still survive). It's not like the General is unprepared, however: he has a spear for extra range, and a shield (with the same health as the Skeletons themselves) for extra survivability! He wants YOU to join the Skeleton Army!
