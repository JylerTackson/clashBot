# Bowler

> *In-game description:* "This big blue dude digs the simple things in life - Dark Elixir drinks and throwing rocks. His massive boulders roll through their target, hitting everything behind for a strike!"

**Related:** [Hero variant: bowler-hero](../heroes/bowler-hero.md)

## Overview

The Bowler is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Rascal's Hideout (Arena 13). It spawns a slow, area-damage, ground-targeting, medium-ranged, ground troop with high hitpoints and high damage. The Bowler launches an attack that travels up to its maximum range, and inflicts knockback, while piercing through enemies. He resembles a massive, indigo-skinned humanoid creature with blue eyes, wearing a pale brown loincloth and team-colored bands on his arms, and carries a boulder. A Bowler card costs 5 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
This huge menacing but also relaxed ogre with high hitpoints throws boulders that pierce through ground units in its path! For 5 Elixir, he can crush and knock back glass cannons, like he was in a bowling alley, and is healthy enough to survive even a Rocket. Despite all this, he does have quite a slow attack rate, so he must be supported well.

The Bowler isn't a bowling one-trick, however, because if his **Hero** is active, you can use his **Stone Swish** ability for 2 Elixir, showing how swag he is as a basketball player! He is able to hurl his basketboulder at make 3 points at any target close enough from him (which is pretty far!), dealing quite the damage! Air troops are safe from rcok-hard madness, though, and knockback is removed for them.

## Strong against

- Ground swarms — the boulder pierces through everything in a line and one-shots Spear Goblins, Goblins and Skeletons.
- Spawner buildings — one of the best counters; landing the final blow destroys the building and the death troops it would have spawned. Witch and Furnace are handled the same way.
- Goblin Barrel — a Bowler behind the Crown Tower, released as the Barrel's shadow passes the enemy Crown Tower so his deploy finishes as the Goblins land, kills all three even if the placement is mixed up.
- Hog Rider, Royal Hogs and Wall Breakers — knockback stops them after at most one Tower hit, leaving the Bowler at full health for a counterpush.
- Battle Ram, Ram Rider and Evolved Royal Recruits — their charges are reset by the knockback so they never connect, while he takes minimal damage.
- Elite Barbarians, Royal Ghost, Lumberjack, Monk and Mini P.E.K.K.A — with Crown Tower help, knockback plus high hitpoints beat them and preserve most of his health.
- Barbarians — constant knockback delays them long enough for him to grind them down.
- Little Prince and Goblinstein — he can line up to hit both the Little Prince and Guardienne, and knockback resets the ramp-up attack.
- X-Bow and Mortar — he has the hitpoints to contest them from range without needing another mini tank, and the linear attack punishes poorly placed defenders.
- Support troops behind a tank — the piercing attack reaches them, and since tanks are immune to knockback he separates the tank from its support.
- Mini tanks — Knight, Valkyrie, Battle Healer and Golden Knight cannot deal with him thanks to his health and knockback.

## Weak against

- All air troops — he cannot target air, so Minions, Minion Horde, Bats, Mega Minion, Phoenix, Flying Machine, Electro Dragon and Inferno Dragon kill him without retaliation, several for a positive trade.
- Swarms placed on top of him — Evolved Skeletons, Skeleton Army, Royal Recruits or Barbarians can overwhelm him if they surround him, because his attack rate is slow.
- Tanks — his slow hit speed means he cannot take down high-hitpoint units on his own.
- Prince and Dark Prince — immune to knockback, so their charges are never reset and they reach him for massive damage.
- Giant Skeleton, P.E.K.K.A and Mega Knight — knockback-immune with the hitpoints and damage to beat him and survive for a counterattack, despite the negative Elixir trade.
- Skeleton King and Goblin Machine — knockback-immune and cheaper, great value counters when placed on top (do not activate the Skeleton King's ability, to avoid needless damage).
- Mighty Miner — if he reaches the Bowler or is placed on top he charges up safely and wins for a positive Elixir trade.
- Cannon Cart and Sparky — knockback-immune and outrange him; the Cannon Cart stays in range even under 50% hitpoints and Sparky two-shots him.
- Defensive buildings — they all outrange him and never get pushed away; the Inferno Tower is especially effective with its ramp-up damage.
- Elite Barbarians, Royal Ghost, Lumberjack, Monk and Mini P.E.K.K.A placed directly on top (ideally surrounding him) — they flip the matchup in their favor.
- Fisherman placed on the fourth tile vertically from the King's Tower and third tile horizontally from the Crown Tower — hooks him into a King Tower activation.
- Firecracker on the fifth tile from the bridge and second tile horizontally from the Crown Tower — her recoil kiting draws him toward the King Tower and activates it on his second attack.

## Notes / synergies

- Role: 5 Elixir ground-only splash support and defensive card — high hitpoints, high damage, slow attack, piercing linear boulder with knockback.
- Effective reach is much longer than his 4 tile range: the rock rolls 7.5 tiles with a 1.8 tile radius, giving about 9.3 tiles of threat, so defenders should be spread out and never placed directly in front of their Crown Tower.
- Knockback works like a Fireball, Giant Snowball or Rocket: it resets attack animations and charges on small and medium ground troops, but barely moves heavy units (Prince, Sparky, Dark Prince, Skeleton King, Giant Skeleton, Goblin Machine, P.E.K.K.A, Mega Knight, Cannon Cart, Mighty Miner).
- Line enemies up with Tornado to maximize the piercing damage.
- Pairs extremely well with Ice Wizard and Electro Wizard — slow and stun plus knockback form a near impenetrable defensive wall; Ice Spirit and Electro Spirit do the same cheaply.
- Against tanks, support him with faster, higher-DPS troops: Musketeer, Wizard, Dart Goblin, Cannon Cart, Flying Machine, Magic Archer, Baby Dragon, Evolved Archers, Archer Queen, Skeleton Dragons, Little Prince or Witch.
- Great support for X-Bow and Mortar, knocking melee attackers back to buy the siege building more attack time.
- Great support for the Electro Giant — he pushes troops into the Zap Pack radius and forces them to damage themselves, while the Electro Giant soaks hits including from air troops neither can attack; knockback-immune cards like P.E.K.K.A and Giant Skeleton beat the duo.
- He is spell-resistant, surviving an equal-level Rocket, which makes him a durable support troop, but he costs more than most supports and can be overwhelmed by swarms.
- Miner is a poor answer to him: the knockback can make the Miner retarget onto another troop, leaving the Bowler healthy to defend the push the Miner was supporting.
- His hitbox is unusually large, matching a Mega Knight's; when surrounded by friendly swarms this can shield them from melee splash troops like Royal Ghost and Dark Prince.
- Executioner is a reasonable substitute — faster hit speed and hits air, but lower health and cannot beat ground mini tanks; the Bowler's knockback covers ground units instead.
- After a successful defense he usually survives with most of his health, making him a natural start to a counterpush.
