# Three Musketeers

> *In-game description:* "An elite trio of powerful, independent markswomen with perfectly coiffed helmets and bayoneted boomsticks. They slash, shoot and slay in the name of justice and honor."

## Overview

The Three Musketeers is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Royal Arena (Arena 7). It spawns three single-target, air-targeting, long-ranged, ground troops with moderate hitpoints and moderate damage. Their deployment is in a triangle formation. If enemy troops are close to them, they switch to being single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troops with the same hitpoints and very high damage, and switch back to their ranged form when enemies are far from them. The Three Musketeers resemble the Musketeer, with the first one having identical eyes and hair, the second one having longer hair with thicker bangs, enough to cover her eyes, and the third one instead being pink-colored, similar to an Archer, and having a scar on one of her eyes. All three wear a team-colored cassock with a gold trim, white breeches, a metal tricorne with a team-colored crest and a gold handle, a belt with a crown buckle, and long boots, wielding longer blunderbuss muskets with a bayonet attached to the front. A Three Musketeers card costs 9 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Three independent, royal, very powerful markswomen with high burst damage, moderate hitpoints and long range. They can stop almost anything with their powerful bayonets, attacking slower than the lone Musketeer, but having more power in their little knives attached to their good old (but improved) boomsticks. However, they're a very heavy investment (9 whopping Elixir!) and they're vulnerable to the heaviest of spells, so watch out for net losses!
