# Dark Prince

> *In-game description:* "The Dark Prince deals area damage and lets his spiked club do the talking for him - because when he does talk, it sounds like he has a bucket on his head."

**Related:** [Hero variant: dark-prince-hero](../heroes/dark-prince-hero.md)

## Overview

The Dark Prince is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Royal Arena (Arena 7). It spawns an area-damage, ground-targeting, melee, ground troop with high hitpoints and high damage with shields in addition to his normal hitpoints. After he travels 3 tiles, he will begin to charge towards his target, and deals double damage if he hits the target whilst charging. The charge will hit all enemies in a 360º area around him. He is able to jump over the river. The Dark Prince takes an appearance as a man who has blue eyes, black eyebrows, dark brown hair, and is mostly covered in dark steel armor, wearing a bucket with a team-colored feather on his head, a team-colored belt, and brown shoes with dark steel plates on them. He carries a spiked club and a shield with a crown emblem on it, and rides a pony which is identical to that of the Prince's, but is darker. A Dark Prince card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
For 4 Elixir, the lesser known Prince has moderately high hitpoints, and a shield to absorb any hit. When attacking, he has a moderately strong melee splash that hits in front, and like his cousin, the Prince, a charge attack that's just like his regular attack albeit dealing double damage alongside a pony that can jump over the river. He is flexible for both defense and counter-attacks, but is vulnerable to high damage units.

If its **Hero** is active, the horse gets promoted to the rhino, while the Dark Prince allows you to use the **Destructive Dismount** ability for 3 Elixir. This lets the Dark Prince to dismount from his trusted (and strengthened) companion, while dealing impact damage. The rhino jumps and charges towards towers as the Dark Prince stays behind with shield and his mace. This will pressure two places at once, but the rhino can be knocked back.
