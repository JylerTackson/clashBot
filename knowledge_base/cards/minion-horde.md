# Minion Horde

> *In-game description:* "Six fast, unarmored flying attackers. Three's a crowd, six is a horde!"

**Related:** [Evolution: minion-horde-evolution](../evolutions/minion-horde-evolution.md)

## Overview

The Minion Horde is a Common card that is unlocked from the Hog Mountain (Arena 10). It spawns six Minions in a hexagon formation. A Minion Horde card costs 5 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Gulp! A horde of 6 Minions for only 5 Elixir! This card has ridiculously high damage per second, allowing it to defeat tanks and pushes in the blink of an eye. But keep caution using them, otherwise you may lose everything... in the blink of an eye.

For 1 Cycle, if their **Evolution** is active, all 6 Minions will gain the **Vanishing Point** ability, making them invincible, invisiblem and protecting them from all sorts of damage and grant invisibility for 3 seconds! However, the ability only activates if the attacks don't instantly kill the Horde, and the Minions will slow down on speed and attack once they do activate it.
