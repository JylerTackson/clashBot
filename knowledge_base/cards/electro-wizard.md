# Electro Wizard

> *In-game description:* "He lands with a "POW!", stuns nearby enemies and shoots lightning with both hands! What a show off."

## Overview

The Electro Wizard is a Legendary card that is unlocked from the Electro Valley (Arena 11). It spawns a fast, multi-target, air-targeting, medium-ranged, ground troop with moderate hitpoints and moderate damage. The Electro Wizard's attack stuns his targets for 0.5 seconds. If 2 or more targets are within his range, his attack will split and attack the closest 2 units. He spawns with a Zap upon deployment. He appears as a Wizard with blue eyes, long ears, black spiky hair, eyebrows and moustache, wearing a team-colored suit with gold linings and wristbands, a belt, and black boots. An Electro Wizard card costs 4 Elixir to deploy.

## Abilities and special mechanics

### Modifiers

#### Common: Zip Zap Zop
> "+3 Electro Spirits"
Places 3 additional Electro Spirits around the Electro Wizard.
#### Rare: Quadruple Ruple
> "Chain attack 5 enemies"
Is able to both stun and chain 5 enemies, including the Crown Towers. Can double attack and stun an enemy again if it still has a chain left.
#### Epic: Chain Reaction
> "Chain attack all enemies"
Is able to both stun and chain every enemy across the board, including the Crown Towers. Will double attack and stun both all enemies and Crown Towers again.

## Strong against

- Hog Rider - reliably countered, allowing only one hit on the Tower, and the surviving Electro Wizard sets up a counterpush.
- Royal Hogs - place him on top so the spawn Zap chips them, then he hits two at a time; they cannot damage him, so he survives at full hitpoints.
- Ram Rider (unsupported) - a very reliable counter, fully countering one with Crown Tower support for a +1 Elixir trade.
- Inferno Dragon, Inferno Tower, Mighty Miner and Little Prince - his stun forces their damage ramp to reset.
- Sparky - his stun completely resets her charge.
- Prince, Dark Prince, Ram Rider, Battle Ram and Evolved Royal Recruits - the stun stops them charging.
- Bandit and Boss Bandit - the stun temporarily resets the wind-up before their dashes; it also interrupts Mega Knight's jumps and the Fisherman's hooks.
- Monk - his attack is not a projectile, so Pensive Protection cannot deflect it and he damages the Monk unharmed.
- Skeleton Army and Bats - his spawn damage (a Zap of equal Level) one-shots them; it also cripples Goblin Gang and Minion Horde.
- Skeleton Dragons - drop him directly on top rather than shooting from range so the spawn damage kills them faster.
- Goblin Barrel - deployed at the front of a Crown Tower he negates all but one stab, killing the front two Goblins via the Tower and one-shotting the last.
- Enemy ranged troops - his attack has no projectile and always connects instantly, so he can engage other ranged units from maximum range.

## Weak against

- Large swarms - he deals no splash damage, so only his one-time spawn Zap helps against them.
- Mini P.E.K.K.A., P.E.K.K.A. and a charging Prince - all can one-shot him.
- Fireball - he survives one of equivalent Level at full health, but he is still Fireball-vulnerable, so he must be spaced out from other support troops.
- Giant Snowball - knocks him back and slows him, letting a Prince connect his charge and defeat him instantly.
- High-hitpoint tanks - his moderate damage (halved again when it splits across two targets) makes little impression on them.
- Electro Giant - attacking it from inside the Zap Pack effectively hits himself twice; his range is longer than the Electro Giant's aura, so keep him as far back as possible.
- Ram Rider's snare - depending on Level difference and placement timing it can severely cripple his hitpoints before a counterpush.
- Slow-attacking troops such as Golem, Executioner, Hunter, Electro Dragon, Firecracker, Princess and Bowler - his zap freezes the attack frame rather than resetting non-charge attacks, so they still get their hits off.
- Mini-tanks and cheap distractions (Skeletons, Bats, Archers, Goblins, Spear Goblins, Minions, a Spirit) placed at least one tile in front of the Tower stop him doing damage; keeping them off the Tower's side also prevents his split attack chipping the Tower.

## Notes / synergies

- Typical role: 4 Elixir support/defensive troop whose value is the stun, not the damage.
- Each zap deals half his full damage, so two targets take half each and a single target takes the full listed amount (effectively double a single hit).
- His spawn damage behaves identically to a Zap of equal Level, but spending him on a swarm is usually a negative Elixir trade - do it only in an emergency or when he will immediately get further value.
- A cloned Electro Wizard does not inflict spawn damage; an Electro Wizard fed by the Royal Chef keeps his spawn damage unaffected.
- A Sparky is immune to stun while being cloned - if the opponent runs Sparky plus Clone, wait for the Clone before dropping him on her.
- Because he resets Inferno Dragon, Mighty Miner, Little Prince and Sparky, he is excellent support for a Giant, P.E.K.K.A, Elixir Golem, Mega Knight, Lava Hound, Balloon, Giant Skeleton or Golem push.
- Pairs well with mini tanks - Knight, Lumberjack, Valkyrie, Dark Prince, Ice Golem, Prince, Mini P.E.K.K.A, Monk, Skeleton King, Golden Knight, Mighty Miner, Battle Healer, Goblin Machine - which tank while his stun makes the Tower retarget onto them.
- Strong with Graveyard: his stun disrupts the Crown Tower's attack speed so more Skeletons spawn; place the Graveyard slightly later so he tanks a few Tower hits first.
- He is best played behind tanks (Knight, Valkyrie, Skeleton King, Golem, Battle Healer, Giant) given his moderate health.
- On offence he can be dropped on top of an Inferno Tower to force it to reset and retarget.
- Against an Electro Giant, pair him with P.E.K.K.A. or Royal Ghost - timed right his stun lands in the same window as their attack, so they take no reflection damage.
- He benefits a lot from Rage, stunning more frequently, though even then he cannot fully lock down slower attackers.
- Against Lumberloon, protect him and make sure the Balloon walks in front of the Lumberjack for a full counter.
- Attacking technique: send him in front of shorter-ranged troops so he tanks a few Tower hits until the shorter-ranged unit passes him, after which his hit makes the Tower retarget - high payoff but high risk if the opponent drops a troop on him.
- Precise Goblin Barrel tech: against a Barrel centred on the Crown Tower, deploying him about 2-3 tiles after it crosses the river lets the spawn damage hit all three Goblins - situational, useful mainly when the Goblins are being tanked for.
- King Tower activation: when he crosses the bridge alone and stops to attack, a Tornado placed 1 tile above and 2 tiles beside the Crown Tower pulls him into King Tower range so his split attack hits both Towers; only attempt at full health.
- Never ignore a lone Electro Wizard unless a Crown Tower can finish him in 1-2 shots - his single-target damage is effectively doubled.
- His multi-target attack does not cause him to retarget: adding Archers after he has locked onto a Tower only puts one Archer in the split; push him out of range of his current target instead.
- Zappies and Electro Dragon can replace him in some decks (split lanes, bulk, flight), but his speed and longer range suit fast decks such as Battle Ram.
