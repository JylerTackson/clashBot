# Inferno Tower

> *In-game description:* "Defensive building, roasts targets for damage that increases over time. Burns through even the biggest and toughest enemies!"

## Overview

The Inferno Tower is a Rare card that is unlocked from the Spell Valley (Arena 4). It spawns a single-target, air-targeting, long-ranged, building with high hitpoints and various damage stage. The Inferno Tower's damage increases over time as it remains focused on a single target. It appears as a tall, narrow building made of iron, straddling on top of a lava pool, with a glowing orange lens on top and two team-colored spokes on the sides. An Inferno Tower card costs 5 Elixir to deploy.

<!-- Card Overviews summary (wiki) -->
Things get really hot with this building. For 5 Elixir, this incinerator will roast even massive tanks and powerful support units in an instant for a neutral, if not positive, Elixir trade. However, beware of swarms and the power of electrickery, as this building will be overwhelmed and paralyzed.
