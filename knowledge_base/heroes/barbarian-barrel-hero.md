# Heroic Barbarian Barrel

> *In-game description:* "They see him rollin', they hatin'! No one clears the path like Hero Barbarian Barrel, who willingly gave up his rock music career for the resilience of a second roll."

**Base card:** [barbarian-barrel.md](../cards/barbarian-barrel.md)

## Overview

The Heroic Barbarian is a Epic card that is unlocked with 200 Hero Shards. It is an area-damage, ground-targeting spell with a short rectangular area and moderate damage. It can only be deployed on the player's own side. Once the spell reaches its destination, it spawns a single Barbarian. It is a spiked barrel with a Barbarian inside it.A Heroic Barbarian Barrel card costs 2 Elixir to deploy.

## Ability: Rowdy Reroll

*Barrels down the lane a second time for maximum impact.* (costs 1 Elixir; 2 + 1 = 3 total)

### **Ability: Rowdy Reroll**

> "Barrels down the lane a second time for maximum impact."
The Hero Knight has a special ability accessible from whatever side of the screen was put in, just above the player's card slots in battle. When activatet, the Barbarian Barrel will roll for a second time, while healling the barbarian for 50% of the damage. The Rowdy Reroll ability costs 1 Elixir to activate.

### **Strategy**

- The ability can get the barbarian closer to the crown tower while healing some hp, forcing more elixir on defense.
- The roll can also kill more swarms, or get behind troops.
