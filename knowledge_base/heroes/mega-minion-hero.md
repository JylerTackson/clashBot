# Heroic Mega Minion

> *In-game description:* "In the Arena full of uncertainty, Hero Mega Minion wants to make sure he can get to the right place in just the nick of time. Learning to shift through the shadows doesn't happen overnight!"

**Base card:** [mega-minion.md](../cards/mega-minion.md)

## Overview

The Heroic Mega Minion is a Rare card that is unlocked with 200 Hero Shards. It appears as an enlarged Minion wearing a set of armor with a mask, chainmail, and a team-colored belt. A Heroic Mega Minion card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

## Ability: Wounding Warp

*Warps to the lowest-hitpoint enemy, dealing damage on arrival.* (costs 2 Elixir; 3 + 2 = 5 total)

### Ability: Wounding Warp

> "Warps to the lowest-Hitpoint enemy, dealing damage on arrival."
The Hero Mega Minion has a special ability accessible from whatever side of the screen was put in, just above the player's card slots in battle. When deployed, a marker will be deployed to the lowest hitpoint target. When that unit dies, the mark will move to the next unit. When the ability is preased, Mega Minion will teleport to that tile, dealing damage. Afterwards the Mega Minion will behave normally, but with -75% on the tower. The Wounding Warp ability costs 2 Elixir to activate.
