# Knight/Hero

> *In-game description:* "Imagine a mustache so magnificent it lures enemies to their doom! Hero Knight takes great care to wax his shield and mustache in equal measure, so as to always twist the tide of battle in his favor."

**Base card:** [knight.md](../cards/knight.md)

## Overview

The Heroic Knight is a Common card that is unlocked with 200 Hero Shards. It spawns one single-target, ground-targeting, melee, and ground troop with high hitpoints and moderate damage. The Heroic Knight takes the appearance of a man who has glowing yellow eyes, a bigger blond-colored mustache, goatee and eyebrows, and wears chainmail, a golden chestplate, a team-colored cape, a belt, platinum arm pads with a golden base, and golden shoes with platinum plates and straps on them, while wielding a large saber as his weapon and carrying a large platinum shield with a golden handle, base, mustache and goatee, along with a Hero Shard in the middle. A Heroic Knight card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

## Ability: Triumphant Taunt

*Gains a shield and taunts nearby enemies, forcing them to attack him.* (costs 2 Elixir; 3 + 2 = 5 total)

### Ability: Triumphant Taunt

> "Gains a shield and taunts nearby enemies, forcing them to attack him."
The Hero Knight has a special ability accessible from whatever side of the screen was put in, just above the player's card slots in battle. After a 1-second delay, the Hero Knight activates his “Triumphant Taunt”, taunting every enemy troop and building in a 7.5-tiles range, causing them to attack him. He also gains a shield. Both the shield and the taunt effect last for 5 seconds, unless the former is destroyed, although enemy troops and buildings will still target him afterwards until he is defeated. The Triumphant Taunt ability costs 2 Elixir to activate.
