# Heroic Wizard

> *In-game description:* "After a decade of demolishing King Towers, Hero Wizard has learned to wield fire for flight! His wings were forged in flame and fortune, but they’re mysteriously cool to the touch."

**Base card:** [wizard.md](../cards/wizard.md)

## Overview

The Hero Wizard is a Rare card that is unlocked by spending 200 hero coins at once. The Hero Wizard takes the appearance of a man who has blonde eyebrows, mustache and goatee, and wears a beige cloak, a team-colored shirt, loincloth and pants, with all of them having a gold outline, a belt with a Hero Shard in the middle, two gold bracelets on his arms, and a pair of brown boots, while donning a pair of fiery golden wings. This card costs 5 Elixirto put on the arena and an additional 2 Elixir to activate his ability.

## Ability: Fiery Flight

*Launches into the air and throws fearsome fire tornadoes.* (costs 1 Elixir; 5 + 1 = 6 total)

### Ability: Fiery Flight

> "Launches into the air and throws fearsome fire tornadoes."
The Hero Wizard has a special ability accessible from whatever side of the screen was put in, just above the player's card slots in battle. After a 1-second delay, the Hero Wizard activates his fiery flight, which makes him take flight for 5 seconds. While he is flying, not only he will get a 50% movement speed increase (now classified as fast), his fireballs also create 3 tile radius tornadoes, which does its own damage (reduced against crown towers), similar to the Evolved Valkyrie. The ability costs 2 Elixir to activate.
