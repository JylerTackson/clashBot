# Heroic Balloon

> *In-game description:* "These skyward skellies were never satisfied while six feet under! Aeri and her skeleton crew are known for their semi-well-crafted wings which allow them to dive-bomb without breaking any bones."

**Base card:** [balloon.md](../cards/balloon.md)

## Overview

The Heroic Balloon is a Epic card that is unlocked with 200 Hero Shards. It spawns a single-target, building-targeting, melee, air troop with high hitpoints and very high damage. When defeated, it drops a bomb which explodes after 3 seconds, dealing moderate damage. It is a hot air balloon with a team-colored envelope and a wooden basket with sacks held together by patches and ropes, and is piloted by a Bomber. A Heroic Balloon card costs 5 Elixir to deploy.

## Ability: Coffin Cadet

*A Skeletrooper soars to the nearest enemy on the ground, dealing landing damage.* (costs 2 Elixir; 5 + 2 = 7 total)

### Ability: Coffin Cadets

> "A Skeletrooper soars to the nearest enemy on the ground, dealing landing damage."
The Hero Knight has a special ability accessible from whatever side of the screen was put in, just above the player's card slots in battle. Once pressed, a skeletropper will fly towards the closest ground unit within 6 tiles, deling spawn damage. The skeletropper will the attack, dealing less damage to the crown tower. The Coffin Cadets ability costs 2 Elixir to activate.
