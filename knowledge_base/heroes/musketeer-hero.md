# Heroic Musketeer

> *In-game description:* "Hero Musketeer has a new best friend: the boom building! This scrappy guy shoots fast and hard, making her boomstick suuuuuper jealous."

**Base card:** [musketeer.md](../cards/musketeer.md)

## Overview

The Musketeer is a Rare card that is unlocked in the Training Camp. It spawns one single-target, air-targeting, long-ranged, and ground troop with moderate hitpoints and moderate damage. The Heroic Musketeer takes the appearance of a woman who has golden glowing eyes, coiffed curly hair and eyebrows, and wears a gold breastplate, chainmail, a team-colored cape, a beige helmet with a gold handle in a similar style helmet as one of the Three Musketeers helmets, a belt, beige shoulder pads with gold handles as their base, brown gloves and boots with golden soles and chains, while carrying a beige blunderbuss-styled musket with a golden trigger and muzzle as her weapon with dark gray spheres as her ammunition. She also wields a turret with a large gold handle and base supported by a metal handle in-between and a long, gold and black striped tube, while having the same hat as the Musketeer. A Heroic Musketeer card costs 4 Elixir to deploy and her ability costs an additional 3 Elixir to activate.

## Ability: Trusty Turret

*Spawns a short-range, rapid-firing auto-turret that deals landing damage in front of the Musketeer.* (costs 3 Elixir; 4 + 3 = 7 total)

### Ability: Trusty Turret

> "Spawns a short-range auto-turret in front of the Musketeer."
The Hero Musketeer has a special ability accessible from whatever side of the screen was put in, just above the player's card slots in battle. After a 1-second delay, the Hero Musketeer activates her “Trusty Turret”, placing a turret 3 tiles forward of the Musketeer, which is used either as a distraction for regular troops, and as a building to attract win conditions. This turret has a lifetime of 10 seconds, meaning that this is meant to be used reactively as it's not meant to last that long, unless the building is destroyed before the Decay kills it. The Trusty Turret ability costs 3 Elixir to activate.
