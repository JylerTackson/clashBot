# Heroic Ice Golem

> *In-game description:* "In a chilling return to the Arena, Hero Ice Golem keeps enemies at bay with a brain-freezing blizzard! The only things he has to fear are fireballs and a hot summer's day."

**Base card:** [ice-golem.md](../cards/ice-golem.md)

## Overview

The Hero Ice Golem is a Rare card that is unlocked by unlocking 200 Hero Shards and choosing the Hero Ice Golem. The Heroic Ice Golem takes the appearance of a large creature made out of snow with glowing yellow eyes and golden ice shards on his head, with his hands being also colored golden and his nose and rocks on his spine being in the form of coal. He also dons a team-colored cape, two heavy golden armpads and gauntlets, and a gold headpiece with the Hero Shard in the center. This card costs 2Elixirto put on the arena and an additional 2 Elixir to activate his ability.

## Ability: Snowstorm

*Conjures a blizzard that damages and slows nearby enemies.* (costs 2 Elixir; 2 + 2 = 4 total)

### Ability: Snowstorm

> "Conjures a blizzard that damages and slows enemies."
The Hero Ice Golem has a special ability accessible from whatever side of the screen was put in, just above the player's card slots in battle. After a 1-second delay, the Hero Ice Golem activates his "Blizzard" ability, which generates a 4-tile aura radius that has 3 pulses, with each one slowing down enemy troops and dealing damage. The Snowstorm ability costs 2 Elixir to activate.
