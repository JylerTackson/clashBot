# Heroic Dark Prince

> *In-game description:* "Like his rider, Rhino has endured many a battle. Now that he and Hero Dark Prince have found each other, they can break through any tower, or Skeleton, together."

**Base card:** [dark-prince.md](../cards/dark-prince.md)

## Overview

The Heroic Dark Prince is an Epic card that is unlocked from the Royal Arena (Arena 7). It spawns an area-damage, ground-targeting, melee, ground troop with high hitpoints and high damage with shields in addition to his normal hitpoints. After he travels 3 tiles, he will begin to charge towards his target, and deals double damage if he hits the target whilst charging. The charge will hit all enemies in a 360º area around him. He is able to jump over the river. The Heroic Dark Prince takes the appearance of a man who has glowing yellow eyes, black eyebrows, dark brown hair, and is mostly covered in somber armor with golden spikes, accents and trim on it, wearing a bucket with a team-colored feather on his head and pearl white horns on the sides, a large pearl white belt with a golden spikes in the center and a gold trim, a team-colored cape, and brown sandals. He carries a golden-spiked mace and a large, cracked somber shield with golden spikes, a yellow base, and a dark Hero Shard on it, and rides a Rhino with a black fur and a mane, two horns on the front and back, with one being larger and having two golden bands and a glowing yellow top, a short, pointed hair on its chin held by a golden ring and wearing team-colored reins, a white saddle with a gold trim on its back, and somber bands on its front legs, upper mane and neck with golden spikes on each. A Heroic Dark Prince card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

## Ability: Destructive Dismount

*Dismount doing damage, attacking on foot while Rhino charges buildings.* (costs 3 Elixir; 4 + 3 = 7 total)

### Ability: Destructive Dismount

> "Dismount doing damage, attacking on foot while Rhino charges buildings."
The Hero Dark Prince has a special ability accessible from whatever side of the screen was put in, just above the player's card slots in battle. After pressing the ability, the Dark Prince will jump off the Rhino, dealing spawn damage on impact. The Rhino charges towards buildings, while the Dark Prince deals splash damage to troops but loses his charge in the process. The Destructive Dismount ability costs 3 Elixir to activate.
