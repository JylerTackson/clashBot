# Heroic Bowler

> *In-game description:* "This globetrotter and his boulder needed new records to break. After baseball broke the bleachers and football flattened all the Goblins, they found their way onto the court. From there, it was nothing but net!"

**Base card:** [bowler.md](../cards/bowler.md)

## Overview

The Heroic Bowler is a **Epic** card that is unlocked by using 200 Hero Coins on the card. It spawns a slow medium-ranged, ground-targeting troop that has high hitpoints and deals high area damage. Like his normal counterpart, he throws a projectile with a large area of effect that travels its maximum range, dealing knockback and piercing through enemies. He takes the appearance of a massive, indigo-skinned humanoid creature with glowing yellow eyes, wearing a white headband with golden outlines, athletic shorts with a leather belt with a Hero Shard in the middle, a plaster on his left arm with team-colored outlines, and golden-and-white sneakers, and carries a boulder that appears as a basketball and has glowing yellow eyes. A Heroic Bowler card costs 5 Elixir to deploy, and a further 2 Elixir to activate his ability.

## Ability: Stone Swish

*Plants his feet and throws boulders with increased range.* (costs 2 Elixir; 5 + 2 = 7 total)

### Ability: Stone Swish

> "Plants his feet and throws boulders with increased range."
The Hero Bowler has a special ability accessible from whatever side of the screen was put in, just above the player's card slots in battle. After 2.5sec of the ability pressed, the bowler will change the attack to a long-ranged mortar attack for 7.3sec. Getting a total of 3 shots during that duration.
