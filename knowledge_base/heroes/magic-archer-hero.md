# Heroic Magic Archer

> *In-game description:* "With every pinecone a target and every branch a new vantage point, Hero Magic Archer was favored to be Forest Gang's first Hero. Being Boss Bandit's right hand comes with a few perks!"

**Base card:** [magic-archer.md](../cards/magic-archer.md)

## Overview

The Heroic Magic Archer is a Common card that is unlocked with 200 Hero Shards. It spawns an area damage, air-targeting, long-ranged, ground troop with moderate hitpoints and moderate damage. The Magic Archer launches an attack that travels up to its maximum range, while piercing through enemies. He takes the appearance of a man with glowing yellow eyes, blonde hair with a team-colored stripe on the front side, bushy eyebrows and sideburns, wears a dark green tunic and pants, a beige cape with a gold lining and a Hero Shard on the center, a brown belt with a triangular copper rune and fingerless gloves with gold cuffs and a beige guard with a gold lining on the right, and dark brown boots with a gold handle on each, and wields a glowing yellow bow with beige stabilizers with gold trimmings. He also appears to be carrying a keychain on his waist that appears to be the Boss Bandit. A Heroic Magic Archer card costs 3 Elixir to deploy.

## Ability: Triple Threat

*Summons a decoy while darting back and fires a triple shot on his next attack.* (costs 2 Elixir; 4 + 2 = 6 total)

### Ability: Triple Threat

> "Summons a decoy while darting back and fires a triple shot on his next attack."
The Hero Magic Archer has a special ability accessible from whatever side of the screen was put in, just above the player's card slots in battle. When activated, the Magic Archer will teleport back, while leaving a decoy in his place. The next attack now shots 3 arrows that travel longer, but with less damage. The Triple Threat ability costs 2 Elixir to activate.
