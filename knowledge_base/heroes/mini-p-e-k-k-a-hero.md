# Heroic Mini P.E.K.K.A.

> *In-game description:* "Hero Mini P.E.K.K.A has finally perfected his pancake recipe by speeding up cooking time with each attack! Stacks on stacks on stacks!"

**Base card:** [mini-p-e-k-k-a.md](../cards/mini-p-e-k-k-a.md)

## Overview

The Heroic Mini P.E.K.K.A. is a Rare card that is unlocked with 200 hero fragments. It spawns one single-target, ground-targeting, melee, and ground troop with high hitpoints and high damage. The Heroic Mini P.E.K.K.A takes the appearance of a robot-looking troop similar to Mini P.E.K.K.A. who wears gold metallic armor with two pearl white horns, shoulder and arm pads, golden spikes on his arms and a single round glowing eye, along with a team-colored cape and faulds, while wielding both a cracked refined sword with a pearl white base, a golden hilt and a wooden grip and a golden spatula with a small golden spike near the top. A Heroic Mini P.E.K.K.A. card costs 4 Elixir to deploy, and his ability costs an additional 1 Elixir to activate.

## Ability: Breakfast Boost

*Eats pancakes to level up. The longer you wait, the more pancakes he cooks!* (costs 1 Elixir; 4 + 1 = 5 total)

### Ability: Breakfast Boost

> "Eats pancakes to level up. The longer you wait, the more pancakes he cooks!"
The Heroic Mini P.E.K.K.A. has a special ability accessible from whatever side of the screen was put in, just above the player's card slots in battle. After a 1-second delay, the Mini P.E.K.K.A. activates his "Breakfast Boost", immediately eats all the pancakes he cooked, granting him extra Levels and healing himself for 30%.

Heroic Mini P.E.K.K.A. can constantly gain progress for his pancake meters. He will need 22 seconds to get one pancake meter filled, but he can also get 10 seconds of progress with every attack. Every filled pancake meter increases the Levels gained when his ability is used by 1, except the last meter grants 2 extra Levels. With no meters, the Heroic Mini P.E.K.K.A. will gain 1 Level, but with a maximum of 3 meters, he can gain 5 Levels. The amount of meters the Heroic Mini P.E.K.K.A. has can be seen in a golden bar above his hitpoint bar.The Breakfast Boost ability costs 1 Elixir to activate.
