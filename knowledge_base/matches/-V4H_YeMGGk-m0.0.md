## Lessons stated by Ryley

All of these are balance-change opinions, not piloting advice; none is tied to a play he makes.

- **39-49s** — "Starting off, guys, which I do think was the most controversial change about the balance changes for the next season, it is the nerf to the spirits."
- **53-63s** — on the version that was tested first: "the one that they had us test on the developer build, was the nerf to the running speed, which I really did not like that one. It just felt like wrong. It just didn't make sense in my mind for the spirits to run as slow as they did."
- **65-83s** — what shipped instead: "spirits are getting a health nerf, which will make them now get one-shot by cards like [archers](../cards/archers.md), [archer queen](../cards/archer-queen.md), [bomber](../cards/bomber.md), [musketeer](../cards/musketeer.md), [Tesla](../cards/tesla.md)… making them just a little bit worse defensively, but not make them completely useless against cards like bowler and [executioner](../cards/executioner.md), still making them always jump on those types of cards, which I am, I guess, a fan of."
- **95-121s** — the Goblinstein change, over his own Doctor on the field: "we are getting a massive buff to the Goblin Giant… they're giving it a 47% buff to the damage on the doctor. You see the doctor right here? It doesn't really do too much damage, but it will be doing 47% more damage next season, but to compensate… they are nerfing the ability. The damage per second will be going down by 12%, which I think it's definitely fair to give a compensation nerf to a card where they gave such a big buff to." (The transcript's "Goblin Giant" is almost certainly a mangling of Goblinstein — see Data gaps.)
- **134-160s** — the [Little Prince](../cards/little-prince.md) charge rule: "they are actually buffing the little prince… It's getting a very strange balance that I've never actually seen in the game before… Do you know how like the little prince gets reset every single time it moves? Now it has a 0.3 second window where it can move but not be reset. It has to be moving for more than 0.3 seconds for it to be reset to lose that maximum charge."
- **161-175s** — why it mattered: "the little prince just when it would move, it would just become so bad. And more often than not, you wouldn't be able to charge it up more than one time every time you had it down… And it should be a lot better." Plus the Guardian: "they are also buffing the guardian damage by 7% as well, which I guess is cool. The guardian does not really do too much damage, but I don't know if it was really necessary."
- **186-210s** — [Mighty Miner](../cards/mighty-miner.md): "They're buffing the mighty miner by 6%. Honestly, I don't think it was necessary. I think that the mighty miner is one of the best cards in the game. And I also thought that it was the best champion in the game, you know, not counting heroes just like the regular champions… It's going from 40 to 43 damage at tournament level, which is pretty cool though, cuz it is my favorite card, but I just didn't think that it needed a buff."
- **211-243s** — [Ronin](../cards/ronin.md) as a bug fix that reads as a nerf: "they are actually nerfing the ronin, but it's a bug fix… The ronin will now shoot 0.1 second slower, which will just kind of fix the bug with the [golden knight](../cards/golden-knight.md) where if you had a golden knight dashing onto a ronin, it would actually get reflected and the Gold Knight would not dash onto anything. I thought that was intentional, but apparently it was a bug and it was also messing up against the [Dark Prince](../cards/dark-prince.md) as well where it would just one-shot the Dark Prince instead of just taking off the shield."
- **244-278s** — [X-Bow](../cards/x-bow.md): "they are making the hit speed go a little bit slower, but in return… they are buffing the damage and they are actually buffing the projectile speed as well. I think that is to fix the X-Bow where like it will shoot at a skeleton three times and it only needs two times to kill the skeleton… a nice quality of life change for the X-Bow players, but it could make X-Bow just a little bit too strong in my opinion if they're not careful."
- **279-313s** — the hero [Ice Golem](../cards/ice-golem.md) rework: "Instead of having a [freeze](../cards/freeze.md) effect… they are now just having it do three different slow downs and in my opinion, that is very good because I do think that the hero Ice Golem was very, very good and way too good against units that it was bad against… If you had a deck where you couldn't really defend their [Hog Rider](../cards/hog-rider.md), they popped the ability, they get like five hits, there's nothing they are able to do. But because they are taking away the freeze effect, they are actually making it do way more damage… It will actually be able to take out [Goblins](../cards/goblins.md), [Minions](../cards/minions.md) and stuff like that."
