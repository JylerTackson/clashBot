This is the third deck of a four-deck showcase, and Ryley names it himself at t=457: "now we're going to
be playing with graveyard poison." The list read from the HUD and the deploy labels is
[Graveyard](../cards/graveyard.md), [Poison](../cards/poison.md), [Furnace](../cards/furnace.md),

[control](../archetypes/control.md): two spawners drip chip and defensive bodies, Knight / Barbarian
Barrel / Ice Spirit hold the left lane, and every offensive commitment is the same Graveyard + Berserker
(+ Poison) package on the opponent's left tower. The opponent — Ryley calls him "Aragon" at t=585 — is a
cheap [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md) list built on [Goblin Drill](../cards/goblin-drill.md),
[Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md), [Bomber](../cards/bomber.md) and an Evolved

match is a one-lane race that Ryley's Graveyard wins: enemy left tower 3052 -> 1560 by clock 1:05, 828 by

Berserker + Graveyard + the ability at OT 1:31 — "Let's pop the ability now. He just can't defend it. Come
on, Berserker. Berserker go crazy. Skelly, let's go. GG's. Nice win" (t=654-659). The running theme of the

(t=607) to "the Berserker not dying gets us so much extra damage, which is just so nice for us" (t=626).

- t=471-479 — a hero ability can license a Graveyard you would otherwise not cast: "Normally I wouldn't,
  but because I have the ability... I can pop the ability now, keep it alive, but it's going to tank for

  damage and force out a lot of extra elixir."

  reduce tower damage whenever it's in the ability... it would do more if there wasn't a reduction."

  bridge, right? Cuz it's not going to connect to the tower anyway."
- t=548-552 — attack into an opponent who is missing his key defensive card: "especially cuz he had no
  berserker in cycle, so it's going to force out a lot more elixir."

  "Actually, let's Goblin Hut first because I think he can't really take this out, and stocking up the
  Goblin Huts could be pretty nice for us."
- t=610-620 — a 3-elixir ability gives you room to be wrong: "The one thing about the Berserker ability
  being three elixir, which I like, is that you do have room to do bad abilities... unlike some heroes,
  right, like the ability being so cheap like goblins, you can never really do a bad one."
