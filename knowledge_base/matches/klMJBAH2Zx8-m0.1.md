Game 2 of 6 in a single-deck session (video time 226.2-518.2s), Ryley on the Evolution Royal Hogs cycle list the whole video is built around: Royal Hogs, Mighty Miner, Cannon, Skeletons, Electro Spirit, Baby Dragon, The Log and Lightning, with the evolution slots on Royal Hogs and Baby Dragon. He also has the evo snowball" — a Golden Knight log-bait shell. He flags it immediately as a bad matchup: "This actually might be one of those difficult matchups that I was talking about here" (250-253s), the specific problem being that Evolution Giant Snowball answers his Evolution Royal Hogs ("whenever I get to the evo piggies, he's going to have the evo snowball for defense", 244-247s) and that back-line Princesses chip him for free while he cannot spend The Log on them.

The middle of the game is a long defensive grind that he explicitly chooses. At 321-334s, after conceding a big chunk on the left (own left tower 4858 -> 2938 HP between t=322 and t=326), he says the damage "was for the greater good just to have like... not to let him have control of the game. At least this way it should be kind of like a stalemate where like I'm just constantly defending his pushes but I'll be able to do it really really well." That is what the next ninety seconds are: Cannon in the middle roughly every 20 seconds, Skeletons and Electro Spirit on everything that crosses, Mighty Miner blocking the bridge, and Royal Hogs sent in split or single-lane for chip whenever the opponent overcommits.

The game goes to overtime and is decided by one push. "That was one of the biggest pushes I've ever made" (492-495s). "Guys, we won. There's no way for them to get to our tower and we already have the Evo baby dragon for defense" (499-504s).

## Opponent

"Yaco" plays a Golden Knight log-bait deck that Ryley identifies in full at 230-240s before seeing most of it: Wall Breakers, Skeletons, Dark Prince, Goblin Barrel, Rascals, Golden Knight, Princess and Evolution Giant Snowball. His read of the matchup is that the deck denies him both of his cheap answers at once — logging the Rascals or a Princess invites "golden knight goblin barrel" to chain onto his Skeletons (307-316s) — so he defends with Cannon plus Skeletons plus Electro Spirit and keeps The Log in hand for the barrel. The back-line Princesses are the opponent's chip engine and the reason six of the seven unidentified opponent events in this game are 269 HP hits on Ryley's right tower with no enemy unit in range; Ryley never answers them, on purpose.

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- 264-268s: log your own tower on read, not on sight. "I always like to just try to read the barrels. It's good practice. There's no reason not to in my opinion."
- 283-301s: deck substitutions if you do not have the Champion. "if you do not have the mighty miner or you don't like it, you can always replace it with say the golden knight. I wouldn't say the skeleton king but like the knight, the valkyrie, the golden knight, just a few cards like that cuz I know not all of you will have the mighty miner upgraded cuz champion cards are really really hard to upgrade. Or if you like the archer queen, you can even take out the baby dragon and put in the archer queen instead."
- 307-317s: do not spend your small spell on the bait when the follow-up chains onto your other answer. "I can't really log the rascals or the princess because he'll just go golden knight goblin barrel and chain up to my skeletons cuz if I do that then I'd have to skeletons."
- 321-334s: taking damage can be the correct play if it denies the opponent control. "this is going to be a lot of damage, guys, but I think it was for the greater good just to have like not necessarily control of the game but not to let him have control of the game. At least this way it should be kind of like a stalemate where I'm just constantly defending his pushes but I'll be able to do it really really well."
- 350-358s: predictive placements come from your own last card, not from guessing. "mighty miner predict a goblin barrel cuz then we can pop off the ability... Perfect prediction right there just knowing that if I log he'll probably barrel."
- 364-369s: a big spell on a chip unit is not worth the tempo it gives away. "these back princesses are so annoying and I don't even really want to lightning them cuz then he'll just pressure super hard."
- 400-405s: spending a Champion ability to keep a 3-elixir building alive is an elixir win. "We need to go for the cannon plus ability cuz I want to keep the cannon alive at least force him to spend more elixir."
- 436-443s: build the counterpush the opponent's spell list allows. "I just have to get a really big counter push, guys, cuz he doesn't have a big spell so I can try to stack up baby dragons if the moment presents itself."
- 475-482s: in overtime, keep re-applying the same pressure card and let it screen your support. "We just need to keep on spamming mighty miner... The baby dragons are still alive cuz the mighty miner walked in front."
