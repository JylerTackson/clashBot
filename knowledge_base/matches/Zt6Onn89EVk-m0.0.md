# Match: Hero Valkyrie is the Most BROKEN Hero of All Time — game 1

## Summary

This is the first of five games in a deck-showcase video whose whole premise is the new Hero
Valkyrie: "we are going to be trying out the new hero Valkyrie that is coming to Clash Royale...
I actually think that the hero Valkyrie is maybe the best hero, maybe of all time" (t=17-27).
The deck he pilots is Miner Wall Breakers control — he names it himself at t=118-121, "Let's go
for the Evo Wall Breakers Miner here, guys. And I think this Miner Wall Breakers deck will be
back, man." Deploy labels confirm [Miner](../cards/miner.md), [Wall Breakers](../cards/wall-breakers.md),
[Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md), [Magic Archer](../cards/magic-archer.md),
[Bomb Tower](../cards/bomb-tower.md), [The Log](../cards/the-log.md), [Tornado](../cards/tornado.md)
and the [Heroic Valkyrie](../heroes/valkyrie-hero.md) ([Valkyrie](../cards/valkyrie.md) as a Hero,
ability "Wild Whirlwind" for 3 elixir), with Evolution slots on Wall Breakers and Skeletons
(t=112, t=118). The opponent ("Aragon", t=266/287) runs a [Graveyard](../cards/graveyard.md) +
[Poison](../cards/poison.md) [control](../archetypes/control.md) shell with
[Cannon Cart](../cards/cannon-cart.md), Evo [Tesla](../cards/tesla.md), Evo
[Firecracker](../cards/firecracker.md), [Guards](../cards/guards.md), Evo
[Archers](../cards/archers.md) — and his own Hero Valkyrie, so this is a Valkyrie mirror.
The game is a long grind that goes to overtime: Ryley never loses a tower but his left tower is
worn from 3052 to 657 by Graveyard, Poison and the opponent's Valkyrie ability (his King also
takes ~730 in one Wild Whirlwind at clock 0:13-0:11), while he chips the enemy right tower from
3052 down to 116 with repeated Miner + Wall Breakers + Valkyrie-with-ability pushes. He calls the
result at t=281: "Let's go. Nice win, guys, and I will see you all in the next match."
