## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=1176-1179: "with these Lava Tombstone decks you usually don't Lava first play" — the Hound

  no support spends the deck's only real damage card for nothing.
- t=1216-1219: "he doesn't even have any support cards. He used his balloon already" — track what
  the beatdown player has spent; once Balloon and support are gone the Hound alone is harmless.
- t=1229-1230: "I can't really defend properly until that gets taken down" — with a Mega Minion
  parked in the other lane he waits for it to be dealt with before committing the building.
- t=1247-1252: "let's just go for the knight here just to tank for the Lava Hound pups. Good
  defense right there" — a 3-elixir mini-tank in front is the cheap answer to pup damage.
- t=1253-1257: "he's going to have to eat this or go for the barbs, but obviously he doesn't want
  to do either of that" — barrel into an opponent who has just committed to a Lava Hound, so
  every answer he has costs him his push.
