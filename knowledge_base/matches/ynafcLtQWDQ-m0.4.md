## Summary

The last game of a five-game, single-deck session with the Royal Hogs list:
Royal Hogs, Tesla, Furnace, Lightning, Berserker (Hero), Barbarian Barrel,
Skeletons and Electro Spirit — roughly 3.1 average elixir, one 5-elixir win
condition, one building, one heavy spell and four cards of 1-2 elixir to cycle
back to the pigs. The opponent is someone Ryley introduces at 2:47 as "the best
Royal Hogs Archer Queen player in the world", but who turns out to be on a
different list: an Evo Cannon / Rocket chip deck with Goblin Drill, an Evo
Bomber, Arrows, Giant Snowball and a Berserker of his own that he spams at the
bridge. Ryley reads the matchup out loud at 2:07-1:55 — no Poison, therefore
Rocket, therefore he can "abuse my furnace really, really hard" — and then plays
exactly that game: a Furnace in the middle of his own half every ~20 seconds,
Berserker plus Barbarian Barrel at the river to meet whatever crosses, Tesla in
front of the tower against Goblin Drill, and Royal Hogs into the right lane
behind them. Regular time ends 0-0 on towers with the enemy right tower down to
3258 HP against his own 4514. Overtime is a long grind of piggies-plus-Lightning
into the same lane while he defends the Bomber and Drill; the enemy right tower
falls from 3258 to 156 HP over the last two minutes and he calls it at 0:07:
"that is going to be good game... Very nice win." The video's outro follows
immediately.

## How Ryley uses his cards

- **Royal Hogs** — seven deployments: (8, 15) dead centre at the river at 2:47,
  a deliberate 3-1 split at (9, 14) at 1:42 ("I'm going to do it like three to
  one"), and then five straight right-lane sets at (15, 13)/(15, 15) from 0:06
  onward. He never switches lanes in overtime on purpose: "I wish we could go
  opposite lane, but I feel like we're too committed at this point" (1:11). He
  values them as much for what they force as for damage: "Forcing out the
  snowball with the piggies is just so strong because then he can't snowball our
  skellies" (0:45), and he treats his Evo pigs as the thing to keep aligned
  against the enemy Cannon ("we unaligned his Evo cannon from our Evo piggies,
  which is huge").
- **Furnace** — the centrepiece of his gameplan, played eight times, always at
  (8-9, 9-13), i.e. the middle of his own half rather than behind a tower. Reason
  given at 2:07 and 1:55: the opponent has Rocket rather than Poison, so a
  centred Furnace cannot be answered cheaply — "I'm going to be able to abuse my
  furnace really, really hard in this match-up now", and later "I think these
  middle furnaces are actually really good against this guy" (0:36). It both
  chips (enemy tower 4622 -> 4291 with no other play at 1:24) and defends: at
  0:28 the Fire Spirit finishes a defence he had budgeted a Barbarian Barrel for
  ("we didn't even need the barb barrel... that fire spirit jump").
- **Tesla** — five plays, two distinct jobs. Early it is the anti-troop building
  at (9, 12) placed so it survives an enemy Berserker ability ("I want the Tesla
  to live"). In overtime it goes far right at (15, 8)/(15, 9), directly in front
  of the threatened tower, against the Goblin Drill and Bomber: "I need to evo
  Tesla in front. Evo Tesla, get down... We got the evo Tesla down. That's huge"
  (1:39). He also spends Electro Spirit purely to protect it: "Just e-spirit here
  to keep our Tesla alive" (1:15).
- **Berserker (Hero)** — the most-played card of the game (nine or more
  deployments): almost always at (14-16, 8-15), on or just behind the river on
  the right, either escorting the pigs or meeting the enemy Bomber/Berserker. One
  exception at 1:09, a pure cycle drop at (9, 0) behind his own king tower ("let's
  cycle my berserker here in the back"). He uses Savage Survival exactly once, at
  0:55 for a guaranteed kill ("the berserker ability to just make sure we take
  out that bomber no matter what"), and explicitly declines it once at 1:27
  ("Could I pop the ability here? It's not worth it, I think").
- **Barbarian Barrel** — seven plays, all reactive and all in the right lane at
  (13-14, 5-14), usually 1-3 seconds after a Berserker as the second half of the
  same defence ("let's go for the berserker here and then we can go for the
  barbarian barrel here as well", 2:18). Twice he cancels the plan when the
  Furnace spirits do the work instead: "we didn't even need the barb barrel"
  (0:28) and "we don't even need a barbarian barrel" (0:20 overtime).
- **Skeletons** — nine plays, three roles: opening cycle in the back corner
  (1, 1) and the side lane (2, 14); mid-lane distraction at (9, 8)/(9, 9)/(9, 13);
  and, repeatedly, insurance for the spawner — "Skellies here as well just to keep
  the furnace alive" (0:05).
- **Electro Spirit** — nine plays at 1 elixir, used as the cheap reset before a
  bigger card, twice explicitly to buy the Tesla time ("Just e-spirit here to keep
  our Tesla alive"), and as the fastest way to reach the next card in hand: "We
  need the e-spirit quickly to get to the barbarian barrel" (0:13).
- **Lightning** — four casts, every one at (11-14, 21-22) on the enemy right

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- 2:07 / 1:55 — read the opponent's heavy spell before committing a spawner:
  "I'm assuming he has a poison in this deck, but we actually — no, he has a
  rocket instead... I'm going to be able to abuse my furnace really, really hard
  in this match-up now."
- 0:36 — placement follows from that read: "I think these middle furnaces are
  actually really good against this guy" (centre of his own half, not tucked
  behind a tower).
- 1:42 — split the pigs when the defence is splash-based: "We can just go for the
  Royal Hog split here. I'm going to do it like three to one."
- 1:11 (overtime) — but do not switch lanes once invested: "I wish we could go
  opposite lane, but I feel like we're too committed at this point."
- 1:27 (overtime) — the Hero ability is not free: "Could I pop the ability here?
  It's not worth it, I think" — versus 0:55, "Let's go for the berserker ability
  to just make sure we take out that bomber no matter what."
- 0:45 (overtime) — count what the win condition drags out, not just its damage:
  "Forcing out the snowball with the piggies is just so strong because then he
  can't snowball our skellies and stuff."
- 1:01 (overtime) — respect the cycle spell in triple elixir: "we have to be
  careful of the rocket cycling in triple elixir cuz we're not actually up all
  that much, so we can easily, easily lose this game."
- 0:23 (overtime) — the win condition of the defence is the tower, not the trade:
  "As long as he doesn't get rockets on our tower, we have a very good chance to
  win this."
