# Match: Ronin Made This Log Bait Deck 100X MORE BROKEN — game 5 of 7 (index 4)

## Summary

Game index 4 of a seven-game single-deck session with the video's
[Ronin](../cards/ronin.md) log-bait list: [Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md),
[Ice Spirit](../cards/ice-spirit.md), [Royal Delivery](../cards/royal-delivery.md),
[Wall Breakers](../cards/wall-breakers.md), [Fire Spirit](../cards/fire-spirit.md),
[Magic Archer](../cards/magic-archer.md), [Bomb Tower](../cards/bomb-tower.md) and Ronin — a
2.9-elixir [bait](../archetypes/bait.md) shell with no small spell at all, so every swarm has to
be answered by Bomb Tower, Royal Delivery or a spirit. Ryley reads the opponent almost
immediately: "Looks like this guy's playing a hyper bait. Bush and log. I'm not sure which
variation this could be" (t=822-826), then "I would assume he has skeleton barrel, but I'm not
100% sure. Goblins and log" (t=828-832). Every one of those guesses lands during the game —
[Suspicious Bush](../cards/suspicious-bush.md), [The Log](../cards/the-log.md), an evolved
[Skeleton Barrel](../cards/skeleton-barrel.md), [Goblins](../cards/goblins.md),
[Dart Goblin](../cards/dart-goblin.md), [Rascals](../cards/rascals.md) and a
[Royal Ghost](../cards/royal-ghost.md) all appear. The shape of the game is a bait mirror that
Ryley wins on throughput: he never stops sending Wall Breakers and Goblin Barrel at the enemy
right tower while Bomb Tower (played four times) and Royal Delivery hold his own half, and he
deliberately concedes chip on his left tower rather than spend on it ("I think I want to eat
that, to be honest, cuz it's on my low lane tower anyways", t=909-912). The enemy right tower
falls from 4858 to 144 HP across the last 70 seconds while his own towers finish at 4135 and
4126; he calls it at t=994-998: "I think he's just going to give up. Come on. Fire spirit. GG's.
Perfect game."
