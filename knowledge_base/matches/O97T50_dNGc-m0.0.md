# Match: I Made it to DAY 2 of Clash Royale League Monthly Finals — game 1

## Summary

This is game one of a CRL Monthly Finals best-of-three played in the draft-style "dual" format Ryley
explains up front: "this is a dual format best of three... to win, you have to beat your opponent two
times, and you are not able to repeat cards" (t=19-27). He names both lists at t=31-33 — "we have a
piggies deck and this guy has an RG deck" — so his win condition is
[Royal Hogs](../cards/royal-hogs.md), supported by two spawners
([Furnace](../cards/furnace.md) and [Goblin Hut](../cards/goblin-hut.md)),
[Berserker](../cards/berserker.md) in her Hero form
([Savage Survival](../heroes/berserker-hero.md)), [Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md),
[Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md), [The Log](../cards/the-log.md) and a
[Lightning](../cards/lightning.md) he never spends. At 3.1 average elixir with a 1/1/2/2 defensive
core it plays as a [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md) deck, and that is exactly how he pilots it: seven
separate Royal Hogs at the bridge (t=8 through t=203) with a spawner permanently on the board behind
them. The opponent is a [Royal Giant](../cards/royal-giant.md) /
[Monk](../cards/monk.md) [control](../archetypes/control.md) list with
[Fisherman](../cards/fisherman.md), [Skeleton Dragons](../cards/skeleton-dragons.md),
[Tombstone](../cards/tombstone.md), [Mother Witch](../cards/mother-witch.md) and — the detail Ryley
flags as decisive — [Void](../cards/void.md) in the small-spell slot instead of
[Fireball](../cards/fireball.md) (t=133-141): "that means he'd have a really hard time taking out my
Royal Hogs." The enemy right tower goes from 2608 HP at clock 2:28 to 247 by t=218 while both of
Ryley's stay near full (2971 and 2766); he closes it with a Berserker ability, a Log and a Lightning
and calls it at t=217-221: "I got my final piggy snivel and I won the game, guys. I Lightened the
tower and that was good game. So, I started up one to zero."

## How Ryley uses his cards

- **[Royal Hogs](../cards/royal-hogs.md)** — the entire gameplan. Seven Hogs deploy labels across the
  game (t=8.0, 56.1, 94.1, 115.1, 134.1, 157.2, 180.2, 203.2), almost all at the river row 15 in the
  right lane at tiles [9, 15], [10, 15] and [15, 15], with one on his own half at [5, 12] to walk
  them in behind a spawner. He never splits them and never waits for a big push: "I was just in like
  a permanent state of just going Royal Hogs over and over again" (t=162-164). His case for the card
  is safety, not raw power — "piggies is a really safe deck... Royal Hogs is such a hard deck to
  counter if you are a top player" (t=54-60) and "if you're a good player, piggies is so solid
  because you're more often than not, you're not really getting countered. You're not countering your
  opponent too hard. You're just always giving yourself a chance to win" (t=78-87).
- **[Furnace](../cards/furnace.md)** — five separate Furnaces (t=9.0 [16, 10], 18.0 [9, 8], 96.6
  [8, 0], 126.1 [9, 7], 144.1 [5, 6], 174.2 [9, 9], 198.2 [9, 5]), placed all over his own half rather
  than from one fixed spot — right side, centre, back line. He treats it as the thing that makes the
  opponent's push impossible to start: "I'm always having a furnace down since my cycle is super
  quick" (t=128-129), and later as an alternative to a big spell: "why not just cycle more furnaces?"
  (t=194-197).
- **[Goblin Hut](../cards/goblin-hut.md)** — three deploys (t=35.0 and t=107.8 both at tile [8, 10],
  plus a HUD-confirmed one at t=58.4), always the same centre-of-own-half placement between the two
  Crown Towers rather than a back corner. It is a read on the specific opponent: "here I went for the
  goblin hut because I had already realized that he was playing RG Monk... I always wanted to have
  some Spear Goblins on the board" (t=108-119), "with having the Goblin Hut here, it is actually
  really hard for him to be able to get a push going" (t=123-128).
- **[Berserker](../cards/berserker.md)** (Hero, [Savage Survival](../heroes/berserker-hero.md)) — his
  only real defensive troop, used six or seven times at tiles [11, 14], [8, 10], [2, 14], [3, 14],
  [10, 14] and [13, 14], i.e. on his own side of whichever bridge the threat is coming to, and twice
  in a row at t=42.9/44.2 when the Royal Giant push arrived. The ability is spent on support troops,
  never on the tower: "I got a really nice Berserker ability as well just to be able to take out the
  Monk and the Fisherman. Berserker hero is just so strong, man" (t=165-171) and "I could go for the
  Berserker ability just to take out that Fisherman" (t=184-185).
- **[The Log](../cards/the-log.md)** — seven casts (t=62.1 [9, 15], 111.1 [3, 15], 133.1 [3, 13],
  138.1 [10, 15], 164.2 [12, 15], 184.5 [13, 15], 208.9 [13, 14]), split between clearing the
  Barbarians/Monk push on his left and rolling through the right-lane defenders in front of his own
  Hogs. "I got a nice Log there and my piggies were going absolutely crazy" (t=142-145) and "I had the
  Log ready here for the Tombstone getting a bunch of damage on that tower" (t=187-190).
- **[Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md)** — cheap reset/cycle, deployed on his own half at
  tiles [12, 7], [8, 10] and [8, 11], each time just ahead of or alongside a Berserker so the stun
  buys her hits. Named directly at t=177: "I went for the E-Spirit and the Berserker as well just to
  make sure to protect that."
- **[Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md)** — the cheapest block in the deck, dropped at [7, 12],
  [10, 7], [2, 15], [7, 14], [9, 9], [11, 13] plus HUD-only plays, mostly in the path of whatever was
  walking at his tower. He states the use explicitly at the end: "I just went skellies here to block"
  (t=214-215).
- **[Lightning](../cards/lightning.md)** — held all game as a deliberate discipline, then cashed once
  at t=217.2 on tile [16, 26] to finish the right tower. Twice he says out loud why he is not casting
  it: "I didn't want to like overspend by ever going for like a Lightning or anything" (t=150-152)
  and "I was low-key tempted to Lightning, but I'm like, 'Dude, this has been working so well. Why not
  just cycle more furnaces?'" (t=191-197). His finishing plan was arithmetic: "I knew that it would
  bring him down to either one or two Lightnings" (t=211-214), then "I Lightened the tower and that
  was good game" (t=219-221).
- **[Spear Goblins](../cards/spear-goblins.md)** — not a deck slot; they are the Goblin Hut's output,
  and the reason he plays the hut. Detections at [10, 9], [9, 8], [2, 12], [3, 16], [4, 15], [12, 20]
  show them trickling into both lanes for most of the game. "I always wanted to have some Spear
  Goblins on the board" (t=117-119), "I always have Spear Goblins on the board" (t=132).
