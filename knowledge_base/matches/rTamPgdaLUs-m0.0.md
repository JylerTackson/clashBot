He names it himself at 50s: "I'm using it here in this minor wall breakers deck because I do feel like it fits really, really nicely." The opponent (called "Aragon" in the commentary) plays [Lava Hound](../cards/lava-hound.md) [beatdown](../archetypes/beatdown.md) with [Skeleton Dragons](../cards/skeleton-dragons.md), [Graveyard](../cards/graveyard.md), [Valkyrie](../cards/valkyrie.md), [Miner](../cards/miner.md), [Tombstone](../cards/tombstone.md), Evo [Zap](../cards/zap.md) and a Hero Berserker of his own. The shape of the game is one-lane trading: the opponent commits Lava Hound after Lava Hound into Ryley's left lane (his left tower slides 3052 → 1181 across the match, most of it in one burst at 2:05-2:01 when the opponent pops his own Berserker ability), while Ryley chips the right lane with Wall Breakers, Spear Goblins and Berserker until the right crown tower falls at roughly 1:01.
