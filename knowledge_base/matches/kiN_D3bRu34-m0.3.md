Game 4 of an eight-game single-deck session (video 543.7s-720.7s), joined at clock 2:52 with the video's

single expensive card and no spell. The opponent is a [Sparky](../cards/sparky.md) deck Ryley identifies
from memory before it appears: "I thought this guy had Sparky. Oh, wait. I'm stupid. He definitely still has
Sparky... Yeah, probably the one with the [Wizard](../cards/wizard.md) and the
[Mini P.E.K.K.A.](../cards/mini-p-e-k-k-a.md)" (t=571-578), later joined by a
[Golden Knight](../cards/golden-knight.md) and [Barbarians](../cards/barbarians.md). The shape of the game
is two-lane chip against one big push: Ryley throws Goblin Barrel + Wall Breakers alternately at both enemy
towers from clock 2:31 onward and deliberately refuses to commit to one lane ("I can't get locked into one
lane too early. If that happens, then I'm just completely cooked", t=604-607), while the Sparky push at
clock 1:15 is answered for five elixir by Cannon + Ice Spirit + Ronin. He takes a tower at clock 0:17
("bang and bang. Let's go. Tower down", t=696-700), realises he has conceded almost nothing —
"I just realized I took zero damage on my tower except for a zap" (t=702-703, later corrected to a
[Tornado](../cards/tornado.md), t=707) — and closes it out: "this was an extremely clean game. GG's. Nice
win... Dude, the Ronin needs an emergency nerf. That was just a ridiculous defense" (t=708-717).

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=604-607 (clock 1:52) — do not commit to a single lane early with this deck: "I can't get locked into one
  lane too early. If that happens, then I'm just completely cooked."
- t=610-619 (clock 1:50-1:38) — you can spend the Wall Breakers offensively when Ronin covers the melee
  threat: "I would like to save them for defense, but I don't really think that's possible... It's not like I
  exactly need them, especially if I have the Ronin for the Mini P.E.K.K.A."
- t=622-628 (clock 1:34-1:28) — pair the Magic Archer with an Ice Spirit: play him "to snipe that down and
  then we can go for the Ice Spirit here just to freeze that and keep the Magic Archer alive."
- t=634-637 (clock 1:22) — Wall Breakers double as a shield for a ranged support card: "if these Wall
  Breakers could tank for the Marcher for a bit, that would be beautiful."
- t=648-665 (clock 1:09-0:52) — the standard Sparky answer in this list is Cannon, Ice Spirit on top, then
  Ronin: "I don't know a single other card in the game that could defend that right there for just five
  elixir. That's insane."
- t=685-691 (clock 0:32-0:25) — pre-empt the opponent's spirit or small spell with your own spirit to keep a
  ranged unit alive: "He might try to spirit or zap, so let's go for the spirit of our own."
- t=678, t=715-717 — the deck's thesis in his words: "Ronin is just so good on defense" and "the Ronin needs
  an emergency nerf. That was just a ridiculous defense."
