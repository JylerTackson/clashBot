# Match: I QUALIFIED for the Clash Royale League WORLD FINALS!! — game 1 of the first duel

## Summary

This is a Clash Royale League qualifier video, and Ryley narrates the games after the fact ("This is
not like live or anything. I'm just sharing my thoughts, how I was feeling", t=16-22). Game 1 is the
first game of a best-of-three duel against "Bet Fist", and the duel rules are why the deck read has to
be per game: "as you see me using Royal Hogs here, I won't be able to use Royal Hogs in the second or
in the potential third game of the duel. And he won't be able to use a Goblin Drill deck... You just
can't repeat cards" (t=36-46). The list he pilots here is the KB deck
[Goblinstein EvoRHogs 3.4 Cycle](../decks/archers-barbarian-barrel-bomb-tower-electro-spirit-goblinstein-lightning-royal-hogs-skeletons.md):
Evo [Royal Hogs](../cards/royal-hogs.md), Evo [Archers](../cards/archers.md),
[Goblinstein](../cards/goblinstein.md), [Bomb Tower](../cards/bomb-tower.md),
[Barbarian Barrel](../cards/barbarian-barrel.md), [Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md),
[Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md) and [Lightning](../cards/lightning.md) — all eight confirmed by
in-game deploy labels, and six of the eight also by HUD elixir drops. He drafted it as an anti-[X-Bow](../cards/x-bow.md)
list because the opponent is "very popular for being the best Expo player in the world" (t=65-75), but
the opponent counter-drafted a [Goblin Drill](../cards/goblin-drill.md) deck — "very smart pick by him
though... having Goblin Drill against a Goblin Stein deck is usually pretty good cuz the Goblin Stein
is really, really bad against a drill" (t=78-89). Ryley's answer is relentless offence: sixteen
Goblinstein plays, eight Royal Hogs and ten Archers in 298 seconds, nearly all at the bridge, against a
defensive core of nine Bomb Towers and eleven Barbarian Barrels. He takes the enemy right tower to
roughly 2587 by t=116, both enemy towers to about 2322 by t=200, and closes it out: "I got a lightning
down, guys. And I was able to win the first game against Bet Fist" (t=286-290).

## Opponent

The opponent is named as "Bet Fist" (t=63-67), a player Ryley calls "the best Expo player in the world" —
i.e. an [X-Bow](../cards/x-bow.md) specialist. Ryley drafted this deck to beat X-Bow, and says the
opponent read that and counter-drafted: "Very smart pick by him though, picking Goblin Drill, making sure
that he gets a pretty nice matchup. Um, I guess he kind of knew that I would try to counter expo with
maybe a Goblinstein deck" (t=75-84). No X-Bow was ever deployed in this game.

What was actually observed on the opponent's side:
[Goblin Drill](../cards/goblin-drill.md) (one clean detection at tile [8, 17], t=42.0, but eleven
transcript mentions and constant references to "drill over and over again", t=169-171),
[Tesla](../cards/tesla.md) (tracked at [9, 19] / [10, 19] repeatedly from t=24.0, the defensive building
Ryley plays around), [Bomber](../cards/bomber.md) (tracked walking down Ryley's left lane through most of
the game, t=30 onward — the card the high-middle Bomb Tower was aimed at, t=148-151), and
[Poison](../cards/poison.md) (never detected visually, but Ryley says "he has to use poison" at t=104 and
"He poisoned the tower" at t=266, and his left tower bleeds 2610 -> 2456 across t=258-272). He also
predicted an Evo [Giant Snowball](../cards/giant-snowball.md) — "he has to use poison and probably an evo
snowball" (t=104-105) — and later describes "a really good evo snowball spear by him" (t=134-136), but no
snowball was detected.

How he read and countered it: the drill spawns are handled by Bomb Tower placed on the likely spawn tile
rather than by chasing goblins (t=250-260), the goblins themselves by Barbarian Barrel, Electro Spirit
chains and Skeletons, and the Tesla by Lightning (t=138-147) and by Royal Hogs + Archers pushes that force
it out (t=222-226). The strategic answer to the drill is not defensive at all — it is to attack first so
the opponent cannot afford a second drill (t=232-245).

## Data gaps

- **No match clock at all.** This video's HUD layout defeated the clock reader, so every `clock` field in
  `context.md` is `None` and the games in this video were split by menu gaps and tower resets rather than
  by the timer. Every timestamp in this file is video seconds (`t=`). Regular time / overtime / triple
  elixir cannot be separated from the data; the only signal is Ryley saying "in triple elixir" at t=170.
- **Hand reads are unreliable** (`hand_conf_mean` 0.52). The HUD hand produced fifteen plays for cards
  that are not in this deck and were never deploy-labelled: Void x6 (t=176.8, 197.9, 236.6, 251.1, 268.9,
  292.3), Bowler (t=164.7), Giant (t=178.5), Tombstone (t=153.3), Rage x2 (t=202.2, 255.6), Zappies x2
  (t=231.4, 249.7), Fireball (t=261.6) and Goblin Gang (t=302.6). All are treated as hand-read noise and
  none is cited above; several coincide in time with a deploy-labelled play of a real deck card (e.g.
  t=202.2 Rage vs t=202.2 Barbarian Barrel at [3, 8]). The hand column of the timeline is likewise full of
  cards from other games (Wall Breakers, Ice Golem, Ice Wizard, Golem, Skeleton Barrel, The Log) and was
  not used.
- **Two truncated deploy labels were re-read.** `'GobliNst'` at t=7.0 tile [16, 12] was emitted as
  "Goblins" and `'BaRbaRiaN Ba'` at t=56.1 tile [15, 14] as "Barbarians"; neither card is in the observed
  deck and both are prefixes of a deck card, so they are read here as Goblinstein and Barbarian Barrel
  respectively. Neither is counted in the per-card totals above.
- **Opponent-side attribution overrides.** The pipeline lists `royal-hogs` (t=17.0, 24.0 at [2, 15];
  t=162.2 at [15, 15]; t=182.2 at [2, 15]) and `lightning` (t=286.3 at [13, 23]) as opponent cards. Both
  are Ryley's own cards, all four Royal Hogs reads sit on the river row adjacent to his own bridge
  placements, and his commentary claims the Lightning at t=288 ("I got a lightning down, guys"). They are
  excluded from `opponent_deck_seen`. `rocket` (t=118.1 at [17, 6]), `zap` (t=156.2 at [3, 2]) and `clone`
  (t=288.3 at [3, 3]) are single medium/low-confidence arena reads on Ryley's own half with no commentary
  support, and are also excluded — the opponent's spell that is actually attested is Poison. As a result
  `opponent.deck_complete: true` in `context.json` should not be trusted: at most four of the opponent's
  eight cards are known.
- **The Goblin Drill was almost never detected.** It is the opponent's win condition by his own account
  (eleven transcript mentions) yet appears once in the events (t=42.0). The 2024-era detector has no
