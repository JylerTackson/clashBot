# Match GQmC6dsl6Go-m0.7 — Evo Ebarbs / Hero Berserker bait, lost in overtime with the opponent's tower on 427 HP

## Summary

The last of eight games in a single-deck showcase, played on the video's [bait](../archetypes/bait.md) list: [Berserker](../cards/berserker.md) (the Hero variant), [Suspicious Bush](../cards/suspicious-bush.md), [The Log](../cards/the-log.md), [Dart Goblin](../cards/dart-goblin.md), [Goblin Gang](../cards/goblin-gang.md), [Skeleton Barrel](../cards/skeleton-barrel.md), [Fireball](../cards/fireball.md) and [Elite Barbarians](../cards/elite-barbarians.md) — three cheap cards that all pull the same small spell, with an Evolved Elite Barbarians and an Evolved Skeleton Barrel as the two evolution slots. Ryley scouts the opponent out loud before he plays a card: "I'm pretty sure this guy plays with giant skeleton balloon with the hunter, espirit, zap, minions, miner, and ice golem" (1267-1274s), i.e. a [Giant Skeleton](../cards/giant-skeleton.md) / [Balloon](../cards/balloon.md) [beatdown](../archetypes/beatdown.md) shell. The game swings his way at clock 2:00 when a [Goblin Gang](../cards/goblin-gang.md) + Elite Barbarians bridge combo takes the opponent's right tower from 4858 to 900 in about four seconds ("that's just tower down. He literally can't defend this", 1318-1320s), and he then trades his own left tower away deliberately to keep pocket pressure on the enemy king ("I'm actually just going to let this go", 1341-1343s).

From there it turns into a grind he never closes. He calls his own turning point at 0:43: the Hero Berserker's Savage Survival used at the wrong moment, letting an [Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md) finish his Dart Goblin — "Oh no, that was a bad ability, bro… That was such a horrible ability" (1391-1396s). Regulation ends 0-0 on crowns and the game runs the full double- and triple-elixir overtime, where a bush-and-barrel siege on the left grinds the enemy tower from 2811 to 427 without ever landing the last hit: "I just need one hit. Come on. One hit… No, I lost" (1484-1489s). His own right tower and king are shredded in the same window. Outro at 1508-1513s: "A good game, bro. I sold to be honest. I did that one really bad play that I called out earlier and it lost me the game. I almost got it back."

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- **Place the Elite Barbarians off the king lane when you are ahead on one tower** — "I still need to pressure his left side tower cuz if I only go for the king tower, then I'm just going to get caught slipping" (1369-1374s).
- **Cheap bait cards are worth playing purely to drain the small spell** — "he's going to have to at least use his electro spirits, which is good for us. Just forcing out more elixir" (1298-1302s).
- **Stack the bait, do not send one card** — "obviously he's going to be able to defend just the skelly barrel" is his reason for adding the bush on top (1482-1484s), and the same logic drives the Skeleton Barrel + Berserker + Dart Goblin stack at 0:50-0:47 (1383-1390s).
- **Sometimes let a tower go to keep a pocket push alive** — "I'm actually just going to let this go and I'm just going to go for the berserker in the pocket here to tank for this tower" (1341-1345s); later, "At least we're not going to lose our whole tower. We're just going to lose most of it… we're not going to lose our tower cuz the thing's not going to retarget" (1412-1419s).
- **Time the Hero Berserker's ability around the opponent's cheap stun** — the negative lesson, stated as self-criticism: "Oh no, that was a bad ability, bro. I didn't think he'd be able to hit the dark ob with the e spirit. That was such a horrible ability" (1391-1396s), and in the outro "I did that one really bad play that I called out earlier and it lost me the game" (1511-1513s).
- **Scout the deck before you commit** — the entire opening is spent naming the opponent's eight cards and planning around the Ice Golem (1267-1310s).
