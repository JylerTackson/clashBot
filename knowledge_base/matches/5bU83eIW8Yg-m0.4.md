## Summary

The fifth and final deck of the showcase, and Ryley names it himself in the opening line: "With the final deck here, guys, we have this a giant Sparky e-barbs deck" (694-699s). It is Giant, Sparky, Elite Barbarians (Evolved), Wizard (Evolved), Valkyrie, Minions, Rage and Arrows — a 4.25-elixir beatdown list with no building and no cheap cycle, whose entire plan is to bank elixir and then throw Evo E-Barbs and a raged Giant/Sparky push at one tower. He is openly unhappy about it: "dude, I can't believe this is actually viable. It is so sad that, you know, this is what the game has actually come to, man. I was really hoping that the game would remain skillful for at least a little bit longer" (699-712s).

The opponent is on Log Bait — Goblin Barrel, Princess (Evolved), Skeleton Army, Knight, Inferno Tower, Dart Goblin (Evolved), Ice Spirit — and Ryley reads it almost immediately: "I assume he has inferno tower, and I'm not actually going to be able to reset that inferno tower" (738-742s), so his stated plan for the matchup is simply "In this type of match-up, I just need to get a lot of elixir up and just spam my e-barbs" (731-737s).

Regulation is a straight tower trade. Ryley's Rage-backed push around clock 1:21-1:19 takes the enemy left tower from 4738 to 640 HP ("That's just tower down, no matter what he wants", 787-790s), while the opponent's counter leaves Ryley's right tower on 125 HP by clock 1:17. He also loses a chunk of that push to lag: "Bro, the lag. Oh my god. Okay, at least we grabbed a Sparky shot... We got at least 1,000 damage. It could have been a lot more though if we didn't get that lag" (795-813s). From triple elixir onward he stops attacking and simply denies: Sparky in the back, Valkyrie on top of everything, Wizard behind, and the opponent never gets a push going — "we're just not letting him attack at all. This is kind of funny. He can't go in" (884-888s).

The game runs 262s, the longest of the video, and goes to overtime at t≈868 (clock resets to 1:59). In overtime Ryley finally cashes in on the right lane: Evo E-Barbs "in the pocket" plus Rage plus Minions at clock 1:21-1:13 takes the enemy right tower from 3122 to 588 HP ("Evo Barbs are on the tower. Minions are on the tower...
