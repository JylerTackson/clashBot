# Match: This Rune Giant Deck is TOTALLY OVERPOWERED!! — game 8 (last of the video)

## Summary

The closing game of a single-deck session on the [Rune Giant](../cards/rune-giant.md) list:
[Rune Giant](../cards/rune-giant.md), [Berserker](../cards/berserker.md) (Hero — he pops
Savage Survival four separate times), [Elite Barbarians](../cards/elite-barbarians.md),
[Mother Witch](../cards/mother-witch.md), [Minions](../cards/minions.md),
[Heal Spirit](../cards/heal-spirit.md), Evo [Zap](../cards/zap.md) and
[Fireball](../cards/fireball.md) — a [bridge-spam](../archetypes/bridge-spam.md) shell that leans on a cheap
4-elixir tank rather than a back-line beatdown build-up. The opponent is a
[Mortar](../cards/mortar.md) [siege](../archetypes/siege.md) deck with
[Cannon Cart](../cards/cannon-cart.md), [Barbarians](../cards/barbarians.md),
[Witch](../cards/witch.md), [Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md), his own
[Elite Barbarians](../cards/elite-barbarians.md) and a [Goblinstein](../cards/goblinstein.md) Champion; Ryley
knows the player ("against Marsh. This guy usually uses [Goblin Drill](../cards/goblin-drill.md)
[Bowler](../cards/bowler.md)", t=1241-1243) but not this variation. The opponent opens Mortar down the middle
at clock 2:45, Ryley answers Minions + Heal Spirit and immediately builds a Rune Giant / Berserker /
Mother Witch push that takes the enemy left tower from 4858 to 2448 HP by clock 1:51. From there the game
inverts: repeated Goblinstein Lightning Link defences and a Cannon Cart / Mortar counter-push drag his own
right tower down to 2959 and he calls it dead ("Yeah, we're dead. Okay, well, this is GG's, I think",
t=1400-1402). Regulation ends with the opponent ahead on damage; Ryley commits fully to defence in overtime
("I think we just need to try to commit to defense fully from now on... there's actually a decent chance that
we can hang on to this", t=1412-1422), then grinds the 2173-HP enemy left tower down with Mother Witch curses,
bridge Berserkers with the ability, and two Fireballs. It falls at overtime clock ~0:50: "The piggy. The
piggy. Let's go. Dude, we won. We just have to cycle back. What a win, man" (t=1481-1485), followed by the
video's sign-off.

## Data gaps

- **Deck-list discrepancy.** The context file's printed video-level consensus lists
  [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md) in place of [Fireball](../cards/fireball.md)
  (`berserker-elite-barbarians-heal-spirit-minions-mother-witch-rune-giant-skeletons-zap`). That is a pooling
  artefact: this game's own read contains Fireball, no per-game read contains Skeletons, and Ryley names and
  casts Fireball repeatedly ("Okay, wait. I can fireball this", "all we have to do is get one more fireball").
  This file uses the Fireball list and the deck key
  `berserker-elite-barbarians-fireball-heal-spirit-minions-mother-witch-rune-giant-zap`.
- **HUD hand reads are unreliable throughout** (mean hand confidence 0.566). The tracked hand shows cards that
  are not in either deck — Rascals, Tombstone, Ice Wizard, The Log, Poison, Skeleton Dragons, Golem, Furnace,
  Dart Goblin, Giant, Valkyrie, Knight, Little Prince — and at t=1490-1492 degenerates to four Giants. Every
  HUD-only play labelled Goblin Barrel (t=1366.2, 1406.7, 1432.3, 1435.6), Barbarian Barrel (t=1395.7,
  1418.9, 1465.0), Giant Snowball (t=1355.2), Skeletons (t=1359.4, 1475.8, 1481.3) and Barbarians (t=1372.4)
  is a misread of a card that is in the eight-card list; where commentary or a deploy label was available it
  has been used instead, and those events are otherwise reported only as "an elixir drop".
- **11 unidentified events.** Own-side drops of 3 at t=1267.1 (aligns with the Mother Witch deploy label at
  [6, 6]), 5 at t=1279.6 and t=1450.3 (both align with Elite Barbarians and with his commentary), 2 at
