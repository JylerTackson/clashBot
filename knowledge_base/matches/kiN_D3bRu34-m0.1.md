# Match: #1 BEST Ronin Deck in Clash Royale — game 2

## Summary

Game 2 of a single-deck Ronin session: Ryley pilots the video-level consensus eight —
[Ronin](../cards/ronin.md), the Hero [Magic Archer](../cards/magic-archer.md), Evo
[Wall Breakers](../cards/wall-breakers.md), Evo [Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md),
[Cannon](../cards/cannon.md), [Fire Spirit](../cards/fire-spirit.md), [Ice Spirit](../cards/ice-spirit.md)
and [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md) — a 2.5-elixir [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md) shell with two cheap
win conditions, so it also plays as [bait](../archetypes/bait.md). The opponent is a heavy
[beatdown](../archetypes/beatdown.md)-flavoured list built on [Giant](../cards/giant.md),
[Bowler](../cards/bowler.md), [Witch](../cards/witch.md), [Graveyard](../cards/graveyard.md) and
[Rage](../cards/rage.md), with [Minions](../cards/minions.md), [Arrows](../cards/arrows.md) and finally a
[Mini P.E.K.K.A.](../cards/mini-p-e-k-k-a.md) — Ryley notes at t=307-309 that "he still has one more card
left to show", and the Mini P.E.K.K.A. is the card that shows up last. The whole game is spirits-and-barrels
chip: Wall Breakers and Fire/Ice Spirits at the bridge every cycle, a Cannon for anything that walks, and
Ronin dropped on top of ranged support. He trades his own right tower down to 182 HP around clock 1:14 while
taking the enemy right tower to 285 HP ("we're going to lose our tower here guys, but he's going to lose most
of his tower as well. That's fine with me", t=264-267), then holds a one-tower-each overtime. The finish is
the exact interaction he had been advertising for two minutes: the opponent finally plays the Mini P.E.K.K.A.
into the Ronin's parry — "look, it's going to get one shot. OH MY GOD! I told you he can't use the Mini
P.E.K.K.A. Good game. Very nice win" (t=357-365).

## How Ryley uses his cards

- **[Ronin](../cards/ronin.md)** (5 elixir, played 4x: t=203.7 [14, 14], t=262.1 [14, 13], t=314.6 [4, 14],
  t=352.4 [1, 15]). Two distinct jobs. Defensively he places it *on top of* the ranged support it cannot
  otherwise reach — on the Bowler at t=203.7 and "on top of the witch" at t=262.1 — which matches the card
  file's note that against high-damage ranged troops he must be placed on top of them. Offensively it is a
  bridge mini-tank at t=314.6 and t=352.4, always paired with a 1-elixir spirit. His framing of the parry is
  precise: it only fires on genuine melee attacks, so Bowler and Sparky do not get reflected, while Mini
  P.E.K.K.A. one-shots itself (t=194-213, t=278-284, t=357-360). He opens the game selling the recent buff:
  "it now hits quicker and its first attack is also quicker" (t=184-190).
- **[Wall Breakers](../cards/wall-breakers.md)** (Evo; 2 elixir, at least 8 deployments: t=175.2 [8, 14],
  t=227.2 [5, 14], t=257.3 [4, 15], t=280.3 [4, 14], t=299.3 [7, 0], t=312.6, t=320.3, t=332.3 [9, 18],
  t=350.4 [4, 14], t=360.4 [9, 17]). The single most-played card in the game. Used as an opening scout split
  at centre (t=175.2), as the every-cycle chip threat at the bridge, defensively deep on his own half at
  [7, 0] (t=299.3), and in overtime alongside Ronin. He also names the king-activation trick off the Bowler

  is softer, paired with the Wall Breakers as the deck's second win condition ("Evo Barrel plus Evo Wall
