## Lessons stated by Ryley

- 194-199s (clock ~2:45): playing a bait deck with no spells into another bait deck is a structural problem, not a mistake — "This is going to be annoying cuz we don't have any spells... We're against log bait with no spells right now but it's all good."
- 201-204s (clock ~2:38): use the Goblin Barrel to strip the opponent's small spell before you need it — "Make him use his log at least."
- 224-231s (clock 2:16-2:08): hold the barrel when you can see their splash answer coming — "He's going to Princess in the middle so I'm not going to barrel just yet. I do want to be a little bit more safe."
- 272-277s (clock ~1:25): once the opponent's building is down at the bridge, spend the spirit pre-emptively rather than reactively — "I guess I'll spirit preemptively because he has the tesla down so he has a really good opportunity."
- 291-296s (clock ~1:08): place cheap chip away from their defensive building — "I don't want to give him tesla value so I think I'd rather bush in the opposite lane."
- 325-331s (clock 0:35): against a card you cannot kill, cap how much value it can get by not deploying too far up — "we're going to go for the bush at the bridge immediately. At least we can try to limit the value just by not putting our cards too high."
- 391-395s (clock overtime 1:29): a cheap mini-tank beats an expensive swarm when the enemy tank is placed in front of its support — "I can berserker this cuz that's in the front. I was going to eat that, but because he messed up and put it in the front, then I was fine with it."
- 398-401s (clock overtime 1:22): time offence off the opponent's cycle, not off your own elixir — "We have a decent chance to grab damage cuz he has no e-spirit in cycle."
- 463-468s (clock overtime 0:16): a single big spell is not lethal if you bank elixir for defence — "He needs more than one rocket, guys, so he can't just rocket us away. As long as we save up for defense."
- 435-438s (clock overtime 0:44): once you are ahead in a bait mirror, keep barrelling rather than sitting on the lead — "Let's barrel again here. Definitely just want to keep up the pressure."
