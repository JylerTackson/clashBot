# Ryley vs a Hero Tombstone bridge-spam mirror-ish Ronin deck (game 6 of the session)

## Summary

Ryley pilots the session's 2.5-elixir Ronin bait list (Ronin, Magic Archer, Cannon, Goblin Barrel, Wall Breakers, Skeletons, Ice Spirit, Fire Spirit) against a top-ladder opponent running a bridge-spam deck with a Heroic Tombstone, Bandit, an evolved Battle Ram, Musketeer, Skeleton Dragons, Heal Spirit and a Ronin of his own — Ryley names the archetype himself at clock 2:07 ("It looks like he's playing bridge spam"). The first two minutes are a defensive exchange he wins cleanly: he surrounds the opponent's Ronin with Skeletons so the parry is wasted, keeps his own Ronin at full HP into a counter-push, and around clock 1:36-1:34 the enemy left tower drops from 4474 to 1885 HP. At clock 1:10 he activates his own King Tower on purpose with an Ice Spirit + Fire Spirit pair against a crossing Bandit ("Beautiful King activation. That's going to make defense a lot easier for the rest of the match"), and his king tower reads 7418 HP from t=1020 onward. A late Bandit dash from the bridge at clock 0:12 takes both his towers down (right 4683 -> 3132, left 4858 -> 3817) but no crown falls, so regulation ends 0-0 and the game goes to overtime at t=1086. In overtime he stacks Ronin at the bridge with a Goblin Barrel and Wall Breakers while cycling Magic Archers non-stop; the enemy right tower falls 3665 -> 3089 -> 2476 -> 923 -> 330 HP between OT 1:12 and 1:07 and reads 136-138 by the end. He calls the win at 1181s: "Let's go. Nice win... What a beautiful game, man. I love this deck so much."

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- **Track the opponent's Tombstone cycle** (t=1028-1032, clock 0:56-0:52): "we have the Evo Wall Breakers offensively, cuz he has no Tombstone in cycle now. I think he messed up a bit by cycling it."
