# Match: I Made it to DAY 2 of Clash Royale League Monthly Finals — match 1, game 3

## Summary

Third readable game of a tournament set (video 1218.0s-1515.1s), picked up at clock 2:50 and played through
double elixir (clock 2:00) and triple elixir (clock 1:00) into a long, clock-broken endgame. This is a
deck-showcase tournament video, so the deck read is per game: the deploy labels and commentary agree on the
[Goblinstein EvoRHogs 3.4 Cycle](../decks/archers-barbarian-barrel-bomb-tower-electro-spirit-goblinstein-lightning-royal-hogs-skeletons.md)
list — evolved [Royal Hogs](../cards/royal-hogs.md) and evolved [Archers](../cards/archers.md),
[Goblinstein](../cards/goblinstein.md), [Bomb Tower](../cards/bomb-tower.md),
[Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md), [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md),
[Barbarian Barrel](../cards/barbarian-barrel.md) and [Lightning](../cards/lightning.md) — and he opens by
naming it: "I did decide to go for this royal hogs deck once again... I really do like the royal hogs, man.
I am a big enjoyer of the royal hogs this season" (t=1219-1226). The opponent is log bait built around
[Princess](../cards/princess.md), [Rascals](../cards/rascals.md), [Dark Prince](../cards/dark-prince.md) and
an evolved [Giant Snowball](../cards/giant-snowball.md), which he says caught him out on the draft:
"here he had a log bait deck, which definitely did catch me off guard. I wasn't expecting no bait. That's
why I cycled my barbarian barrel at the bridge first play" (t=1232-1239). His stated problem all game is
that his only two spells are Barbarian Barrel and Lightning, so "the Princess and the Rascal Girls get a
ton of value for him because I am literally unable to take them out" (t=1267-1274). He takes the lead once,
with split Royal Hogs plus an evolved Archer at clock 1:36, and then defends the rest of the game: the
enemy left tower goes 2940 -> 2043 in that one push and only 2043 -> 1352 across the remaining two and a
half minutes, while his own towers settle at 2044 and 2545. He wins — "I was able to win for a very nice
2-0 sweep, guys, and we were doing it really, really well in CRL. We only had to win one more game to
qualify" (t=1506-1512) — without either side losing a tower in the readable window.

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=1258-1262 — against bait, stop spending your small spell on cycle: "now that I knew he had log bait, of
  course, I wouldn't be wasting my barbarian barrel or anything like that."
- t=1266-1276 — know which of your cards the opponent's deck makes dead: "I have barbarian barrel and
  lightning as my spells, which means that the Princess and the Rascal Girls get a ton of value for him
  because I am literally unable to take them out. So, I knew that to win this game I would have to be very,
  very careful."
- t=1314-1334 — take chip to bank a lead, because the lead changes who has to commit: "I was comfortable
  with taking this damage just to get that early lead, and if I have a lead like that, it does force him to
  be the one who has to be aggressive, and that's good for me because if he's just passive stacking up
  Princesses, stacking up Rascals, I would probably lose this game."
- t=1348-1352 — a win-condition play can be a cycle play: "another reason I went for the Piggies was cuz my
  cycle was a little bit messed up, and I kind of wanted to fix it."
- t=1414-1418 — on the opponent's error, hoarding a spell: "he should have just went for the evo snowball,
  but for some reason, he's just never using it offensively and just trying to save it for defense."
- t=1471-1480 — closing out: "you also don't want to get overly confident cuz when you get too confident,
  that's when you just start choking and stuff like that."
