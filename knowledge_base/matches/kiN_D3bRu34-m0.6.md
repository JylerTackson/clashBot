## Summary

Game 6 of 8 in Ryley's Ronin video, played with the same 2.5-elixir cycle list all
session: Ronin, Cannon and Magic Archer for defence, Goblin Barrel and Wall Breakers
as the two chip win conditions, and Ice Spirit / Fire Spirit / Skeletons as the cycle.
The opponent ("Lil 50") plays a Sparky beatdown list — Sparky behind the tower twice,
a Hero Giant, Wizard, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Graveyard, Goblins, plus Tornado and Vines that
Ryley names in commentary. Ryley opens with Goblin Barrel and a Wall Breakers connection
that activates the enemy King, then loses roughly 2,100 damage on his own left tower when
the opponent Tornadoes a Sparky shot into it at 1:45 ("He just did like a 180 trick shot
for 2,000 tower damage"). He answers with a right-lane Ronin + Goblin Barrel + Fire Spirit
prediction that takes the enemy right tower from ~3,700 to ~870, then wins the game in the
3x-elixir minute off a left-lane Ronin that survived the defence and walked in with spirits
("The Ronin just hard carried that defense so hard"). He calls it at 0:17: "Let's go! Very
nice win."

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=1225 (2:15): "Evo barrel isn't as effective with the king activated, but of course, it's
  still a great pressure regardless" — keep barrelling even after you have woken the King.
- t=1233 (2:07): "I want to put it a little bit higher than normal, just to force him to use
  the NATO" — placing Magic Archer further up the board is a deliberate Tornado bait.
- t=1239 (2:02): "let's go for the ice spirit here as well, just to be able to splash on top
  of the Sparky" — Ice Spirit is a splash/reset on the Sparky, not just a cycle card.
- t=1247 (1:53): "We have to Ronin here. That's going to take out the mini Pekka... The Ronin
  should take out the Sparky" — Ronin is the designated answer to single heavy melee threats.
- t=1263 (1:37): "All because I missed the ice spirit" — one missed spirit on the Sparky was
  the whole tower.
- t=1286 (1:15): "I'm going to go for the Ronin barrel cuz he might Sparky the back again" —
  when the opponent invests behind the tower, counter-push the opposite lane rather than
  defending in place.
- t=1317 (0:44): "Fire spirit here, that's going to one shot the mini pekka as well."
- t=1325 (0:34): "The Ronin just hard carried that defense so hard" — Ronin's defensive value
  is measured by whether he survives to counter-push.
- t=1337 (0:23): "Let's go for the Ronin like this so he can't predict me with the ability" —
  offset your Ronin placement against a Hero Giant's Heroic Hurl.
