# Match: TOP 5 Decks in Clash Royale for the New Meta — game 4 of 5

## Summary

Game 4 of a five-deck showcase, joined in progress at clock 2:50. Ryley names the list himself as he starts:
"here, guys, we have this a log bait spam deck with the hero Valkyrie" (t=531-533), and the cards that show up

[bait](../archetypes/bait.md) shell that also cycles ([cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md)). The opponent runs a

[Battle Ram](../cards/battle-ram.md), [Barbarians](../cards/barbarians.md), [Zappies](../cards/zappies.md) and

0:28 and the enemy King Tower is activated and chipped (7728 to 6095) before the opponent concedes:
"It looks like he gave up though cuz he called a good game... Very nice win, guys" (t=682-684).
