## Summary

Game 6 of an eleven-game deck-showcase cut from Clash Royale League broadcast footage with Ryley's voice-over (video 1103.1s-1371.2s). The bottom player is Ryley himself — he says so repeatedly over the clip: "this is my favorite deck of all time and I was telling you guys that this deck is back after the addition of the Evo Princess. And I wasn't kidding... I'm playing Mighty Miner Bait in competitive" (1141-1157s). The list is classic Log Bait with Rocket and a Mighty Miner: Princess (evolved), Ice Spirit, Cannon (evolved), Skeletons, Goblin Barrel, Rocket, Mighty Miner and The Log. The opponent is Lucas ("one of the best players, you know, currently and of all time... a six time six time world finalist", 1105-1204s) on a Miner control list — the cards Ryley names or the field shows are Magic Archer, Miner, Wall Breakers, Bats, Bomb Tower, Berserker and a card the auto-transcript renders as "the delivery", which the arena tracker sees as lone Royal Recruits, i.e. Royal Delivery (see Data gaps). The clip is partial: it starts mid-game with no readable match clock (every play logs `clock None`) and no readable enemy tower HP for most of its length. Ryley plays the whole clip as a patient bait deck — Princess stacks in the middle, Cannon and Skeletons held for the Miner and Magic Archer, Ice Spirit saved for the specific threat it beats — and only commits Goblin Barrels once he has counted the opponent's Royal Delivery out of cycle. He wins: "right here is when I knew that I had officially won the game... he actually ended up giving up. So I was in a 1-0 lead in the duel for the qualification" (1351-1368s).

## How Ryley uses his cards

- **Princess** (evolved — he calls her "the Evo Princess" and once "the evil prince") — by far his most-played card in the clip and the card he credits for the deck's return: "this deck is back after the addition of the Evo Princess. And I wasn't kidding" (1145-1150s). Nine separate Princess placements are logged: an opener at (9, 9) (t=1109.9), the middle-lane pair at (8, 11)/(8, 10)/(8, 9) over t=1136-1197, deep centre placements at (8, 2) behind the King Tower (t=1223.4, t=1324.7), and the stack at (9, 11)/(8, 12)/(8, 12)/(8, 13) over t=1235-1301. He plays her for two reasons he states: to bait and to survive a Log — "I just went for the Evo Princess here. Just making sure to get more value out of it going for the log" (1250-1253s) and "I went for the evil princess here just to keep that princess alive. And I actually was able to keep that alive, guys. It was insane" (1302-1305s) — and as accumulated board control that ends the game: "there was no way that he was going to be able to come back from the rocket cycle and the princess stack just having two princess on the board" (1358-1362s).
- **Cannon** (evolved) — held, not spammed. Deploy labels put it at (9, 11) three times, (9, 12), (9, 13), (9, 15) twice and (11, 12), all in the standard 3-4 tiles-from-river pocket. The stated rule for holding it is cycle-dependency: "you guys may be wondering why I deny cannon... That is because I didn't have my ice spirit in cycle. And after he let the princess lock, he would just go for the bat split and then he'd minor wall breakers with it. And I wouldn't have my cannon or my ice spirit for anything" (1159-1172s). When he does play it he plays it immediately for the evolution's deploy barrage: "I got a really good EVO cannon right there. That was just absolutely gorgeous. I hit everything right there with that cannon. I guess he didn't expect me to play it right away or something" (1243-1250s). It is also his Magic Archer answer: "I went for the evo cannon as well, just to take out that magic archer with the help of a log" (1341-1345s), and he keeps it in reserve as insurance — "I had the Evo Cannon ready, just chilling here" (1328-1330s).
- **Ice Spirit** — the most frequently cycled card in the clip (fourteen 1-elixir steps and deploy labels at (14, 16), (15, 10), (2, 18), (9, 2), (14, 12), (16, 14), (15, 17), (14, 8), (9, 17), (3, 17), (3, 13), (14, 4)). - 1141-1157s — the deck endorsement itself: "this is my favorite deck of all time and I was telling you guys that this deck is back after the addition of the Evo Princess.
