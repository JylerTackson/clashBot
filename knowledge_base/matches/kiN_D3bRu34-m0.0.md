## Summary

Opening game of a single-deck showcase video published the morning Ronin released ("Ronin released this morning about 2 hours ago", 10-12s). Ryley's list is Ronin, Cannon, Goblin Barrel, Wall Breakers, Magic Archer, Ice Spirit, Fire Spirit and Skeletons, and he names the shell himself: "I have made this new Log Bait deck here with the Ronin cuz I feel like the Ronin synergizes really, really nicely with these quick cycle Log Bait decks" (21-27s). The Magic Archer slot is the **Hero** variant ([magic-archer-hero.md](../heroes/magic-archer-hero.md)) — at 149-151s he says "I have the ability ready in case he tries to do anything. I'm going to have ability preemptively", and the elixir trace shows a ~2-elixir spend with no hand change at that moment, matching Triple Threat's 2-elixir cost.

The opponent plays an Elixir Golem beatdown shell: Elixir Golem at clock 2:33, Skeleton Dragons at 2:19, Golden Knight at 1:46, a Wall Breakers pair at 1:33, P.E.K.K.A. at 1:25, Graveyard at 1:13 and Electro Dragon at 1:08. That is close to the ideal matchup for the card he is showcasing — three of their four heavy cards are melee.

Ryley plays the whole game as cheap-cycle bait: Cannon plus one-elixir spirits and Skeletons hold the left lane, and the Goblin Barrel goes up at 2:48, 1:59, 1:35 and 0:31 with Wall Breakers layered behind it. Ronin is used both as the defensive anchor (tiles [4, 11] at 36s, [8, 5] at 97s, [6, 2] at 132s) and, after 0:19, as the card that finishes the P.E.K.K.A. push. The enemy left tower falls 4858 -> 4352 -> 3584 -> 1312 -> 160 HP across the match and the right tower 4858 -> 3669 -> 704 -> 128, with the enemy King Tower reading damage (7728 HP) by 0:31. His own left tower is worn down to ~800 HP by the P.E.K.K.A./Golden Knight pressure but never falls. He calls the result at 167s: "Very nice win guys and I will see you all in the next match."

Most of the commentary is a Ronin explainer rather than play-by-play: the parry, its cooldown behaviour, what it beats, and what resets it.

## Opponent

An Elixir Golem beatdown deck. Observed: Elixir Golem (clock 2:33, again around 1:13 and 0:29), Skeleton Dragons (2:19 and 0:25), Golden Knight (1:46, and still alive at 0:11), a Wall Breakers pair (1:33 and 0:51), P.E.K.K.A. (1:25), Graveyard (1:13) and Electro Dragon (1:08).

Ryley's answers were the same shape every time: Cannon to pull the ground pusher into his half, Ronin on top of it, spirits and Skeletons to strip the support. He never spends more than one card above the cheap cycle on any defence, and the towers show why he could afford it — his left tower is worn to ~800 HP but every push dies short. The two cards he singles out as problems are Electro Dragon, because the stun resets the parry ("E Drake can get really annoying whenever you have the Ronin because of the resets", 124-127s), and by implication anything ranged, which he never says outright here. The P.E.K.K.A. is the one he wanted for the video, and the parry reflection is what he claims almost killed it outright (138-140s).
