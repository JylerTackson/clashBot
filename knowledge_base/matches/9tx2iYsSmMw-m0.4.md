## Summary

CRL broadcast footage of Ryley's own duel against Assaf, game three of the series ("I was in the next match here, guys, for game number three", t=933). The clip is a 170 s mid-game fragment: it opens with Ryley's left tower already at 2725 HP and ends just after he wins, so the opening and the crown count are missing. Ryley is the **bottom** player here — at t=1000 his own commentary is "the dark prince still splashes my tower for a ton of damage" while the bottom-left tower drops 2272 -> 1740, the small ground units in the bottom half are the enemy's, and at t=1093 he says "I saved my tower to win the game" with the bottom towers still standing. His deck is the one he names in the voice-over, not the low-confidence HUD read: a heavy bridge-spam list built around [Mega Knight](../cards/mega-knight.md) and [Golden Knight](../cards/golden-knight.md) ("this Mega Knight deck might be my best chance to have a winnable matchup", t=964; "Mega Knight Golden Knight is such a crazy combo", t=1027), with [Bandit](../cards/bandit.md), [Royal Ghost](../cards/royal-ghost.md), [Mother Witch](../cards/mother-witch.md), evolved [Battle Ram](../cards/battle-ram.md) and [Electro Wizard](../cards/electro-wizard.md); the eighth slot is never named or cleanly observed. Assaf is on a bait deck — Ryley expected log bait ("I saw Assaf go for the [Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md) and the [Fire Spirit](../cards/fire-spirit.md) ... he's probably going to go for a log bait deck", t=934-942) and the game shows [Royal Hogs](../cards/royal-hogs.md), a hero [Dark Prince](../cards/dark-prince.md) with an ability, [Vines](../cards/vines.md) and an evolved [Dart Goblin](../cards/dart-goblin.md). ## Opponent

Assaf plays a bait deck. Ryley predicts it before the footage resolves — "I saw Assaf go for the Goblin Barrel and the Fire Spirit ... he's probably going to go for a log bait deck" (t=934-942) — while noting "Assaf can play more than just log bait in the third game, so I'd account for other things as well" (t=974). What actually shows up: [Royal Hogs](../cards/royal-hogs.md) ("the piggies", t=992), a hero [Dark Prince](../cards/dark-prince.md) that splashes his tower and uses its ability twice (t=1002, t=1066: "for some reason, he did the dark prince ability. I was very confused by that"), [Vines](../cards/vines.md) held for his push ("look at that vines hitting everything except the tower ... well played by him kind of keeping the vine", t=1043-1049), an evolved [Dart Goblin](../cards/dart-goblin.md) (transcript "evo dark goblin", t=1068), [Arrows](../cards/arrows.md) spent inside his own half at t=1070, and a [Goblin Hut](../cards/goblin-hut.md) on the enemy side at [8,22] from t=940.9 that keeps feeding small ground units. Ryley's reads: answer the hogs with Mother Witch rather than a spell, use Bandit's dash to try to beat the Dark Prince's shield-and-splash, and treat every Arrows or ability spent as permission to send Golden Knight at the bridge. His closing read is that the opponent could not defend precisely enough against a Mega Knight/Golden Knight deck (t=1016-1027).

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=964 — Pick the deck that gives you a winnable matchup, not the strongest deck in a vacuum: "I did feel like this Mega Knight deck might be my best chance to have a winnable matchup ... there wasn't many good options for me in my opinion."
- t=974 — Do not lock your plan to one read of the opponent: "Assaf can play more than just log bait in the third game, so I'd account for other things as well."
- t=981 — Send the Golden Knight when the opponent has nothing tanky to absorb him: "I can just Golden Knight because he didn't have really a good tank for anything."
- t=988-990 — Use the dash ability at a moment the opponent has not budgeted for: "I don't think he expected me to use the ability right there."
- t=1016-1027 — A heavy Mega Knight/Golden Knight push forces near-perfect defence: "he would have a hard time defending my pushes ... it would have to be so so perfect. And against a deck like this, it is so so hard to be like so precise because the deck is just so broken in my opinion. Like Mega Knight Golden Knight is such a crazy combo."
- t=1050-1054 — When your tower is nearly dead, keep applying pressure rather than turtling: "I went for the ghost here plus the evo battle ram knowing that I definitely had to apply a lot of pressure."
- t=1072-1077 — Punish a spent spell immediately: after the Arrows go down, "now I could just golden knight the bridge which I did."
- t=1093 — The last defensive card can be worth more than a counterpush: "I reset both of them. And I saved my tower to win the game."
