Game 2 of 7 in a single-deck video (video time 174.3-370.0s). Ryley pilots the Ronin log-bait list the video is built around — Goblin Barrel, Wall Breakers, Ice Spirit, Fire Spirit, Magic Archer, Bomb Tower, Royal Delivery and Ronin — against a player he calls "Francis", running a Miner / Evo Inferno Dragon control deck with Goblin Cage, Goblin Brawler, Mega Minion, Ice Golem, Zap and Fireball. So it's going to be kind of difficult for both of us on the defensive end" (178-185s).

That is exactly how it plays out. The opponent chips with Miner and Goblin Brawler on the left (own left tower 4858 -> 4025 HP between t=204 and t=214) and repeatedly threatens with an Evolution Inferno Dragon that Ryley openly fears — "This is where it gets really scary cuz my deck does not have a lot of air defense, man. The Evo push gets just so difficult to stop" (289-294s). Ryley answers it with layered cheap defence (Bomb Tower, Royal Delivery, Ice Spirit stun, Magic Archer) and never loses a tower.

His offence is pure bait pressure: Goblin Barrel plus Fire Spirit in one lane, Wall Breakers in the other, over and over, forcing Zap, Goblin Cage and Mega Minion out of the opponent's hand every cycle. "Just force out more elixir every single time" (283-284s). I feel like after the Ronin nerf it's not really a crazy card on top ladder, but it's a really good card for people who are either under-levelled or face a lot of Mega Knights and maybe a lot of mid-ladder decks as well, but as far as the top ladder meta goes, I do feel like it's nothing insane, honestly" (225-243s).
