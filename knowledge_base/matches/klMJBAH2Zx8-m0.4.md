## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=1122-1129: "I'm not going to log though because I know that he has the Skeleton Army. So, I don't really want
  to give him that um opportunity to abuse the Skeleton Army." — spell discipline is matchup-specific.
- t=1130-1143: "I'd rather just take the Hog Rider shot knowing that this guy can't really defend split piggies
  that well... since I want to go for piggies often, I need to have my log for the Skeleton Army." — take the
  chip damage to keep the answer for your own win condition's counter.
- t=1147-1152: "I don't really need to log right away. I'm going to wait to see if we get more value." — delay
  the spell until the target commits.
- t=1197-1200: "I didn't want to just have him go for the Fireball, though. So, that's why I didn't log right
  away." — logging early would have let the opponent's Fireball answer the hogs for free.
- t=1220-1236: "I'm just never going for the random pre-logs in this matchup because I know that the only way
  that I could actually lose this, in my opinion, is if I go for some prediction and then miss it... I also want
  to just make sure that I ensure the win." — when ahead, cut the variance out of your own play.
- t=1201-1206: "we had the Piggy tank for the Mighty Miner on the right, if you notice. So, then it forced out an
  extra response from him, which is super good." — pair the cheap win condition with a second threat to drain
  extra answers.
- t=1266-1274: "they keep changing the spell damages so often that I don't know exactly how much every single
  spell does currently... I'm going to double check though." — check the actual spell numbers before committing
  to a kill sequence.
