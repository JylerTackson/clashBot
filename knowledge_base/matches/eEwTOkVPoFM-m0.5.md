## Summary

The sixth game (index 5) of a seven-game single-deck session, played with the Ronin log-bait list
(Goblin Barrel, Wall Breakers, Magic Archer, Ronin, Bomb Tower, Royal Delivery,
Ice Spirit, Fire Spirit). The opponent opened Evo Inferno Dragon behind a
support troop; Ryley identified the deck out loud within twenty seconds —
"He has the Evo Inferno Dragon... I guess it's going to be lumberloon freeze"
(t=1013-1016) — and then spent the whole game playing the bait game plan: a
Goblin Barrel on the enemy left tower on essentially every cycle (t=1009, 1036,
1088, 1146, 1166, 1182 — all at tile (3,25) or (2,23)), Wall Breakers into the
opposite lane, and Magic Archer laid down in the back "like crazy" as the
answer to the Evo Inferno Dragon.

The chip worked: the opponent's left princess tower fell from 4858 to roughly
600-1200 HP and the right from 4858 to about 1359, while Ryley's own towers
sat at ~4108 (left) and 4474 (right) as late as t=1208. No princess tower is
ever read at 0 HP in the capture, but the last four seconds show his left tower
collapsing (4108 -> 3991 at t=1210 -> 2222 at t=1212) under a Freeze plus Evo
Inferno Dragon push, with Ryley saying "Oh no, he's going to get the freeze off
with the inferno dragon... No, no, no, no, no" (t=1208-1209). The segment then
resets to a fresh board at t=1214 with no win or loss call in the transcript.
Because the game ran ~210 s of play — past regulation, i.e. into overtime, where
a game ends only when a tower falls — the most likely reading is that his left
