# Ryley's best predictions of 2026 — highlight montage (segment 2 of 2)

## Summary

This is **not a single match**: video time 255.4 s to 615.9 s is the closing
run of a "best predictions" montage, and the match clock jumps upward at
roughly t=324, 346, 366, 403, 410, 438, 495 and 580 s (and again inside the
first stretch at ~270, ~284 and ~310), so the segment is a chain of short
clips lifted from at least nine different games, each with a different deck
and a different opponent, each cut down to one prediction play with Ryley's
voice-over on top. The through-line is a single skill, not a deck: Ryley
calls the opponent's next card out loud *before* he commits, then places a
spell or a cheap troop on the tile that card is about to occupy — "pre-log
the skarmy", "evo snowball predict on his RG", "miner here to block a
potential E-Barbs placement", "Dark Goblin to predict his Hog Rider". Across
the clips he predicts with spells (The Log, Giant Snowball, Fireball, Zap,
Earthquake, Vines), with tank-blocks (Knight, Ice Golem, Berserker, Ice
Spirit) and with building placements (Tesla, Cannon, Goblin Hut), and twice
the prediction is a decision *not* to play — at t=405 he holds a Hog Rider
because "he might predict us", and at t=364 he calls a counter-prediction:
"he's going to musketeer predict me... Bro, I predicted his prediction."
The montage as a whole has no result; the clip at t=438-495 ends with the
enemy towers at 178/287 HP and Ryley saying "I think it's going to end up
getting him three-crowned. GG's, bro", and the last clip (t=580-615.9) ends
on "That is good game. Let's go, man. We had to do so many predictions to
win that", so those two clips at least are wins.

The decks on show change every clip. The engine's per-game HUD read
(Ice Spirit / Goblin Barrel / Barbarian Barrel / Electro Spirit / Berserker /
The Log / Fire Spirit / Knight) is a blend of several of them and is not a
real list, which is why `own_deck_key` is null. Interpreting loosely, the
clips at t=324-403 look like Hog 2.6-style **cycle** (Hog Rider, Cannon,
Skeletons, Ice Spirit, Ice Golem, Fireball, The Log, Musketeer) and the clips
from t=438 on look like **bait** — Ryley says so himself at t=451 ("this guy
hates log bait") while holding Goblin Barrel, Princess, Dart Goblin,
Skeleton Army and Wall Breakers — but no single archetype covers the segment,
so `own_archetype` is null.

## How Ryley uses his cards

Grouped by card across the whole montage, since no clip is long enough to give
a card a full profile on its own.

- **The Log** — the signature prediction spell of the segment, and he never
  logs reactively. Three separate pre-logs: on a Mega Minion before a Fireball
  follow-up at t=322.3 tile (8, 15) ("let's pre-log the mega minion, then we
  fireball... what a log prediction"); on a Skeleton Army at t=340.3 tile
  (14, 14) ("we pre-log the skarmy. There's no point not to go for it"); on
  Skeletons at t=379.4 tile (7, 14) ("let's pre-log the skellies"). At
  t=484.5 tile (12, 21) he logs Spear Goblins on the enemy side of the river:
  "it was literally on the perfect tile. Picture-perfect log here on the
  spears."
- **Miner** — used as a *placement* prediction more than as a chip win
  condition: onto an Inferno Dragon spot (t=272.3, tile (15, 20)), onto a
  Princess spot (t=346.3/349.3, tiles (9, 19)/(8, 21) and again t=470.5 tile
  (9, 24)), onto a Wizard (t=309.3 tile (7, 17)), and onto an Elite
  Barbarians landing zone (t=426.4 tile (2, 23)) — "minor here to block a
  potential E-Barbs placement. WHAT A GOOD MINOR PREDICTION."
- **Giant Snowball** — thrown at a Royal Giant's landing tile before the RG
  exists, specifically to buy a lock-on: "evo snowball predict on his RG to
  try to get my expo to lock onto the tower. That could instantly win me the
  game" (t=288.3, tile (2, 17)).
- **X-Bow** — placed at (2, 12) at t=282.3/285.3 as the payoff for the
  snowball prediction; the whole play is set up so the X-Bow gets its lock-on
  ("let's expo here").
- **Berserker** — always as a cheap body placed *ahead of* a card he expects,
  not as damage. At t=359.4 tile (9, 14) "Berserker here as well, continue
  block"; at t=495.5 tile (2, 13) held with Vines because "he might try to go
