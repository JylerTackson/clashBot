# Match: NERF the Hog Rider NOW!!! — game 1

## Summary

The opening game of a six-game single-deck session, and Ryley names the list himself in the first ten
seconds: "we are going to be playing the best Hog Rider Earthquake deck in Clash Royale" (t=6-9). The list
is the 2.6-style cycle shell — [Hog Rider](../cards/hog-rider.md), [Cannon](../cards/cannon.md) (Evo),
[Mighty Miner](../cards/mighty-miner.md), [Firecracker](../cards/firecracker.md) (Evo),
[Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md), [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md),
[The Log](../cards/the-log.md) and [Earthquake](../cards/earthquake.md); the pipeline's video-level
consensus put [Heal Spirit](../cards/heal-spirit.md) in that eighth slot, but it rests only on a
0.466-confidence HUD hand read while Earthquake is backed by his own naming of the deck and three casts
into the opponent's half (see Data gaps). The KB's closest neighbour is
[Hog MM EvoCannon 2.6 Cycle](../decks/barbarian-barrel-cannon-earthquake-electro-spirit-firecracker-hog-rider-mighty-miner-skeletons.md),
which shares seven of the eight cards.
The opponent is a mid-ladder [Elixir Golem](../cards/elixir-golem.md) beatdown deck that Ryley identifies
piece by piece — "I guess this guy is Elixir Golem freeze" (t=67), "I think he's playing the double freeze
variation... that means he has the [Goblin Machine](../cards/goblin-machine.md)" (t=93-100) — backed by
[Night Witch](../cards/night-witch.md), [Mini P.E.K.K.A.](../cards/mini-p-e-k-k-a.md),
[Vines](../cards/vines.md) and an Evo [Skeleton Army](../cards/skeleton-army.md). The game is one-sided in
lane terms: his left tower never takes a point of damage all match (4858 the whole way) while both players
grind the right lane. A [Freeze](../cards/freeze.md) on an E-Golem push around clock 1:53 costs him roughly
2300 HP off his right tower (4858 -> 2536 between t=50 and t=68) and he never recovers that lead in regular
time, ending it behind on damage (own right 2065 vs enemy right 2916) and going to overtime at 0-0. In
overtime he wins on chip: Mighty Miner ability plays and a fourth and fifth Cannon hold the golemites while
Hog Rider plus Earthquake plus Evo Firecracker take the enemy right tower from 2916 down to 129 HP, and he
calls it at t=272 — "let's go for the earthquake. Let's go. Very nice win."
