# Ryley vs a Three Musketeers pump deck (game 6 of the session)

## Summary

Ryley pilots his Mother Witch bridge-spam list (Bandit, Battle Ram, Elite Barbarians, Zappies, Mother Witch, Heal Spirit, Rage, Freeze) against a Three Musketeers deck with an Elixir Collector, an Evolution Minion Horde, Dark Prince, Ice Golem and Bandit. He calls the opponent's deck at 2:45 ("I think this guy plays three Musketeers, but I'm not 100% sure") and plays the whole match around one idea he states outright: he does not believe he can defend a good Evo Minion Horde push, so he takes the left tower early rather than sit back. An aggressive Freeze on the enemy left tower at clock 2:04 takes that tower from 3942 to 326 HP, and Elite Barbarians finish it around 0:58 (tower reads null from t=1220). The opponent's Minion Horde push then chews his right tower down from 4858 to 1883 between 0:45 and 0:38 and takes it at 0:03, so regulation ends 1-1 and the game goes to overtime. In overtime the Mother Witch's Cursed Hogs kite the Three Musketeers push almost indefinitely while Ryley is at 0-2 elixir, and a Battle Ram he plays as a 3M prediction connects on the enemy side at 1:14 OT for the win ("Yes, let's go! ... It just distracted forever and we won because of that").

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=1112 (clock 2:47): split a cheap unit behind the king tower as an opener to learn the opponent's deck — "splitting my Zappies here. Try to figure out what this guy is playing."
- t=1156 (clock 2:04): a Freeze on a tower is worth the risk when the alternative is defending a push you cannot answer — "Low-key, I know it's aggressive, but I'm going to get a lot of damage."
- t=1166-1179 (clock 1:53-1:41): pick the race deliberately when a matchup is bad on defence — "I feel like I can't defend a really good Evo minion horde push ... that's why I felt the need to get the tower cuz I'm probably going to take a ton of damage when he just goes for his minion horde push anyway."
- t=1211 (clock 1:08): choose the cheaper threat when the opponent's answer is expensive and predictable — "Actually, he might 3M, so maybe I'll just go for the battle ram instead."
- t=1250 (clock 0:28): answer a swarm with a Mother Witch behind it, not just a spell — "I need to get a minion horde down, a mother witch down on this minion horde. That's what I need to do."
- t=1306-1317 (OT 1:32-1:22): the Mother Witch's Cursed Hogs are a defensive resource, not just chip damage — "That Mother Witch piggy is kiting everything ... look at this card, guys. It's just distracting and we're surviving."
- t=1324 (OT 1:14): Freeze exists to guarantee a connection, not to kill — "Let's freeze this as well so we can get the connection on the tower."
- t=1333-1337 (end): "It just distracted forever and we won because of that. We had no Elixir but it just keeps spawning a win condition in front of his push."
