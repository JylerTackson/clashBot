## Lessons stated by Ryley

- 1405-1423s — do the post-mortem after the set, not during it: "there's no point like sitting there sulking in the middle of a duel when, you know, I'm still able to win the duel set. Of course, I can look over mistakes later... But there's no point of doing it in the moment cuz all that's going to do is affect me for the next game and just make it harder to win in the future."
