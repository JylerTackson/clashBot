## Summary

First game of a five-deck showcase. Ryley opens the video with what he calls a "hyper bait deck" — Skeletons, Berserker, Ice Spirit, Wall Breakers, Dart Goblin, Skeleton Barrel, The Log and Rocket (2.6 average elixir) — and frames it as the post-balance-change version of the archetype: "Hyper bait's probably dead, you know, the spirits got nerfed. But guys, instead of having a lot of spirits in hyper bait, now you just have a bunch of win conditions with a rocket and a log" (39-46s). The Berserker slot is the **Hero** variant ([berserker-hero.md](../heroes/berserker-hero.md)): he calls it "the Hero Berserker" at 56s and explicitly leans on Savage Survival's minimum-1-HP effect — "you're able to just abuse the fact that it cannot be taken off of the board" (65-67s). Two of his slots are evolutions by his own account, the Evo Skeleton Barrel (87s, 163s) and Evo Wall Breakers (150s).

The opponent answers with The Log, Little Prince, Princess, Spear Goblins, Elite Barbarians and (by Ryley's read of their hand) Battle Ram, applying most of their pressure with paired Elite Barbarians down a lane. Ryley never defends passively: from clock 1:39 onward almost every defensive placement is immediately followed by a Wall Breakers, Skeleton Barrel or bridge Berserker in the other lane, which is exactly the plan he states — "you just never want to let go. You just always want to keep on going aggressive. Never really give them a chance to breathe" (95-100s).

The scoreboard follows the pressure. The enemy right tower reads 4858 HP at 0:06 in-video, 4487 by 1:52, 1657 by 1:12 after the Wall Breakers / Skeleton Barrel window around 86-98s, and 460 by 0:14 after the last Berserker + Wall Breakers wave. A Rocket at the very end ("just rocket cuz that princess locked in", 172-174s) takes the enemy left tower down as well. He calls the result himself at 176s: "Very nice win to start off the video."
