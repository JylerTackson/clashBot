## Summary

Sixth game of a single-deck "Piggies Lightning" session: Royal Hogs, Lightning,
Furnace, Cannon, Berserker, Barbarian Barrel, Electro Spirit and Skeletons — a
3.0-elixir cycle deck that wins by holding with cheap bodies and a building
while a Furnace and Royal Hogs chip. The capture only becomes readable at clock
2:00 in double elixir, with a Golem push already walking into Ryley's left lane,
so roughly the first minute of the game is missing. The opponent is a Golem
beatdown player — Golem, Skeleton Dragons, evolved Electro Dragon, evolved
Elite Barbarians and an Elixir Collector, i.e. the shape of the knowledge base's
[Golem Double Dragon Nado Pump](../decks/barbarian-barrel-berserker-electro-dragon-elite-barbarians-elixir-collector-golem-skeleton-dragons-tornado.md)
deck. Ryley survives the opening Golem push for about 1,200 tower HP by blocking
with Berserker and Electro Spirit and then spending Lightning on the support,
counters immediately with Royal Hogs in front of his Furnace, and from 1:07
onwards never lets the Golem player build again: he keeps a Furnace on the board
at all times, blocks each Skeleton Dragon/Electro Dragon wave with 1-2 elixir
cards placed high on his own half, and grinds the enemy left tower from 4150 to
about 400. The opponent concedes at 1:38 of overtime ("Yeah, he gave up. Let's
go. Good game. Very nice win.").

## Opponent

Golem beatdown. Ryley reads the deck out loud at 2:00-1:58 — "I'm pretty sure
it's like skelly drags, evo e drag, and evo ebarbs in this deck" — and the
observed cards match: [Golem](../cards/golem.md) (a push already arriving at
2:00, a second one at 0:10), [Skeleton Dragons](../cards/skeleton-dragons.md)
(waves at 1:57, 0:53, 0:48, 0:31, 0:25),
[Electro Dragon](../cards/electro-dragon.md) (1:53, 0:42, 0:23),
[Elite Barbarians](../cards/elite-barbarians.md) and
[Barbarians](../cards/barbarians.md) (1:26-1:20 counterpush, evolved Barbarians
again at 0:03) and an [Elixir Collector](../cards/elixir-collector.md) at [12,26]
at 1:07. That is six of the eight cards in the knowledge base's
[Golem Double Dragon Nado Pump](../decks/barbarian-barrel-berserker-electro-dragon-elite-barbarians-elixir-collector-golem-skeleton-dragons-tornado.md)
list. Ryley's counter-plan is elixir denial rather than a race: block each dragon
wave with 1-2 elixir cards placed high on his own half, keep a Furnace running so
the opponent has to keep answering, and punish with Royal Hogs whenever the
Golem player spends. He tracks the opponent's rotation explicitly ("now he
doesn't have the skelly drags for the evo piggies. And if he golems, obviously he
won't be able to afford the evo e drag") and treats the Collector as acceptable
because his own pressure is already faster.
