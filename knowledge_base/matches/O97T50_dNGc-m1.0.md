# Match O97T50_dNGc-m1.0 — Piggies Lightning Tesla vs Evo Royal Giant / Monk, game 1 of a CRL Monthly Finals set

## Summary

A tournament-standard game from Ryley's Clash Royale League Monthly Finals day-2 vlog, picked up in double elixir at clock 1:59 and run to the end of the game. He is on the Tesla version of his Royal Hogs list — [Royal Hogs](../cards/royal-hogs.md), [Tesla](../cards/tesla.md), [Furnace](../cards/furnace.md), [Lightning](../cards/lightning.md), [Barbarian Barrel](../cards/barbarian-barrel.md), [Berserker](../cards/berserker.md), [Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md), [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md) — the deck the knowledge base already holds as [Piggies Lightning Tesla Ryley](../decks/barbarian-barrel-berserker-electro-spirit-furnace-lightning-royal-hogs-skeletons-tesla.md), and he says so himself at t=576: "again, I had this piggies deck, so almost a exact mirror match of the one that we played in the first set against the Riku player". This is a deck-showcase-style vlog where each game uses a different list, so this file is scoped to this game only.

The opponent is on an evolved [Royal Giant](../cards/royal-giant.md) control deck with [Monk](../cards/monk.md) as the champion, [Fisherman](../cards/fisherman.md), [Skeleton Dragons](../cards/skeleton-dragons.md), [Mother Witch](../cards/mother-witch.md), [Fireball](../cards/fireball.md) and [Tombstone](../cards/tombstone.md) ([Barbarians](../cards/barbarians.md) and a [Wizard](../cards/wizard.md) also register on the unit tracker — see Data gaps). Ryley says he had the match-up scouted going in: "I was pretty happy with it cuz I know exactly how to play this match-up. I did assume that he would have fireball though instead of having a [Void](../cards/void.md)" (t=586-593).

The whole game is Ryley farming Furnace value while keeping his Tesla alive, and taking Royal Hogs pressure at the right bridge only while he is already defending. The decisive stretch is the opponent spending Fireball on his defence at t=679: he answers with Evo Royal Hogs at the bridge plus Lightning behind them, and the enemy right tower falls from 2164 to 820 between t=682 and t=694. The opponent's Monk counterpush and a second Fireball trade his own right tower down from 3052 to 1705, but a second Royal Hogs + Barbarian Barrel + Tesla package at t=743-748 takes the enemy right tower to 154 ("look at that guys, the piggies took the tower", t=753) and a final bridge Royal Hogs at t=771 finishes it. Ryley closes: "he just, you know, recognized that he was giving up because he missed his lightning block. And I started off one to zero" (t=772-776) — a win, first game of the set.

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- **t=598** — "I was just trying to make sure that I was careful, always putting my piggies" — Royal Hogs pressure as a constant, careful drip rather than a committed push.
- **t=603-606** — "you're not really able to activate king tower too easily off of royal hogs": Royal Hogs are a low-risk chip card against king-tower activation.
- **t=611-612** — "I just wanted to cycle my barbarian barrel here and I just wanted to keep on spamming my furnaces": cheap spell on the back edge purely to reach the Furnace again.
- **t=621-630** — "just making sure to keep my Tesla live... having the Tesla die would be really, really bad for me": in the Royal Giant match-up the Tesla is the card you spend Skeletons and Electro Spirit to protect.
- **t=636-643** — "making an RG push without monk is not really ideal, especially when I have a furnace cuz I knew that my furnace would get a crazy amount of value now as long as I protected it from the barbarian barrel."
- **t=679-688** — "because he used the fireball, guys, I did want to go for the evo piggies": send the evolved Royal Hogs on the cycle after the opponent's splash spell is spent.
- **t=693-704** — "always good to just get as many fire spirits coming out as possible because the furnace like ever since that rework it got, it is so so strong defensively that I just always want to have a furnace on the board with this deck to be honest."
- **t=738-743** — "I could have panicked Tesla, but I realized that it would be probably better just to eat like 800 damage to a thousand just to have my cycle perfect": take tower damage to keep the cycle in order.
- **t=745-746** — "here I went for the piggies just to pressure while defending."
- **t=757-765** — "I was just waiting for him to try to lightning block me with a tombstone... I just wasn't taking any risks. There's no point, you know, for me to take that risk": do not fire the Lightning finisher into a live block; wait and chip with Royal Hogs instead.
- **t=670-676** (opinion, not piloting) — "I do feel like the skelly queen might need a little bit of a nerf... it has more HP than a giant."
