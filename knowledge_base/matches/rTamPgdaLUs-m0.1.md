## Summary

Game 2 of 4 in a deck-showcase video built around the Hero Berserker ([heroes/berserker-hero.md](../heroes/berserker-hero.md)): each game uses a different list, and this one is X-Bow siege with Rocket — X-Bow, Tesla, Hero Berserker, Electro Spirit, Fire Spirit, Skeletons, The Log, Rocket (2.9 average Elixir), i.e. the X-Bow 2.9 shell with Rocket in the spell slot and Fire Spirit where most lists run Archers. He announces the plan in the first seconds: "we're going to be trying out the hero berserker with X-Bow… I have actually found that the hero berserker is pretty strong in siege decks" (177-185s). The opponent is a Golem list with a Skeleton King tower troop: The Log, Rage, Miner and an 8-Elixir Golem are confirmed on the field, and the commentary adds their Rocket, an Evolved Tesla and their own Hero Berserker. The whole game is one loop — X-Bow at the river, Berserker at the bridge, then Savage Survival to erase whatever is dropped on the X-Bow — and it forces the opponent into pure Rocket defence, which is exactly what Ryley wants ("this is basically just guaranteeing him have to defend with the rocket, which is really good for us", 270-273s). Regulation ends with the towers alive (his 2982/2122, the opponent's 2472/2112), and in double/triple Elixir overtime two Rockets at tile (12, 22) plus a final X-Bow lock take the enemy right tower to 354 HP; he calls it at 449s — "Very nice win, dude.
