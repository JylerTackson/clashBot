## Summary

The last game (6 of 6) of a single-deck session on the list of the video title: Mighty Miner, Cannon, Skeletons, Evolved Baby Dragon, Lightning, Electro Spirit, Evolved Royal Hogs and The Log — a 3.25-elixir Royal Hogs cycle deck whose whole plan is to defend cheaply and convert the opponent's spent elixir into Lightning value. The opponent (named "Zoro" at 1305s) is a log-bait variant Ryley has faced before and describes card by card before it appears: "I know this guy plays like a weird log bait variation... he has the firecracker... He has like skeleton barrel and stuff like that. I think he has like golden knight and inferno tower, too, if I'm not mistaken... He also has like a NATO [Tornado]" (1306-1323s).

Ryley never builds a big push. He back-places Royal Hogs when the opponent's cycle is awkward, defends everything else with Cannon, Skeletons, Electro Spirit and the Evolved Baby Dragon, and cashes in with Lightning: the enemy right tower loses exactly 423 HP four separate times (4858 -> 4435 at 1332s, 3727 -> 3304 at 1504s, 3304 -> 2881 at 1560s, 2881 -> 2458 at 1572s), which is roughly 1700 of the 2400 damage he does all game. The rest is hog chip — "Little two nibbles right there, dude. Piggy nibbles do so much damage" (1436-1440s) — and one Mighty Miner connection.

The strategic thesis is stated out loud at 1409-1427s: the opponent "literally can't break through... he's just basically getting no damage and I can just continue lightning his inferno towers over and over again. Sometimes even like lightning his firecrackers and stuff cuz there's no reason not to." He does exactly that, including a deliberate 6-for-3 Lightning on a Firecracker "cuz we're in elixir" (1500-1502s).

No crown falls on either side in the 289s of readable video, but the tower reads tell the story: enemy right 4858 -> 2458 HP against his own lowest tower at 4024 HP. He calls it at 1583s ("Okay, yep. It's just over. Let's go") and signs the video off at 1586s: "Very nice win. Thank you all for watching." A win.
