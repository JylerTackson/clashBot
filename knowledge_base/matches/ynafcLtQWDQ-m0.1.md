## Summary

I think he might have the Royal Hogs Goblin Stein deck, which is with the Evo E-barbs" (295-300s) — a read confirmed later by Goblinstein, Goblin Hut, Spear Goblins, Inferno Dragon, Fireball and a Barbarian Barrel of their own.

Regular time was almost pure chip: Ryley cycled Furnace and Tesla behind hogs at the right bridge and traded, but by the end of normal time only ~550 HP had come off his right tower and ~1130 off the enemy right. Overtime opened badly — a Goblinstein-plus-hogs wave took his left tower from 4858 to 3560 while he narrated "Oh, no. I I think we just lost. No tower fell; the game ran out the overtime clock with the enemy right tower the lowest on the board, and he calls the result himself: "That was not an easy win, man. This is what I'm talking about, dude. This deck can really win anything if you play properly. Holy. Good game" (552-558s).
