# Ryley's Hog 2.6 cycle vs Electro Giant / Heroic Bowler beatdown — a 129 HP overtime win (game 6 of 6)

## Summary

This is the last game of the "NERF the Hog Rider NOW!!!" session and Ryley is on the same fast Hog cycle he has run all video: Hog Rider, an evolved Firecracker, an evolved Cannon, Mighty Miner, Skeletons, Electro Spirit and The Log, with a 3-Elixir spell he repeatedly calls "earthquake" (he names the list himself at t=1267 — "e-giant actually nowadays has decent chances against **Hog EQ**"). The opponent is an Electro Giant beatdown player with a Heroic Bowler, an Evo Baby Dragon, an Evo Goblin Cage, Zappies and Lightning; Ryley works the list out live, from "he has goblin cage and baby dragon, this is probably going to be e-giant or golem" (t=1253) to "oh, he has a Bowler, okay. I guess that means he has E-Giant" (t=1287) to "okay, he has Zappies. What the heck is this?" (t=1306). The middle of the game goes badly — a combined Bowler/Electro Giant/spell answer around clock 0:58 takes his left tower from 4282 to 2665 HP and kills his Firecracker, and all he can say is "bro, that is disgusting. That is all I can really form a sentence for" (t=1366-1372). From there it becomes a pure race: the enemy left Princess Tower steps down 4858 -> 3597 -> 2596 -> 2046 -> 1450 -> 943 -> 653 HP off repeated Hog Rider connections, Earthquakes and Firecracker chip, while his own left tower falls to 1545 and then ~620. The opponent needed two Lightnings to close it and only ever got one — "he needs two lightnings ... he's going to be able to get the second one, I believe" (t=1520-1523). Ryley cycles as fast as the deck allows in the final fifteen seconds (Log, Skeletons, Electro Spirit, Earthquake, Log) and takes the enemy left tower from 653 to 129 HP: "by the the hair of our chinny chin chin, we win. Yes, let's go. He didn't get the lightning. Oh my god, bro. Good game" (t=1533-1539).
