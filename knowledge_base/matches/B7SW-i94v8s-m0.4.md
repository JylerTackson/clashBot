# Match: My MAIN Deck for Season End in Clash Royale — game 5 (of 6)

## Summary

Game 5 of a single-deck season-end session, and the one that gets away. Ryley pilots the video's consensus
eight — [Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md) (Evo), [Wall Breakers](../cards/wall-breakers.md) (Evo),
[Magic Archer](../cards/magic-archer.md), [Berserker](../cards/berserker.md),
[Bomb Tower](../cards/bomb-tower.md), [Royal Delivery](../cards/royal-delivery.md),
[Ice Spirit](../cards/ice-spirit.md) and [Fire Spirit](../cards/fire-spirit.md) — the
[bait](../archetypes/bait.md) / [bridge-spam](../archetypes/bridge-spam.md) list catalogued as
[Ryley Log Bait Bridge Spam](../decks/berserker-bomb-tower-fire-spirit-goblin-barrel-ice-spirit-magic-archer-royal-delivery-wall-breakers.md).
He scouts the opponent in the first four seconds — "This guy plays piggies, earthquake, evo cannon, log,
skelly spirit" (t=1019-1022) — plus an [Archer Queen](../cards/archer-queen.md) he sees at 2:38; the cards
that actually appear match the knowledge base's
[EvoRHogs AQ EvoCannon 2.9 Cycle](../decks/archer-queen-cannon-earthquake-ice-spirit-royal-delivery-royal-hogs-skeletons-the-log.md)
list exactly, a [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md) deck that never has to attack. The game turns on the first
twenty seconds: the enemy King Tower is activated at clock 2:23-2:21 (first reading 7499, then 7270 HP), and
Ryley immediately calls it — "this is probably game over already with him being able to activate the king"
(t=1044-1047). From there it is a four-minute grind of barrels and Wall Breakers into a three-tower defence.
Regular time ends 0-0 on crowns with the damage narrowly his way (enemy 4503 / 3750, his own 4624 / 4858),
but in a two-minute overtime the Archer Queen and Royal Hogs chew his left tower down while he can only take
the enemy right tower from 3750 to 3174. He loses on the lowest-tower tiebreak with his left tower at 2982 HP
against the opponent's 3174, and says so at t=1304: "Oh, I didn't even get one. We lost. Man, if I got one, I
would have won the game. GG's."
