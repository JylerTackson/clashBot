# Match: This Elite Barbs Evolution Deck is UNBEATEN — game 1

## Summary

The opening game of a single-deck session built around the brand-new Evolved
[Elite Barbarians](../cards/elite-barbarians.md). Ryley names the deck himself at t=62-67 —
"The deck I'm going to be playing it with today is just this hog rider e-barbs deck. A little bit of an OG
deck right here for a OG card" — and the eight cards he plays match the video-level consensus:
[Hog Rider](../cards/hog-rider.md), Evo [Elite Barbarians](../cards/elite-barbarians.md), Evo
[Firecracker](../cards/firecracker.md), Hero [Ice Golem](../heroes/ice-golem-hero.md),
[Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md), [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md),
[The Log](../cards/the-log.md) and [Earthquake](../cards/earthquake.md) — a 2.75-elixir
[cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md) shell with a 6-elixir [bridge-spam](../archetypes/bridge-spam.md) threat
bolted on. The opponent is a heavier list: their own (non-Evo) Elite Barbarians,
[Tombstone](../cards/tombstone.md), [Phoenix](../cards/phoenix.md), [Goblins](../cards/goblins.md), a
[Mega Knight](../cards/mega-knight.md) at clock 0:42 and, on the arena detector, a
[Golem](../cards/golem.md) in the last 40 seconds — Ryley reads them as a beatdown player and says so at
t=113-115 ("I'm going to ice golem preemptively cuz he might golem"). The game is one-sided: the Evo E-Barbs
spear-and-rage wins the E-Barbs mirror at the bridge outright at clock 1:21-1:11 ("his e-barbs just get
absolutely destroyed"), the enemy left tower falls around t=128 (clock 0:45, "It's going to be completely
tower down"), and Ryley spends the rest of triple elixir camped in the opponent's left half. He takes the
enemy right tower from 4858 to 2086 HP while his own two towers finish at 4589 and 4781 of 4858. He calls it
at t=172: "GG's. Nice win."

## How Ryley uses his cards

- **[Elite Barbarians](../cards/elite-barbarians.md) (Evolved)** — three sets, all offensive, all at or
  beyond the bridge: [14, 14] at t=47 (right bridge, own side), the bridge again at t=91.9 into the
  opponent's own E-Barbs, and [3, 21] *inside the opponent's left half* at t=139.1 once that tower was down.
  He never uses them defensively and says why: "This card is really aggressive-based. I feel like it's not...
  very defensive at all" and "the Evo not being too great defensively" (t=76-90). The Evo's value in this
  game was the mirror at t=92-104 — the spear damages the enemy pair and the rage trail speeds his own —
  and the anti-distraction he closes on: "the E-Barbs are so much harder to distract now... Just makes them
  so much better offensively. Not even like with the rage, just with having that like Spear Goblin damage."
- **[Hog Rider](../cards/hog-rider.md)** — four deploys (t=17.9, t=73.1, t=115.1, t=155.5), always the left
  bridge at tile [1, 14] / [1, 15] until the final one. He uses it as low-commitment chip pressure rather
  than as the centre of a push: "We can still go for the hog rider here just to apply some pressure"
  (t=72-74) at 2 elixir, and he holds it deliberately while pre-placing: "I want to have the hog rider ready"
  (t=115).
- **[Firecracker](../cards/firecracker.md) (Evolved)** — always behind his own towers, tiles [8, 1], [8, 6],
