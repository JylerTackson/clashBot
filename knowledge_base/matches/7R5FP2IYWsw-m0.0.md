## Summary

Game 1 of a single-deck session on classic log bait: Goblin Barrel, Princess, Goblin Gang, Knight, Ice Spirit, Inferno Tower, Rocket and The Log, with the Knight played in his Hero form and Evolutions on Goblin Barrel and Princess ("because of the synergy between the Evo Barrel and the Goblin Gang and the Hero Knight, you are actually just able to win pretty much most matchups in this season", 55s). The opponent runs a Miner / Evo Wall Breakers cycle deck with Evo Bats, Spear Goblins, Hero Berserker and The Log — a matchup Ryley calls out immediately as "one of the more annoying matchups" (73s). Regular time ends level and the game goes to overtime, where he swaps to Rocket cycling on the opposite (right) lane while still barrelling ("we can start rocket cycling on the right side. I would rather rocket cycle the opposite lane anyways", 248s). The enemy right tower is nearly flattened by the end and Ryley signs off "Good game. We played that really well, man" (290s).
