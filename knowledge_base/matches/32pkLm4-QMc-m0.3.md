## Summary

Fourth game of a six-game single-deck session (video 703.4s-994.0s), picked up at clock 2:52 and run through
regulation and a full two-minute overtime. Ryley is on the video's consensus Mega Knight bait list —
[Mega Knight](../cards/mega-knight.md), an evolved [Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md) ("we do have an

finisher and, as he keeps pointing out, no spell at all: "we just keep facing Log Bait without having any
spells, man. It's really unfortunate" (t=714-717). The opponent is a mirror-image bait deck —
"This guy plays with Evo Skeletons, Log Bait" (t=705-707) — with Goblin Barrel, Princess, Goblin Gang, Knight,
The Log and, decisively, an [Inferno Tower](../cards/inferno-tower.md). Ryley wins the first exchange
outright: a Princess-plus-Berserker read at clock 2:03 catches the opponent's swarm, the opponent is left
without a Knight in cycle, and the enemy right tower falls from 4858 to destroyed between clock 2:00 and 1:24
("that's tower down already. Beautiful tower down", t=797-798). He pays for it immediately — his own left
tower goes 3729 -> 686 in the same window and dies at clock 0:54 ("That's tower down for sure. There's nothing

this one. I'm not going to lie. I tried, man") and t=972 ("Princess, yeah, there's just no way to win this").

- t=711-717 — the matchup problem stated up front: "I feel like we just keep facing Log Bait without having
  any spells, man. It's really unfortunate."

  cycle, he's just completely cooked... he lost almost his entire tower just trying to get back to that
  Knight."
- t=778-784 — the cost of a committed push: "He got a lot of damage back, but obviously when we overspend to
  go for a play like that, you know, stuff like this is bound to happen."
- t=784-797 — how he sequences the bait: bridge Bush, then Berserker in the opposite lane, then the Barrel
  placed against the answer he expects — "he has to watch out for that bush, dude. That bush is really
  menacing."
- t=800-808 — a tower lead does not fix a bad matchup: "with the type of deck that he has, us really having a

- t=902-905 — play your spell-vulnerable support after their spell is gone: "we can dark goblin too because he

- t=952-962 — the summary lesson of the loss: "Like I feel like if we didn't lose our tower for basically like

  t≈993 with his right tower at 1285 and king at 3960 against the enemy left tower at 3031 (the enemy right
  tower has been destroyed since clock 1:24 and its reading is frozen at 459). He concedes twice on air —
  "I don't think we're going to be able to win this one. I'm not going to lie. I tried, man" (t=912-914) and
  "there's just no way to win this" (t=972-974) — and signs off with "Whatever, man. What could we even have
  done here? I'll see you all in the next match" (t=987-990). `result: loss` comes from that, plus the
  lowest-tower tiebreak going against him; it is not observed directly.
- **Tower HP readings never reach zero and glitch upward.** Own left reads 136 at t=745 and 4258 again at
  t=747, 6585 at t=772 and 6686 at t=790; enemy left reads 8231 at t=967; own right reads 285 at t=978 and
  1285 again at t=979. A destroyed tower appears to freeze at its last value (enemy right at 459 from t=797,
  own left at 417 from t=821 and then 1616 from t=858), so tower destruction is read from the commentary
  ("that's tower down already", t=797; "That's tower down for sure", t=821) plus the king tower starting to
  take damage at t=827.
- **HUD card names are unreliable** (hand confidence 0.53; own-elixir drift abs mean 0.99, max 8.47). The play
  list contains eleven own cards that are not in the deck at all — Tombstone (x3), Ice Golem (x4), Electro
  Spirit (x3), Zap, Barbarian Barrel, Bowler — always with a real elixir drop but a mis-read hand slot. Only
  own plays backed by a deploy label, or by the commentary, are cited in this file. The three "Goblins" HUD
  events and the five 'GobliN' / 'obliN' / 'GobliNY' labels are truncated Goblin Barrel and Dart Goblin
  reads: the clearest case is t=981.6, where a 'GobliNY' label at [2, 24] and a 'GobliN BaRRel' label at
  [3, 24] one second apart describe the same Barrel he narrates at t=979-982. That is why this file uses the
  video-level consensus deck (Goblin Barrel in the eighth slot) rather than the per-game read, which listed
  [Goblins](../cards/goblins.md).
- **The Berserker's ability is invisible to the pipeline.** Savage Survival costs 3 elixir and empties no hand
  slot, so it only shows up as unreadable drops. Three own UNIDENTIFIED events of exactly -5 (t=851.7, 858.2,
  917.6) match Berserker (2) plus the ability (3); the t=851.7 one is narrated as "I'm going to berserk here
  for the princess" with a Berserker label at [14, 15] the same second. Two more own drops cannot be
  attributed: -6 at t=887.1 (narrated as a Mega Knight, which costs 7) and -2 at t=958.1, plus a -8 at
  t=993.8 as the game ends.
- **The opponent deck read from the pipeline is not usable as given.** `deck_known` is marked "complete" as
  goblin-barrel, skeletons, spear-goblins, princess, wizard, guards, earthquake, knight, but there are 29
  cycle violations against 4 confirmations, and it omits the Inferno Tower that Ryley names five times and
  that the tracker sees at seven different tiles. [Spear Goblins](../cards/spear-goblins.md) rows are almost
  certainly the [Goblin Gang](../cards/goblin-gang.md) halves he names at t=766 and t=937.
  [Wizard](../cards/wizard.md) (4 detections) and [Guards](../cards/guards.md) (1) are never mentioned and do
  not fit a bait list. [Earthquake](../cards/earthquake.md) is logged twice at [14, 30], the opponent's own
  back corner, once tagged for each side in the same frame — most likely an artifact of Ryley's Goblin Barrel
  landing there. A single Rocket detection at t=882.9 and the 1000 HP unattributed hit on his right tower at
  t=977.5 hint at a big spell in the eighth slot, but nothing confirms it, so `opponent_deck_seen` lists only
  seven cards.
- **Unattributed damage to Ryley's towers.** Nine opponent events are inferred purely from tower HP loss with
  no enemy unit in range: 269 at t=777.8 and t=780.8 (left), 323 at t=780.8 (right), 269 at t=836.8 (king),
  546 at t=884.4 (right), 323 at t=917.9 and t=918.9 (king), 200 at t=919.4 (right), 1000 at t=977.5 (right).
  Some of these are probably Evo Skeletons or Goblin Gang chip that the tracker lost, but no card can be read.
- **Detector hallucinations.** The 2024-era unit detector produces cards neither player has — dark-prince,
  hog-rider, battle-healer, bomber-evolution, mighty-miner, magic-archer, archer-queen, little-prince, bat,
  rascals. The single [Little Prince](../cards/little-prince.md) "mention" in the context header is likewise
  an artifact: the word "Prince" only ever appears at t=815-818 as a cut-off "Princess", and Ryley never
  discusses that card. Three opponent events are `UNIDENTIFIED` units (t=727.7 at [4, 21], t=986.0 at [9, 25]
  and [5, 19]) — post-2024 cards the weights cannot name.
- **Deploy-label timestamps drift a few seconds from the HUD.** Several labelled plays carry `clock None` and
  a timestamp 2-5 seconds before the matching commentary (for example the Goblin Barrel label at t=738.2
  against "Let's just go for the Goblin Barrel here" at t=739-742, and the Mega Knight label at t=983.0
  against "We have to get a Mega Knight in the pocket" at t=983-985). Times in this file are the event times
  as logged; the clock values quoted come from the timeline row at that second.
