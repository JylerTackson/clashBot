## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=1382-1384 (clock 2:32): defensive buildings can be placed for King Tower activation, not just for the
  defence — "I'm going to go for the Tesla here to try to activate the king tower."

  produces the activation — "the fire spirit up top just to make the bowler not target the tower and that should
  activate the king tower."

  and a Freeze at the right moment denies it entirely: "no because the the troops moved it too much... Great

  evo barrel off to the side. Make it a little bit harder to activate. He might bowler on the left side which
  would be good to not let him activate."

  opponent must spend on it, then play the building late so they cannot answer both: "If I Ronin at the bridge,
