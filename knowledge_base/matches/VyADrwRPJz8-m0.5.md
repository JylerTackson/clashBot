# Match: #1 TOP Ladder Push with Log Bait — game 6 of the session

## Summary

Sixth readable game of a single-deck top-ladder session, and the one Ryley calls the worst possible
pairing for his list. He is on the session's [bait](../archetypes/bait.md) eight —
[Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md), [Wall Breakers](../cards/wall-breakers.md),
[Magic Archer](../cards/magic-archer.md), [Berserker](../cards/berserker.md),
[Bomb Tower](../cards/bomb-tower.md), [Royal Delivery](../cards/royal-delivery.md),
[Ice Spirit](../cards/ice-spirit.md) and [Fire Spirit](../cards/fire-spirit.md) (2.5 average elixir) —
against an [X-Bow](../cards/x-bow.md) siege deck with [Elixir Collector](../cards/elixir-collector.md)
and an Evo [Valkyrie](../cards/valkyrie.md). He reads the matchup out loud before anything happens:
"against Clouse. Oh no, guys. This guy has expo pump, man. This is probably the worst possible matchup
we could have gotten for our deck, but we did beat this matchup yesterday" (t=1097-1106). His plan is
one-lane, damage-first: barrel and spirit chip on the enemy left tower plus repeated Magic Archers at
the bridge, "just grab every single possible lick of Magic Archer damage that we can, cuz in this
matchup if we don't take an entire tower we are going to lose the game" (t=1130-1139). It works — the
enemy left tower goes 4858 -> 169 HP between clock 2:43 and 1:47 — and the opponent trades his own left
tower back at about clock 1:50 ("we just tower trade him", t=1160). From there the X-Bow moves to the
right lane at clock 1:28 and simply outranges him: his right tower bleeds from 4683 to ~1246 while he
never has the elixir to both defend and answer the Collector. He concedes it out loud with 25 seconds
left ("we just simply lost, dude... there's nothing that we can do", t=1241-1244) and the segment ends
in overtime with an Evo Valkyrie and a spirit seconds from his last tower: "Well, I found the deck that
can beat us, guys" (t=1265). Recorded as a loss from the commentary and the tower state; no end screen
was read.
