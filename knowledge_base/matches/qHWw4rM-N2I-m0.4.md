## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=974-988 (clock ~2:31) — Spending your small spell badly can cost you the *placement*
  card you need later: `Okay, bad log, too, cuz now he isn't able to go for a hog rider in
  front ... so he could ... block the mother witch. This is nice. He kind of has to go for
  guards.` Mother Witch punishes the substitute answer.
- t=988-992 (clock ~1:52) — `Could I bandit in front of this cuz this is going to tank for
  the mother witch` — put a body in front of Mother Witch so the defenders die to her and
  become Cursed Hogs.
- t=998 (clock 1:42) — `we can just go for these low zappies here, and this will just take
  out everything` — Zappies as the catch-all against a stacked, low-HP defensive wave.
- t=1007-1012 (clock 1:33-1:28) — On the deck as a whole: `whenever I'm playing this deck,
  like their tower just magically just depletes ... I don't know where his tower got to
  this HP from, but it's just there.`
- t=1024-1033 (clock 1:16-1:07) — A balance observation stated as a takeaway: the Heroic
  Valkyrie ability no longer kills a Firecracker — `I guess firecracker is alive ... Okay,
  it's no longer killing firecrackers. That is good to know.` — but the Firecracker being
  dragged into the other lane still counts (`it's technically dead, I guess`).
- t=1042-1045 (clock 0:58-0:55) — `the E-Barbs throwing their spears made that not happen
  and we got a spear on the tower as well` — the Evolved Elite Barbarians' spears both
  cancel the opponent's play and add free tower chip.
- t=1063-1066 (clock 0:37-0:34) — `I'm going to need to get like a good freeze or something
  on defense. Otherwise, I'm not sure I'm supposed to defend.` — with this deck Freeze is
  the defensive answer you have to bank.
- t=1072 (clock 0:28) — `let's go for the Mother Witch here just to force him to go for
  something` — play the threat to spend the opponent's card, not only to deal damage.
- t=1090-1094 (clock 0:08) — Cycle your own cards to deny the opponent's big spell:
  `we can go for the Zappies, too, cuz he's going to try to get like some type of lightning
  down, maybe. Actually, he can't even afford lightning just yet.`
