## Summary

Game 2 of a five-deck showcase (video 286.5s-466.5s), and the second deck Ryley brings to his Hero Valkyrie video: a Royal Giant / Fisherman / Hunter cycle list — Royal Giant, Heroic Valkyrie, Evolution Hunter, Fisherman, Barbarian Barrel, Fireball, Skeletons and Electro Spirit, roughly 3.1 average elixir. It is the RoyaleAPI "EvoRG FishBoy Hunter 3.0 Cycle" shell with Royal Ghost swapped out for the Heroic Valkyrie (4 elixir + a 3-elixir Wild Whirlwind ability, 7 total). The opponent — Aragon, who Ryley thanks for helping record the video — runs the same hero in a Miner / Magic Archer / Bomb Tower control deck with Evolution Skeletons, so the whole game is a Valkyrie mirror, and Ryley uses that to make his central argument: a card looks weaker than it is when both players hold it and the copies cancel out. He builds a small lead with a Barbarian Barrel + Electro Spirit sequence at clock 1:21-1:17 that cleans up a Miner and predicts the Evo Skeletons, adds chip with a Royal Giant at the bridge in triple elixir while the Valkyrie occupies the other lane, and at clock 0:17 goes Valkyrie-at-the-bridge plus Wild Whirlwind instead of the Fireball he had queued. The ability shreds the tower from 1,517 to 222 HP, two Fireballs finish it in overtime, and he takes the crown at t=~460 ("Good game").

Like imagine like the Valkyrie hero against your regular average player who plays like og barbs mega knight or something like that.
