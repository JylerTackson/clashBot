## Summary

Game 3 of a seven-game single-deck session (video 370.5s-662.5s). Ryley pilots the log-bait list the video is built around — Goblin Barrel, Wall Breakers, Fire Spirit, Ice Spirit, Magic Archer, Bomb Tower, Royal Delivery and Ronin — against an opponent he expected to be a Mortar Bait player ("This guy usually plays with Mortar Bait", 370s) and who turns out to be running a cycle/bait list of his own. At 387-395s Ryley reads the opponent's deck out loud: "He has Miner, Wall Breakers, Magic Archer... that's not going to be the nicest match-up either though cuz he's going to be able to really defend easily with having the Log and a bunch of quick cycle cards, but if everything goes in my favor, I should be able to win this." Evolved Bats are the other card he keeps naming, and they end up deciding the game.

His plan for the whole segment is dual-lane barrel/Wall Breakers pressure plus a stack of Magic Archers placed deep on his own half, with Bomb Tower and Royal Delivery holding the ground and Ronin used almost exclusively as a defensive body. The pressure works: two big damage windows (enemy towers 4858 -> 3514/3857 across 446-450s after a fake-lane Goblin Barrel, then enemy right 4298 -> 3011 across 526-532s off a Wall Breakers connection) put him ahead, and by 598s the enemy left tower is down to 1809 and the right to 1809/1417.

The problem is the return traffic. From 526s onward Evolved Bats keep reaching his right tower in the opposite lane while his answers are committed on the left; his right tower falls from 4858 (526s) to 652 (640s) and his left from 3507 (468s) to 1423 (644s). At 632s he calls it himself: "I think we're going to lose our tower to the bats in the opposite lane, to be honest." Nobody takes a crown — the last stable reads at 656-658s are own {left 1423, right 652} against enemy {left 1809, right 702} — so the game goes the full distance and is decided on the lowest-tower tiebreaker, which Ryley loses by about 50 HP. His closing line at 654-660s: "Oh my god, we took too much bats damage, guys. That was close, though."
