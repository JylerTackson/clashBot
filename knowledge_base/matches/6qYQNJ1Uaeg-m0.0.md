# Match: This Hero Berserker Deck has a 100% WIN Rate — game 1

## Summary

Game 1 of a seven-game single-deck session, and Ryley states the premise in the opening seconds:
"we are going to be playing the best Hero [Berserker](../cards/berserker.md) deck in Clash Royale…
the balance changes are here and the Hero Berserker has just been added into the game and it is
absolutely crazy" (t=7-17). The list is a 2.6-average-elixir Miner / Wall Breakers chip shell —
[Miner](../cards/miner.md), Evo [Wall Breakers](../cards/wall-breakers.md),
[Spear Goblins](../cards/spear-goblins.md), Evo [Bats](../cards/bats.md),
[Bomb Tower](../cards/bomb-tower.md), [Poison](../cards/poison.md), [The Log](../cards/the-log.md)
and the Hero [Berserker](../heroes/berserker-hero.md) — and he names it himself mid-game: "we do have
this nice [Miner] wall breakers deck" (t=48-49). All eight cards are backed by deploy labels or
commentary in this game; the per-game HUD read that put [Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md)
in the Poison slot is rejected (see Data gaps), so the video-level consensus deck is used.
The opponent runs an Evo [Mortar](../cards/mortar.md) siege/bait list — Mortar,
[Rascals](../cards/rascals.md), [Goblins](../cards/goblins.md), Evo Bats,
[Skeleton Barrel](../cards/skeleton-barrel.md), [Minions](../cards/minions.md) and a
[Cannon Cart](../cards/cannon-cart.md) — and never gets a push going: his own left tower sits at 4386
HP untouched from clock 1:50 all the way to 0:24, and his right tower ends the game at full 4858.
The game turns on one Berserker at clock 0:56. He holds the ability through an entire earlier push
("I'm not even going to pop the berserker ability", t=98) purely because the *threat* of it forces a
defence, then presses Savage Survival when a Berserker is about to die on the enemy left tower: that
tower falls from 2215 to 579 HP between clock 0:56 and 0:45. He finishes on chip — Miner, Spear
Goblins and a last [The Log](../cards/the-log.md) at clock 0:07 — with the tower on 145 HP:
"Is that tower down? No, we just need a log. Let's go. Very nice win, guys" (t=166-167).

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- **t=26-42 (2:28-2:12)** — the card is underrated and scales with skill: "Berserker was already a fun
  card to use and in my opinion, very underrated. One thing that I noticed about the Berserker is
  that it was very popular in competitive play and top ladder, but it wasn't really used at all in
  like the other ranges… I feel like with this card, the higher you get, the better it gets."
- **t=51-67 (2:02-1:46)** — a small nerf does not change a card's role: "Even though the wall breakers
  did get a nerf in the balance changes, I do think that wall breakers are just an extremely strong
  card in general… they just have so much utility and just like one or two small nerfs. Can't really
  like take that away from the card."
- **t=69-79 (1:46-1:36)** — what Savage Survival actually gives you: "if you don't know what the Hero
  Berserker does is when you pop the ability, it gets 5 seconds of invincibility and it like hits at
  a massive super quick speed."
- **t=98-104 (1:16-1:10)** — a hero on the board changes the opponent's defence for free: "I'm not
  even going to pop the berserker ability. See there, normally he wouldn't defend the berserker, but
  because it's hero, he had to."
- **t=104-118 (1:10-0:56)** — the same lesson stated as a principle, and the most quotable line of the
  game: "One thing I'm noticing about the berserker is that sometimes the threat of me going for the
  ability is actually stronger than me literally clicking the button. Cuz in a lot of situations, it
  just makes them have to defend a berserker that they wouldn't normally have to."
- **t=118-126 (0:56-0:45)** — when you do press it, press it to survive, not to open: "Wait, ability
  here. Keep this alive… It's on one HP. It can't die because whenever you pop the ability, it's
  literally invincible and it's so ridiculously good."
- **t=139-141 (0:35-0:33)** — use a body to deny a siege building its lock: "we can go for the
  berserker to tank just so the mortar doesn't lock to our tower."
