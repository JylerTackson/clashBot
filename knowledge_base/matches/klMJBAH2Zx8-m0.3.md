## Summary

Game 4 of 6 in a single-deck session (video 815.8s-1094.8s, 279 readable seconds — long enough that the game almost certainly ran into overtime). Ryley pilots the video's consensus list: Mighty Miner, Cannon, Skeletons, Baby Dragon, Lightning, Electro Spirit, Royal Hogs and The Log, with the Royal Hogs, Cannon, Skeletons and Baby Dragon Evolutions all referenced in commentary. The opponent plays a Three Musketeers / Elixir Collector bridge-spam list — Ryley calls it at 826s ("it looks like this guy is playing a bridge spam deck") and names Three Musketeers, Ice Golem, Elite Barbarians, Bandit and the "evo ghost" over the course of the game; the arena tracker confirms Bandit, Royal Ghost, Elite Barbarians, Heal Spirit, Ice Golem and three separate Elixir Collectors.

The defining decision comes at 845-856s: he refuses to Lightning the first pump because Lightning is his only real answer to Three Musketeers ("I can't lightning that elixir collector now cuz then he'll just 3M in the back and I'll lose the game"). That concedes an enormous elixir deficit — he is "down three elixir collectors" by 977s and "down six elixir right now after that pump" at 1042s — and turns the match into a pure defensive grind: Cannon in the middle, Skeletons and Electro Spirit to reset and distract, Mighty Miner onto the Royal Ghost and the Musketeers, Baby Dragon and The Log for the swarms.

His damage comes almost entirely from opportunistic Royal Hogs sets at the bridge, thrown whenever he reads a pump (873.9s, 924.9s, 945.9s, 1078.1s), plus two enormous Lightnings. The first, at 951.0s onto the right-lane push, drops the enemy right tower from 2734 to 1485 HP; the second, at 1020.0s, catches two more Musketeers. His own left tower is chipped from 4858 to 2259 and his right from 4858 to 1848, but neither falls. At 1078.1s a final Royal Hogs set goes in as the opponent commits Three Musketeers at the bridge instead of the middle; the enemy right tower reads 1373 -> 1137 -> 547 -> 311 over 1084-1090s and Ryley calls it: "that's going to be tower down. There's no way that he can save this. Let's go. GG's." (1086-1088s). A win, and he closes with "Dude, that was stressful" (1092s).

## Opponent

Ryley identifies the deck as bridge spam within ten seconds of the start (826s) and then, once the first Elixir Collector goes down at 848.8s, as a Three Musketeers pump list. "this is still damage and I think it's getting nearly enough damage where I can just defend for the rest of the game... I definitely do trust my defense guys on just defending the whole match, especially having like Evo baby dragon and stuff."

"that one musketeer in the back will stay alive on one HP cuz the mighty miner of course didn't like actually damage it, but it's going to know that mighty miner log plus e-spirit leaves musky's on basically one HP."
