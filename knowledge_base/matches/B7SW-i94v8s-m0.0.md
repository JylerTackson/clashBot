# Match: My MAIN Deck for Season End in Clash Royale — game 1

## Summary

This is the opening game of a six-game single-deck video, and Ryley names the list in the first fifteen
seconds: "my main deck for the season end is going to be the super cycle log bait magic archer deck"
(t=11-16). The eight cards observed match the video-level consensus exactly —
[Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md), [Wall Breakers](../cards/wall-breakers.md),
[Magic Archer](../cards/magic-archer.md), [Berserker](../cards/berserker.md),
[Bomb Tower](../cards/bomb-tower.md), [Royal Delivery](../cards/royal-delivery.md),
[Ice Spirit](../cards/ice-spirit.md) and [Fire Spirit](../cards/fire-spirit.md) — the 2.5-elixir
[bait](../archetypes/bait.md) / [bridge-spam](../archetypes/bridge-spam.md) shell already in the knowledge
base as [Ryley Log Bait Bridge Spam](../decks/berserker-bomb-tower-fire-spirit-goblin-barrel-ice-spirit-magic-archer-royal-delivery-wall-breakers.md).
The opponent, whom Ryley calls Talal and rates as "one of the best recruits players actually, if not like the
best recruits bait player in the game" (t=74-78), plays Evo [Royal Recruits](../cards/royal-recruits.md) with
[Wizard](../cards/wizard.md), [Mighty Miner](../cards/mighty-miner.md),
[Goblin Gang](../cards/goblin-gang.md), [Skeleton Barrel](../cards/skeleton-barrel.md) and small-spell
coverage. The shape of the game is a two-lane chip race: Ryley never stops throwing barrels, Wall Breakers and
1-elixir spirits at the bridge (at least 14 Ice Spirits, 8 Fire Spirits, 7 Goblin Barrels and 4-9 Wall
Breakers in under four minutes) while holding a repeatable centre-tile Bomb Tower plus Berserker plus Royal
Delivery defence for the Recruits. Regular time ends with no crowns but both enemy towers cut (left 2268 of
4858 by clock 0:49, right 3338), and in overtime the barrel/Wall Breakers pressure on the right lane takes
that tower from 1646 HP at clock 1:30 to 465 HP at clock 1:10 while a Mighty Miner chips his left. He calls it
at t=233-234: "that should be good game. Come on. Let's go. GG's. Nice win."
