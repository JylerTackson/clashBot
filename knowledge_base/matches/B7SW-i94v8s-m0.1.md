## Summary

Game 2 of a six-game season-end session (video 237.4s-535.5s, the longest game of the video at 298 s). Ryley pilots the same list all video — [Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md), [Wall Breakers](../cards/wall-breakers.md), [Magic Archer](../cards/magic-archer.md), [Berserker](../cards/berserker.md), [Bomb Tower](../cards/bomb-tower.md), [Royal Delivery](../cards/royal-delivery.md), [Ice Spirit](../cards/ice-spirit.md), [Fire Spirit](../cards/fire-spirit.md) — with the Magic Archer in its Hero form: the 2-elixir *Triple Threat* ability (the Hero darts back and leaves a decoy) is what he calls a "little James Harden step back" (422s) and uses four times to keep the Archer alive.

The opponent, "Vitinho", is read out loud at 253s as "Balloon Freeze": [Bowler](../cards/bowler.md), [Baby Dragon](../cards/baby-dragon.md), [Inferno Dragon](../cards/inferno-dragon.md) and [Little Prince](../cards/little-prince.md) are all tracked on the field, and Ryley names [Balloon](../cards/balloon.md), [Freeze](../cards/freeze.md) and [Tornado](../cards/tornado.md) (auto-transcribed as "NATO") repeatedly. He explicitly rules out [Knight](../cards/knight.md) and Barbarian Barrel from the opponent's list at 272-274s ("He has no Bar Barrel and no Knights. That's just a guaranteed Bowler from him") and [Electro Giant](../cards/electro-giant.md) at 263-266s, so this is a variant of the knowledge base's Bowler/Balloon control shell rather than that exact list.

Regulation ends level — the clock resets to 1:59 at t=409 with all four towers standing — and the whole game is decided in overtime on chip. Ryley never lands a full push; he drips Goblin Barrels, Wall Breakers and 1-elixir spirits at the bridge on every cycle while his defensive shell (Bomb Tower centre, Berserker as the plug, Royal Delivery for committed ground, spirit in front) blanks each Bowler/Baby Dragon/Inferno Dragon stack. The enemy left tower falls from 4858 HP at 240s to 218 HP by 506s and the right from 4858 to 425 by 520s, against his own 2565/3003, and no tower is destroyed — the win comes on the overtime tiebreaker. His own closing verdict at 513-529s: "that's just good game. He could not break through, man. We actually had a defensive master class this game. We did not let this dude breathe, man, with the offense as well... we can just pressure them so hard that they're unable to make the proper push, and we win the game."
