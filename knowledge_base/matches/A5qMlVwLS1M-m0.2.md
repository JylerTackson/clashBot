## How Ryley uses his cards

- **Battle Ram** — his opening card and his closing card. Bridge placement at (2, 13) at 2:48 before he knows the matchup ("start off with the battle ram here at the bridge"), a second one at 1:58 the moment the opponent is at ~0 elixir after committing to the Hound, then repeated Evo rams aimed at the King tower at 0:54 and 0:25 ("a evil ram on the king cuz he might berserker on top of this"). He treats it as the pressure card that forces the beatdown player to spend defensively instead of building.
- **Barbarian Barrel** — used twice as push support and twice as defence/cleanup: behind the opening ram at (3, 13) at 2:35, at the bridge (3, 15) at 1:50 "just to stay tanky from my mother witch cuz that's going to spawn a piggy in a second", then at (8, 19) at 0:47 and (8, 20) at 0:18 to clear the small stuff sitting on his pocketed Goblinstein ("And we barb.
