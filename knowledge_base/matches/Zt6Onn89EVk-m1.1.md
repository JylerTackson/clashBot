# Match: Hero Valkyrie is the Most BROKEN Hero of All Time — game 4 of 5

## Summary

This is the last game of a five-deck showcase built around the Hero Valkyrie, and Ryley names the list in his
first sentence: "With the final deck here, we have Hog Executioner Tornado" (t=766-767). The eight are
[Hog Rider](../cards/hog-rider.md), [Executioner](../cards/executioner.md) (Evolved — he calls it "Evo XE"),
[Tornado](../cards/tornado.md), the Hero [Valkyrie](../cards/valkyrie.md)
([Wild Whirlwind](../heroes/valkyrie-hero.md), 4 + 3 elixir), [Ice Spirit](../cards/ice-spirit.md),
[Goblins](../cards/goblins.md), [The Log](../cards/the-log.md) and [Rocket](../cards/rocket.md) — a
[control](../archetypes/control.md) shell with a [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md) floor, and one he explicitly
frames as a heritage list: "this deck is kind of iconic, honestly. This is the deck that um a lot of pro
players kind of came up using" (t=793-798). The opponent (named "Aragon" in commentary) plays Miner Wall
Breakers with Evo [Bats](../cards/bats.md), [Spear Goblins](../cards/spear-goblins.md),
[Poison](../cards/poison.md), a [Bomb Tower](../cards/bomb-tower.md) and his own Hero Valkyrie —
"Aragon's playing Miner Wall Breakers, actually" (t=782). The game is a long defensive grind: regulation ends
0-0 on crowns with only chip damage on both sides ("we haven't really got any damage outside of like one hog
rider shot", t=954), and it runs the full double- and triple-elixir overtime. The break comes in the last
thirty seconds of overtime, when a Valkyrie + Hog Rider pair at the left bridge with the ability held back
takes the enemy left tower from 1654 HP down to 822 while his own towers sit at 1749 and 2699; he calls it at
t=1051 — "Let's go. Very nice win."
