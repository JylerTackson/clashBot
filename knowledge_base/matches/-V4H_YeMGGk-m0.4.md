## Summary

This is the fifth game of a six-game deck-showcase video, and the deck changes
again: Archers, Bats, Bomb Tower, Fire Spirit, Magic Archer, Skeletons, The Log
and Wall Breakers — a 2.4-elixir Wall Breakers cycle list with Bomb Tower as the
only building and Magic Archer as the single piece of ranged splash. The
opponent plays a slow Miner/Graveyard control deck: Miner (level 16, played
almost every cycle), Cannon, Ice Golem, Musketeer, Barbarians, Graveyard, Bowler
and Goblin Drill. Almost the entire game is fought in Ryley's left lane: the
opponent chips with Miner and drops Graveyard on the left tower at 1:03 and again
in overtime, while Ryley answers with Bomb Tower in the centre-left, Fire Spirit,
Skeletons and The Log, and cycles Wall Breakers to the bridge in return. Regular
time ends 0-0 on towers destroyed (own left 4583, enemy left ~2693 by 0:02) and
the game goes to double- then triple-elixir overtime, where both left towers keep
falling — enemy left down to ~1179 and Ryley's own left to ~1527 by t=1286.
The readable window ends at t=1300 (end of the readiness period), so the finish
is cut off and the result is unknown. Ryley's commentary throughout is not about
the game at all: he is reading out and reacting to the balance-change list,
including nerfs to two cards he is holding in this very deck (Wall Breakers and
Magic Archer).

## Lessons stated by Ryley

All of Ryley's talk in this window is balance-change opinion, not in-game
coaching; he never explains a play he makes.

- t=1005-1023 — Evo Goblin Cage cycle going 1 -> 2: "Goblin Cage was the only one
  cycle Evo for four elixir and I do think that it showed in how strong that it
  actually ended up being. Very very in agreeance with that one."
- t=1031-1057 — Evo Mega Knight will knock back "every other attack instead of
  every attack", with knockback going from 2.5 to 4 tiles: "it will knock the
  troops back further, but it won't knock them back every hit like before, making
  it quite a bit less polarizing."
- t=1059-1092 — Evo Valkyrie tornado damage reduced 50%: "it will no longer one
  shot skeletons or bats. You will have to be getting two swings... which I think
  is a fair change. Evo Valkyrie has just been way too strong for way too long."
  He adds "graveyard gets a pretty big indirect buff there because of that."
- t=1103-1140 — Bowler and Executioner projectile range 7.5 -> 7: "I have
  complained and complained that the Bowler and the Executioner were basically
  just magic archers with 7,000 HP... Half of the time when you're against those
  cards, they are the things that take out your tower, not even like the win
  condition, which is crazy to say."
- t=1144-1183 — Goblin Drill loses crown tower damage: "there will no longer be
  any guaranteed damage from just dropping a Goblin Drill on the tower and I am
  not too sure if I agree with this... I personally think that it is just too much
  and maybe might need to be reverted in the future if the Goblin Drill usage rate
  drops too low."
- t=1184-1215 — Wall Breakers damage -20% (also affecting Evo Wall Breakers):
  "they're two elixir and they take out like a fourth of your tower... they will
  literally lose you the game if they connect to the tower like two times. Like
  that's half your tower gone just like that. And just for two elixir. That was
  definitely far too strong."
- t=1224-1245 — Magic Archer nerf: "I was honestly surprised that they added this
  to the balance change... it was still clearly way too strong."
- t=1269-1287 — Royal Delivery nerf: "Royal Delivery has just been too good for
  too long now and it's kind of like the staple of all of these decks that have
  like crazy cycle cards and shouldn't really get away with it. People have
  started to put Royal Delivery in them just making it so impossible to break
  through."
- t=1288-1299 — Zappies hit speed nerf: "Very big fan of the hit speed nerf to the
  Zappies cuz I have felt like the Zappies were too good for a little bit too long
  now. I just think that the Zappies got maybe one too many buffs."
- t=1245-1253 — on the patch as a whole: "makes me pretty hopeful for the balance
  team in the future... I feel like this is definitely the most feedback driven
  set of work in progress changes I've ever seen."
