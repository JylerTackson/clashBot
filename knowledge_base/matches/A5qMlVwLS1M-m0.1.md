## Summary

Game 2 of 7 in the "8 BEST cards in a single deck" session (video 269.4s-569.6s), Ryley on the video's consensus list: Barbarian Barrel, Battle Ram, Cannon Cart, Elite Barbarians, Goblinstein, Heal Spirit, Mother Witch and Zap (3.6 average elixir, evolution slots visibly on Elite Barbarians and Battle Ram — he calls them "Evo Ebarbs" and "Evo battle ram" throughout). The opponent, "Zthol", plays X-Bow siege, which Ryley calls before he sees a card at 271-274s: "Zthol. I'm pretty sure this guy plays Expo if I'm not mistaken." The pieces that show up over the game — X-Bow, Tesla (Evo Tesla), Evo Archers, Knight, Electro Spirit, Skeletons, Fireball and The Log — are the standard X-Bow 3.0 cycle shell. The match is a slow, ugly damage race in which neither tower ever falls: a bad opening (Barbarian Barrel in the back, then an X-Bow at the bridge) drops his right tower 4858 -> 4106 inside twenty seconds, and he says at 314-321s "in this type of matchup, it really is just dependent on if he messes up on defense because if he plays solid defense, there is zero chance for me to actually win this game." From double elixir onward he does exactly one thing — bridge spam ("I just have to keep on bridge spamming this guy", 531-532s) — feeding Elite Barbarians, Battle Ram, Goblinstein and Cannon Cart into a defender who is repeatedly at 10 elixir but out of the right cards. A left-lane Battle Ram plus Zap plus Heal Spirit at OT 1:11-1:07 takes the enemy left tower 4781 -> 4056, putting him "only behind by six damage" (own right 4050 vs enemy left 4056 — literally 6 HP) at 503s. He then loses more of his right tower (3775) and has to break through again; a right-bridge Elite Barbarians / Battle Ram / Cannon Cart flood in the last twenty seconds takes the enemy right tower 4704 -> 3451, winning the tiebreaker with about 324 HP to spare. "Give us one. Give us one. Yes. Let's go. Oh my god. What a win, man. We barely grabbed that."
