# Ryley vs Mugi — log bait mirror (CRL 2026 broadcast)

## Summary

This is CRL broadcast footage of one of Ryley's own games, with his voice-over
on top; the bottom ("own") side is Ryley, which the commentary confirms
repeatedly ("I just dropped my double spirit with the cannon", t=281; "I
decided to cycle my magic archer in the back", t=289 — both match the bottom
player's deploys at t=276.3/278.3 and t=287.3). He is playing a
Magic Archer / Wall Breakers / Goblin Barrel bait list against Mugi (the
auto-transcript spells the name Mugie / Mooi / Mugi / Mookie), and at t=179 he
calls it "a log bait mirror match up" — both players show Goblin Barrel, Wall
Breakers, Magic Archer, Valkyrie, Cannon, Skeletons and spirits, which is why
side attribution in the raw events is unreliable here. Ryley's plan is to bait
and barrel the left tower while paying for the right one: he lets the
opponent's Wall Breakers and Goblin Barrel through on the right (own right
tower 3052 -> 1152 over the clip) and in exchange grinds the enemy left tower
from 3052 to 174 by t=244. From roughly t=273 he switches to pure defence
("I just had to defend against Mugi in a mirror match up for 51 seconds"),
wins the Magic Archer cycle race by playing his own from the back with an
elixir lead, and by t=314 says "right here is when I knew that I had won".
The clip cuts before the end screen, but he states the result himself; no
tower was actually destroyed on either side in the readable window.

Clock note: this is broadcast overlay footage with no readable match timer, so
every event below is cited by video second (`t=`) and the in-game clock is
unreadable throughout.

## How Ryley uses his cards

- **Goblin Barrel** (t=171.2, 194.2, 219.2, 233.2, 267.4, 278.9) — six barrels
  in 110 seconds, five of them at (3, 24)-(3, 26), i.e. always the same enemy
  left Crown Tower. He never trades it defensively; it is the card the whole
  match plan feeds. The most productive one is the t=219.2 barrel thrown right
  after he had "forced out the ice spirit" (t=216), which starts the run that
  takes the tower to 174 HP.
- **Wall Breakers** (t=182.8, 222.2, 269.6, 308.3) — paired with the Barrel
  rather than sent alone: "the evil goblin barrel plus evil wall breakers"
  (t=221-224) at t=219.2/222.2, and again at t=267.4/269.6 after the opponent
  fumbled his Skeletons. He deploys them the moment the opponent's cheap
  answer has just been spent.

  the bridge behind Valkyrie (t=200.2, "magic archer plus Valkyrie at the
  bridge", t=202); defensively/for tempo from the very back at (9, 1)

  protect mine because I have the elixir advantage, my magic archer will win

## Opponent

Mugi brings the mirror: Goblin Barrel, Wall Breakers, Magic Archer (t=180.2 at
(2, 15)), Valkyrie, Cannon (a friendly-side Cannon sits at (9, 20) for most of
the first 80 seconds), Skeletons, Ice Spirit (t=162.2, t=254.3) and Fire Spirit,
plus Goblins at (1, 26) at t=222.2 and t=236.2 to defend the incoming Goblin
Barrels. Ryley's read on the match-up is that in a mirror the winner is
whoever forces the other's cheap answers out first and whoever holds the
elixir lead when the Magic Archers trade — hence forcing the Ice Spirit at
t=216 before barrelling, and cycling his own Magic Archer from the back at
t=287.3. He is explicitly happy about the pairing: "when I saw Mugi playing log
bait, I just couldn't not smile, man" (t=185-189), and he credits two opponent
errors at t=263-268 ("Mugi messed up the skellies which also led him to mess up
the wall breaker") for the sequence that took the left tower to 174 HP.

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=203-208 — Time a bridge push against a Cannon deck so the Cannon cannot be
  placed in time: "I knew that even though he had cannon in cycle and elixir
  for it, he couldn't really place it down in time if I just waited till the
  very last second."
- t=209-219 — Taking chip damage to force a cheap answer is worth it: "I
  decided to ignore the wall breakers and skellies to predict the cannon...
  forcing out the ice spirit right there, guys, is very important because now
  it allowed me to go for the evil goblin barrel plus evil wall breakers."
- t=231-247 — Do not spend your mini-tank on defence if it is the enabler for
  your offence: "I don't want to be dropping my Valkyrie because of the magic
  archer lineup... I just made sure to keep doing fire spirit until my cycle
  was a bit better cuz typically I want to have double spirit or spirit plus
  skellies."
- t=249-259 — Break that rule when you have counted the opponent's elixir:
  "I knew that because he wouldn't be able to afford a Valkyrie in front, I
  would just be able to predict the magic arch with my spirits."
- t=287-299 — In a mirror, cycle your key ranged card first and defend it:
  "if I cycle it first and protect mine because I have the elixir advantage,
  my magic archer will win the 1v1 because obviously I have more elixir to
  support it with."
- t=304-308 — Cover the empty lane pre-emptively when ahead: "I just made sure
  to ice on the left just in case. I don't really want to give him any window
  to change lanes or anything."
