## Summary

Game 3 of 7 in the video, played on the same Ronin log-bait list Ryley uses all
session: Goblin Barrel, Wall Breakers, Ice Spirit, Fire Spirit, Magic Archer,
Bomb Tower, Royal Delivery and Ronin. The opponent ("Game Freak") opened with
Bomber and Fire Spirit chip, then revealed a heavy beatdown/control shell —
Goblin Cage, Inferno Dragon, Bowler, Baby Dragon, Electro Giant and Graveyard —
which Ryley diagnosed live from the first 20 seconds ("Normally he does use
beatdown decks... it could be E-Giant, actually", t=667-676). Ryley never built a
big push; he chipped with Magic Archer at the river, layered Goblin Barrel with
Wall Breakers, and answered every ground commitment with cheap spirits, Royal
Delivery and (late) Bomb Tower. Both enemy Princess Towers were ground down —
the left tower from 4858 to 768 HP by t=778 and the right from 4858 to 644 HP by
t=812, with the enemy King Tower reading a few hundred HP at t=814-816 — while
Ryley lost no tower (own left 4858 to 4551, own right 4858 to 3472). He calls it
at t=810-815: "I knew he was giving up, man. Let's go. Very nice win... Perfect
game."

## How Ryley uses his cards

- **Wall Breakers** — three uses, all as a second layer rather than a lone
  threat. Opening split at (8, 14) at t=662.7 purely to scout ("Just going to
  split my Wallbreakers here, try to figure out what this guy is playing");
  behind the Goblin Barrel at (3, 14) at t=695.7 ("Let's barrel and then we last
  second wall breakers sticking in front of him"); and split at the bridge tile
  (8, 15) at t=767.9 after the Ronin went to the far lane. He also uses them as a
  building answer in advance at t=735-740: "I think he's going to go for the evo
  cage, but then the evo cage will instantly pop from the wall breakers."
- **Goblin Barrel** — four throws (t=695.5 at (2, 24), t=735.7 at (2, 25),
  t=768.8 at (3, 26), t=803.8 at (14, 21), t=811.1 at (8, 29)), always paired
  with another threat so the opponent's splash cannot cover both. He explicitly
  holds it once at t=730 ("maybe let's not goblin barrel just yet, actually") and
  times it around the Bowler at t=769-772: "we go for the evo barrel cuz he has
  to bowler, but then the bowler won't be able to take out the goblin barrel now."
- **Magic Archer** — the damage engine of the game, played six times (t=676.7 at
  (8, 11), t=719.6 at (8, 8), t=757.8 at (4, 2), t=765.8, t=777.8 at (4, 14),
  t=790.3). He uses it defensively against the Goblin Cage ("go for the marcher
  here. Help take out that Goblin Cage", t=678), then as a river chipper ("I want
  to marcher the bridge... cuz he went e-giant in the back", t=778-781). Nearly
  1700 HP of enemy left tower disappears at t=702-706 with no other play on the
  field: "Look at all that magic archer chip. Beautiful." At t=685 he says
  "Luckily, we could step back, and then we go for the delivery... Keep the
  marcher a little healthy", alongside an unexplained 2-elixir spend — the Hero
  Triple Threat signature.
- **Royal Delivery** — used twice, both times purely defensively as the card
  intends. At t=685.7 on (5, 14) to clean up the push he had already committed
  Ronin into ("then we go for the delivery, as well, just to take this all out"),
  and at t=791.8 at (2, 19) to erase a badly placed Inferno Dragon ("that was a
  really bad inferno dragon placement. Let's just delivery here and there goes
  the inferno dragon").
- **Fire Spirit** — five plays (t=671.7 at (2, 14), t=712.7 at (9, 10), t=747.7
  at (8, 6), t=783.8 at (4, 14), t=804.8 at (15, 14)), used as a 1-elixir splash
  answer and as tower chip. Against the Bowler: "We could go for the fire spirit
  here just to block that" (t=712-715). Against the Electro Giant push he uses it
  offensively: "we can go for the fire spirit here to threaten to finish off that
  tower" (t=783).
- **Ice Spirit** — the cheapest reset in the deck, used to stall the Bowler
  ("Let's go for the ice spirit here to block that bowler", t=723-725, deployed at
  (8, 13) t=724.6) and stacked with Fire Spirit on the counter-push at t=749.4 and
  t=806.6 at (8, 20). Ryley states the ordering rule explicitly at t=748-754.
- **Ronin** — four plays (t=680.7 at (5, 14), t=722.7 at (1, 14), t=734.1 at
  (2, 14), t=764.8 at (15, 14), t=809.8 at (8, 20)), always at the back of his
  own half at row 14 and never as a lone bridge rush, which he explains twice:
  "the Ronin is going to be a really weak card in this matchup, but it's not the
  end of the world" (t=707-709) and "Dude, I want a Ronin wall breakers to bridge

  The one time he lets it fight, it pays: "The Ronin just took out the brawler

- **Bomb Tower** — sat in hand almost the entire game and was placed once, at
  (8, 9) at t=788.8, pre-emptively against the Electro Giant that had just been
  dropped in the back: "Okay, let's just early bomb tower" (t=790).
