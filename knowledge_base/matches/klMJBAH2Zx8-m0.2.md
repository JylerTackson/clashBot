# Match: I Am #1 in the World with this GAME BREAKING Deck — game 3 of 6

**Clock caveat:** the in-game clock OCR failed for this entire video, so every timeline row has no clock and
the six games were split by tower-HP resets instead. Every timestamp below is a **video second**; the clock

## Summary

Ryley plays the video's single consensus list — [Royal Hogs](../cards/royal-hogs.md),
[Mighty Miner](../cards/mighty-miner.md), [Cannon](../cards/cannon.md), [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md),
[Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md), [Baby Dragon](../cards/baby-dragon.md),
[The Log](../cards/the-log.md) and [Lightning](../cards/lightning.md) — a 3.25-average
[cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md) shell with Evolved Royal Hogs as the win condition and a heavy spell held for
the end. The opponent is a deck he cannot place and keeps being surprised by: he opens expecting
"Mega Knight, wall breakers, firecracker, prince" (t=519-524) and instead runs into
[Mortar](../cards/mortar.md) ("Okay, why does he have a mortar?", t=549),
[Ram Rider](../cards/ram-rider.md) ("Bro, what? Why do you have a ram rider?", t=560),
[Mega Knight](../cards/mega-knight.md), [Royal Ghost](../cards/royal-ghost.md),
[Minions](../cards/minions.md), [Firecracker](../cards/firecracker.md), a [Miner](../cards/miner.md) and a
[Lightning](../cards/lightning.md) — "This is like one of the strangest decks I've seen in a while" (t=595),
"He's just surprising me again and again and again" (t=716-719). The shape of the game is Ryley splitting
Royal Hogs for chip whenever the opponent's Mega Knight is spent or committed to the wrong lane, defending
everything else with Cannon + Mighty Miner + spirits, and letting Mighty Miner run the counter-push. The
decisive stretch is t=693-706: hogs to the left bridge behind an Evo Baby Dragon while the opponent's
Mega Knight is busy on the right, and the enemy left tower falls 4032 -> 2281. He takes chip damage late from
a Mega Knight + Mortar combination on his own left tower (4167 -> 3317, t=760-772) but never loses a tower,
and closes with a Lightning on the enemy left at t=798.8. Result on his own words at t=808-812: "Yes, let's
go. Okay, we won on both sides anyway. Very nice win."

## How Ryley uses his cards

- **[Royal Hogs](../cards/royal-hogs.md)** — five 'Royal Hogs' deploy labels on his own half at t=526.7,
  594.3, ~651.7/668.7, 693.7 and 792.8. Two are deliberate splits early ("I don't really want to go for
  piggies in the same lane knowing that he probably has a Mega Knight", t=526-531; "I'm just going to split
  my piggies", t=592), and the later ones are same-lane, timed to a spent or mislaned Mega Knight
  (t=668, t=693, t=792). He weighs split vs same-lane out loud at t=634-641 and lands on "I feel like same
  lane might just be better... I think it depends on the situation". The t=792 hogs are purely defensive
  utility: "could I piggies to bridge to block the Mega Knight? Then if I block the Mega Knight, the Ram
  Rider is not a threat."
- **[Mighty Miner](../cards/mighty-miner.md)** — played more than any other card (deploy labels at t=544.5,
  567.2, 609.6, 637.6, 689.2, 710.7, 730.7, 743.7, 757.8, 788.8), almost always defensively at rows 8-12 in
  the middle or at his own King Tower tile [9, 0], then allowed to walk out as a counter-push. Explosive
  Escape at t=656-658 was pre-emptive against a Mega Knight ("Ability cuz he might Mega Knight. Okay, good
  ability regardless"). His thesis at t=677-691 is that the card is elixir-positive by default.
- **[Cannon](../cards/cannon.md)** — ten placements, mostly in the central band around [8-9, 9-12] so it
  pulls into either lane (t=548.5, 618.6, 643.6, 664.7, 682.7, 703.7, 714.7, 740.7, 761.8, 803.8), with two
  reactive edge placements at [13, 12] (t=682.7) and [4, 13] / [1, 10] against the late left-lane pushes. He
  states the intent plainly at t=739-741: "We need a cannon in the middle again."
- **[Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md)** — the cheapest tool in the deck and the most frequently cycled
  (t=539.7, 633.6, 647.6, 672.7, 709.2, 738.6, 753.8, 769.8, 805.8). Used to surround single-target
  attackers ("we can go for the Skeletons to surround the Ghost", t=647-649), to add DPS behind the Cannon
  ("Cannon low skellies", t=754), and as pure cycle at the end (t=805.8, elixir 2 -> 1).
- **[Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md)** — the anti-Minions and anti-swarm reset (t=551.6, 561.7,
  606.6, 653.7, 699.7, 706.7, plus two low-confidence 'SpiRit' labels at t=716.4 and 746.6). Twice narrated
  as a first move in a sequence: "let's E-spirit and then we can go for the mighty miner here" (t=563), and
  "Let's E-Spirit here for the Minions and then we can go for the Mighty Miner as well" (t=607). One is
  played at [9, 0] on his own King Tower tile in the King-activation window (t=653.7), with "Nice E-Spirit
  connection. Thankfully, it didn't activate" (t=649-650).
- **[The Log](../cards/the-log.md)** — used for Mortar removal and for lane clean-up rather than as a
  finisher: [8, 11] rolling up the centre into the bridge Mortar (t=627.6, "that's going to take out the
  Mortar"), [3, 15] into the Evo Mortar at (2, 17) (t=736.7), and defensive rolls at [14, 12], [14, 9],
  [8, 11], [14, 15], [3, 8], [14, 8] and [2, 9]. At t=758 he calls it "the Mighty Mighty Mighty log here",
  and at t=804-806 it is his panic answer under an incoming Lightning: "Log log log."
- **[Baby Dragon](../cards/baby-dragon.md)** — four placements, all defensive or as a push shield: [2, 9]
  into Minions (t=655.3), [4, 1] in the back to lead the hog push (t=683.7), [9, 1] in the back again
