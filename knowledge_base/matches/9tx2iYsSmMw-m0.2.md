## Lessons stated by Ryley

- 365-371s: "One thing that I do try to do in these types of tournaments is I try to be, you know, as comfortable as possible with my decks" — pick comfort decks in tournament play (here, Mortar Bowler expecting bridge spam).
- 376-383s: "since his RG isn't EVO, it doesn't actually do anything to go for those early game RGs" — a non-evolved Royal Giant played early achieves little.
- 383-397s: playing the Royal Giant with support instead of naked was still defensible because the opponent did not know his deck — "I guess he just didn't know what I had and he didn't want to do a naked RG ... thinking about it now, that was probably a pretty good play."
- 397-402s: "the only thing that gives him trouble is the bowler" — identify the one card the opponent struggles with and build the game around it.
- 415-427s: "I always try to finesse people with the quick spirits cuz people do always use the spirits to outrun the bowler throws and then fully counter it. So, if you can quickly drop your spirit or something like that, then you can quickly get a lock on the tower."
- 431-443s: know your deck's damage ceiling — "in my deck, I don't have a minor. I don't have like a direct source of damage. I only have skelly barrel and mortar ... They're not like a hog rider where you can comfortably always get them to the tower."
- 443-450s: play your win condition around the opponent's hard counter's cycle — "I went for the skelly barrel cuz he had no goblin cage in hand and goblin cage does fully shut me down every time."
- 463-483s: recognise when your own deck forces spell value for the opponent (Rascals, Mortar and Dart Goblin all have to sit near the tower) and treat being spell-cycled as the losing condition.
- 495-501s: use Barbarian Barrel to leave extra skeletons on the map before a Skeleton Barrel lands so the enemy Firecracker cannot walk into that lane.
- 511-517s: "I have to be making sure that I'm applying pressure to the point where he doesn't feel comfortable with going for spell cycling" — pressure as a defence against a chip plan.
- 517-522s: spread Rascals so one Fireball cannot hit them all.
- 528-532s: hold a second Mortar in cycle so a second Royal Giant is not scary.
- 549-562s: "if I ever get a chance to fireball, I do need to be taking it" — in a spell-race, take every free Fireball on the tower.
- 572-579s: "going RG the back against a bowler in triple elixir maybe isn't always the move because then I just get a free bowler."
- 597-605s: count spells — "I'm up a fireball cuz we both only have fireball and barbarian barrel. So, he needs more damage or else I'm just going to win."
- 616-624s: when ahead on HP, do not risk the Mortar at the bridge into a Firecracker — "I wasn't taking any risks, man ... And I win the game by 52 HP."
