## Lessons stated by Ryley

- 214-222s: bridge spam is hard to defend because the defender is forced to bunch up — "It is so hard to stop these pushes because usually when you're against a bridge spam deck, you need to put a bunch of cards in a very like similar position, you know what I mean?"
- 222-234s: that bunching is exactly what splash/dash cards punish, which is why the Heroic Valkyrie (and the Golden Knight) belong in bridge spam — "they always have to line their cards up. You can get good dashes. And hero Valkyrie is the same, but probably even a little bit better in this way ... it just makes your offensive life so much easier."
- 273-279s: when you are already far ahead on tower damage, defend cheaply instead of hunting value — "since we got a lot of damage anyway, there's no need to be greedy and try to like get a bunch of extra value out of this."
- 266-270s: against a Heroic Mega Minion, wait for it to be dealt with before committing to a defence, because the ability teleports it onto whatever you place.
- 251-255s: Heal Spirit is worth spending on your own defenders when the opponent's Mega Minion is chipping them — "It's going to heal up off of the mega minion as well."
- 287-296s: a tank body in front of a squishy unit beats hoping a spell whiffs — the Mother Witch would not have "blocked" Arrows, but by tanking for the Baby Dragon she kept the Zappies alive.
