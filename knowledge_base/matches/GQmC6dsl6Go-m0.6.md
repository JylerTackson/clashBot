# Match: #1 BEST Log Bait Deck in Clash Royale — game 7 (m0.6)

## Summary

Ryley pilots his Hero Berserker log-bait list — Berserker (Hero), Evolved Elite Barbarians, Evolved Skeleton Barrel, Dart Goblin, Goblin Gang, Suspicious Bush, Fireball, The Log — against "Red Rum" on an Evo Mortar siege deck with Goblinstein, Cannon Cart, Evolved Barbarians, Barbarian Barrel, Minions and Skeletons. The opening is a disaster: he goes Elite Barbarians at the bridge on the Mortar at 2:47 (t=1002.3), the Mortar gets its shot off anyway, and by 1:31 his left tower is down from 4858 to 3003 after a misclick at 1:47 that put Goblin Gang in the corner instead of the Berserker's Savage Survival ability. From roughly 1:14 he stabilises on the bait pattern — Suspicious Bush at the bridge, Skeleton Barrel and Dart Goblin to drag out the Barbarian Barrel and Minions, Berserker at the bridge as the punish — and takes the game to overtime tied on towers. In overtime the Fireball-plus-Log combo on the Mortar (OT 1:54, t=1175-1176), a Berserker ability at OT 1:36 and a final Bush/Goblin Gang/Elite Barbarians sequence break the enemy right tower from 3769 to 130 and the game ends with "What a win" at t=1259. Ryley's stated theme for the game is spell/ability baiting: making the opponent spend the Goblinstein ability and the Barbarian Barrel on cheap cards.
