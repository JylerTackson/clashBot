## Summary

Last game of the video, played with the same Royal Hogs / Lightning list as the rest of the session: Royal Hogs, Lightning, Furnace, Cannon, Berserker (Hero, "Savage Survival"), Barbarian Barrel, Skeletons, Electro Spirit. The opponent is a spell-less bridge-spam deck — Bandit, Royal Ghost, Battle Ram, Electro Wizard, Little Prince, Mother Witch, Golden Knight and (to Ryley's surprise) P.E.K.K.A. rather than the Giant Skeleton he expected. Ryley reads it off the first card: "this guy has the bandit, meaning he's going to be playing some type of bridge spam deck... I feel like it's going to be that golden knight giant skeleton deck that's been going around that doesn't have any spells" (1364-1376s), and sets a plan around it — "with him not having any spells, I feel like I can just defend, get to my evo royal hogs and like double elixir and just chill" (1387-1394s).

That is exactly how it plays out. Through single elixir he spends nothing on offence: Cannon in the middle, Skeletons and Barbarian Barrel on the Bandit and Royal Ghost, Berserker as the ground answer, Furnace behind. His first Royal Hogs go in at clock 1:52 as double elixir starts, and the enemy right tower goes 4858 -> 4173 over the next 30 seconds. He never gets a clean Berserker ability in regulation because the opponent parks the Mother Witch centrally — "he actually made it so I definitely could not get an ability off because he put the mother witch in the middle so I wouldn't be able to kill it, which is smart" (1458-1465s) — and regulation ends with all six towers standing.

Overtime is where the deck earns the title. A Berserker + ability at 1:51-1:44 (OT) is meant to kill the Royal Ghost but goes at the tower instead, taking the enemy left tower from 4532 to 1710 in eight seconds. Ryley then survives a P.E.K.K.A. + Golden Knight + Mother Witch + Battle Ram + Little Prince pile-up on his left with Electro Spirit resets, two Cannons, Skeletons, Berserker and Barbarian Barrel — his own left tower drops to 2000 but holds ("That was a very hard but very clean defense", 1578s) — and counter-pushes with "berserker plus royal hogs" plus the ability at 1:11 (OT). That push chews the enemy right tower from 3229 to 366 HP, and with the opponent out of blockers Ryley finishes with the game's only Lightning: "he can't block anymore. So just go for the lightning. Let's go" (1594-1596s), followed straight by the video's sign-off.
