# Match: The Most UNDERRATED Card in Clash Royale!! — game 1

## Summary

Ryley opens the video announcing "the best mother witch deck in Clash Royale" (t=7-9) and pilots the
video-level consensus list: [Mother Witch](../cards/mother-witch.md), [Battle Ram](../cards/battle-ram.md),
[Bandit](../cards/bandit.md), [Elite Barbarians](../cards/elite-barbarians.md),
[Zappies](../cards/zappies.md), [Heal Spirit](../cards/heal-spirit.md), [Freeze](../cards/freeze.md) and
the [Heroic Valkyrie](../heroes/valkyrie-hero.md) ([Valkyrie](../cards/valkyrie.md) as a Hero, ability
"Wild Whirlwind" for 3 elixir). He calls
the plan himself at t=98: "we are playing bridge spam so we can always counter push off of that", which
matches the [bridge-spam](../archetypes/bridge-spam.md) gameplan. The opponent runs a Mortar shell —
Evo [Mortar](../cards/mortar.md), Evo [Archers](../cards/archers.md), [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md),
[Ice Spirit](../cards/ice-spirit.md), [Earthquake](../cards/earthquake.md),
[Little Prince](../cards/little-prince.md) and their own Heroic Valkyrie — i.e. a
[siege](../archetypes/siege.md) deck, and it wins the early exchange (Ryley's right tower falls from 4858 to
3777 by clock 2:25 to unidentified spell chip and an archer push). From clock 1:35 onward the game becomes a
Valkyrie-plus-ability race: Ryley chips the enemy left tower down in three waves (4858 -> 2890 by clock 0:31),
the opponent's Valkyrie answers by taking ~1300 HP off Ryley's left tower in overtime, and Ryley closes it
with a Bandit + Battle Ram + Valkyrie-at-the-bridge stack that drops the enemy left tower to 226 HP.
He calls it at t=248-250: "it's going right to the tower. Good game. Nice win."

## How Ryley uses his cards

- **[Battle Ram](../cards/battle-ram.md)** — opened the game with it at t=5.7 from 10 elixir, then used it as
  a bridge threat in the overtime pushes (t=216.7 at tile [2, 14], and again around t=238). He never plays it
  defensively; both later drops follow "at the bridge" / "keep up the pressure" commentary (t=214-218,
  t=236-238), and he counts it as one of his two evolutions in cycle: "we do have both of our evos in cycle,
  evo battle ram and evo e-barbs" (t=103-106).
- **[Bandit](../cards/bandit.md)** — five HUD-confirmed plays, in two distinct roles. Back-of-court cycle
  placements at tile [8, 0] (t=102.3 "we just got a bandit in the back here", t=141.1), and dash answers at
  the bridge or into a lane threat: t=23.0 at tile [9, 4] against the archer push, t=212.5 at tile [0, 6]
  ("let's bandit here again on this Valkyrie") and t=237.6 at tile [2, 14] in the winning stack. He also uses
  it as a prediction card: "I want to bandit predict the mortar" (t=171).
- **[Zappies](../cards/zappies.md)** — both plays are splits behind the towers at tile [8, 0] (t=68.6) and
  again at t=131.6, described as "let's just split our zappies again here. He might activate king" (t=124).
  The tracker shows them walking up as slow pressure from t=81 to t=91, and he adds a third at t=228 ("we can
  just go for the zappies here") to keep the opponent's defenders busy: "let's zappies here in the middle
  though just to keep distracting" (t=182).
- **[Heal Spirit](../cards/heal-spirit.md)** — five HUD reads, always as a 1-elixir support behind or on top
  of an existing push: t=8.5 at tile [13, 13], t=81.2 window ("we go heal spirit up top for skellies", t=81),
  t=143.3 at tile [9, 0], t=200.0, and t=218.6 at tile [4, 13] behind the overtime Battle Ram ("let's go for
  the heal spirit as well", t=219).
- **[Freeze](../cards/freeze.md)** — two HUD-read casts (t=108.4 at clock 1:05, t=196.0 in overtime). Its job
  in the deck is stated explicitly and twice: "That is why we are playing freeze in this mother witch deck
  today" (t=37) and "The only way to counter this card, guys, is just a freeze on it. That's exactly why I
  have it in the deck here, man" (t=163-168), both about the enemy Heroic Valkyrie. He also declines it on
  purpose when the value is not there: "I would freeze that but because the archer's on it, I'm not going to"
  (t=205-209).
- **Rage** — not in this deck. The HUD logged a "Rage" cast at t=111.9 (clock 1:02) and showed Rage in the
  hand read several times, but the video-level consensus across all seven games has no Rage: those reads are
  hand-slot misclassifications, and the HUD "Rage" plays line up with Freeze casts. Nothing in the commentary
  ever names Rage.
- **[Elite Barbarians](../cards/elite-barbarians.md)** — one HUD-attributed play at t=145.3 and one called out
  in overtime: "I'm going to go for the e-barbs in the opposite lane and then I'm going to pop the ability as
  soon as I'm able to" (t=195-197); elite-barbarian allies show on the field at clock 1:37 and again at
  t=245-248 in the winning push. He treats them as the opposite-lane pressure card, not as defence, and
  counts Evo E-Barbs as his second evolution (t=106).
- **[Mother Witch](../cards/mother-witch.md)** — the theme of the video. No clean event row carries her name,
  but he narrates two plays ("we can even go for a mother witch like this just to be annoying", t=144;
  "Dude, this mother witch nice it's spawning a lot OF PIGGIES", t=198-200) and one refusal ("I was going to
  high middle Mother Witch, but thank god that we didn't", t=238-241). His stated value case: "it literally
  just spawns a win condition. Like it spawns royal hogs and the royal hogs from the mother witch just do way
  too much damage, and she shoots really, really quick as well" (t=43-53), and the payoff he points at is
  "look at the mother witch piggies just taking out the entire mortar, basically" (t=154-156), with the enemy
  Evo Mortar parked at tile [8, 19] through that window. He also regrets the placement once: "Ooh, we should
  have put it one tile back" (t=147).
- **[Heroic Valkyrie](../heroes/valkyrie-hero.md)** (Hero, base card
  [Valkyrie](../cards/valkyrie.md)) — the card that actually wins the game. Four Valkyrie deploy labels land
  in this game (t=19.2 tile [9, 12]; t=78.6 tile [4, 13]; t=220.2 and t=242.7 tile [2, 15], the left bridge),
  each a 4-elixir drop, and in every case he narrates a bridge placement immediately followed by "pop the
  ability" — Wild Whirlwind costs 3, which matches the repeated unattributed 3-elixir own drops around
  t=124.1, 133.6, 152.3, 156.7, 176.0, 182.0, 187.0, 194.4 and 220.9. His claim about it: "it is really
  actually nearly impossible to defend against that card. Every time your opponent pops the ability, they will
  be going right to your tower" (t=27-35), "it's just guaranteed to go up to the tower" (t=134-137), and
  "how does he stop it? It's just TOO BROKEN" (t=244-246).

How Ryley read and answered them:
- Archers are the card he fears, not the Mortar: "the thing that we have to be really, really cautious of is
  of course the evo archers or just archers in general, cuz archers will get a lot of value against me"
  (t=106-115). His answer is Zappies splits and Bandit dashes rather than a spell.
- The Mortar he handles with pressure rather than a dedicated counter — Bandit prediction ("I want to bandit
  predict the mortar. Come on. Ooh, that was a good mortar down the middle", t=171-175) and, when that fails,
  Mother Witch Hogs eating it (t=154-156).
- He credits the opponent for the only clean answer to his Hero: the opponent repeatedly pulls the Valkyrie
  back with a Tornado — "he has to just bring it back, which I like that play a lot, but the Valkyrie is
  guaranteed to end up getting to the tower... It's the best way that I've seen someone manage it so far
  though. So I'm going to have to steal that from this guy" (t=85-96), and again "he nadoed it back. It didn't
  work" (t=204-205) and "He has to nado this back" (t=226). Tornado never appears in the event rows.
