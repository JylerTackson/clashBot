# Match: Ryley's BEST Games of CRL 2026 — game 1 of 11 (CRL broadcast)

## Summary

This is Clash Royale League broadcast footage of one of Ryley's own duel games, cut out of a compilation and
voiced over afterwards, so there is no readable match clock — every timestamp below is video time. The bottom
("own") side **is** Ryley: he names the list himself in the first ten seconds — "in the second match here, I
had this Mega Knight deck with the Evo Lumberjack in the Hog Rider" (t=4-10) — and the deploy labels on that
side match ([Mega Knight](../cards/mega-knight.md), [Lumberjack](../cards/lumberjack.md) with the Evolution,
[Hog Rider](../cards/hog-rider.md), [Minions](../cards/minions.md),
[Golden Knight](../cards/golden-knight.md), [Knight](../cards/knight.md), plus
[Earthquake](../cards/earthquake.md), which he claims in commentary at t=137). The eighth slot was never
readable. Across from him is a Mega Knight / [Balloon](../cards/balloon.md) list with
[Miner](../cards/miner.md), [Giant Skeleton](../cards/giant-skeleton.md),
[Tornado](../cards/tornado.md), [Tombstone](../cards/tombstone.md) and
[Skeleton Dragons](../cards/skeleton-dragons.md) — the matchup he least wanted: "unfortunately, he played the
deck that was the best against what I had. But I just had to work with what I had and just trust that I would
be able to beat it" (t=56-64). The clip is a partial game (it opens mid-match at 10 elixir with towers already
being read and ends on the transition to the next clip), and it goes badly for a long stretch — the opponent
Tornados Ryley's opening Hog Rider into the King Tower to activate it (t=18-38), then a raged Balloon takes
Ryley's left tower from 2847 to 1967 HP (t=90-98). Ryley's stated plan is to survive it: "I know my deck is
super aggressive. If I beat him, I'm probably going to beat him in like one big push or maybe two big pushes.
So, as long as I defend well for the rest of the game, then I should be fine" (t=92-101). It ends the other
way round: he Earthquakes the Tombstone and sends a Hog immediately after (t=136-141), and the opponent's
Tornado on defence drags Ryley's Lumberjack onto his own King Tower — the enemy left tower falls 2265 → 1623 →
993 → 684 HP between t=146 and t=152 while Ryley shouts "look at his king tower, guys. I was screaming right
here... look, he three crowned himself" (t=150-155). He confirms the result at t=156: "And I won the first
duel two to zero. I was at four and zero."

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=29-32 — order matters against Tornado: "My hog rider had to go in front of the lumberjack for that to
  possibly be prevented" — i.e. put the Hog Rider ahead of the Lumberjack so a Tornado cannot drag the pair
  into the King Tower.
- t=25-27 — read the deck off the cheap building: "the second I saw that tombstone, I knew that he had the
  [Tornado]".
- t=39-41 — and off the support card: "with the executioner, I knew for sure that he had [Mega Knight]
  balloon".
- t=74-77 — cycling is worth elixir: play the Lumberjack on a Miner "just to get to my EVO".
- t=92-101 — how to play an aggressive deck from behind: "I know my deck is super aggressive. If I beat him,
  I'm probably going to beat him in like one big push or maybe two big pushes. So, as long as I defend well
  for the rest of the game, then I should be fine."
- t=117-125 — defensive Hog Rider: "I do like to use defensive hog rider sometimes because if you do a
  defensive hog, then they still have to defend the hog rider itself. So you get a lot of value out of that
  type of play."
- t=132-137 — holding a card against a prediction: "I was waiting for him to predict my minions with the
  skelly drags, but then it never happened. That's why it took so long on the minions."
- t=138-141 — spell into win condition: "I just earthquake the tombstone. I don't think he know I had
  earthquakes. So then I instantly went for a hog rider."
