## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=1341-1344: "He might go for the RG at the bridge opposite lane to be honest, but I'm
  fine with that" — deploying Elite Barbarians in the back is acceptable even into a Royal
  Giant bridge punish.
- t=1348-1351: "we can go for the heal spirit on top of this Royal Ghost just to heal up our
  Ebarbs" — Heal Spirit placed on an enemy troop still heals your own push.
- t=1355-1362: "I'm going to go for a battle ram at the bridge in the opposite lane... He's
  going to go for a hunter, which is fine" — pressure the empty lane and accept that the
  answer costs the opponent a defensive card.
- t=1379-1383: "I don't like that play. It's going to get one swipe on the tower, but that's
  not too bad in this matchup" — acknowledging a bad spell placement and weighing the cost.
- t=1385-1400: "We just need to really, really abuse the Evo Ebarbs. The defense is just
  really easy for him... But if we just get one good sequence with the Earbs, we have a
  chance to win" — pick one win condition in a bad matchup and build around it.
- t=1406-1409: "He did waste his Berserker, though. I don't really know about wasting the
  berserker like that" — a Hero spent on nothing is the opening.
- t=1423-1424: "I'm going to zap here to retarget that to the piggy" — Zap as a retarget
  tool onto a Mother Witch Cursed Hog.
- t=1436-1442: "that was a bad hunter though cuz now after using that hunter that's just a
  free cannon cart and he can't even go for the RG because I have Ebarbs at the bridge on
  it" — track which defensive card the opponent has spent, then play the card it answered.
- t=1454-1455: "let's go for the mother witch here too low key. I want to do it late though"
  — delay the Mother Witch so she is not the first thing spelled.
- t=1457-1463: "I put it right as he dropped the fisherman. So now we can't really block for
  the fisherman so we can get a free counter on that heal spirit" — deploy on the opponent's
  commitment frame.
- t=1467-1471: "I'm going to put in front of the king tower, though, in case he tries to
  time it with an RG at the bridge" — central Cannon Cart placement covers both lanes.
- t=1478-1480: "He could go for a good fireball, which is fine cuz he won't be able to take
  out the mother witch" — choose follow-ups that survive the opponent's spell.
- t=1498-1502: "Let's ram a little bit back so we can't fireball immediately. And we're
  going to barbarian barrel at the bridge, too. Maybe could tank for the battle ram" —
  delay the Battle Ram out of spell range and tank for it with Barbarian Barrel.
