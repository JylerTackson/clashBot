## Lessons stated by Ryley

- 1336-1345s: on the Hero Tombstone sight-range nerf — "They're also nerfing the sight range by
  27%. So now the Hero Tombstone will be easier to be kited around the map because obviously
  right now it is very very hard to drag it around."
- 1349-1358s: how the change plays out against a building — "with buildings like say I have a
  bomb tower here and they have the Tombstone walking right here. The ability will actually just
  end up going towards your tower, which is pretty good, I would say."
- 1364-1377s: "I'm very glad that they did some compensation nerfs after giving it so many buffs
  because they also increased the hit points by about 180... it went from 4,000 to 4,200 or so."
- 1380-1400s: on the Evolved Minion Horde — "The hit speed slow down is now going from 50% to
  33%... whenever the Evo minion horde gets damaged out to it, it will go invisible, but the hit
  points will be slowed by 50% now. That will only be 33%, so the minion horde will just do a lot
  more DPS while being slowed down."
- 1401-1416s: on the Evolved Princess — "this is my favorite buff for sure. I love this card so
  much, man... I'm like one of the only people who actually upload this card and use it and stuff
  like that on top ladder. I think I was actually like one of like two people who used it in the
  top 200 last season."
- 1418-1432s: the Evolved Princess change itself — "The Evo princess will now slow every other
  attack instead of every three attacks. And the slow duration is getting a nerf because of that...
  from 7 seconds to 5.5 seconds, which is definitely a very big overall buff because there will
  just be more slows from the princess overall."
- 1436-1473s: on Goblin Gang — "They're also buffing the spear goblins by 6%. They're making it
  hit quicker... what is happening to the goblin gang? Because the goblin gang got two buffs with
  the goblins and the spear goblins. So, goblin gang is going to be looking extremely strong next
  season... they never really like mentioned the fact that goblin gang got two like inherent buffs
  out of this balance change, but I definitely do think that it is needed for the individual
  cards... Just that Evo skeleton army was kind of like overshadowing it. Maybe it helps bridge
  those two cards a little bit more."
- 1474-1499s: on Suspicious Bush — "They're also doing a buff to the suspicious bush. Um it will
  be getting an 11% damage buff, which is cool. I do feel like that ever since bush got that most
  recent nerf, it did fall out of the meta for sure. I think the nerf was that it spawned like one
  tile away from the tower or building whenever it popped instead of going directly to the tower,
  which was definitely a great change. I don't disagree with it cuz bush was like a top two most
  annoying card in the game outside of recruits."
- 1523-1560s: on the Furnace — "The furnace is getting a spawn speed buff which as you guys will
  need to remember I discussed this at the very start of the video so maybe you forgot the furnace
  or the fire spirits obviously right are getting that big nerf where they don't connect to the
  tower which heavily affects the furnace. So now they are giving the furnace a compensation buff
  of a spawn speed of 7 seconds to 5 seconds... Honestly I don't think the furnace will be any
  good at all. I think that furnace will actually be one of the worst cards in the game because of
  this."
