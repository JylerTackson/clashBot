# Match CInrqMTlVkg-m0.0 — 2.6 Cycle with Minion Giant in the Hog Rider slot vs Evo Mortar siege

## Summary

Game 1 of a deck-showcase video in which every game uses a different list built around the unreleased **Minion Giant**; Ryley states the shell outright at 70-76s: "it's just like somehow a better Hog Rider. That's why we're using it right now in 2.6 Hog Cycle. I just replaced the Hog Rider with the Minion Giant." Everything else is the standard 2.6 package — Evo [Cannon](../cards/cannon.md), Evo [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md), [Ice Golem](../cards/ice-golem.md), [Ice Spirit](../cards/ice-spirit.md), [Musketeer](../cards/musketeer.md), [The Log](../cards/the-log.md) and [Fireball](../cards/fireball.md) — so the deck plays as [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md): chip with the win condition every rotation, defend with 1-3 elixir cards, never build one big push. The opponent (called "Aragon" on screen, as in the rest of the channel's series) played Evo [Mortar](../cards/mortar.md) [siege](../archetypes/siege.md) with [Spear Goblins](../cards/spear-goblins.md), [Goblins](../cards/goblins.md), [Skeleton Barrel](../cards/skeleton-barrel.md), [Rage](../cards/rage.md), a Skeleton King tower troop and the card Ryley names as "Goblin Stein" ([Goblinstein](../cards/goblinstein.md)). The match is one-sided in damage and slow in result: the opponent's left crown tower falls from 3052 to 1575 during regular time in clean 189-HP steps (each one a Minion Giant tower hit) while Ryley's own pair are barely touched, but nothing dies, so the game goes to overtime 0-0. In overtime the same loop finishes it — five straight connections at OT 1:57-1:50 take that tower from 1351 to 595, and it reads 147 at OT 0:57 as Ryley calls it ("that is going to be good game. I will see you all in the next match", 244-245s). Ryley finishes with both his towers alive at 2411 and 2728. The commentary is really a card review: he repeats that the Minion Giant is played "at the bridge similar to Hog Rider", that it only targets buildings and towers, that being air makes it near-unanswerable, and that a bug is eating some of its damage.
