## Summary

Clash Royale League broadcast footage, game 8 of 11 in a deck-showcase video, with Ryley voicing over his own CRL 2026 games. This one is a duel against Sandbox at the World Finals: "So guys, now we were in the next duel against Sandbox, who is one of the best players in the world" (1944-1948s), and the stakes are explicit — "if I won this duel right here, I was guaranteed to qualify for the monthly final" (1969-1973s). The bottom (own) side is Ryley: the HUD deck matches what he narrates, and he names his own cards throughout. His list is a 2.6-elixir Rocket cycle built entirely around defence — Berserker (the Hero variant, whose Savage Survival ability he calls out twice), Ice Spirit, Skeletons, The Log, Evo Princess, Royal Delivery, Evo Cannon and Rocket. He states the plan up front: "I was really confident with this rocket cycle deck here. I just felt like any matchup I trust my defense, I can win" (1953-1957s).

Sandbox brings bridge spam — Battle Ram, Elite Barbarians, Giant Skeleton, Mother Witch, Wizard, Zappies, Barbarian Barrel and Royal Hogs — and Ryley rates him: "Sandbox is one of, if not the best bridge spam player in the world" (2030-2032s), while still liking his side of it: "I do truthfully think that I have a good matchup here, and I do think that I counter him quite nicely" (2025-2028s).

For most of the readable clip the defence holds. He absorbs an evo Battle Ram plus Giant Skeleton double-lane push around t=2093-2110 for no tower damage ("Was taking absolutely no damage, but I was down five elixir", 2108-2113s), lines his Evo Cannon up against Mother Witches, blocks a Giant Skeleton with the Berserker ability at t=2165-2170, and by t=2176 says "All I had to do was defend for another 50 seconds and this game would be mine." Then it unravels: he declines a Rocket on a back-placed Giant Skeleton ("I got scared of rocketing, man. I just should have rocketed", 2196-2200s), misplaces his Cannon ("I misclicked my cannon and I had to log ice spirit the wizard", 2217-2219s), and the Mother Witch and Elite Barbarians survive. From t=2230 the towers collapse — left 2859 -> 363 HP, right 2683 -> 1009 HP, king 4449 -> 266 HP by t=2248. A loss, and he says so plainly: "I actually lost because of that, man... And I lost with a good matchup. So, I was 0 to 1" (2232-2247s).

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- 1953-1957s — a low-cost defensive cycle deck is a matchup-agnostic pick if your defence is good: "I was really confident with this rocket cycle deck here. I just felt like any matchup I trust my defense, I can win."
- 1985-1993s — on competitive mindset: "you never want to like put your fate in other people's hands. I'd rather just put it in mine and trust myself, like trust my practice, trust my skill."
- 1999-2003s — "there's no point to sulk about it cuz there still is a chance. But of course, you always want to do it off of your own merit."
- 2013-2016s — taking a Mother Witch at the bridge with a spell is correct even as a "smart play by him": "They're just making sure to take no princess damage early on."
- 2046-2056s — the standard he holds himself to: "if I play 100% perfect, I should win this game... you're always trying to play perfect in this game. Like even if it doesn't happen very often, that's still what you like strive for."
- 2066-2076s — bank a defensive building before you chip: "because I had a free cannon on the board, I also knew I could princess the bridge... it's still good to make these plays in my opinion. Just trying to get some, you know, slick damage on the tower here and there."
- 2085-2095s — sometimes you have to spend a big spell on defence to keep your hand playable: "I knew that this might be a little bit risky, but I did think that I would be able to defend, but I also knew that like sometimes I need to do plays like that otherwise my hand is just going to be way too awkward to try and defend here."
- 2115-2129s — track the opponent's Evolutions, not just their cards: "he had both of his evos right there. So since he had both of his evolutions, every other time I do that, it won't be as difficult to defend because the other cards will be a little bit weaker. So that was a important thing to keep in mind for like the future."
- 2136-2140s — a placement rule: "I was doing a good job at lining up my evo cannons with the mother witches."
- 2146-2148s — "You have to be really careful here" against Royal Hogs chip once you are ahead on damage.
- 2196-2207s — do not save a Rocket you should throw: "I got scared of rocketing, man. I just should have rocketed. And I don't know why I didn't. I think I just got scared of doing it, and I chose not to because of the like I thought I could just rocket at the end. Um, but, you know, I couldn't take the decision back."
- 2241-2247s — "it's not an easy deck to play perfect against, but I still expect being perfect out of myself."
