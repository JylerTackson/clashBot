# Match 5nOaTBlYLlg-m0.2 — Lava Hound / Rune Giant beatdown, played out for crowns in an already-won CRL qualifier game

## Summary

The second game of Ryley's CRL qualifier set against "Ian", played on a [Lava Hound](../cards/lava-hound.md) air beatdown list: [Rune Giant](../cards/rune-giant.md) as the buff-tank, [Inferno Dragon](../cards/inferno-dragon.md) and [Skeleton Dragons](../cards/skeleton-dragons.md) as the air payload, [Goblin Cage](../cards/goblin-cage.md) and [Berserker](../cards/berserker.md) as the cheap defence, [Fireball](../cards/fireball.md) and [Zap](../cards/zap.md) as the spells. Ian is on a cheap ground cycle deck — [Goblin Drill](../cards/goblin-drill.md), [Cannon](../cards/cannon.md), [Bomber](../cards/bomber.md), [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md), a [Giant Snowball](../cards/giant-snowball.md) and a Berserker of his own — and, as Ryley explains throughout, that deck has no air defence at all. The unusual framing is that the game was already decided before it was played: Ian had repeated a card between games and forfeited the result, so Ryley is not playing to win but to farm crowns, because the qualifier tiebreakers run game difference first and crown difference second (t=857-875), and he had personally qualified two months earlier on a single extra crown (t=889-903).

Observation matches that story. He runs the Hound into the left lane repeatedly with Rune Giant and dragon support, kills the enemy left crown tower around t=936, and then spends nearly ninety seconds camped on the opponent's half — Rune Giant labels at [8, 21], [8, 20] and [8, 20] again, Skeleton Dragons at [3, 21], Berserker at [8, 20], Zap and Fireball dropped straight onto the surviving right tower — hunting a three-crown that never comes. The enemy right tower falls from 2551 HP at t=845 to 152 HP by t=1062 without dying. Meanwhile Ian's Goblin Drills (labelled at [2, 6], [14, 6], [15, 6]) plus a Bomber grind Ryley's own left tower from 1717 down to 152, which is exactly the risk Ryley names out loud at t=1013: this lava list has no drill answer. At around t=1000 (after Ian snowballs one of his pushes) he gives up on the three-crown, switches to "win the game normally", stabilises with Goblin Cage on defence and fireball-cycles the last of the right tower. At t=1067 he says "I beat Ian two to zero to bring me to a really good spot to qualify for the world finals."

## Opponent

The Giant Snowball is what makes him abandon the crown hunt: "here after he snowballed, I was like, 'Okay, like I'm just never going to get the three crown'" (t=1000).

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- **t=857-875** — "there is a lot of different tiebreakers, but the two main tiebreakers are number one game difference, so your win-to-loss difference... and the second tiebreaker is the crown difference. So if you have a completely tied match history... then it goes to the crowns. So I actually played this out wanting to get two to three crowns if possible." Play out won games for crowns in a league format.
- **t=889-903** — "in the third month, two months ago, guys, I actually qualified because I got one more crown than my other competitor... we had the exact same win rate, same game difference, everything. The only thing that was different was the crowns."
- **t=920** — attack the axis the opponent cannot answer: he goes all-air for the crown hunt specifically "cuz I knew that Ian's deck had no air defense."
- **t=935-940** — on the repeat rule that decided the game: "of course you're never going to repeat a card on purpose or anything like that cuz then you just completely lose out."
- **t=1008-1028** — know when the crown hunt stops being worth it: "if I kept going for it, there was a chance that I could lose cuz in this uh lava deck... I don't actually have any drill defense... So there was a real possibility that I could choke the game if I wasn't careful. So I just decided like screw this, bro. I'm just going to win the game normally."
- **t=1041-1049** — count out the finish before you commit to it: "I made sure to cage on defense once again because I knew that I only needed like two more fireballs and two zaps to finish off the game. I did zap first just to help get to the EVO."
