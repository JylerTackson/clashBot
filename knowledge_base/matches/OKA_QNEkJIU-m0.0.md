# Match: This SUPER FAST 2.0 Log Bait Cycle is BUSTED!! — game 1

## Summary

The opening game of a single-deck session. Ryley names the list in the first fifteen seconds — "we are
using a 2.0 log bait cycle deck right now" (t=11) — and pilots the video-level consensus eight:
[Berserker](../cards/berserker.md) (Hero), [Cannon](../cards/cannon.md),
[Dart Goblin](../cards/dart-goblin.md) (Evo), [Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md),
[Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md) (Evo), [Ice Spirit](../cards/ice-spirit.md),
[Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md) and [Wall Breakers](../cards/wall-breakers.md) — 16 elixir over 8 cards,
exactly the 2.0 average he advertises. It is a [bait](../archetypes/bait.md) shell run at
[cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md) speed: no spell at all, two cheap win conditions (Goblin Barrel, Wall
Breakers), and a defensive core of Cannon plus 1-elixir spirits. The two cards he credits are the Hero
Berserker and the Evolved Dart Goblin: "the hero berserker just works so, so nicely in this type of deck"
(t=21-24) and "You're just able to defend pretty much anything with hero berserker plus evo dark goblin
cycle. Ever since the dark goblin evo got the buff, I feel like it's been like a sneakily underrated card"
(t=38-47). The opponent is a double-[Elixir Collector](../cards/elixir-collector.md) Three Musketeers deck
with Evo [Knight](../cards/knight.md) and Evo [Battle Ram](../cards/battle-ram.md), which Ryley reads early:
"I'm pretty sure this guy has three musketeers. We have to be a little bit careful" (t=54-56). He commits to
one lane on purpose — "I only really want to attack one lane because I know that this is going to be a one
tower game" (t=68-71) — and stacks Goblin Barrel, Berserker and Wall Breakers onto the enemy left tower until
it reads 155 HP at clock 1:15, then spends the whole of double and triple elixir defending. The last minute
is one long hold against Evo Battle Ram, Evo Knight, Minions and the Musketeers (own right tower 3569 -> 1429
between clock 0:31 and 0:27); it survives, and he calls it at t=162: "Very nice win to start off the video",
then "Let's go. What a great win" (t=166).
