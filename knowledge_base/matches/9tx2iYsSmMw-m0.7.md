## Lessons stated by Ryley

- 1669-1683s — deck choice as information warfare: "I decided to go for Lava Hound because I did feel like Lava Hound would be pretty like unpredictable for me. I hadn't used a single Lava Hound deck in all of CRL this month... I just thought that it would be a very very good idea to just remain unpredictable because I didn't think that he would end up countering it."
- 1693-1701s — in duel format you cannot repeat cards between your decks; repeating one hands the game over: "Ian not only repeated Berserker, but he also repeated skeleton."
- 1721-1740s — win the game anyway, for the tiebreakers: "the two main tiebrea[k]ers are number one game difference... and the second um tiebreaker is the crown difference. So if you have a completely tied match history... then it goes to the crowns."
- 1760-1780s — why he cares: "in the third month, two months ago, I actually qualified because I got one more crown than my um other competitor... we had the exact same win rate, same game difference, everything. The only thing that was different was the crowns. So ever since then, I've always made it a very big thing for me to try to get as many crowns as possible, even in an already won game."
- 1804-1810s — nobody repeats on purpose: "of course you're never going to repeat a card on purpose or anything like that cuz then you just like completely lose out."
- 1874-1899s — know when to stop chasing the extra crown: "I just kind of accepted it that he was not going to let me get the three crown. And if I kept going for it, there was a chance that I could lose cuz in this lava deck... I don't actually have any drill defense... So I just decided like, screw this, bro. I'm just going to win the game normally because if I keep going for this, then I could actually lose."
- 1911-1919s — count the finish before you play it: "I knew that I only needed like two more fireballs and two zaps to finish off the game. I did zap first just to help get to the EVO."
