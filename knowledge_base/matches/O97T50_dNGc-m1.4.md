## Summary

This is a qualification duel inside a deck-showcase tournament video — "we are in the duel to qualify us to CRL stage number two" (t=1515-1518) — and the deck for this game is the Evo Royal Hogs / Evo Tesla cycle list: Royal Hogs, Tesla, Furnace, Berserker, Electro Spirit, Barbarian Barrel, Skeletons and Lightning. Ryley calls it "my number one most comfort deck. I was playing this deck in almost every single duel" (t=1526-1530). The opponent mirrors the win condition with the other build of the same shell — Royal Hogs, Bomb Tower, Evo Archers, Goblin Giant, Electro Spirit, Lightning, Barbarian Barrel and Skeletons — which Ryley reads as slightly favoured against him ("he had the exact deck that I had in the previous game, and I do personally think that his variation has a slight advantage", t=1549-1553), because Goblin Giant at the bridge is hard for a cheap defence and Evo Archers outlast a Furnace. The readable window picks the game up at 2:50 with both towers full, and the game is decided by two things: a Berserker Savage Survival activation at 1:57 that opens the enemy left tower, and a bridge Royal Hogs set at 1:20 that connects while forcing the opponent to spend "almost his entire deck worth of elixir" on defence. From there Ryley cycles Furnace into the lane the opponent wants to attack, holds Barbarian Barrel for Evo Archers, and takes the game in overtime — "right here is when I knew I won. I instantly Evo piggies... I was able to win" (t=1795-1801).

## How Ryley uses his cards

- **[Royal Hogs](../cards/royal-hogs.md)** — the only win condition, and only ever sent at the bridge from the left-hand pocket, tile [2, 14] or [2, 15] (t=1605.2, 1666.3, 1743.3), never from the back. Every set goes in while a defence is already running or while he knows the opponent's cycle is bad for it: "I went for the Evo Piggies here just knowing that I would get a guaranteed good sequence because his cycle wasn't really the greatest for it" (t=1607). He explicitly cycles cheap cards to see the Evolution as often as possible — "I just want to get my Evo piggies down as many times as possible" (t=1636) — and the Evolution's Hog Flight is what makes the last set unanswerable: "I instantly Evo piggies... he wouldn't be able to defend mine" (t=1796-1800).
- **[Tesla](../cards/tesla.md)** — played seven times, always centre or centre-right in front of the tower ([9, 11] 2:46, [9, 12] 1:53, [8, 12] 0:30, [9, 13] and [10, 13] in overtime, [11, 13] twice late). It is both the anti-Royal-Hogs building and a cycle card: at t=1654.8 he drops it at 9 elixir with nothing to defend, "I cycled the high Tesla here... I need to control the pace of the game." He tracks the Evolution against the opponent's: "this meant that my Evo Tesla and his Evo piggies were unaligned in cycle, which is scary because then the next time he goes piggies I have to do the regular Tesla and it doesn't get as much damage on the piggies as the Evo would" (t=1713-1723). He is also aware it is spell-fodder — "this is where stuff gets scary, right, is when I have to use these Teslas and he has the Evo archers to snipe it down" (t=1699-1702).
- **[Furnace](../cards/furnace.md)** — eight placements, split between the deep back line ([9, 0] at 2:22, 0:42 and in overtime; [8, 0] at 1:34) and lane-side placements in front of the incoming push ([7, 6], [8, 9], [9, 6], [9, 10], [16, 10]). The opponent's Royal Hogs come almost exclusively into the left lane from [2, 15] (t=1556.2, 1630.2, 1681.3, 1766.4, 1795.4), which is exactly the lane Ryley keeps pre-loading with Furnace.

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=1571-1576 — take the free early tower damage with the Berserker ability, because it changes who has to take risks: "whenever you get this early damage, it does put you in a good spot where they have to be more aggressive."
- t=1591-1601 — on why Royal Hogs is the pro pick: "you know how it's going with Piggies the whole time, there is a lot of outplay potential, and the reason that all the pros love it so much is because as long as you play good, you are able to make things happen with this deck."
- t=1607-1611 — send the win condition when the opponent's cycle is wrong for it, not when you have the elixir: "I went for the Evo Piggies here just knowing that I would get a guaranteed good sequence because his cycle wasn't really the greatest for it."
- t=1612-1613 — hold the small spell for the card that actually needs it: "I didn't Barb Barrel cuz I wanted to save it for the Evo Archers."
- t=1616-1624 — value a push by what it drags out, not only by damage: "while also forcing out like almost his entire deck worth of elixir... He basically played his whole deck on defense."
- t=1624-1628 — spend into a window where the big spell is unaffordable: "I went for the barbarian barrel and the low Evo Tesla just knowing that he wouldn't be able to afford a lightning."
- t=1653-1663 — cycling is a pacing tool, not only an Evolution tool: "I cycled the high Tesla here... not only to get to the Evo but just in general I need to control the pace of the game."
- t=1713-1723 — track Evolution alignment against the opponent's: "this meant that my Evo Tesla and his Evo piggies were unaligned in cycle, which is scary because then the next time he goes piggies I have to do the regular Tesla and it doesn't get as much damage on the piggies as the Evo would."
- t=1728-1735 — pre-place the spawner into the lane the opponent wants: "it's really annoying to go Royal Hogs into a guy who has stuff down."
- t=1752-1758 — place buildings so they do not give the big spell value, and pre-load chip before your own push: "obviously not to give lightning value and also just get some fire spirits flowing, cuz I knew when I went for the piggies it'd be even more annoying to stop with the furnace."
- t=1760-1766 — baiting the spell is worth as much as dodging it: "He did get a good lightning, but me baiting out that lightning made it so he didn't get the lightning on my low HP tower."
- t=1784-1793 — the overtime discipline: "I was doing a really good job of just not letting him gain momentum... just to make sure that I always had something down."
