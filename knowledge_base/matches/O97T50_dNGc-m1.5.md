# Ryley's Evo Ram / Hero Wizard bridge spam wins the CRL qualifier decider (partial capture, last ~3 minutes)

## Summary

This is the closing segment of the video: the decider of a best-of set at 1-1, captured only from clock 2:47 onward (177 s, 45 events), so the opening of the game is missing. ## How Ryley uses his cards

- **[Battle Ram](../cards/battle-ram.md)** (3 deploys: [15, 13] clock 2:12, [2, 14] clock 1:14, [2, 14] clock 0:30) — the deck's information tool and its cycle anchor. The first one is explicitly a probe of the opponent's bait deck and building (t=1849); the second is chosen over a Vines finish specifically to keep the Evolution in rotation (t=1901-1916); the third goes down in the same beat as Elite Barbarians in the other lane and survives the opponent's Tesla ("He missed the Tesla", t=1960).
- **[Elite Barbarians](../cards/elite-barbarians.md)** (3 deploys at the bridge: [3, 14] clock 1:30, [14, 14] clock 0:30, [14, 14] clock 0:05) — always at the bridge, never in the back, and always with the evolution ability used as a prediction: "I went for the ability predicting him going Valkyrie and look at that... I dodged the log and I dodged the Valkyrie" (t=1891-1895). They carry the left tower from 2377 to 962 and land the last swing (t=1966).
- **[Mother Witch](../cards/mother-witch.md)** (3 deploys: [4, 10] clock 1:56, [10, 6] clock 0:57, [15, 14] clock 0:05) — used both as a value threat ("really annoying for him to deal with", t=1864) and as a defensive screen in the last seconds against a Magic Archer he expects (t=1970-1971).
- **[Wizard](../cards/wizard.md) (hero)** ([8, 0], clock 1:48) — placed in the back in reaction to the opponent's Magic Archer, and named as his defensive insurance later: "I wasn't scared cuz I knew that I had the hero wizard for defense" (t=1938). He credits it with tower damage on the clock-1:30 push ("I got a lot of damage with the wizard and the e-barbs", t=1899).
- **[Giant Skeleton](../cards/giant-skeleton.md)** ([8, 0], clock 0:48; earlier mentioned as an unplayed intention at clock 1:45) — the big-push starter once he has read the opponent as low on elixir; at clock 1:45 he wanted it at the bridge but the opponent's answer ("He did a smart play here", t=1878) stopped him.
- **[Zappies](../cards/zappies.md)** (3 deploys: [9, 0] clock 2:45, [9, 8] clock 1:00, [9, 10] clock 0:12) — always centre-right on his own half, and explicitly the Wall Breakers answer: "I went for the zappies on the wall breakers. I was able to counter everything nicely" (t=1924-1927). Held ready during the final push ("I had my Zappies ready", t=1965).
- **[Vines](../cards/vines.md)** (2 deploys: [3, 13] clock 2:37 defensively, [11, 20] clock 0:25 offensively) — the offensive one is the game-winning use, killing the Tesla so the Battle Ram connects (t=1957). He also names it as the play he deliberately did *not* make at clock 1:14 (t=1902), and as half of his closing cycle ("Arrows plus Vines", t=1977).
- **[Arrows](../cards/arrows.md)** (2 deploys: [14, 9] clock 1:40 on his own half, [3, 21] clock 0:07 on the enemy left tower) — the second is the stated finisher pairing: "all I had to do was go for the Arrows plus Vines" (t=1977). No commentary explains the first.

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=1838-1847 — deck choice as a tournament read: "I went for this bridge spam deck. Once again, just like how against my other opponent, I just thought that this would be a little bit unpredictable and I thought that I play this deck pretty well, so I was pretty confident."
- t=1849-1861 — use a cheap bridge threat to identify the opponent's archetype: "I went for the battering here, just trying to figure out, you know, his type of bait deck, his building... I realized that he had the log because he was scared of me playing log bait."
- t=1891-1895 — spend the evolution ability as a prediction, not as a reaction: "I went for the ability predicting him going Valkyrie and look at that, guys. I dodged the log and I dodged the Valkyrie."
- t=1901-1918 — take the cycle over the last few hits: "I could have went for the vines, but I preferred to just go for the battering cuz I had a feeling that if I went for the vines, I wouldn't have taken the tower and I wouldn't have cycled my Evo... So, I think that was the right play, but I'm still not sure."
- t=1936-1943 — attack into a spell you can already answer: "I had a feeling that after I went for this, he would pressure me with the magic archer, but I wasn't scared cuz I knew that I had the hero wizard for defense."
- t=1951-1956 — ignore chip you cannot profitably answer: "I just ignored the goblin barrel, just knowing right that there was no point for me to even bother trying to deal with that."
