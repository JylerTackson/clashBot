# Match: Playing the 8 BEST Cards in a SINGLE Deck — game 1

## Summary

This is the opening game of a single-deck session built on an external ranking rather than on synergy: "I
asked Royal API what the eight highest ranked cards right now are in Clash Royale, and I have gotten a deck
of these eight cards" (t=9-20). The eight are [Barbarian Barrel](../cards/barbarian-barrel.md),
[Battle Ram](../cards/battle-ram.md), [Cannon Cart](../cards/cannon-cart.md),
[Elite Barbarians](../cards/elite-barbarians.md) (Evo), [Goblinstein](../cards/goblinstein.md),
[Heal Spirit](../cards/heal-spirit.md), [Mother Witch](../cards/mother-witch.md) and
[Zap](../cards/zap.md) — and Ryley classifies the result himself at t=88: "this looks like a very real
bridge spam deck. So it does kind of go to show that bridge spam is really strong in the game right now,"
which is how he pilots it ([bridge-spam](../archetypes/bridge-spam.md): Battle Ram, Elite Barbarians,
Goblinstein and Cannon Cart dropped at or near the bridge, never a back-line push). The opponent is a
[Hog Rider](../cards/hog-rider.md) / [Earthquake](../cards/earthquake.md) cycle player
([cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md)) with Evo [Firecracker](../cards/firecracker.md), Evo
[Knight](../cards/knight.md), [Bomb Tower](../cards/bomb-tower.md),
[Barbarians](../cards/barbarians.md), [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md) and his own
[Barbarian Barrel](../cards/barbarian-barrel.md) — a defensive core Ryley calls a hard counter to what he is
holding ("Him having Bomb Tower is really, really bad for us cuz it's going to make his defense a lot easier
having the Knight, Firecracker and Bomb Tower", t=106-112). He is behind for most of the game: the Hog
connects twice and his right tower falls from 4858 to 2345 by clock OT 1:28, and by t=192 he says the only
route left "is just based off of a really good set of Evo E-Barbs". That is exactly what happens — in
overtime a Mother Witch curse turns the opponent's Skeletons into Cursed Hogs while the Evo Elite Barbarians
land on the right tower (enemy right 4603 -> 1700 between t=222 and t=230, "just like that, we're actually
in the damage lead"), and a last Cannon Cart plus Heal Spirit finishes the tower (1068 at t=258, 174 at
t=260). He calls it at t=259-266: "One more. Bang. Let's go. What a win, bro. We were even able to beat the
hard counter. That was beautiful gameplay outside of the Zap."

## Opponent

A [Hog Rider](../cards/hog-rider.md) / [Earthquake](../cards/earthquake.md) cycle deck that Ryley reads
correctly at t=40: "it looks like we're up against a Hog Rider Earthquake player here." Observed:
Hog Rider (tracked on his right tower at t=86-88 and again at t=200), Evo [Firecracker](../cards/firecracker.md)
(ten separate tracks, the most-mentioned card in the game), Evo [Knight](../cards/knight.md),
[Bomb Tower](../cards/bomb-tower.md) (five deployments, two of them alive at once from t=160),
[Earthquake](../cards/earthquake.md) (t=140.1), [Barbarians](../cards/barbarians.md) (t=160.2),
[Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md) (tracked t=18-50, and the units his Mother Witch curses at t=222) and a
[Barbarian Barrel](../cards/barbarian-barrel.md) used to clear his Evo E-Barbs at t=96.

How he handled it: he never contested the Hog directly with a building — the deck has none — and instead
put Cannon Cart in front of the King Tower when he expected it (t=171). Against Firecracker he used King
Tower activation early (Mother Witch, t=44) and the Goblinstein ability later ("we can take out the
Firecracker with that", t=130, followed by "we got out the one Firecracker, but now we have to deal with the
next Firecracker"). The Bomb Tower is what he considers the real problem (t=106-112), and his answer is
volume rather than a counter: he notes at t=159 that the push "forced out a second Bomb Tower, which is
something." He also credits the opponent for a defensive decision at t=187 — the opponent declines to play
Skeletons into a live Mother Witch: "Doesn't go for the skellies, which is smart by him."

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=29-36 — the premise of the whole video: "sometimes like eight cards don't really have synergy together.
  Just because they're all really good doesn't mean they actually work in a proper deck together."
- t=42-47 — spell-bait style activation: play Mother Witch so that the opponent's Firecracker activates your
  King Tower — "just to make sure that we activate that King Tower off of the Firecracker."
- t=50-55 — do not commit a bridge Goblinstein when the opponent has just spent on a defender you have to
  play through: "after he went for that Firecracker, I don't think that it's a little bit worth it."
- t=88-91 — "this looks like a very real bridge spam deck. So it does kind of go to show that bridge spam is
  really strong in the game right now."
- t=106-112 — why the matchup is bad: "Him having Bomb Tower is really, really bad for us cuz it's going to
  make his defense a lot easier having the Knight, Firecracker and Bomb Tower."
- t=118-122 — pick the bridge push by the opponent's cycle, not by your own elixir: "I know that he won't
  really have the greatest cycle to defend this."
- t=192-196 — pick one win route and defend around it: "literally the only possible way to win this is just
  based off of a really good set of Evo E-Barbs."
- t=219-221 — bridge threats are about forcing bad spending: "I'm just going to try to get the Evo E-Barbs
  at the bridge cuz he's going to have to overspend."
- t=73-78 — his own criticism of the ranking: cards he thinks are missing from the top eight are
  "the [Rune Giant](../cards/rune-giant.md) or maybe like the hero [Tombstone](../cards/tombstone.md)."
- t=66-70 — on the list overall: "I don't really hate any card on here. Like there's not a card on here that
  I think is necessarily bad."
