## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=649-653: pre-place the cheap distraction for the threat you expect, and skip the support troop when it
  will die anyway — "skellies here at the bridge in case he cannon carts... I'm not going to do my Berserker
  though cuz if I do the Berserker, it's still going to die."
- t=655-657: taking the X-Bow lock is worth conceding the counter-push — "right there we just take our lock.
  We're very happy about it."
- t=665-666: clear the air support before the tank — "we're not going to take out the cannon cart until we
  take out the minions."
- t=669-680: the X-Bow rework rewards locking onto a tower when the opponent has no swarm, and it now hits
  an enemy Mortar harder — "that's where the expo rework is a buff in some ways as well... it just does more
  damage to the tower and more damage to like the mortar where it's just trying to take it down." He hedges:
  "I'm like fully not sure on if it's a buff or a nerf. I do think it's a buff, but I could be completely
  wrong" (t=689-696).
- t=715-722: when a lone attacker is already through, ignore it and spend the elixir on your own win
  condition — "Do I even want a Berserker? I don't think that I do... it might be better to just eat some
  damage here and just go for an Expo plus Berserker push."
- t=733-734: X-Bow with the Fireball slot is a hard counter to Mortar decks.
