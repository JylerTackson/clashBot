## Lessons stated by Ryley

All of these are balance-change opinions, not plays — this is a patch-notes video and the game is background.

- (521-534s) The Archer Queen is the headline emergency buff, and he disagrees on principle: "this really confused me because I think that the Archer Queen is actually extremely powerful."
- (539-551s) A card can be buffed without a stat change: "the archer queen got a very big indirect buff in the last balance changes cuz now with the archer queen one-shotting spirits, I do feel like that it got a big buff in like a very significant way as well."
- (551-558s) New cards buff old ones by association: "Same with like the hero musketeer, just the musketeer in general, right? When things like that come into the game, it does just inherently make the other cards better."
- (562-579s) On sizing a damage buff: "the archer queen is getting a 3% damage buff from 225 to 232, which I do think that it's not going to be that significant. I mean, maybe it changes like an interaction or two. Like for example, maybe it kills a knight in one faster shot ... which would actually be very very big for the card."
- (583-609s) His general design objection: "I just don't agree with the buff at all. I just feel like it did not need a buff in any type of way ... This is how you make cards extremely broken, right? You make them good and then you just keep on buffing them over and over and then you just end up with like a completely unbalanced unfair game."
- (617-631s) Why the buff exists at all, and why he thinks it will not work: "obviously Archer Queen is very underused. And I do think that even with the nerf to the Goblin Stein, Archer Queen would continue to be an underused card anyway. So I can't even really blame the Goblin Stein. And I guess a lot of people do use the Hero Musketeer over the Queen. So I guess that's just super soft trying to find the queen some usage."
- (641-660s) On the Little Prince: "that is the buff to the fan favorite little prince. And it just seems like it's a ritual at this point just to buff the little prince in every single balances ... Could not go a single set of balances without buffing this guy."
- (662-693s) The number, and his reaction: "the little prince will be getting a massive massive buff ... the little prince is getting his charge damage from 256 to 320. So I guess that's like the max amount of damage that he's able to do when he's completely charged up. Or maybe that's the guardian. They didn't really make it too clear. But that is a massive buff. That is 25% and a 25% buff to anything is really good."
- (693-713s) What a buffed card actually needs: "with all of the buffs it has been receiving recently, all it needs is like that one breakthrough meta, kind of like how I was talking about the Goblin Stein ... where people were just underrating the card until it got that one buff that it just absolutely needed and just made the card go absolutely crazy."
- (713-742s) He names his own bias: "I guess I don't really mind it as much cuz I'm a really big fan of the card. That's where like the personal bias comes in. But at least I am acknowledging that ... let me know what you think about the buffs to the champions, cuz I feel like these are the most controversial ones ... Personally, just don't like the queen, but I really do like the little prince."
- (754-771s) On the Electro Giant: "We are getting a damage buff to the electro giant. And really, I'm not sure why. I feel like they've been trying to buff the electro giant for a long time now. I'm not going to act like the electro giant's like an overpowered card or anything, but I do think that it's definitely an underrated card as well."
- (771-806s) Why repeat buffs to one card can miss: "They've been buffing a lot of the same cards recently. Like they buffed the E giant for like the last three balance changes now ... Now they're giving it a buff to the damage by 13%. Like 13% isn't anything crazy, but I do feel like that after all of the positive things they've given it, maybe they could slow down a little bit just to let people find the card. Like I think that sometimes right after you buff something, especially in like a meta like this, right, where everyone is using [E-Barbs], maybe it's kind of hard for Egiant to fit in a meta where the best card in the game kind of completely counters them."
- (806-822s) Closing on the Electro Giant: "I can't act like Egiant isn't like a card that's kind of bad. Maybe some people may disagree with my opinion there because obviously Eg is a really toxic and a really annoying card, but it is hard to deny that it is a strong card cuz not many people really use it."
