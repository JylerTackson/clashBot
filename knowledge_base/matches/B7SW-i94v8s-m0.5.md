- t=1391-1398: "I don't want to waste it too much, but I do want to apply some pressure, especially since I feel like he committed a lot of elixir." — pressure is timed off the opponent's elixir commitment.
- t=1424-1427: "Let's wall breakers here in the middle cuz he's going to Evo Exe. Maybe I could grab a connection cuz that's distracted." — split them centrally against a splash defender.
- t=1458-1461: "The only way we grab him is with some like pocket wall breakers and stuff when he has no [Tornado] in cycle, but even then I feel like it's going to be extremely difficult." — track the opponent's spell cycle before committing the win condition.
