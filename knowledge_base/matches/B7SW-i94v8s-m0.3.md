# Match: My MAIN Deck for Season End in Clash Royale — game 4

## Summary

Game 4 of a six-game, single-deck session. Ryley is on his season-end
[Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md) / [Wall Breakers](../cards/wall-breakers.md) bait-bridge-spam
list — Goblin Barrel, Wall Breakers, [Magic Archer](../cards/magic-archer.md),
[Berserker](../cards/berserker.md), [Bomb Tower](../cards/bomb-tower.md),
[Royal Delivery](../cards/royal-delivery.md), [Ice Spirit](../cards/ice-spirit.md) and
[Fire Spirit](../cards/fire-spirit.md) (2.5 average elixir), which the knowledge base already holds as
[Ryley Log Bait Bridge Spam](../decks/berserker-bomb-tower-fire-spirit-goblin-barrel-ice-spirit-magic-archer-royal-delivery-wall-breakers.md).
The opponent is an [X-Bow](../cards/x-bow.md) siege list built around repeated
[Elixir Collector](../cards/elixir-collector.md) — Ryley calls it a "super pump cycle" (t=763) and writes the
game off at t=765-772: "this game is um probably completely lost already, to be honest. I have no chance
against any elixir collector decks." He converts the opponent's pump tempo into free left-lane damage in
the first forty seconds (enemy left tower 4858 -> 1559 HP by t=788), then spends all of double and triple
elixir defending X-Bows with Bomb Tower plus a spirit plus Berserker while trickling barrels and Wall
Breakers back at the same tower. Regular time ends 0:00 with no tower down and both his own towers badly
chipped; in overtime the game becomes a pure race, his own towers fall to roughly 250 / 790 HP, and a Wall
Breakers plus Goblin Barrel plus Magic Archer sequence finally cracks the enemy left tower at t=1008 —
"No way. No way. YES, LET'S GO... How did I win this match up?" (t=1008-1013). Win, in triple-elixir
overtime, off the one tower he had been beating on since the opening.
