## Summary

Third game of a deck-showcase video in which every game is a different list built around the new Minion Giant; this one is the Minion Giant + **Mirror** experiment, and Ryley states the premise in the first seconds of the readable window: "because of how good the minion giant is, I wanted to try this card out with mirror. If this card passes the mirror test, that's how you know it needs a massive nerf" (514-522s). The rest of the deck is the Evo Tesla / Evo Archers 3.0-cycle shell — Tesla, Archers, Knight, Skeletons, Electro Spirit, Fireball — the same six cards as the [X-Bow HeroKnight 3.0 Cycle](../decks/archers-electro-spirit-fireball-knight-skeletons-tesla-the-log-x-bow.md) list, with Minion Giant and Mirror in place of X-Bow and The Log; he confirms both evolutions himself at 627-629s ("We have evo tesla and one archer away from evo"). The opponent plays Lava Hound beatdown with Evo Inferno Dragon, Skeleton Dragons, Valkyrie, Tombstone, Evo Zap and Fireball — Ryley reads it at 542-544s ("Looks like he's just playing a lava hound deck"). The game is a one-lane chip grind: Minion Giant goes to the bridge on the right lane over and over, the enemy right tower falls 3052 -> 258 across the readable window while Ryley never over-commits, and everything else in the hand is spent on defence against the Lava Hound and Skeleton Dragons. He wins it — "that is going to be good game ... Let's go, bro. We passed the mirror test" (666-673s) — with his own towers at 1636 and 2146 against the enemy right tower's 258 at the 0:00 buzzer.

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- 518-537s: the Mirror test as a balance heuristic — "If this card passes the mirror test, that's how you know it needs a massive nerf, man. Whenever cards are able to be used with mirror, it's like, 'Okay, this card got to go' ... only broken cards usually end up work[ing] with the mirror. Not always, but usually."
- 547-552s: play a bridge win condition as a question the opponent has to answer — "Let's go for a minion giant at the bridge as well cuz if he ignores this, it's going to do a lot of damage."
- 556-561s: value a card by what it takes away, not by what it kills — "He had to inferno dragon out. That's really good for us though. Spending that four elixir and it's not going to help support as well."
- 570-573s: re-apply the win condition the moment the counter is spent — "he didn't get the fireball down in time anyways. We can just instantly go for the minion giant at the bridge."
- 584-592s: his proposed balance change, and the reason — "It just always somehow gets that final hit. I feel like a good nerf would be like a tiny bit of HP off cuz he's just always getting that last hit."
- 615-623s: the comparison he wants viewers to take away — "It's literally exactly like the hog rider guys, except it's just even more annoying somehow. Like how do you have a card more annoying than hog rider?"
- 625-632s: stop attacking once the tower is effectively dead and bank the evolution cycle — "We just need to set up a defense. We have evo tesla and one archer away from evo, so I think we'll be completely fine, but we still need to make sure that we're locked in on defense."
- 648-661s: pre-empt the spell answer and hold a Mirror for it — "I know that he's going to take these out with either a fireball or an evo zap or a fireball ... then we can just mirror our fireball and we'll be completely fine."
