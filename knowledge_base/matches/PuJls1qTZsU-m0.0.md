## Summary

Game 1 of a seven-game session on what Ryley calls an "anti-meta counter Piggies
Lightning deck": Royal Hogs, Lightning, Furnace, Berserker, Cannon, Skeletons,
Electro Spirit and Barbarian Barrel (3.0 average elixir). He runs it as a cycle
deck — a cheap defensive core of Cannon plus 1-elixir Skeletons and Electro
Spirit, Berserker as the 2-elixir melee answer, Furnace for passive chip and
swarm cover, and Royal Hogs plus Lightning as the win package. The opponent is
"a different variation of Piggies Lightning" (t=47s): their own Royal Hogs
alongside Bomb Tower, Graveyard, Poison, Goblinstein ("the Stein" / "the
scientist"), Evolved Archers and Ice Spirit. Nothing landed in regular time
beyond chip — Ryley's left tower fell to 4004 from an early Graveyard he was
caught out by (t=82-132s), the opponent's left tower to 4435 from one Lightning
(t=69.6s) — and the game went to overtime with both left towers still up.
Overtime turned into a pure left-lane race: Ryley cycled Royal Hogs at the left
bridge four times (t≈160, 178, 202, 265s) with Lightning behind them to clear
the Bomb Tower and the Archers, while defending Graveyard-plus-Goblinstein
pushes with Cannon, Skeletons, Barbarian Barrel and Electro Spirit. His left
tower dropped to 856 but the enemy left tower fell at roughly t=283s: "Two
amazingly timed Royal Hogs to secure the win" (t=280s). Result: win in overtime.

- t=27-32s: "it's impossible for a deck to have no bad matchups, but this one has
  a very, very limited amount" — the deck is pitched as an anti-meta answer deck,
  not a hard counter to everything.

  there" — on being surprised by the Graveyard, and the value of knowing the
  opponent's cards.
- t=106-112s: "with this deck, not only are you able to outplay every matchup,
  but usually there's just always some type of opening."
- t=115-119s: do not spend Lightning on a single Bomb Tower — "I'm not going to
  lightning though. I do think that lightning would not be the greatest play."
- t=137-141s: "I'm not going to play them yet, though. Don't want to be going for
  them too randomly" — hold the evolved Royal Hogs for a real opening.
- t=154-160s: "I didn't want a piggies before obviously because we would have
  been going into things" — do not send hogs while you still have to defend.
- t=171-176s: play Furnace early when the opponent has just used Poison and it is

- t=178-180s: "Let's go for another set of piggies right away. Don't want to give
  him a chance" — keep the pressure on immediately after they spend on defence.
- t=223-225s: same principle again — "I think I'm going to furnace preemptively

- t=252-257s: count the opponent's building — "he can't afford bomb tower, guys...
  The piggies are going to land on the tower."
