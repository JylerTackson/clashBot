# Match: This Mega Knight Bait Deck is EXTREMELY BROKEN!! — game 3

## Summary

Ryley pilots the video's single list — [Mega Knight](../cards/mega-knight.md) (Evolution),
[Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md) (Evolution), [Princess](../cards/princess.md),
[Dart Goblin](../cards/dart-goblin.md), [Ice Spirit](../cards/ice-spirit.md),
[Suspicious Bush](../cards/suspicious-bush.md), [Skeleton Army](../cards/skeleton-army.md) and the
[Heroic Berserker](../heroes/berserker-hero.md) ([Berserker](../cards/berserker.md) with the 3-elixir
"Savage Survival" ability) — and names the archetype himself at t=524: "I didn't expect mega knight log
bait to be this strong, but it's working, man." That reads as [bait](../archetypes/bait.md): three of the
cheap threats (Goblin Barrel, Princess, Dart Goblin, Skeleton Army) all want the same small spell.
The opponent, "Abdu", is a 2.6 Hog [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md) deck with a Hero
[Musketeer](../cards/musketeer.md) — Ryley calls it at t=506-514 ("2.6 is going to be a pretty rough one,
I feel like, cuz I'm not really able to kill the musketeer, and he has a super super quick cycle") — i.e.
the shell of [Hog HeroMusk 2.6 Cycle](../decks/cannon-fireball-hog-rider-ice-golem-ice-spirit-musketeer-skeletons-the-log.md).
The match is a chip race that neither tower race wins in regular time: Ryley's right tower is ground down
from 4858 to 1816 by Hog Rider hits and repeated ~237-275 HP spell/ability chips (clock 1:46 through 0:23),
while he answers with Berserker-at-the-bridge plus the Hero ability (enemy right 4858 -> 2516 between clock
1:35 and 1:25) and a late Mega Knight + Princess + Evo Barrel stack that leaves the enemy right tower at
267 HP as regular time expires at clock 0:03. It goes to overtime and he finishes it: "OH MY GOD. NICE.
Let's go. Good game. Beautiful win. Wow, that was not easy, guys, but we got it" (t=696-701).
