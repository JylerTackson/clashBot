## Lessons stated by Ryley

This game is narration of the balance changes, not coaching; the only in-match line is the last one.

- t=799-813 — Barbarians: "the barbarians hit points are getting buffed by 4% but in return the evolved
  Barbarians hit point bonus is going from 10% to 0%. So now the regular Barbs and the Evo Barbs will have
  the same HP. I am definitely a fan of that. I don't really know why they had different HPs anyway."
- t=823-838 — "the evolved Barbs rage duration is also getting a buff. Your Barbs will now be raged for five
  seconds instead of three seconds, which I think is a pretty fair buff. I did feel like that the rage from
  the Barbs went away a little bit quickly anyway."
- t=846-852 — on the size of the change: "I know the goal was to buff them overall cuz Barbs are not like a
  very used card but still seems pretty big. I'm all right with it though."
- t=854-873 — Goblins and Hero Goblins: "they are nerfing the Hero Goblins, making it so every time you pop
  the ability instead of getting three Goblins, you will now get two. But it will only take one second for
  the Goblins to deploy now. So the Goblins, even though there's less of them, they will be coming out
  quicker. So you have to respond to them faster."
- t=875-891 — "the Goblin damage overall all across the board is getting a 4% buff. So that applies to Goblin
  Barrel, Goblin Drill, and all those types of cards that just have any type of Goblins like Goblin Gang. And
  then even the Evo Mortar Goblin as well will do a little bit more damage."
- t=892-905 — his verdict on the patch as a whole: "so many balance changes this season but I can't say that
  I'm not a fan of it... I do feel like that we were long overdue for a massive set of balance changes."
- t=906-923 — Evolved Archers: "there is a nerf to the evolved archers. Evolved archers have been super
  popular so I think this is a pretty solid nerf right here... their power shot damage is going down by 17%,
  which I say is pretty sizable. Will change quite a few interactions."
- t=928-946 — Evolved Snowball: "the roll range is now 4.5 to 4. So the Evo snowball just won't be grabbing
  as many units and throwing them as far back as it was before, which I definitely think is a very good
  change cuz the Evo snowball was kind of just like a ridiculous card anyway in my opinion."
- t=958-987 — Evolved Goblin Barrel: "the evolved Goblin Barrel is getting a decoy damage nerf for 26% of its
  damage which I think is quite a lot. But I also think that it is quite deserved especially considering that
  basically the Goblin Barrel got a pretty decent buff with the damage. So they're just kind of returning the
  favor by nerfing the decoy." He adds that it makes him sad but "I would be straight lying if I said it
  wasn't extremely extremely deserved" (t=961-966) and jokes about banning it: "maybe I'll have to boss ban
  it to this list" (t=966-969).
- t=953-956 — the only in-match remark: "Hold on guys. I'm actually having trouble yapping about the balance
  changes while defending here."
