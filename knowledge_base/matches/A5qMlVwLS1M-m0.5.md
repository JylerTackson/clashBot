# Ryley's "8 best cards" deck vs an Evo Inferno Dragon Balloon cycle (game 6 of 7)

## Summary

Ryley is running the single deck the whole video is built around — Barbarian Barrel, Battle Ram, Cannon Cart, Elite Barbarians, Goblinstein, Heal Spirit, Mother Witch and Zap — against a Balloon cycle player he introduces as "one of the best balloon cycle players in Clash Royale" (t=1138). The opponent's list shows Balloon, an Evolved Inferno Dragon, Goblin Cage, Ice Golem, Mega Minion, Miner, Fireball and Zap. Ryley states the matchup problem in the first fifteen seconds — "we don't really have that much air defense in these types of games. Without much air defense, you have to be really, really smart offensively" (t=1148-1152) — and plays accordingly: he never builds a slow push, he alternates bridge threats between lanes (Elite Barbarians left at clock 1:30, Goblinstein left at 1:05, Battle Ram right at 1:01), and he uses Mother Witch as the answer to the Mega Minion and the swarm-turner behind everything. That chip works: the enemy left tower steps down 4858 -> 4245 -> 3023 -> 2159 -> 1253 HP across the game, and he never gets a crown for it. The Balloon does. At clock 0:32 a Balloon reaches his left lane at tile (3, 7-8) and the left-tower HP slot jumps from 2716 to a king-tower-sized 6672, i.e. that Princess Tower fell; he finishes the game hunting a last Elite Barbarians connection that does not land — "The Earb. The Eb. The Earb. Ah, rip. GG's, man" (t=1299-1300). His own verdict: "I feel like this just balloon fireball with the cage. It's just so impossible to break through, man" (t=1303-1307).

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=1148-1155 (clock 2:39-2:31): a deck without air defence has to win on offence, not defence — "we don't really have that much air defense in these types of games. Without much air defense, you have to be really, really smart offensively. Otherwise, you just get completely destroyed by like these random pushes."
- t=1159-1164 (clock 2:26-2:23): do not send Elite Barbarians at the bridge into a deck holding Ice Golem — "I can't go at the bridge cuz then he could kite with the ice golem. I just have to make sure that I'm putting them in the back here."
- t=1167-1169 (clock 2:20): attack the moment the opponent's damage is out of cycle — "We can just go for a goblin[stein] at the bridge here. He has no DPS in cycle."
- t=1186-1191 (clock 2:02-1:58): pair the Mother Witch with an opposite-lane Battle Ram so the opponent cannot afford to over-defend either — "we can go for the battle [ram] at the bridge with it as well because he has to be careful here to not let that battle [ram] get too much damage."
- t=1193-1197 (clock 1:53-1:51): a cheap defensive card that forces a spell is a win because it protects the expensive one — "Probably has to fireball and then we can just keep this mother witch alive which is very nice."
- t=1233-1237 (clock 1:12-1:10): place the Mother Witch against an ability card so that using the ability is itself a bad play — "the mother witch on the mega minion, but I want to do it to where like if he pops the ability, it just wouldn't be a good play."

- t=1254-1257 (clock 0:53-0:48): count forced Elixir as damage even when the push is stopped — "He's forcing out all the elixir which is not bad."
- t=1265-1271 (clock 0:42-0:37): commit into a Fireball you have already predicted rather than hold — "I know he's probably going to fireball, but it's all right. I think even if he fireballs."
