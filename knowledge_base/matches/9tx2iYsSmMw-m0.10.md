## Summary

Last clip of the video: CRL broadcast footage of game three of a series Ryley describes as "the final match here. Winner takes it all. Loser goes home with nothing" (t=2455-2458). Ryley is the **bottom ("own") player** in this clip — his commentary ("I went for the royal ghost at the bridge", "I lost half of my tower just misclicking", "I went for the Evo Ram") lines up with the bottom-side deploys and with the bottom towers' HP drops — but the perception pipeline's side heuristic split his deck in half, filing everything he dropped at or past the river (rows >= 15) as "opponent" plays. Reassembled from the deploy labels plus commentary, his deck is a bridge-spam list built as a bait counter: Battle Ram, Elite Barbarians, Mother Witch, Royal Ghost, Valkyrie (the Heroic Valkyrie — he pops Wild Whirlwind at t=2606), Zappies, Heal Spirit and Arrows. It is the same family as his Bandit/Freeze bridge-spam list, with Royal Ghost and Arrows in those two slots.

He states the deck choice explicitly: "me and my coach actually chose this deck, assuming that he could go for a log bait deck" (t=2464-2468), "here we went for a deck with arrows just to have a good chance. We even had mother witch, valky, ghost. We just really needed as many bait counters as possible in this matchup" (t=2488-2496). The opponent is on log bait — Skeleton Army (an Evolution, per "the Evo Skarmy"), Princess, Dart Goblin, Dark Prince and a Valkyrie he had already spent.

The readable middle of the clip is Ryley ahead on plan: a bridge Royal Ghost cleans up the Dart Goblin, a Mother Witch dropped on top of the Skeleton Army converts it into Cursed Hogs, and Arrows behind it buys "an early lead". A Heal Spirit then saves his whole left side ("I took zero damage on the left side", t=2538). The one clear mistake is a misclicked Battle Ram kite at t=2564 that costs him roughly half of his right tower (3052 -> 1613 HP). He answers with Elite Barbarians at the bridge purely to disrupt cycle, kills the Dark Prince with them, then builds the Evo Battle Ram + Valkyrie + Heal Spirit + Arrows push he says took "the entire tower in one push". The clip cuts mid-sentence at t=2614, so the game result is not shown.

Cards evidenced: **Skeleton Army** (opened with it — "he starts off with the skeleton army at the bridge", t=2462 — and later an Evolution: "he did a really nice play with the Evo Skarmy", t=2527), **Princess** (arena tracks at [7, 24] t=2472.5, [4, 20] t=2484.5, [15, 21] t=2592.6), **Dart Goblin** (tracks at [6, 25] t=2492.5, [6, 26] t=2522.5, [14, 22] t=2604.6), **Dark Prince** and a **Valkyrie** he had already spent ("he had a dark prince over the valkyrie, because he had already used the valkyrie", t=2502-2509).

How Ryley attacked it: he loaded the deck with splash and swarm answers ("we just really needed as many bait counters as possible in this matchup", t=2493), used Royal Ghost at the bridge to force out a Dark Prince rather than to hit the tower, dropped Mother Witch directly onto the Skeleton Army to convert it into Cursed Hogs, and used Arrows both to open a damage lead and to close the last push. His stated read on the Dark Prince — that the opponent hated spending it on the Royal Ghost, and that he then removed it with the Elite Barbarians — is what let him commit to the Evo Battle Ram push at the end.

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- t=2464-2468 — deck choice is a read on the opponent's tendencies: "me and my coach actually chose this deck, assuming that he could go for a log bait deck."
- t=2488-2496 — against bait, stack answers: "here we went for a deck with arrows just to have a good chance. We even had mother witch, valky, ghost. We just really needed as many bait counters as possible in this matchup."
- t=2496-2500 — bridge units can be aimed at a card, not a tower: "I went for the royal ghost at the bridge, making sure to take out the dark goblin."
