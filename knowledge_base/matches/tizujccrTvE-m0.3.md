## Lessons stated by Ryley

- 558-563s: a Cannoneer tower troop plus the Evolved Goblin Barrel puts the defender "in a complete scramble to defend" — the tower troop does enough on its own that his own cards stay free for the barrel plan.
- 567-571s: place the Magic Archer on the side of your own half "so they can't activate the king tower off of it. Just makes life a little bit easier."
- 579-583s: taking a chunk of tower damage early is not a reason to change plan — "That's just a bit of damage. It's not like the end of the world."
- 585-594s: a Cannon is a bad answer to a Goblin Barrel plus Wall Breakers in one lane — "The cannon here is going to sell him very hard. He has to go for like yep, a building plus spear goblins ... The cannon here is doing absolutely nothing for defense."
- 596-599s: the deck's cycle advantage is conditional on rationing the 3-elixir defensive spell — "Our cycle should be a bit quicker than him as long as we don't use the royal [delivery] too often."
- 601-605s: "I don't want to Tesla the wall breakers too often, but I feel like recently I've been a bit forced to" — spending the building on a 2-elixir card is a concession he tries to avoid.
- 610-613s: when the opponent holds The Log, prefer Royal Delivery over Fire Spirit — "He could even try like a pre-log on the fire spirit, so I think I'll rather delivery on top."
- 636-638s: split the Wall Breakers on defence to absorb a charge attack — "We can split our wall breakers to take one of the dark prince charges."
