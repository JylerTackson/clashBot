# Match: EMERGENCY Balance Changes Announced!! — game 4 (last game of the video)

## Summary

This is the closing game of a balance-change commentary video, and it is the one Ryley explicitly says he
will play out to the end: "I will finish out this match here though, just for you guys, who are trying to
see me play with some of the new cards" (t=950-955). The deck read from his own deploy labels and HUD is
[Electro Giant](../cards/electro-giant.md), [Bowler](../cards/bowler.md),
[Baby Dragon](../cards/baby-dragon.md) (he calls it "the Evo baby dragon", t=1005),
[Tornado](../cards/tornado.md), [Void](../cards/void.md), [Golden Knight](../cards/golden-knight.md),
[Goblin Hut](../cards/goblin-hut.md) and [Barbarian Barrel](../cards/barbarian-barrel.md) — a
[beatdown](../archetypes/beatdown.md) shell with a heavy [control](../archetypes/control.md) spine
(Tornado + Bowler + Baby Dragon behind a 7-elixir tank, with a hut for passive chip). This video is a
deck showcase, so this list is the per-game read only; the other games in the video use different decks.
The opponent plays a fast [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md) deck built around
[Mighty Miner](../cards/mighty-miner.md) with its ability, [Hog Rider](../cards/hog-rider.md),
Evo [Firecracker](../cards/firecracker.md), [Tesla](../cards/tesla.md) and
[Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md) — Ryley reads it out loud repeatedly ("I do definitely think he has the
slight edge having the quick cycle firecracker", t=975-978). Regulation ends 0-0 and the game goes to a full
two minutes of overtime; Ryley keeps stacking Electro Giant pushes but never breaks a tower, while the
Mighty Miner and Firecracker grind his right tower and eventually his King down (own king 6714 -> 2775,
own right 4858 -> 2555, enemy towers still at 4386 and 4272 at the final readable frame). He calls it
himself at t=1094-1099: "Nah, this is good game. Not really much that we could have done. Oh, we're going to
get three crowned, bro." Most of the talk over the first ninety seconds is not about the game at all — it is
his (very negative) verdict on the announced Void buff.
