# Match: This Hero Berserker Deck has a 100% WIN Rate — game 4 of 7

[Berserker](../cards/berserker.md) list: [Miner](../cards/miner.md),

video-level consensus list holds for this game. The opponent — "Doodle Bob" (t=527) — is a fast
cycle deck built around [Goblin Drill](../cards/goblin-drill.md) and a
[Furnace](../cards/furnace.md), backed by [Cannon](../cards/cannon.md),
[Giant Snowball](../cards/giant-snowball.md), [Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md),
[Bats](../cards/bats.md) and a Hero [Valkyrie](../heroes/valkyrie-hero.md); Ryley reads it early —
"It looks like this guy's a super quick cycle goblin drill deck" (t=550) — and immediately calls the
matchup bad for him: "he just has so much answers for our bait units" (t=602-603). The middle of the
game goes exactly that way: his [Bomb Tower](../cards/bomb-tower.md) at [3, 9] draws a Hero Valkyrie
whose ability tears the left tower from 4786 down to 3312 HP between t=616 and t=620 ("Yo, Valk,
don't pop the ability on me. No, no, no, no... Dude, that card is also something else, man",
t=611-615). He adapts by moving the building behind the tower ("Maybe we bomb tower behind it and
then we can get good value like that just to not give many lineups to the tower", t=628-632), keeps
recycling Miner + Bats + Wall Breakers + Spear Goblins into the far lane, and a Poison on the Furnace
plus the chip puts him ahead — "And just like that, we're actually in the damage lead now" (t=666).
Regulation ends level ("And we actually survived", t=693) and he wins in overtime: another Miner /
Wall Breakers / Bats / Berserker rotation grinds the enemy left tower from 2964 HP (t=720) to 190
(t=730) and down, with the Berserker's Hero ability popped on a defending Valkyrie at the end — "I'm
just going to pop the ability. I think we take tower. Let's go. Beautiful win, guys. This deck is so
good, man" (t=730-733).
