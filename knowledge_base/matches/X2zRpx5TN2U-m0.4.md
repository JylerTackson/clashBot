## Lessons stated by Ryley

- 814-821s: answer a back-line tank with bridge pressure, not with defence — "Let's go for the Rune Giant at the bridge here. And then we can go for the E-Barbs as well. I do want to be really aggressive right now cuz I just need to take this Giant out."
- 824-830s: the Berserker Hero ability is a resource to hold, not to spend on sight — "we can go for the Berserker in front as well just to try to get the ability if we need it. I don't think we're going to need it actually."
- 841-846s: judge a push by what it already extracted, not by whether the units survive — "if we just let the Mother Witch die, then we're just four elixir down. But at least that way we get a lot of value out of the cards that we already placed."
- 847-856s: against a Giant deck whose plan is to answer every card with the Giant, take the bridge trade — "He wants to go Giant whenever you play a card and just completely take it out. But if we can win the bridge battle like that, it's usually going to reward us pretty nicely."
- 870-875s: choose the Rune Giant's enchant target deliberately, and revise it between games — "I'm going to boost my E-Barbs with the Rune Giant right away this time. Last time I boosted the Mother Witch first."
- 884-886s: spend the Heal Spirit to stop a push being erased before it connects — "Just make sure that my E-Barbs don't instantly get taken off the field."
- 890-903s: when you are behind on defensive DPS, plan to trade towers rather than to hold — "he has a little bit more DPS than me just cuz his Giant has more HP than the Rune Giant ... We're just going to have to get like one or two good counter pushes that end up taking his whole tower cuz we're probably not going to be able to hold on for the entire game."
- 910-913s: pre-place at the bridge against a predictable back-line tank — "he's might [going to] Giant at the back. So, I'm going to E-Barbs predicting the Giant."
- 944-948s: time the finisher spell off the enemy tower troop's ammo — "Evo zap here as well cuz the dagger doesn't have ammo."
- 953-958s: an opponent's ability spend is your window, and refusing to trade abilities is the point — "because he goes for the ability, then we can just go for the E-Barbs. He was hoping that we would ability so then we wouldn't have any elixir for defense."
- 966-970s: read the opponent's cycle before judging his placement — "I knew that he went for a bad giant cuz he didn't have his dark prince back in cycle yet and that's like his main defense."
