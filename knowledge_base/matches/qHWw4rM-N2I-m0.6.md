## Lessons stated by Ryley

- 1360-1366s — own up to speculative plays: "He will get a counter push off this but I just wanted to see if that was worth it to be honest. It probably wasn't but if it works out then it'd be funny."
- 1371-1372s — "Okay, let's Battle Ram kite this" — the Battle Ram is a defensive kiting tool, not only a bridge threat.
- 1375-1377s — let the opponent's own unit do the work: "The Mother Witch can take itself out cuz it's actually tanking right now."

- 1404-1412s — a bridge Bandit is for forcing a card, not for damage: "I want him to use a set of zappies if possible or a mother witch is good too for me."
- 1414-1419s — don't ram-predict into a spell you expect: "He might go for a vine so... let's not ram predict it. There's no need to do that, I think."
- 1441-1449s — don't burn your defensive card on offence: "I'm not going to go zappies yet... I didn't want to do like a bad zappies where then he can just ram opposite lane."
- 1455-1462s — commit to one tower: "I do just want to make sure that I take out this entire right side tower... if he goes for the zappies then I think we could just go for the valkyrie plus ability here at the bridge."
- 1480-1483s — information is a resource: "We do have a freeze... I don't think he knows that cuz if I remember correctly we haven't played the freeze yet."
- 1487-1495s — kiting doubles up: "Let's ram kite this ghost cuz then it will help defend the wizard as well cuz it's distracting everything."
- 1517-1521s — hold the hero for defence when you are behind: "Do I want this? No, I don't. I need the Valkyrie in the pocket then pop the ability that's what I need to do."
