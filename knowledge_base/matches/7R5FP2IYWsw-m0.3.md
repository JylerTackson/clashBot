# Match: Classic Log Bait RETURNS to Clash Royale!! — game 4

## Summary

Game 4 of a six-game single-deck session on the classic log bait list: [Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md),
[Princess](../cards/princess.md), [Goblin Gang](../cards/goblin-gang.md), [Knight](../cards/knight.md),
[Ice Spirit](../cards/ice-spirit.md), [Inferno Tower](../cards/inferno-tower.md), [Rocket](../cards/rocket.md)
and [The Log](../cards/the-log.md), with the Goblin Barrel and Princess both on evolution ("Let's evo
princess", t=897; "now we can go for the evo barrel here", t=913). The opponent is a Hog Rider cycle deck that
Ryley reads off the first two cards he sees — "He has a mighty minor and a log... Probably going to be a hog
rider deck. I'm just confused by the mighty minor. I feel like no deck other than a hog rider right now really
uses mighty minor" (t=820-834) — and the rest of the game confirms it: [Hog Rider](../cards/hog-rider.md),
[Mighty Miner](../cards/mighty-miner.md), Evo [Firecracker](../cards/firecracker.md),
[Cannon](../cards/cannon.md), [Earthquake](../cards/earthquake.md), [The Log](../cards/the-log.md),
[Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md) and [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md). The pattern of the match
is that Ryley defends the Hog + Mighty Miner + Firecracker traffic cleanly (Inferno Tower, Knight, Goblin Gang,
Ice Spirit, and one defensive Rocket at t=963) but cannot convert: the opponent holds Electro Spirit and The
Log for every barrel, so his chip crawls while the Hog and the Firecracker keep landing shots. Regular time
ends with no crown and the game runs deep into double- then triple-elixir overtime, where he spends Rockets on
the tower (t=1048, t=1069, t=1098-1102) and still cannot break through. He calls it himself at t=1095 ("Oh,
GG's") and t=1106 ("I think we lost anyway though"), closing with the thesis of the video: "with the log it's
so bad man. It's just so hard to break through" (t=1111-1114).
