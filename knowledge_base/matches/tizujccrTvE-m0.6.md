## Summary

Game 6 of an eight-game single-deck session, and by far the longest of the video (252 readable seconds, all the way into double-elixir overtime). Ryley is on the session deck: **Ronin, Goblin Barrel, Wall Breakers, Magic Archer (Hero, 'Triple Threat'), Tesla, Royal Delivery, Skeletons, Fire Spirit** — 2.9 average elixir, two cheap win conditions that both bait small spells, and a single defensive building. The opponent is on a cheap **Balloon / Miner cycle**: Ryley names the Miner at 1116s, "minor balloon with the Evo musketeer" at 1125s, Barbarian Barrel at 1137s, Bomb Tower at 1162s, Giant Snowball at 1268s and an Ice Golem at 1279s (the closest knowledge-base deck is [EvoMusk Miner 2.9 Cycle](../decks/balloon-barbarian-barrel-bomb-tower-giant-snowball-ice-golem-miner-musketeer-skeletons.md), which matches every card he names except a one-off "e-spirit" call at 1143s).

The whole game is one plan stated out loud at 1158-1202s: "I think I can force out the bomb tower here. If I can keep forcing out more and more elixir from him… I just want to keep on forcing out more and more elixir to not give him any opportunities to attack me early on, cuz in double elixir we'll have more elixir to spam those Teslas down." He runs Wall Breakers, Goblin Barrel and the occasional Ronin at the bridge on a near-permanent loop, defends every Balloon/Miner/Evo-Musketeer package with Tesla + Skeletons + Royal Delivery, and never lets the opponent bank a push.

It works: the opponent's right Crown Tower is ground from full down to **3374 HP by 1:40 in regulation, ~1744 by 0:20, and 101 HP by 1:26 in overtime**, while Ryley's own left tower is never scratched (4858 HP at the final frame) and his right tower only ever drops to 3041. From overtime 1:22 he is openly counting the finish — "This guy can't do anything, even with the balloon in the back. It just cooked for him, cuz we just need one more magic archer shot or one more barrel connection" (1320-1324s) — and closes it with a deliberately offset "trick barrel" at overtime 0:50, saying "GG's. That was actually pretty intense, man. This guy did not play bad, I feel like" (1358-1362s) before the next game starts. Recorded as a **win** (overtime tower kill on the right lane).
