## Summary

Game five of the single-deck session, and Ryley is on the same 2.5-elixir Hog cycle list all video: Hog Rider, Evolved Elite Barbarians, Evolved Firecracker, Ice Golem, Electro Spirit, Skeletons, Ice Spirit and The Log. Every card in it is deploy-label confirmed in this game except Ice Spirit, which is HUD-confirmed twice.

The opponent is an air-heavy spam deck with no clear win condition. Mega Knight, Inferno Dragon, Baby Dragon, Flying Machine and Bandit are all confirmed on the field, and Ryley reads it as "some type of bridge spam deck" at 1001s, then keeps complaining about the shape of it: "Why does he have so many air cards? He hasn't even played a win condition. He's just spamming us with air" (1152-1157s). Because The Log and Skeletons are dead weight against half of that deck, his only real answer to air is the Firecracker, and he says so: "Pretty hard to defend against a bunch of air cards perfectly whenever I only have a firecracker for defense and he has triple air cards for some reason" (1134-1139s).

The game is decided by two things he sets up early. First, a king tower activation in the opening 20 seconds: Skeletons at [10, 8] to kite the Baby Dragon into his king's range, which lands ("Beautiful king activation", 993s) — his king HP first reads at 994s. Second, a policy of never letting the opponent bank: from 1105s onward he repeatedly plays Ice Golem and cheap cycle to bait the Mega Knight out at the bridge, on the theory that "if he Mega Knights the bridge, right, he's just not able to build up a big push, and if he's not able to build up a big push then I don't think he'll be able to break through" (1107-1113s).

Regulation ends 1-1 on damage rather than crowns: the enemy right tower is worn down from 4858 to 1673 HP by clock 0:15, while Ryley's own right tower is chipped from 4858 to 2528 by the air spam over the same window, and he survives a Mega Knight at the bridge at clock 0:03 with a last-second Elite Barbarians and Firecracker ("Oh my god, we barely got those things down, man. That was clutch", 1147-1149s). Overtime is a clean Hog cycle kill: Hog Rider at the bridge plus Evo Firecracker plus a pre-emptive Log, an Elite Barbarians drop in the opposite lane to overload his defence, then Log and Skeletons to clear the Goblins on the last shot — enemy right tower goes 1054 -> 547 HP and the game ends on "Come on, one more. Bang, let's go. Good game. Beautiful win" (1199-1201s).

## Lessons stated by Ryley

- 986-991s (clock 2:41-2:38): use a cheap distraction to pull a splash unit into king range on purpose — "let's go for the skeletons here just to kite the baby dragon away. I do want to try to activate king tower here off of that baby dragon."
- 997-1003s (clock 2:28-2:23): against bridge spam, an early king activation is worth going out of your way for — "Activating king early will be very nice here... It looks like he's playing some type of bridge spam deck. So having the king up will be really beneficial."
- 1007-1016s (clock 2:19-2:11): do not send the Hog into the opposite lane when the enemy Inferno Dragon is on the board and your cycle to an answer is bad — "Normally I would hog rider opposite lane, but because of the inferno dragon being there and not having a good cycle for it... I did not want to hog rider immediately just to be safe."
- 1039-1043s (clock 1:49-1:43): hold your only splash answer when the opponent might have a Balloon, and use their non-punish of your cycle as evidence — "I'm going to save my firecracker, but the thing is, if he had balloon I feel like he would have already used it when I cycled my firecracker."
- 1069-1080s (clock 1:16-1:09): the Evo Elite Barbarians spear throw has a range and does not always fire before contact — "they didn't get time to throw... for some reason, you know, the e-barbs can't always throw. I mean obviously there is a range on it."
- 1105-1113s (clock 0:42-0:35): force the Mega Knight to be spent at the bridge on defence — "just to force him to go for the mega knight, cuz if he mega knights the bridge, right, he's just not able to build up a big push, and if he's not able to build up a big push then I don't think he'll be able to break through."
- 1164-1168s (overtime, clock ~1:49): pre-log the defence you expect rather than reacting to it — "I'm going to pre-log as well cuz he's either going to Goblins or Mega Knight, but regardless that will ensure us to grab a shot unless he overspends."
- 1179-1181s (overtime, clock ~1:28): track the opponent's spell availability before committing the Firecracker — "He can't buy Zap for our Firecracker. That's still a shot on the tower."
- 1188-1192s (overtime, clock 1:20): a bridge play in the opposite lane is an elixir problem for the opponent, not a damage play — "I'm going to E-Barbs here at the bridge opposite lane cuz if he goes for the Mega Knight, then he can't defend the Hog Rider."
