## Lessons stated by Ryley

- 355-360s: the pitch for the archetype itself — "with 2.0 log bait cycle ... if you only lose one game then you get a 98% win rate which is kind of crazy" (he is 1 loss deep on a run to Ultimate Champion).
- 393-400s: when your deck has no spell for the opponent's key cards, lean on cycle volume instead of trying to answer them cleanly — "I don't really have a spell to take out the mother witch or the zappies but because of how crazy our spam is in this deck, we could actually have just a really good sequence randomly."
- 376-383s: against a hero, place your own hero as a distraction body and pop the ability immediately, because you expect the opponent to pop theirs — "we have to get an immediate ability cuz I know he's going to pop his ability so mine just needs to be there to keep the wizard distracted."
- 420-424s: keeping the Berserker ability unspent is itself defence — "We have the ability if he goes for like a mother witch or something or even like a ghost."
- 425-428s: and spend it the moment it converts into a tower — "I'm going to pop my ability here cuz this will be tower down."
- 464-483s: hold the Evolution Dart Goblin in cycle for the target that matters rather than firing it into the current push — "I'm just saving my Evo Dark Goblin in cycle so I don't waste it here ... we can go for the berserker in the back to just making sure that we save out our Dark Goblin for the right moment."
- 385-388s: watch what your Dart Goblin gives away — a stray shot at the wrong moment hands the opponent a King Tower activation.
- 409-413s: defending both lanes with one well-placed mid-board unit is the cheap way out of a split push — "We'll defend both lanes which is nice."
