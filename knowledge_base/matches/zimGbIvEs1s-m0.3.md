Fourth game of a six-game single-deck session (video 684.9s-978.0s), joined at clock 2:52 and run through
regulation, a 1-1-less draw and a full two-minute overtime. Ryley is on the video's consensus Hog cycle list:

misreads, see Data gaps.) It is a [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md) deck with a heavy
[bridge-spam](../archetypes/bridge-spam.md) second win condition — 1-elixir spirits and Skeletons rotate him
back to a 4-elixir Hog while 6-elixir Elite Barbarians go to the bridge whenever the opponent is short.
The opponent is a [Graveyard](../cards/graveyard.md) [control](../archetypes/control.md) deck that Ryley

[Golden Knight](../cards/golden-knight.md), [Executioner](../cards/executioner.md) and
