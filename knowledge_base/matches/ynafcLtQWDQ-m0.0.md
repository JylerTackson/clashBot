# Match: I Got a 98% WIN Rate with this Royal Hogs Deck — game 1

## Summary

Game 1 of a five-game single-deck session. Ryley names the deck in the first ten seconds — "we are going to
be using the Royal Hogs deck that got me a 98% win rate in Clash Royale" (t=7-13) — and every one of the
eight cards is confirmed by a deploy label in this game: [Royal Hogs](../cards/royal-hogs.md) (Evo),
[Tesla](../cards/tesla.md) (Evo), [Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md),
[Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md), [Berserker](../cards/berserker.md) (the
[Heroic Berserker](../heroes/berserker-hero.md) — he calls the ability twice),
[Barbarian Barrel](../cards/barbarian-barrel.md), [Furnace](../cards/furnace.md) and
[Lightning](../cards/lightning.md). At 2.9 average elixir it is a [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md) list, the
Tesla version of the knowledge base's
[Piggies Lightning Ryley](../decks/barbarian-barrel-berserker-cannon-electro-spirit-furnace-lightning-royal-hogs-skeletons.md)
(same eight cards with Tesla in the Cannon slot). The opponent he first reads as "some type of Goblin Drill
Giant Skeleton deck" (t=39-48); what actually shows up is [Giant Skeleton](../cards/giant-skeleton.md),
[Balloon](../cards/balloon.md), an evolved [Bomber](../cards/bomber.md), [Cannon](../cards/cannon.md) and
Lightning, plus single low-confidence reads of [Princess](../cards/princess.md) and
[Bats](../cards/bats.md) — a [control](../archetypes/control.md)-shaped deck with Balloon as the win
condition. The match is decided by defence: a Giant Skeleton bomb takes his left tower from 4858 to 3317 at
t=62-66 ("that's kind of what happens when you get surprise factored sometimes, but it's not the end of the
world"), and from there he never loses another tower while chipping the enemy right tower with Royal Hogs
sets and Furnace spirits. Regular time ends 0-0 on crowns with the enemy right tower at 2633; in overtime he
keeps stacking Hogs, Berserker and Furnace on the right and finishes at t=257-260 with Lightning into the
defending Bomber — "We got the E-spirit on the tower and that's good game. Let's go. Very nice win to start
off the video" (t=257-262). Result: win.

## How Ryley uses his cards

- **[Royal Hogs](../cards/royal-hogs.md)** (5) — the only offence, always at the right bridge, never with a
  tank in front: [15, 13]/[15, 15] at t=77-83, again at t=119 (label [15, 15]), t=148, t=202.2, t=232.2 and
  t=248.2. Every set is stated as pressure taken *while* defending, not as a committed push: "let's Evo
  Piggies here to pressure while defending cuz I do feel like he could have like a lightning" (t=119-124),
  "Let's go for the piggies now. Pressure while defending" (t=249). The first set took the enemy right tower
  4858 -> 3955 on its own.
- **[Tesla](../cards/tesla.md)** (4, Evo) — the main defensive building, placed centre-ish on his own half and
  re-placed constantly: [9, 11] (t=34), [7, 9] (t=84.1), [17, 8] (t=121.1), [9, 9] (t=159.2), [10, 13]
  (t=176.2), [7, 9] (t=191.2), [6, 7] (t=210.2), [9, 13] (t=224.2). He calls it out for the big-unit defence
  at t=159 ("Let's go for the Evo Tesla here... We don't want to let that big guy reach our Tesla either") and
  explicitly declines it when the lane is already handled: "there's no point to even Tesla. I'll just
  berserker here" (t=226-227).
- **[Furnace](../cards/furnace.md)** (4) — played five times, four of them on his own back line ([9, 10]
  t=53.1, [9, 1] t=106.1, [9, 6] t=136.1, [9, 0] t=171.2) and once off to the right ([16, 6] t=223.7, plus a
  sixth 4-elixir drop at t=239.2 at [9, 7]). He treats it as a defensive spawner with chip as a bonus: "this
  is where the deck gets super annoying. Even though the furnace did get nerfed, guys, it's still actually
  extremely powerful because you just kind of use it as a broken defensive unit now and don't really worry
  about getting any offensive pressure with it" (t=173-183). The chip still mattered — "we're getting fire
  spirit jumps on the tower. Look at all that damage" (t=156) as the enemy right tower fell 3531 -> 2633.
- **[Berserker](../cards/berserker.md)** (2, [Heroic](../heroes/berserker-hero.md)) — the melee answer, placed
  both deep ([8, 5] t=44) and at the bridge on top of a Hogs set ([9, 14] t=116.8, [3, 15] t=189.2, [3, 14]
  t=205.2, [12, 15] t=227.2, [16, 6] t=241.2). The Savage Survival ability (3 elixir) is used twice and both
  times explained: to survive a Giant Skeleton — "I'm going to berserker ability as well. It's going to keep
  this alive and make sure to take out the giant skelly or at least most of it" (t=150-154) — and to keep her
  hitting the tower in overtime, "Let's pop the berserker ability... The berserker's on the tower" (t=251-253).
  At t=235 he also says "I'm not going to pop the ability this time", so it is a considered spend, not a
  reflex.
- **[Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md)** (1) — pure cycle and distraction, dropped at [6, 8] (t=12.3),
  [3, 14] (t=49), [11, 8] (t=141.1), [9, 15] (t=181.2), [9, 11] (t=193.2), [3, 14] (t=220.2) and twice more
  off HUD at t=227.4 and t=243.2. Twice they are the king-activation / lane-transfer tool: "Let's go for the
  skeletons here and then we can go for the barbarian barrel plus berserker" (t=140-146) and "let's skellies
