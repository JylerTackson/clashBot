# Match: Classic Log Bait RETURNS to Clash Royale!! — game 3

## Summary

Ryley plays the video's single list, classic log bait: [Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md)
(Evolution), [Princess](../cards/princess.md) (Evolution), [Goblin Gang](../cards/goblin-gang.md),
[Knight](../cards/knight.md), [Ice Spirit](../cards/ice-spirit.md),
[Inferno Tower](../cards/inferno-tower.md), [Rocket](../cards/rocket.md) and
[The Log](../cards/the-log.md) — a [bait](../archetypes/bait.md) deck whose three cheap threats all
want the same small spell. He identifies the opponent immediately at t=528: "this guy's just playing a
hog EQ", and the events bear that out — [Hog Rider](../cards/hog-rider.md),
[Earthquake](../cards/earthquake.md), an Evo [Firecracker](../cards/firecracker.md),
[Mighty Miner](../cards/mighty-miner.md) with its ability, an Evo [Cannon](../cards/cannon.md),
[Barbarian Barrel](../cards/barbarian-barrel.md), [Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md) and
[Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md), i.e. the
[Hog MM EvoCannon 2.6 Cycle](../decks/barbarian-barrel-cannon-earthquake-electro-spirit-firecracker-hog-rider-mighty-miner-skeletons.md)
shell ([cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md)). The game is decided by Ryley's offence, not his defence: an
opening Evo Barrel for ~600 damage (clock 2:50), a deliberately over-aggressive Evo Barrel + Log while
low on elixir at clock 1:54 (enemy right 4858 -> 4222), and then a long overtime sequence of
Knight-at-the-bridge, Goblin Gang, Evo Barrel and Evo Princess that walks the enemy right tower from
3953 down to 1322. He is behind on damage at the end of regular time ("He actually is barely in the lead
now, which is super unfortunate", t=684) and wins it in overtime, closing with Inferno Tower and a Rocket
at t=805.8: "we can go for a rocket, guys, and we actually won this game. That was such good offense,
man. Wow. I didn't even have to like do really good defense in rocket cycle."

## Data gaps

- **Deck override.** The video-level consensus deck lists Ice Golem in place of Rocket. This game's
  evidence goes the other way: Rocket has a clean deploy label at t=805.8 ([12, 24]) and two commentary
  references ("we can go for a rocket", t=804; "rocket cycle", t=811), while Ice Golem never appears in a
  deploy label or in the commentary — its 6 "own Ice Golem" events (t=645.6, 652.7, 688.0, 750.8, 763.9,
  784.5) are all HUD-only. The per-game read (with Rocket) is used here.
- **Hand reads are unreliable this game** (hand confidence 0.525). The HUD hand repeatedly lists cards not
  in the deck (Mega Knight, Royal Giant, Suspicious Bush, Zappies, Golem, Elite Barbarians, Goblinstein,
  Skeleton Dragons). Consequently the HUD-sourced "own plays" of Mega Minion (t=523.9, 627.5, 709.5),
  Tombstone (t=554.6, 557.4, 570.1), Giant Snowball (t=578.9, 629.3, 791.0), Zap (t=580.9, 714.5),
  Valkyrie (t=677.8, 803.8, 806.6), Goblinstein (t=703.9), Ice Golem and the eight Electro Spirit events
  are treated as misreads and are not used above; the elixir drops they carry are real, the card names are
  not.
- **Unidentified events (10).** Own elixir dropped with no readable hand change at t=611.4 (-2), t=612.9
  (-2), t=639.8 (-4), t=689.4 (-2) and t=716.9 (-2). Opponent spell damage was inferred from tower HP with
  no unit in range at t=663.7 (own right -306), t=702.7 (own left -159) and t=734.7 (own right -507) —
  consistent with Earthquake and Evo Firecracker chip, but the card was never read.
- **Hero variant unresolved.** The header flags that Knight may be the [Hero Knight](../heroes/knight-hero.md)
  (2-elixir "Triumphant Taunt"). The four unexplained 2-elixir drops above are the only candidate evidence,
  and two of them are 1.5s apart (t=611.4 / t=612.9), which an ability cooldown would not allow. Ryley says
  "Ability keep this alive. Come on. Nice, good ability" at t=756-758, but he uses the same word for the
  opponent's Mighty Miner ability at t=634 and t=721, so this cannot be resolved from the context. Knight
  is recorded as the ordinary card.

  Princess, Cannon, Earthquake, Firecracker, Mighty Miner) is partly Ryley's own cards mis-sided: every

  "opponent Goblins" (t=522.5, 645.6, 750.8, 776.8) labels are likewise on his own half. The "Prince"
