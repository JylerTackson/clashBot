# Match: This Piggies Lightning Deck is UNSTOPPABLE!! — game 4

## Summary

Fourth game of the single-deck session (video 671.8s-875.4s), read from the opening seconds
(clock 2:47, "start off with the Royal Hogs here", t=675) through triple elixir and into overtime.
Ryley is on the video's deck: [Royal Hogs](../cards/royal-hogs.md) (evolved),
[Furnace](../cards/furnace.md), [Cannon](../cards/cannon.md) (evolved),
[Berserker](../cards/berserker.md), [Skeletons](../cards/skeletons.md),
[Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md), [Barbarian Barrel](../cards/barbarian-barrel.md) and
[Lightning](../cards/lightning.md) — a 3.0-elixir [cycle](../archetypes/cycle.md) list whose whole plan
he states out loud at t=765-783: "always abusing our cycle and our ability to even counter his piggies for
cheaper than he's able to counter ours ... not only are we defending cheaper but we're also cycling more
cards so we're able to outcycle him."
It is close to a mirror match. He first guesses "this guy's playing Expo 3.0" (t=685), corrects himself
two plays later — "never mind, he's playing royal hogs, not expo" (t=692) — and adds "this actually the
deck that we just played against not too long ago ... having a fine match up against this deck is very,
very important" (t=697-705). The opponent runs Royal Hogs of his own plus
[Archers](../cards/archers.md), [Bomb Tower](../cards/bomb-tower.md),
[Wall Breakers](../cards/wall-breakers.md), [Barbarians](../cards/barbarians.md),
[Guards](../cards/guards.md), [Earthquake](../cards/earthquake.md) and (from Ryley's commentary, never
seen cast) a Lightning of his own.
Ryley defends every hog wave with 1-3 elixir of Skeletons / Electro Spirit / Berserker / Barbarian Barrel,
keeps a Furnace alive on the board almost continuously, and answers each defended push with piggies at the
bridge. No tower fell in regular time (own 4858 / 4504, enemy 3019 / 4133 at clock 0:05), the game went to
overtime, and Furnace chip plus one more Royal Hogs wave took the enemy left tower from 3019 to 1727 before
the opponent quit: "And he gives up. Let's go. Very nice win ... This was just perfect game play."
(t=865-872).

## How Ryley uses his cards

  once (t=784.3) purely to force him below Bomb Tower cost. He names the rule at t=719-721 ("time my Royal
  Hogs with when he's out cycling us so I don't take too much damage") and tracks the evolution charge out

  protect the furnace".
