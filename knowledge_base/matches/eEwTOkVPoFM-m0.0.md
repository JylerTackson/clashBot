# Match: Ronin Made This Log Bait Deck 100X MORE BROKEN — game 1

**Clock caveat:** the in-game clock OCR failed for this entire video, so every timeline row has no clock and
the seven games were split by tower-HP resets. All timestamps below are **video seconds**; the clock is
unknown throughout.

## Summary

This is the opening game of a single-deck session, and Ryley names the list in the first twenty seconds:
"we are going to be playing the best Ronin log bait deck in Clash Royale... here we have this log bait Ronin
deck here with the wall breakers and with the magic archer" (t=6-17). He pilots the video-level consensus
eight — [Goblin Barrel](../cards/goblin-barrel.md), [Wall Breakers](../cards/wall-breakers.md),
[Ronin](../cards/ronin.md), [Magic Archer](../cards/magic-archer.md), [Bomb Tower](../cards/bomb-tower.md),
[Royal Delivery](../cards/royal-delivery.md), [Ice Spirit](../cards/ice-spirit.md) and
[Fire Spirit](../cards/fire-spirit.md) — a [bait](../archetypes/bait.md) shell at 2.9 average elixir with a
5-elixir mini-tank bolted into the win-condition slot. The opponent is a Mega Knight / Lightning list he
identifies on sight and then criticises: "I'm pretty sure this guy plays mega knight lightning, but without
the Evo mega knight he has like Evo ghost and Evo snowball. Very strange deck in my opinion" (t=52-57);
the tracker also shows [Little Prince](../cards/little-prince.md), [Royal Ghost](../cards/royal-ghost.md),
[Ram Rider](../cards/ram-rider.md), [Electro Spirit](../cards/electro-spirit.md) and
[Goblins](../cards/goblins.md). For the first two minutes it is a grind — his own left tower falls
4858 -> 3692 while the enemy right tower loses only 384 HP to one barrel — and then the game turns in a
fifteen-second window from t=118 to t=142: the opponent commits a [Mega Knight](../cards/mega-knight.md) in
the lane opposite the Ronin, Ryley answers with a full barrel + Wall Breakers + spirits sequence, and the
enemy left tower drops 4858 -> 277 while his own King Tower is activated by the incoming damage. He calls it
himself at t=143-151: "What a clean sequence man. Out of all of that we took his entire tower and we activated
our king all because he wanted to go aggressive and mega knight opposite lane of the Ronin." The game ends
"Let's go. Beautiful win" (t=171).
