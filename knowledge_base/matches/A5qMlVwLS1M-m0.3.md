# Match: Playing the 8 BEST Cards in a SINGLE Deck — game 4

## Summary

Game 4 of a seven-game single-deck session (video 733.9s-914.9s, 181 s readable), against a player Ryley
names as "Endrish" (t=735). The list is the video's premise — the eight highest-rated cards on RoyaleAPI
crammed into one deck: [Elite Barbarians](../cards/elite-barbarians.md) (evolved),
[Battle Ram](../cards/battle-ram.md) (evolved), [Barbarian Barrel](../cards/barbarian-barrel.md),
[Cannon Cart](../cards/cannon-cart.md), [Heal Spirit](../cards/heal-spirit.md), [Zap](../cards/zap.md),
[Mother Witch](../cards/mother-witch.md) and [Goblinstein](../cards/goblinstein.md). Ryley's own read of it,
delivered over the opening thirty seconds, is that it accidentally works: "this just looks like a regular
very solid [bridge spam](../archetypes/bridge-spam.md) deck, but it is literally the eight highest ranked
cards in the game... I'm going to call this a perfect deck" (t=749-760). The opponent he cannot place —
"I'm really not sure what this guy is using to be honest. He might be playing that one
[control](../archetypes/control.md) deck where he doesn't even have a win condition and he literally just
has a Tombstone, Skeleton Queen for a win condition" (t=811-830). The game is one-sided from the first
exchange: a bridge Elite Barbarians / Battle Ram pair takes the enemy left tower from 4858 to 2885 before
clock 2:11, a second Ebarbs-at-the-bridge push with Heal Spirit adds "a thousand damage" (t=796-799), and a
Battle Ram plus Barbarian Barrel plus Zap sequence around clock 0:55 grinds the same tower from 2287 down to
376. Ryley's own left tower reads 4818 of 4858 at the end. He calls the result on air at t=902-908 — "He's
calling it good game. He kind of knows it... And that is good game. Very beautiful win. Man, we are
destroying everybody. This guy only got a [little] on my tower."

- t=740-760 — the premise, and why the list is coherent rather than random: "these are the eight like highest

  elixir: "Let's go for the Evo Ebs here at the bridge again because he did use a little bit of stuff... I was
  thinking that he did use a lot of good cards for the Ebarbs, so just going them at the bridge could get us

  he has to defend the barbarians again."

- t=883-885 — hold your defensive card for the threat you have already read: "by later, I'll have the Ebarbs
