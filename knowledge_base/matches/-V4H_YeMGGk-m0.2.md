- 647-664s, on Void's tower damage: "they are also doing a Crown Tower damage buff. - 667-691s, why the buff exists: "Massive buff to Void, but although it does take, you know, two more Elixir to play every single time. And I guess the reason behind it was that there's never been a five Elixir spell before and there has been, you know, a lot of cards in the game that were only able to be taken out by the six Elixir spells like Rocket and Lightning and Clash Royale just wants like experiment, I guess, with having a lower Elixir cost than six on the spells, but also taking out those units, but not being as effective overall."
- 694-712s, on the **Hero Mega Minion**: "they are doing a rework to the hero mega minion. They are making the hero mega minion have a tower damage reduction from 15% to 27% which I very much agree with that. There was nothing more annoying than just having a hero mega minion just completely destroy your tower. I hated that so much man. It was one of the most annoying things possible to happen in the game." And 713-719s: "the tower damage reduction is from 5 seconds to now permanent."
- 721-742s, the Mega Minion teleport: "they are changing the teleport damage from 373 to 399 which is a 7% buff which I think is fair cuz they did definitely hurt the mega minion all over the board but it will change some key interactions because before this mega minion was not able to take out a lot of units with a mega minion plus a spell. But now it will actually be able to do that making it a lot better offensively but not like as annoying against crown towers."
- 754-767s, uncertainty about the next batch: "they are also doing a bunch of balances to the mortar, the barbarians and the goblins. It affects so many different cards and dude truthfully this is the one that I am most unsure about to be honest cuz there's just so many different things that are being affected at once and I really don't know how I agree with this."
- 769-796s, on **Mortar** and the hero/evo problem generally: "the hero and the evo versions of these cards were very much overshadowing the regular version. So they are nerfing the mortar hit speed or the evolved mortar hit speed from 4 seconds to 4.7 seconds but they are changing the regular mortar hit speed from 4 seconds to 4.7 seconds. So now they are equal instead of being different."
- 797-799s, cut off at the game boundary: "Guys now to the barbarians. The barbarians are also getting toned down."
