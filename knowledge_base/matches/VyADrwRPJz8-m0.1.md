## Lessons stated by Ryley

- 277-284s — protect the Magic Archer rather than the tower in front of it: "Make sure that we keep this magic archer completely healthy because now he's going to have to block for the magic archer, otherwise it will line up on that bowler. That's where the magic archer can get so annoying."
- 290-294s — a defensive building placed early also removes an offensive option from the opponent: "I'm going to go for the bomb tower early here just to help take this out. And he can't really go for the loon on the left because we have the magic archer ready."
- 296-299s — do not spend elixir defending a building that has already done its job: "I'm not even going to protect the bomb tower from the bowler. There's no point."
- 306-312s — an over-aggressive opponent play is elixir you no longer have to spend: "That baby dragon was a bit aggressive in my opinion. No need. Just gave me extra value cuz I was going to have to spend a lot of elixir on defense."
- 312-318s — count the opponent's cycle before committing: "he's one card off of the barbarian brill [Barbarian Barrel]. So, I'm just going to go for the wall breakers plus [goblin barrel]."
- 327-335s — track the removal spell, not just the threat: "I do have to watch out for a potential inferno dragon behind the bowler. But because he has no [Tornado], he's not going to be able to take out our magic archer."
- 359-362s — a damage lead converts an opponent's all-in into a loss: "with all the damage that he's took, I think he's just going to lose if he goes all in."
- 381-383s — cheap pressure has value even when it does not connect, because it drains answers: "Just keep up the pressure cuz he's going to have to spend at least a spell on this, if not a bowler."
- 389-392s — never concede while your evolutions are in cycle: "He has double evos. I guess there's no point to ever give up when you have your evos in cycle."
