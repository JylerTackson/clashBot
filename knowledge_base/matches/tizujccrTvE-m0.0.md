This is the opening game of the video and Ryley names the deck himself in the first ten seconds: "I'm going
to be playing the Ronin deck that got me a 96% win rate in Clash Royale" (t=6-11), adding "I currently have a

[Fire Spirit](../cards/fire-spirit.md) — every one of the eight is confirmed by an in-game deploy label at

guy has a Golem deck") and refines it at t=55 ("this guy's playing with Golem Healer"), and the arena tracker
later confirms [Golem](../cards/golem.md), [Battle Healer](../cards/battle-healer.md),
[Baby Dragon](../cards/baby-dragon.md), [Electro Dragon](../cards/electro-dragon.md),
[Elixir Collector](../cards/elixir-collector.md), [Poison](../cards/poison.md) and [Zap](../cards/zap.md).
Ryley never lets the beatdown deck build: he trades Wall Breakers and Goblin Barrels into the left tower
almost from the start (enemy left 4,858 HP at t=10 down to 301 by t=90), keeps the Ronin cycling as both
defensive anchor and bridge threat, then swings the right lane with a Magic Archer over the bridge in triple
elixir. By the last readable frames both enemy Crown Towers are at roughly 300 HP while Ryley's own left
tower has only lost about 1,100 HP and his right tower is untouched. He closes the game with "Easy first win
though. I will see you all in the next match" (t=154-158), so the result is a win.
