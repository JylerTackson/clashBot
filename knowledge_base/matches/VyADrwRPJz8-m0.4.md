mirror: Evolved [Tesla](../cards/tesla.md), Evolved Goblin Barrel, [Princess](../cards/princess.md),

so broken... Just march on the princess and this is game over, man" (t=1065-1078), then "Very nice win. And I will
see you all in the next match" (t=1091-1093).
