## Summary

Game 4 of an eight-game single-deck session, played against an opponent Ryley calls "Andrew".
He is on the video's consensus log-bait list — Goblin Barrel, Wall Breakers, Magic Archer,
Ronin, Tesla, Royal Delivery, Skeletons, Fire Spirit — and the per-game HUD read matches it
exactly. The Magic Archer is the **Hero** variant: Ryley repeatedly talks about being able to
"step back" with it (t=725, t=776), which is the Triple Threat teleport-and-decoy, and three
own-elixir drops with no hand change (2, 2 and 3 elixir at t=824, t=828, t=837) sit where an
ability activation would. The opponent plays an Electro Giant beatdown shell with Phoenix,
Bowler, Tombstone, Evolution Archers, Barbarian Barrel, Tornado and Lightning.

The window covers the whole game from clock 2:52 through overtime. Ryley opens with a probing
Goblin Barrel and a deliberately aggressive early Magic Archer, and the game is effectively
decided at clock 1:50-1:39: the opponent commits an early Electro Giant, spends Barbarian
Barrel on the Magic Archer instead of the barrel, and a Wall Breakers + Goblin Barrel double
takes both enemy towers from 4400/4527 to 2304/1907 in about four seconds. Ryley's verdict —
"this guy just got completely cooked already... early E-Giants against this type of log bait
deck is just like a death wish." From there he cycles cheap defence (Skeletons, Fire Spirit,
Tesla, Royal Delivery) against Phoenix, Bowler and Archers while chipping with barrels, drives
both enemy towers to 610 and 225 HP by overtime, and closes on "that's good game. Let's go.
Beautiful magic archer geometry." No tower is read as destroyed inside the window, but the
towers, the sign-off and the immediate cut to the next match make this a win.
