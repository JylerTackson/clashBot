\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:concl}

\cb{} shows that a non-embedded agent can play Clash Royale live on a laptop, with knowledge-graph, retrieval and simulation tools feeding a fine-tuned LLM with the final say, at 323\,ms from capture to click. Fine-tuning turned a chance-level LLM into one that picks the pro's play half the time, but tuned simple models match it, it ignores the tools' notes, and the agent does not yet beat Training Camp reliably (one early win, then 0/10). No zero-shot LLM comes within 14 points of the fine-tuned d1 (Claude Sonnet 5.5 36.4\%, Qwen3-14B 30.4\%, Claude Haiku 5.5 27.9\%; chance 25\%), nor any KB or KG method alone within 18. % src: decision_models_v1.md (Sonnet-long diff -0.142, the smallest zero-shot gap; 0.364, 0.304, 0.279, 0.250); retrieval_methods_v1.md (smallest delta vs d1: BM25 -0.189)
Next steps: fix the trial failures of Sec.~\ref{sec:disc} and re-run the 10-battle trial with automatic result logging, measure perception against labelled frames, and train d1 on lines where notes and leak-free KG facts carry signal the state lines do not.
